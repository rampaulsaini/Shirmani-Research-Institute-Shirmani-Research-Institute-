About Integrated Human Development Model (IHDM)

The Integrated Human Development Model (IHDM) is a holistic framework designed to cultivate cognitive clarity, emotional balance, identity awareness, and ethical social integration in individuals and communities.

Founder

Shirmani RamPal Saini – Initiator of IHDM

Vision

To develop conscious individuals capable of rational thought, emotional stability, and ethical social engagement.

Mission

  • Enhance cognitive and emotional growth
  • Encourage self-awareness and reflection
  • Promote ethical social responsibility
  • Build a global conscious community
