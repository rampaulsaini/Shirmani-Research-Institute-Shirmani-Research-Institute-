## 2. Initial customer segments
1. Small businesses and consultants needing bilingual website/blog content.
2. NGOs, educators, and community organizations needing sourced research summaries.
3. Researchers and founders needing a background brief or literature scan.
4. Agencies needing overflow Hindi/English writing support.

Start with a small, relevant list. Use public contact information only where appropriate, respect privacy laws, anti-spam rules, recipient preferences, and platform terms. Do not bulk-blast email or WhatsApp. Identify the sender, explain relevance, and honor opt-outs. Keep human approval before external messages.

## 3. Sales process
1. **Discover:** Capture buyer, goal, audience, language, intended use, source material, word count, deadline, budget, and approver.
2. **Qualify:** Confirm capability and timeline; decline deceptive or unsupported requests.
3. **Quote:** Write deliverables, exclusions, price, timeline, revision count, usage rights, confidentiality, and payment milestones.
4. **Approve & deposit:** Start only after written acceptance and agreed initial payment.
5. **Deliver:** Provide editable files, references, evidence/uncertainty labels, and the agreed revision.
6. **Close:** Record acceptance; request optional honest feedback. Obtain permission before publishing a case study.
7. **Repeat:** Offer a recurring package only after successful delivery.

## 6. 30-day validation plan
**Week 1 — Prepare:** Publish a clear service page, 2–3 labelled illustrative samples, enquiry form, quote template, terms, and verified contact route. Samples must not be represented as client work.

**Week 2 — Outreach:** Research 30 relevant prospects; send 5–10 personalized messages per working day where appropriate. Track replies, opt-outs, and next steps. No mass blast.

**Week 3 — Sell and deliver:** Hold discovery conversations, issue scoped quotes, collect agreed deposits, and deliver only approved work. Ask for feedback.

**Week 4 — Review:** Calculate actual funnel metrics and change the offer based on evidence.

**Learning targets, not forecasts:** By day 30, aim to research 30 relevant prospects, get 3 qualified conversations, send 2 scoped quotes, and complete 1 paid pilot if demand exists. Report actual results honestly, including zero.

## 7. Weekly scorecard
Track: prospects researched; personalized messages sent; replies; qualified conversations; quotes sent; quotes accepted; deposits received; jobs delivered on time; revision rate; **cash collected**; direct costs; hours per job; customer satisfaction/repeat intent; opt-outs and complaints.

- Collected revenue = payments actually received, not unpaid quotes.
- Gross contribution = collected revenue − direct delivery costs − transaction fees.
- Quote conversion = accepted quotes ÷ quotes sent.
- On-time rate = jobs delivered by deadline ÷ jobs due.
- Effective hourly contribution = gross contribution ÷ delivery hours.

Restrict access to customer data and define a retention/deletion policy.

## 8. Product and presentation release gates
Do not market planned systems as finished products. Each product needs a working demo, documented tests, version/scope/limitations, privacy/security review, accessibility checks, support terms, human approval before sending messages/charging/publishing, and rollback path.

For avatar/voice presentation, test authorization, speech recognition, Q&A, evidence handling, voice output, lip-sync, latency, and end-to-end behavior. A video URL alone does not prove live Q&A or a working end-to-end integration. Label status accurately: planned, prototype, tested, or production-verified.

## 9. Automission loop
1. Collect inbound enquiry or prospect.
2. Deduplicate and check opt-out/suppression status.
3. Draft a personalized response from approved service information.
4. Flag unsupported claims and missing details.
5. Require human approval before sending or quoting.
6. Track replies, quotes, payments, delivery, and revisions.
7. Produce a weekly scorecard.
8. Improve copy based on measured results; never auto-send to large lists without compliance checks and operator review.
