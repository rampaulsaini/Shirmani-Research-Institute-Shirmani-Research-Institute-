Sample 02 · Research-summary format

Research question: What decision should this summary help the reader make?

  1. Scope: topic, audience, date range, and boundaries.
  2. Sources: source title, publisher, URL, publication date, and access date where available.
  3. Findings: concise statements linked to the relevant sources.
  4. Limitations: missing data, conflicting information, and unresolved questions.
  5. Next steps: what can be decided now and what needs further research.

यह structure demonstration है, completed source-verified research report नहीं। वास्तविक स्रोत और निष्कर्ष agreed client brief के अनुसार जोड़े जाएंगे।

Sample 03 · A respectful client proposal

Objective: Prepare a concise Hindi/English page that explains the service, intended audience, and next step.

Proposed deliverable: One agreed draft, headings, a short call-to-action, and one revision round.

Before starting: Confirm audience, source requirements, word count, delivery date, price, revision terms, and payment conditions in writing.

Acceptance check: Match the agreed scope, proofread language, check source links when applicable, disclose uncertainty, and deliver in the agreed format.
