Four-stage production pipeline

Honest release states

Scheduled means a workflow ran. Produced means an artifact exists. QC passed means documented checks passed. Independent verification requires reproducible evidence. These states are not interchangeable. No fabricated reviews, guarantees, partner endorsements, or test results.
