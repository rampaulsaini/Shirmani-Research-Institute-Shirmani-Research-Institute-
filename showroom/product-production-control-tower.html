Release checklist

  • Artifact works and public link opens.
  • Visual is a real product-specific screenshot or image; QR target resolves to the correct product passport.
  • Demo video actually exists, or a demo script is clearly labelled as a script.
  • Usage instructions and limitations are visible.
  • QC code points to recorded tests; blocked tests remain blocked.
  • Price/offer and enquiry/order path are clear before customer commitment.
  • Paid order and revenue are recorded only from actual payment/order evidence.
  • Real customer feedback becomes a versioned improvement task.
