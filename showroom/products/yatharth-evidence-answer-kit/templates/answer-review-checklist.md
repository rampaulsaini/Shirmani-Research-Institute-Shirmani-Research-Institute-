# Answer review checklist
- [ ] Question and audience are clear.
- [ ] Important factual claims map to a source or reproducible check.
- [ ] Source date and scope support the claim.
- [ ] Quotations and paraphrases are attributed accurately.
- [ ] Interpretation and philosophical statements are labelled.
- [ ] Uncertainty is explicit; unsupported claims say “Insufficient evidence available.”
- [ ] Independently checked claims include a reproducible method and reviewer.
- [ ] No fabricated ratings, testimonials, guarantees, partnerships, or test results.
- [ ] Language is clear, accessible, and respectful.
- [ ] Limitations and version are disclosed.
- [ ] Links work and the published artifact matches this release.
