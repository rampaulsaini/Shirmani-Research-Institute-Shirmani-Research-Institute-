2. Five explanation layers

  1. Plain answer: answer the main question directly.
  2. Everyday understanding: explain unfamiliar words with a simple example.
  3. Reasoning and evidence: separate facts, assumptions and interpretation.
  4. Expert view: add technical terms or equations only when supported and useful.
  5. Action and audit: say what to do next and what remains untested.
