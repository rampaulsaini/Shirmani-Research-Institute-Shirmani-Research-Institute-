05 · Explanation standard

Plain answer

Start with the direct answer in everyday words.

Everyday example

Use a familiar example; explain that an analogy illustrates but does not prove.

Reasoning & evidence

Separate observation, source, inference and personal philosophy.

Expert layer

Use technical terms and equations only when justified; define variables and limitations.

The character's quality is measured by clarity, fairness, evidence, uncertainty honesty and correction—not by claims of guaranteed influence over every listener.
