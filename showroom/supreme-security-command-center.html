Incident quick response

  1. Contain the affected workflow, deployment, token or integration using the relevant official console.
  2. Revoke and rotate suspected exposed credentials; do not copy them into issue comments or logs.
  3. Preserve timestamps, run IDs and relevant logs while limiting access to sensitive evidence.
  4. Assess affected releases, customer data and downstream integrations; notify the appropriate parties when required.
  5. Restore from a known-good version, verify the fix, and record a prevention task.

If a credential may have been exposed, assume it is compromised. Deleting the visible text alone is not sufficient; revoke/rotate it at the provider.
