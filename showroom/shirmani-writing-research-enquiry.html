How an enquiry becomes a deliverable / काम कैसे होगा

  1. Share the brief. Tell us the purpose, topic, language and intended reader.
  2. Confirm scope and price. We first agree in writing on deliverables, word or page range, schedule, revisions and price.
  3. Start after approval. Work begins only after the scope and payment terms are agreed.
  4. Review and deliver. The agreed output is supplied for review; any extra work is discussed before it is added.

पहले काम का दायरा और कीमत लिखित रूप में तय होंगे। कोई तयशुदा मूल्य, ग्राहक, testimonial, certification या बिक्री का दावा नहीं किया जा रहा।

Quality and evidence / गुणवत्ता और प्रमाण

  • Research claims should be traceable to the sources actually consulted.
  • Opinions, user-provided perspectives, and independently checked facts should be distinguished.
  • Unverified details are labelled as such; sources or credentials are never invented.
  • Confidential or sensitive material should not be submitted unless a suitable handling arrangement has been agreed.

गुणवत्ता का अर्थ केवल आकर्षक भाषा नहीं—सही दायरा, स्रोतों की जाँच, स्पष्ट सीमाएँ और समय पर तय deliverables भी हैं।
