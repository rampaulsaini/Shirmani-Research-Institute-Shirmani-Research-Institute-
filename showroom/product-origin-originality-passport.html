3 · What this protects — and what it cannot

Useful controls

Stable IDs, semantic versions, timestamped records, commit/evidence references, licence boundaries, and repeatable SHA-256 fingerprints make later comparison and auditing easier.

Not a magic anti-copy lock

A JSON passport or hash alone does not prove legal ownership, prevent copying, timestamp independently, encrypt private data, or establish infringement. For high-value work, preserve original files and dated records, restrict source access, use suitable contracts/licensing, and seek qualified legal advice when needed.

Release rule: do not publish private voice/photo source files, consent documents, credentials, customer information or internal security details in the public passport. Share only approved references and record what evidence is genuinely available.
