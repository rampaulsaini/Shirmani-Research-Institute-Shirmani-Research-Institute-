Sample excerpt · English

Why research summaries should show their sources

A useful research summary does more than shorten a long document. It separates the main claim from the evidence supporting it, links readers to original material, and explains important limitations. When sources disagree, the summary should describe that disagreement rather than hide it. Readers can then judge what is known, what remains uncertain, and what should be checked next.
