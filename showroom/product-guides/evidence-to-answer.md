# Evidence-to-Answer Brief

**Product ID:** EVD-005 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Readiness:** Prototype / specification

## Brief template
- Question / decision
- Claim
- Source and publication date
- Evidence excerpt or data location
- Confidence and rationale
- Limitations / counter-evidence
- What remains unknown

## How to use
Keep sourced facts separate from inference and philosophical statements. If evidence is insufficient, state that plainly. A source link alone does not mean independent verification has been completed.

## Limitations
This is a structured research template, not an autonomous fact-checker. Demo video not yet published.
