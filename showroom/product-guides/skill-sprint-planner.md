# Supreme Skill Sprint Planner

**Product ID:** SKL-003 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Readiness:** Specification

## How to use
1. Choose one skill and define an observable result.
2. Schedule short practice sessions.
3. Mix practice, retrieval, and feedback.
4. Record evidence of progress, not just time spent.
5. Review weekly and adjust task difficulty.

## Limitations
This is a planning specification; no hosted coaching engine or demo video is published yet.
