# Digital Product Passport Builder

**Product ID:** TOOL-004 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Readiness:** Prototype / specification

## Passport fields
Product ID, name, version, owner, purpose, intended audience, requirements, limitations, data handling, usage guide, demo URL, price/currency, support route, release notes, and readiness status.

## Release checklist
- Ensure links work and claims are supported.
- Keep prices, licence, refund terms, and support scope explicit.
- Do not label a specification as production-ready.
- Add a product-specific demo and screenshots before claiming they exist.

## Limitations
This page defines the passport format; a fully interactive passport builder and demo video are not yet published.
