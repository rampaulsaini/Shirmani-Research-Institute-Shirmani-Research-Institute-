# Yatharth Fair-Decision Worksheet

**Product ID:** NYA-002 · **Version:** 0.1.0 · **Readiness:** Specification

## How to use
1. Define the decision and deadline.
2. List realistic options, including doing nothing.
3. Record evidence and source dates for each key claim.
4. Note affected people, risks, unknowns, and trade-offs.
5. Explain the chosen option and what would change the decision.

## Limitations
This is a structured worksheet, not a court, lawyer, or automated legal decision-maker. It must not be represented as legal advice. Demo video not yet published.
