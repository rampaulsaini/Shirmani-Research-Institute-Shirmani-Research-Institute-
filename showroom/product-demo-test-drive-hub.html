    Release checklist for each product

    □ Real product page opens and main task works
    □ Mobile viewport checked
    □ Keyboard and readable labels checked
    □ Screenshot captured from the actual product
    □ Product-specific demo video recorded and reviewed
    □ Product passport, QR destination and limitations checked
    □ QC/gate and dispatch states match reality
    □ Price, offer and order/payment route tested

    A checked item is a human-recorded task, not an automated certificate. Keep screenshot/MP4 assets and their paths in the product passport before marking them published.
