Five checks before sharing online information

  1. Find the original source rather than relying only on a repost.
  2. Check publication date and context.
  3. Review the author or organisation and relevant expertise.
  4. Compare with independent, credible sources where available.
  5. If evidence is incomplete, state the uncertainty instead of presenting the claim as established fact.
