## Institutional form

Shirmani Research Institute is maintained in this project as a **fully digital research institute**.

This designation describes the project's engineering and knowledge-delivery form. It does not imply a physical campus, building, laboratory, office, or geographic facility.

## Digital-first architecture

The institute's operational surface is the digital repository and its connected, traceable research infrastructure, including:

- protected user-source material;
- provenance and canonical records;
- reasoning and evidence structures;
- verification queues and registries;
- deterministic quality-control tooling;
- research-evidence graph structures;
- review packets and fail-closed promotion gates;
- machine-readable schemas and generated artifacts;
- digital publication and web interfaces;
- reproducible continuity checkpoints and handoffs.

## Preservation boundary

The protected user-source layer remains unchanged.

Preservation rule:

**Preserve first. Reason second. Verify third. Transform only as a traceable derivative.**

No generated engineering artifact may silently replace, normalize, shorten, reinterpret, or overwrite protected user-source material.

## Verification boundary

Digital infrastructure, hashes, generated records, QC passes, or repository commits do not by themselves constitute independent scientific or substantive verification.

Any VERIFIED state requires genuine review evidence under the repository's fail-closed verification policy.
