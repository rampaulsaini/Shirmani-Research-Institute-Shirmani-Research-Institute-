# AI-Agent Governance — Yatharth Yug Foundation

## Purpose

This repository may use multi-level, multi-layer AI-agent automation to preserve, organize, transform, test, package, and publish the project's research and livelihood materials. The governing foundation is **निष्पक्ष समझ**: inspect claims and outputs through explicit evidence, provenance, reproducible rules, and traceable review rather than treating authority, generated text, or model confidence as proof.

The phrase **सर्बभौमिक सत्य प्रत्यक्ष समक्ष शाश्वत वास्तविक स्वाभाविक सत्य** is preserved here as a framework proposition and philosophical foundation. It is not silently converted into an independently verified empirical claim.

## Agent hierarchy

1. Intake / Source — collect and canonicalize inputs.
2. Reasoning — derive claims, hypotheses, countercases, and formulations.
3. Evidence — attach source references and provenance.
4. Verification — test whether evidence supports a claim; default to NOT_VERIFIED when evidence is insufficient.
5. Product — package approved derivatives such as digital products, services, music, documentation, and archive assets.
6. Marketing — prepare truthful descriptions, SEO, creatives, and campaign drafts without fabricated testimonials, sales, statistics, or guarantees.
7. Economic / Transaction — prepare external-service payloads only through explicitly authorized integrations.
8. Security / Audit — validate permissions, provenance, integrity, policy compliance, and continuity.
9. Publishing — publish only artifacts that pass the applicable gates.
10. Continuity — resume deterministic work from durable state without duplicating or silently skipping queue segments.

## Authorization boundary

AI agents are operators inside defined policies, not independent owners of the repository or arbiters of truth.

- The repository owner remains the authorization authority for irreversible repository, financial, legal, account, or external-service actions.
- Automation must use least-privilege credentials.
- Generated output must carry provenance or an explicit reason for being non-provenance-bearing.
- Unverified material must remain visibly unverified.
- No agent may promote its own output to `verified` merely because another model agrees.
- No agent may fabricate evidence, citations, experiments, transactions, customers, revenue, testimonials, or completed external actions.
- External integrations remain disabled until the required official access and secrets are actually present.
- Public pages expose results and status, never secrets or privileged control inputs.

## Multi-layer fail-closed gate

A publication path is eligible only when the relevant layers pass:

`source → provenance → reasoning → evidence → verification → QC → product/presentation → safety → publication`

If a required gate fails or evidence is missing, downstream action must stop or remain a draft.
