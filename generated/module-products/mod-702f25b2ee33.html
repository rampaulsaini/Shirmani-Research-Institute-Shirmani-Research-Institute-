# Yatharth Governance Control Plane This module translates the Yatharth / Shiromani governance research into a bounded software architecture. It is **not** a claim that a real government, judiciary, constitution, or universal service system has been deployed. ## Design objective The research direction is to move from a single family context toward a scalable public-interest model: **individual/family → community → local administration → national institutions → wider ecological and physical-world stewardship** The architecture treats “heart-view” as a research/ethical orientation—clarity, completeness, care, and impartial consideration—not as a software privilege or a license to override law, evidence, consent, or human rights. ## Institutional separation - **PM / President / elected executive:** executes lawful public policy within constitutional limits. - **Legislature:** makes and revises laws through accountable democratic procedures. - **Independent judiciary:** interprets law and decides cases independently. - **Independent oversight:** audits conflicts, corruption, rights impacts, procurement, and system failures. - **Supreme control layer:** a constitutional constraint-and-audit layer, not a person with unchecked power and not an AI sovereign. - **AI / ML / NLP / Automission:** research, evidence mapping, forecasting, translation, anomaly detection, workflow automation, and decision support. No component may make the AI the final authority over elections, courts, irreversible punishment, rights removal, or its own verification. ## Essential human needs The framework
