📈 Production-first metrics

Production

New pages, records, artifacts, reports, datasets and public deliverables.

Continuity

Recurring cycles, durable outputs and recovery from interruptions.

Traceability

Generated output points back to its source or producing module.

Quality

Tests, QC and verification evaluate produced results.

Operational rule: workflow success is not automatically scientific proof. Production, QC, evidence and independent verification remain distinct states.
