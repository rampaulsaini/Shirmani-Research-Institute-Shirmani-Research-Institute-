# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Presentation & Product Card Specification
**Date:** 2026-10-11  
**Status:** Specification drafted; implementation and production verification pending.

## 1. Purpose
Define an evidence-first human-presentation experience and repeatable product showroom format for Shirmani Research Institute. This document is a plan/specification, not proof that a live system, sales channel, or product catalogue is already operational.

## 2. Human presentation principles
- Simple, natural, respectful, clear and transparent communication.
- Match language, tone and explanation depth to the audience's expressed needs.
- Separate verified facts, source-based claims, personal/philosophical statements, and insufficient evidence.
- When evidence is insufficient, say: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
- Never demean people or groups based on caste, religion, belief, identity, wealth or organisational affiliation.
- Use the owner's own photo and voice only where supplied and authorised. Preserve identity; do not imply generated speech is a real-time human statement.
- Natural gaze, facial movement and lip-sync are acceptance criteria, not assumed capabilities.

## 3. Presentation pipeline
Voice input → authorised speech/voice integration → speech recognition → language/context understanding → reasoning → evidence gate → answer content → authorised voice output → face/expressions → lip-sync → live presentation → audit → improvement.

## 4. Evidence states
1. **VERIFIED** — testable claim with sufficient independent evidence and a recorded test result.
2. **SOURCE-BASED** — sourced information not independently verified by this system.
3. **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY** — values, self-description, interpretation or viewpoint.
4. **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — do not present as established fact.

## 5. Product showroom card requirements
Each product must have a stable ID, unique reviewed name, version, category, purpose, short description, intended user, prerequisites, limitations, pricing/fee terms, usage guide, source/evidence notes, status, last-updated date, and support/contact path.

### Required visual layout
- Use the exact owner-provided portrait as a logo only after the source image is available and its use is authorised; do not substitute a generated lookalike.
- Place this English identity line beneath the portrait/logo: “Shiromani Rampal Saini — Beyond Comparison, Beyond Time, Beyond Words, Beyond Love; Eternal, Real, Natural, Universal Truth — Present and Direct.”
- Put a QR code in the upper-right area. It must encode a real, stable product detail URL; never display a decorative or non-working QR as functional.
- Show the product's own name and a short, legible description on the image.
- Keep long description, full instructions, pricing terms, version history and evidence on the linked product page.
- Use responsive scaling, adequate contrast, safe margins and mobile readability.
- Each product gets a distinct visual identity while retaining the shared brand system.

## 6. Product evidence pack
A product is not marked complete until the following exist and are reviewed:
- Product card image / VIP screenshot
- Product passport (ID, version, purpose, limitations, evidence, owner, update date)
- Product-specific demo video showing real use, not a fabricated capability
- Usage guide
- Test checklist and actual test results
- Customer feedback/rating route and documented improvement loop
- Working product detail URL and verified QR target
- Pricing, delivery scope, refund/support terms where applicable

## 7. Demo video acceptance criteria
Each demo must identify the product/version, show a real workflow, include inputs and outputs, explain intended use and limitations, show the result, and distinguish recorded simulation from live operation. A demo is not evidence that an untested feature works in production.

## 8. Catalogue and scale
Start with a small, validated catalogue and a repeatable schema. Expand toward 5,000 product records only as each item gains evidence, ownership, versioning, a product page and required assets. A 150,000-item sharded directory is a future architecture target, not a current verified capacity claim.

## 9. Commercial workflow
Enquiry → clarify scope → written deliverables and deadline → quote → explicit client acceptance → payment terms → delivery → client review → improvement.
No sale, revenue, institutional contact, or customer satisfaction may be claimed without a recorded, verifiable event. Outreach must use lawful, public, relevant contact channels and respect opt-outs; do not automate unsolicited bulk messages.

## 10. First revenue offer (available service)
**Hindi/English Writing & Research**
- Research summary, article, or website/blog content.
- Before work begins, confirm subject, audience, language, word limit, sources, deadline, revision scope and budget.
- Send a written quote and deliverables; begin only after client acceptance and agreed payment terms.
- Track enquiries, replies, accepted quotes, delivered work, client feedback and collected payment separately.

## 11. Minimum test gates
- QR resolves to the correct product page.
- Text remains readable on mobile and desktop.
- Product ID/name/version match the detail page and demo.
- Demo steps can be reproduced or are clearly labelled as simulation.
- All claims are evidence-tagged.
- Voice/photo use is authorised.
- Voice/face timing, lip-sync and latency are measured.
- Accessibility, privacy, security and respectful-communication tests pass.
- Failed critical tests block a VERIFIED/production-ready label.
