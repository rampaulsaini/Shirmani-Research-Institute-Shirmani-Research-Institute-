Deliverable & Acceptance Quality Review

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO AUTOMATIC ACCEPTANCE

काम के deliverable को evidence, agreed criteria, quality review, revision और final human acceptance से जोड़ने की audit-ready परत।

Quality Review Flow

Deliverable Submitted → Evidence Check → Criteria Review → Quality Assessment → Accepted / Revision Required → Human Acceptance → Fulfilment Record → Settlement Candidate → Audit

Review Record

  • review_id — unique review reference
  • work_execution_reference — source execution
  • deliverable_reference — submitted output
  • criteria_reference — agreed quality/acceptance criteria
  • evidence_reference — supporting evidence
  • reviewer_reference — authorized reviewer reference
  • findings_reference — findings/observations
  • revision_reference — requested revision when applicable
  • acceptance_reference — human acceptance evidence
  • fulfilment_reference — downstream completion

Lifecycle

SUBMITTED → EVIDENCE_CHECK → CRITERIA_REVIEW → QUALITY_REVIEW → ACCEPTED

Alternative/governance states: REVISION_REQUIRED · REJECTED · DISPUTED · WITHDRAWN · CANCELLED · NOT_VERIFIED · ARCHIVED

Acceptance Boundary

Acceptance केवल agreed criteria और available evidence के आधार पर authorized human decision के रूप में दर्ज होगा। Review record अपने-आप customer satisfaction, legal compliance, payment या income सिद्ध नहीं करता।

SUBMITTED ≠ REVIEWED · REVIEWED ≠ ACCEPTED · ACCEPTED ≠ PAID · ACCEPTED ≠ INCOME CLAIM

Safeguards

  • कोई invented deliverable, evidence, reviewer, quality result, acceptance या payment नहीं।
  • Automatic quality approval और automatic acceptance disabled.
  • Revision और dispute अलग lifecycle states हैं; पुराने findings silently overwrite नहीं होंगे।
  • AI criteria normalization, checklist, evidence organization और draft review summary में सहायता कर सकता है; final quality/acceptance decision नहीं करेगा।
  • जहाँ external standards या contractual requirements लागू हों, संबंधित human/external review आवश्यक है।
  • Privacy boundary: केवल आवश्यक review और evidence references.
