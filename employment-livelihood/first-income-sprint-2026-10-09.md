# First-Income Sprint — Shirmani Research Institute
Date opened: 2026-10-09

## Source-derived starting point
The repository README describes a public research/archive platform, production showroom, public product catalog, and existing separation between content, operations, evidence and verification. It also explicitly states that product production is separate from sales/payment/dispatch and that ledger entries are not income. The first income action should therefore be a clearly scoped service with a real buyer and proof of delivery/payment, not a claim based on workflow activity.

## Offer to test
Public landing page: `writing-research-services.html`

1. Short writing/editing — proposed pilot price ₹399 for up to 500 words.
2. Hindi ↔ English adaptation — proposed pilot price ₹699 for up to 800 words.
3. Source-based research brief — proposed pilot price ₹999 for up to 1,200 words with links and limitations.

These are experimental introductory prices set for testing, not researched market averages. Quote each project in writing after confirming scope, deadline and client requirements. Avoid regulated advice, fake citations, plagiarism and promises of outcomes.

Do not buy a paid plan or proposal credits until the profile and samples are ready.

## Seven-day execution plan
- Day 1: publish/inspect the service page and prepare three honest samples: Hindi writing, English writing, source-based research brief. Label samples as demonstrations if not commissioned.
- Days 1–2: complete one freelance profile and submit 5 highly matched, tailored proposals; do not mass-spam.
- Days 1–7: send 20 relevant, personalised enquiries to suitable small businesses, creators, nonprofits or research teams using publicly provided business contact routes.
- Follow up once after 3–4 days, politely.
- Quote fixed scope, deliverable, date, revision limit and price before starting.
- For larger jobs, use a protected marketplace milestone or a written agreement and advance payment. Never share OTPs, passwords or pay an alleged client a fee to receive work.
- Record all prospects, replies, proposals, orders, delivery links and actual payments in a private tracker. Do not publish personal client/payment details.

## Targets (not achievements or guarantees)
- 3 demonstration samples published.
- 20 relevant personalised enquiries sent.
- 5 tailored marketplace proposals submitted.
- 3 qualified replies target.
- 1 paid pilot order target.
- Revenue target: record actual receipts only; current confirmed revenue from this sprint = ₹0 until evidence is recorded.

## Evidence and decision gates
A lead is not an order. An order is not delivered work. A sent invoice is not payment. Count income only after actual payment evidence; store the evidence privately. At day 7, review replies, conversion, hours spent, actual net receipts and client feedback. If there are no qualified replies, revise the niche and samples before spending money on ads or subscriptions.
