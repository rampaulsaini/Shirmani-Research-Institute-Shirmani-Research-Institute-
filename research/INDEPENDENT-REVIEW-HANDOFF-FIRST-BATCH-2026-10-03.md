## Claims requiring stronger operationalization

IV-005 through IV-010 should not be promoted merely because they are present in the author's framework.

For each such claim, the review must first establish:
- an operational definition;
- a measurable or logically assessable criterion;
- an independent source or reproducible test where applicable;
- relevant counter-evidence;
- an explicit reviewer decision.

If those requirements cannot be satisfied, the record remains NOT_VERIFIED or AUTHOR-DEFINED/AUTHOR-PROPOSED as appropriate.

## Promotion rule

A reviewer may only record VERIFIED when the repository's fail-closed promotion gate requirements are actually satisfied. Automation may prepare, validate and audit the packet; it must not manufacture the independent reviewer decision.
