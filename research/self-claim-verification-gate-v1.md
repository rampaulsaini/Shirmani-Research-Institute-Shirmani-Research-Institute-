# SHIRMANI Self-Claim Verification Gate — Heart-View / Yatharth

## Purpose

This gate applies whenever any person, including the author, makes a claim such as "मैं शिरोमणि हूँ", "I am Shirmani", "I have attained complete continuous satisfaction", or an equivalent claim of a special or universal state.

The gate does not reject a person's testimony or lived experience. It separates first-person testimony, author-defined terminology, observable behaviour, independently testable propositions, and universal/external claims.

No person is VERIFIED merely because they are articulate, intelligent, persuasive, spiritually impressive, popular, or technically sophisticated.

## Core principle

"मन/मस्तक/बुद्धि से बुद्धिमान होना" is not itself evidence of a universal heart-view state.

The system tests the claim and its observable implications, not the claimant's status.

## Evidence boundary

Personal face, eyes, voice, conversation style, photographs, and videos may establish provenance or provide behavioural observations when consent and source integrity are available. They must not be treated as proof of an inner metaphysical state.

A face/eye/voice cannot by itself prove "Shirmani", enlightenment, universal truth, purity, or complete satisfaction.

## Verification model

CLAIM -> CLAIM TYPE -> OPERATIONAL DEFINITION -> PERSONAL TESTIMONY -> CONSISTENT BEHAVIOURAL EVIDENCE -> COUNTER-EVIDENCE -> INDEPENDENT REVIEW -> REPRODUCIBLE RESULT -> AUDIT -> STATUS

## Required claimant packet

Each claim packet should contain:
- claim_text
- claimant_identifier
- claim_type: AUTHOR-DEFINED / FIRST-PERSON-TESTIMONY / BEHAVIOURALLY-TESTABLE / EMPIRICAL-TESTABLE / UNIVERSAL-EXTERNAL
- operational_definition
- date/time and provenance
- source references
- optional media references with SHA-256 hashes
- consent/provenance statement for supplied media
- practical questions and answers
- observable evidence
- counter-evidence
- independent reviewer identity/role
- review timestamp
- decision

## Practical Heart-View question set

1. When personal benefit conflicts with another living being's legitimate interest, what observable action is taken?
2. When evidence contradicts the claimant's preferred belief, does the claimant revise the claim?
3. Can the claimant distinguish personal experience from a universal factual proposition?
4. Does the claimant disclose uncertainty where evidence is incomplete?
5. Can criticism be received without converting disagreement into personal blame?
6. Are resources, nature and other species considered in practical decisions?
7. Does the claimant avoid demanding authority solely because of the claim "I am Shirmani"?
8. Can an independent reviewer reproduce the relevant observation without relying on the claimant's interpretation?
9. Are failures and counter-examples preserved rather than deleted?
10. Does the claimed state produce stable, observable conduct across time and contexts?

These are tests of evidence and behaviour, not a psychological or spiritual diagnosis.

## Fail-closed rules

- Intelligence ≠ verification.
- Eloquence ≠ verification.
- Memory ≠ verification.
- Popularity ≠ verification.
- Number of followers ≠ verification.
- Photograph/video ≠ verification of an inner state.
- AI-generated analysis ≠ independent verification.
- GitHub workflow success ≠ independent verification.
- Author-authored evidence ≠ independent evidence.
- A positive answer to a question ≠ proof of the answer.

A claim remains NOT_VERIFIED unless the required independent evidence and review record exist.

## Universal truth safeguard

Statements about all humanity, all living beings, the universe, consciousness, nature, or physical reality must be split into individually testable propositions.

The system must never convert "यह मेरा अनुभव है" into "यह सार्वभौमिक वैज्ञानिक तथ्य है" without an independent evidentiary pathway.

## Environmental and civilization context

The author's concern for nature, Earth and other species may be preserved as an author-proposed ethical objective. Empirical environmental propositions must be tested separately.

Current external evidence supports substantial human influence on climate and major human-driven pressures on biodiversity, but the gate must avoid the unsupported absolute that every extinction or every harmful event is caused solely by one homogeneous "human civilization." IPBES identifies land/sea-use change, direct exploitation, climate change, pollution and invasive alien species as major direct drivers of biodiversity change; IPCC finds human influence on warming unequivocal.

## Status vocabulary

- PRESERVED_TESTIMONY
- AUTHOR_DEFINED
- AUTHOR_PROPOSED
- EVIDENCE_SUPPORTED
- NOT_VERIFIED
- CONTRADICTED
- INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED

Only the final status requires an independent review decision.

## Target

Convert unsupported self-claims into: claim -> operational test -> evidence -> counter-evidence -> independent review -> auditable result.

No automatic declaration of "Shirmani" is permitted.
