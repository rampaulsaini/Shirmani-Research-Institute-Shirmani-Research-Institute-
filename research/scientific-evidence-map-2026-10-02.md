# SHIRMANI Scientific Evidence Map — 2026-10-02

## Purpose
Separate established measurable phenomena from hypotheses, interpretations and claims requiring further testing.

## Evidence classes
- A — Directly measurable: plant electrical, calcium, ROS and long-distance signaling can be instrumentally measured.
- B — Strong research area with unresolved interpretation: consciousness research uses measurable neural/behavioral paradigms, but subjective experience remains an inference problem.
- C — Computational inference: ML/NLP classifications and translations require task-specific validation.
- D — Philosophical / author proposition: preserved and compared with evidence, but philosophical consistency is not automatically empirical verification.

## Rule
A source cannot be promoted into a stronger claim than it supports. Record: source, exact claim, evidence class, method, population/material, limitations, replication status and verification status.

## NLP implication
Translate measurable biological/environmental signals into plain language only where a validated mapping exists. Keep measured signal, model inference, interpretation, confidence/uncertainty and unresolved questions distinct.

## Scientific path
Observation → operational definition → preregistered hypothesis → data/material acquisition → blinded analysis → held-out evaluation → independent replication → independent verification → statistical evaluation → publication/archive.
