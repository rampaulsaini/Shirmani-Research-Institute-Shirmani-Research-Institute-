# निष्पक्ष समझ — Multi-Angle Analysis Parameter Framework

यह framework किसी दावे को स्वतः सत्य घोषित नहीं करता। इसका उद्देश्य एक ही शब्द, अनुभव, संकेत, फोटो, कथन, सूत्र या घटना को कई स्वतंत्र कोणों से दर्ज करना है।

## 1. मूल रिकॉर्ड
- exact_source
- date/time as stated
- context
- original wording
- medium: text/photo/audio/video/observation
- source_hash / provenance when available

## 2. शब्द-विच्छेदन (Word Deep Analysis)
हर महत्वपूर्ण शब्द के लिए:
- literal meaning
- linguistic origin / morphology
- ordinary usage
- technical usage
- contextual meaning
- metaphorical meaning
- philosophical meaning
- possible ambiguity
- translation variants
- what the author means vs what a reader may infer

## 3. Multi-Angle Claim Analysis
- author perspective
- observer perspective
- independent evidence
- alternative explanation
- counter-evidence
- falsifiability
- reproducibility
- scope and boundary
- confidence/status

## 4. दृश्य/संवेदी निरीक्षण
### Finger / vein sensing
इसे केवल claim category के रूप में दर्ज करें:
- what is visibly/physically observable
- whether a vein pattern, finger movement, pulse, pressure, temperature, skin feature, or sensor output is actually present
- measurement method
- sensor/device specification
- calibration
- repeatability
- false-positive / false-negative possibilities
- privacy and consent
- distinction between observation and interpretation

### Eye / visual analysis
- eye direction / gaze as observable geometry, not inferred intention
- pupil/iris/eyelid/lighting conditions where technically measurable
- image quality, resolution, compression and occlusion
- temporal sequence if video exists
- alternative explanations
- no inference of identity, mental state, truthfulness or health from appearance alone

## 5. Body/biometric signals
For any proposed signal:
- measurable variable
- unit
- acquisition method
- baseline
- noise
- calibration
- repeat trials
- independent replication
- uncertainty/error bounds
- data retention and consent

## 6. Language + meaning
For each sentence:
- syntax
- semantics
- pragmatics
- implied premise
- explicit premise
- conclusion
- metaphor vs literal statement
- testable vs non-testable portion

## 7. Mathematical/formula audit
- symbol definitions
- dimensions/units
- domain/codomain
- tensor/vector/scalar type
- operations validity
- boundary/initial conditions
- limiting cases
- numerical test
- reproducibility
- comparison with established equations

## 8. Evidence ladder
1. source statement
2. direct observation
3. independently repeatable measurement
4. independent replication
5. peer-reviewed/authoritative evidence
6. competing evidence
7. audit conclusion

No automated layer may promote a record to VERIFIED merely because workflow/QC passed.

## 9. Status vocabulary
- SOURCE_RECORDED
- OBSERVED
- INTERPRETATION
- PERSONAL_EXPERIENCE
- PHILOSOPHICAL_PROPOSITION
- TESTABLE_HYPOTHESIS
- PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- INDEPENDENTLY_SUPPORTED
- DISPUTED
- CONTRADICTED
- UNVERIFIED
- NON_FALSIFIABLE

## 10. Safety and privacy gate
Biometric/visual sensing must use consent, minimal data, transparent purpose, no hidden identification, and no unsupported conclusions about health, identity, intention or mental state.

## 11. Audit record
Every reviewed claim should preserve:
- reviewer
- timestamp
- source version/hash
- evidence references
- countercase
- reproduction/test performed
- limitations
- final status
- reason for status
