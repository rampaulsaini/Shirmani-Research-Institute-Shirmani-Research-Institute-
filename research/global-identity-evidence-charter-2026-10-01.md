# Global Identity & Evidence Charter — 2026-10-01

## उद्देश्य
यह charter शिरोमणि रामपॉल सैनी की सार्वजनिक डिजिटल पहचान को research, authorship, creative work, livelihood और open knowledge के साथ traceable रूप में जोड़ने के लिए है।

## पहचान
- Canonical public name: **शिरोमणि रामपॉल सैनी**
- Identity records must distinguish author-declared identity from independently verified identity.
- The platform must not manufacture credentials, awards, citizenship, endorsements, testimonials, revenue, users or scientific validation.

## Evidence states
AUTHOR_DECLARED → REGISTERED → EVIDENCE_PENDING → QC_CHECKED → INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED → PUBLISHED

A workflow success or model agreement is not independent verification.

## Global participation
“विश्व की नागरिकता” is treated as a non-legal public-goal phrase meaning global accessibility, participation and knowledge exchange. No page may imply that this platform grants legal nationality, citizenship or government status.

## AI and Automission
Observe → Collect → Normalize → Analyze → Reason → Execute → Test → Verify → Audit → Learn → Improve.

High-impact, irreversible, financial, legal, identity and external-account actions remain human-authorized. Self-improvement is versioned and test-gated, not silent mutation.

## Livelihood
Public surfaces may present digital products, Yatharth AI Music, services, freelancing, marketplace preparation, licensing and lawful monetization. Real sales/revenue claims require platform evidence.

## Preservation
Research, source material, provenance, audit history and public explanations should remain recoverable and versioned. The platform should prefer durable records over unsupported claims of permanence.

## Quality gate
Before publication: source integrity → provenance → reasoning → evidence → verification state → QC → safety/privacy → presentation.

## Success definition
The platform succeeds by making the user's work understandable, discoverable, reproducible and honestly represented—not by declaring unsupported superiority.
