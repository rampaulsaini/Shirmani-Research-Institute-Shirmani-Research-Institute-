# ꙰ SHIRMANI Independent Verification Review Packets
## 2026-10-03

Purpose: convert the existing 10-record fail-closed queue into reviewer-ready, claim-specific verification packets.

**Important:** These packets do not create or imply VERIFIED status.

## Review protocol

For every packet, the independent reviewer should record:
1. exact claim reviewed;
2. operational definition;
3. independent evidence or reproducible test;
4. strongest material counter-evidence;
5. reproducible result / observation;
6. reviewer identity and role;
7. review timestamp;
8. explicit decision;
9. audit record bound to the exact queue task.

A workflow run, generated artifact, author statement, or AI-generated assessment is not by itself an independent verification decision.

**Current source status:** NOT_VERIFIED  
**Current verification status:** NOT_VERIFIED
