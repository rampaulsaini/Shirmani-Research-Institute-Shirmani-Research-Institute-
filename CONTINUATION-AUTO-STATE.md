- Operating principle: Automission is the continuous production engine; QC/gate/dispatch and independent verification are downstream result-quality and promotion layers.
- Production cadence: 5-minute multi-layer cycle.
- Default production batch: 500 work units/cycle.
- Concrete product factory: 1,016 configured product records across 25 executable browser-engine families, with product-specific IDs, objectives, QC codes, gate numbers and dispatch state.
- Production lanes: research, ai-ml-nlp, content, platform, economic, social-media, federation, security-quality, automation.
- Public visibility requirement: every completed cycle must publish queue status, concrete production results, product catalog/passports, module-wise production map and telemetry.
- Independent verification remains separate and must never be manufactured from workflow success.
- Quantum boundary: quantum-inspired/deterministic orchestration may be used; no active quantum hardware execution is claimed without an actual configured backend.
- Continuity: do not stop after one module or one verification task; rotate through repository modules and product families and keep producing source-bound artifacts.
- Chat independence: scheduled GitHub Actions are designed to continue without a new chat message; chat is not the execution trigger.
Preserve first. Produce continuously. Reason second. QC/gate/dispatch downstream. Verify produced results after production. Transform only as a traceable derivative.
