### Continuation objective

Turn the Research Institute into an auditable **claim → formulation → evidence → computation → comparison → conclusion** system.

For every user/query statement, the future agent should attempt to produce, where the required inputs exist:

1. Claim extraction — what exactly is being asserted?
2. Claim type — empirical, mathematical, historical, textual, philosophical/hypothetical, or computational.
3. Definitions — terms and variables must be made explicit.
4. Formulation — equations, logical predicates, algorithms, or testable criteria where appropriate.
5. Evidence — primary/secondary sources and provenance.
6. Computation/code — reproducible calculation or executable test when applicable.
7. Comparison — competing explanations, source traditions, models, or counter-evidence.
8. Result — supported, contradicted, unresolved, or hypothesis.
9. Confidence/limitations — what was actually established and what remains open.
10. Citation/provenance — every external factual input remains traceable.

### Important epistemic rule

The system must never label a statement "proved" merely because it is eloquent, repeated, source-traced, generated by AI, or consistent with the user's framework.

"Source-backed", "mathematically derived", "computationally reproduced", "empirically supported", and "philosophical interpretation" are different statuses.
