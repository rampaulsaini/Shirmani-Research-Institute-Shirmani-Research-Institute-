## Product / lab / showroom policy
- Preserve original user source wording; label derived service copy as a derivative.
- A product remains in lab until its reproducible test checklist passes; only then move it to QC review.
- A showroom listing is not proof of a physical product, completed packaging, order system or sale.
- Physical products require a defined prototype, bill of materials, safety/legal review where relevant, unit-cost estimate, packaging prototype, batch checks and a real fulfilment path before accepting orders.
- Digital tools need versioned passports, known limitations, sample output, test evidence, documentation and a visible support/contact route.
