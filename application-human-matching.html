Application & Human Matching

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO AUTOMATIC HIRING OR PAYMENT

Verified व्यक्ति/creator और evidence-backed opportunity के बीच application, proposal, review और human decision को auditable बनाने की परत।

Application Flow

Verified Profile → Opportunity → Application / Proposal → Evidence & Terms → Eligibility Review → Match Proposal → Human Decision → Agreement → Work-to-Value Pipeline

Application Record

  • application_id — unique application reference
  • applicant_reference — verified profile/work reference
  • opportunity_reference — registered opportunity
  • proposal_reference — applicant proposal
  • skills_evidence — relevant verified work/skills
  • terms_reference — opportunity terms
  • review_reference — human review
  • decision_reference — acceptance/rejection/withdrawal decision

Lifecycle

DRAFT → SUBMITTED → ELIGIBILITY_REVIEW → MATCH_PROPOSED → HUMAN_REVIEW → ACCEPTED → AGREEMENT_PENDING → AGREEMENT_RECORDED → WORK_PIPELINE_READY

Governance: REJECTED · WITHDRAWN · EXPIRED · DISPUTED · CANCELLED · ARCHIVED

Human Decision Boundary

AI may assist with opportunity discovery, skill comparison, summaries, proposal drafting and routing. It may not accept an offer, claim employment, invent an employer/customer, create a contract, promise compensation or record income.

APPLICATION ≠ MATCH · MATCH_PROPOSED ≠ ACCEPTANCE · ACCEPTED ≠ CONTRACT · CONTRACT ≠ PAYMENT · PAYMENT ≠ INCOME WITHOUT EVIDENCE

Safeguards

  • कोई invented applicant, opportunity, employer, customer, application, acceptance, contract, payment या income नहीं।
  • Application केवल registered opportunity और identifiable applicant reference से जुड़ती है।
  • Final acceptance और contracting human-controlled हैं।
  • Rejection, withdrawal, expiry और dispute अलग lifecycle states हैं।
  • Actual payment/accounting evidence के बिना income को factual नहीं माना जाएगा।
  • Identity substitution disabled; creator-authored identity remains canonical.
