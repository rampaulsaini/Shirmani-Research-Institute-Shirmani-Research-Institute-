## Product-specific media contract

Every released product must have:

1. Unique product ID, version, and product-specific name.
2. Product-specific visual with original photo/logo, proportionally scaled only.
3. English identity line beneath the logo/photo.
4. Product-specific QR in the upper-right corner.
5. Short, readable description on the visual.
6. QR destination containing the long description/product passport, version, intended use, limitations, and usage guide.
7. A real product-specific VIP screenshot. If not captured from the running product, label it **ILLUSTRATIVE MOCK-UP**.
8. A real product-specific demo video showing what the product actually does. Until recorded, label it **STORYBOARD — NOT RECORDED**.
9. Mobile QR scan test and destination check.
10. Evidence that the described feature matches the tested product.

Do not label artwork “4K” unless its actual dimensions are checked; target artwork size is 3840×2160.
