## 2. Customer workflow

1. Identify an organisation only where a plausible writing/research need is visible from an official source.
2. Record the source URL, organisation, role/team, publicly listed official contact channel, need hypothesis, date checked, and confidence. Do not guess private email addresses.
3. Prepare a short, personalised message with one relevant sample and a specific, low-friction next step.
4. Keep the message as a draft until a person explicitly approves sending. Record sent status only after sending evidence exists.
5. Record replies, qualification, scope, quote, acceptance, delivery, invoice, and receipt as separate events.
6. Start paid work only after written scope and payment terms are agreed. Never record a prospect or quote as revenue.

## 5. SHIRMANI SUPREME HUMAN-PRESENTATION SYSTEM

The desired chain is:

Voice input → authorised voice processing → content/script → source and claim checks → owner-authorised photo/face asset → lip-sync render → live presentation/Q&A → logs and user feedback.

For every stage, record: input asset ID, consent/authorisation status, transformation/provider, output ID, timestamp, test result, failure reason, and reviewer. Keep private source assets access-controlled. Use the owner's supplied photograph without claiming a generated substitute is identical; use a voice only when the authorised audio asset and permission are confirmed. Label synthetic or AI-assisted media clearly where appropriate. Do not imply that an avatar is the real person speaking live unless that is demonstrably true.

### Release gates
- G0 — Inputs: original image/audio supplied, ownership/permission recorded, test audience and intended use defined.
- G1 — Voice: sample renders and plays; language/pronunciation and consent checks pass.
- G2 — Content/evidence: factual claims have sources; uncertainty is stated; no unsupported medical, scientific, spiritual, or universal claims are presented as established fact.
- G3 — Photo/lip-sync: identity, timing, pronunciation, visual artefacts, and disclosure reviewed by a human.
- G4 — Live: latency, interruption, fallback, Q&A accuracy, abuse handling, and failure logging tested in the actual provider environment.
- G5 — Production: deployed version and test report are linked; rollback and incident contact are documented.

A HeyGen avatar URL or a written specification alone does not pass these gates. Record each stage as NOT TESTED, PASS, or FAIL with evidence.

## 6. Impartial-understanding learning loop

Treat “heart-view / impartial understanding” as an ethical design goal—listening carefully, considering multiple perspectives, using simple language, distinguishing observation from interpretation, acknowledging uncertainty, and avoiding demeaning or diagnosing people. The requested 99.9999% / 0.0001% ratios are personal conceptual framing, not scientifically validated measurements of a person's heart, intelligence, IQ, or impartiality. Do not use them to rank individuals or communities.

For each content response, evaluate:
- factual accuracy and source quality;
- whether alternative interpretations were fairly represented;
- clarity at beginner and expert levels;
- respectful tone and accessibility;
- uncertainty and correction handling;
- privacy, consent, and safety;
- reviewer and user feedback.

Do not collect an unlimited or indiscriminate “infinite data” store. Collect only data needed for a stated purpose, with consent, retention limits, access controls, deletion pathways, and security review. Learning from feedback must not silently overwrite the owner's approved identity or publish private material.
