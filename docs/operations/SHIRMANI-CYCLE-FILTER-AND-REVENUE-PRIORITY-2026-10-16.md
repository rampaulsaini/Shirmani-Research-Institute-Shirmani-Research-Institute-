# SHIRMANI Cycle Filter & Revenue-First Execution Plan
Date: 2026-10-16
Status: Operational proposal; no revenue, customer, deletion, or production capability is claimed.

## Purpose
Keep work that directly improves customer value, safety, reliability, evidence, or near-term income. Reduce duplicate effort without damaging production, useful workflows, source records, or rollback options.

## The contribution filter
Every proposed task must pass at least one gate:
1. **Customer value:** improves an actual product/service a user can use.
2. **Revenue path:** helps a real prospect understand, request, approve, buy, or receive an available service.
3. **Safety and integrity:** protects security, privacy, source/media integrity, payments, or reliable operation.
4. **Evidence:** creates reproducible proof of behavior, quality, deployment, delivery, or customer outcome.
5. **Necessary dependency:** is demonstrably required by a task that passes one of the gates above.

If none applies, mark **HOLD / REVIEW** and do not add it to the active cycle. A vague “AI/quantum/ultra” label, high workflow count, or repeated success badge is not evidence of contribution.

## Safe removal filter — no destructive guessing
A file, workflow, integration, or automation may be disabled, archived, or deleted only after all checks pass:
- Confirm its exact identity and current state.
- Search references, workflow triggers, navigation links, package/build use, deployment use, and downstream dependencies.
- Confirm recent run history and whether another workflow relies on it.
- Establish a verified backup or recoverable Git history and a tested rollback.
- Check that removal cannot weaken security, truth gates, production persistence, accessibility, or deployment.
- Prefer deduplication, clear naming, concurrency fixes, or disabling an unused trigger over deletion.
- Record before/after evidence and owner approval for irreversible deletion.

**Never delete because a workflow is cancelled, queued, duplicated by name, or apparently unhelpful.** If dependency or rollback evidence is incomplete, leave it intact and mark **NOT SAFE TO REMOVE**.

## Order-to-payment chain
1. A real enquiry arrives through a working, monitored channel.
2. Confirm purpose, audience, language, output length, sources, deadline, budget and intended use.
3. Send a written quote with deliverables, exclusions, timeline, revision count and payment terms.
4. Obtain written acceptance and agreed advance payment before beginning, where that is the agreed term.
5. Draft and quality-check: verify links and quotations, distinguish sourced claims from interpretation, mark uncertainty, and check language.
6. Deliver the agreed editable file and request one consolidated revision/acceptance.
7. Record payment only after actual receipt; record a sale only after actual customer acceptance.
8. Obtain permission before publishing commissioned work or testimonials.

A generated form, proposal, email draft, workflow success, or page view is not a sale.
