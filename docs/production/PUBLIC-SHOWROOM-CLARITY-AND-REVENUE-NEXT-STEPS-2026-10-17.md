# Public Showroom Clarity and First-Revenue Execution Plan
Date: 2026-10-17
Status: proposed implementation plan; not proof of deployment, outreach, sales, payment, or income.

## 1. Immediate objective
Reduce visitor confusion and make the next useful action obvious. Prioritise a small, honest, customer-ready path over adding more disconnected pages.

Primary path:
1. Understand the institute in one screen.
2. Choose a real service or a clearly labelled demo.
3. Inspect a sample and exact deliverables.
4. Submit a structured enquiry.
5. Agree scope, price, deadline, revisions and payment terms in writing.
6. Deliver and record customer acceptance/payment evidence.

## 2. Public homepage information architecture
Recommended top-to-bottom order:
1. **Hero:** “Hindi & English Writing, Research Summaries and Website Content” with two actions: “View samples” and “Request a quote”.
2. **What we can deliver now:** writing polish, source-based research summary, website/blog draft. Label all prices as proposed until individually confirmed.
3. **Samples:** clearly mark illustrative samples as examples, never as paid client work or testimonials.
4. **How it works:** brief → written quote → confirmation/payment terms → draft → review/revisions → delivery.
5. **Product showroom:** searchable categories with a readiness label on every card: Demo, Needs integration, QC pending, or Ready for order.
6. **Research and public-benefit mission:** education, nature, accessibility and human wellbeing, without unsupported outcome claims.
7. **Trust and evidence:** source policy, privacy/data handling, limitations, support and correction route.
8. **FAQ and contact/enquiry form:** one consistent route; no fake account, checkout, live AI, video, or messaging claims.

## 3. Catalogue governance
Each product record should have:
- Stable product ID and version.
- Plain-language problem solved and intended user.
- Exact features that work today.
- Demo URL and a short test script.
- Required inputs, outputs and known limitations.
- Price basis, licence, support and refund terms where applicable.
- Screenshot/cover and product-specific media, with rights/consent recorded.
- QC evidence, date, reviewer and unresolved defects.
- State machine: Draft → Built → QC pending → Ready for order → Published → Retired.
- Separate commercial states: Enquiry → Quote sent → Accepted → Delivered → Payment confirmed.

Never infer a sale from a published page, a catalogue count, an automated workflow, or a generated demo.

## 4. First-revenue offer (proposed; confirm scope before accepting)
Start with one low-complexity service and one buyer type at a time:
- Hindi/English writing polish: ₹499 pilot anchor.
- Source-led research summary: ₹999 pilot anchor.
- Website/blog draft: ₹1,499 pilot anchor.

These are proposed starting anchors, not verified market rates or guaranteed conversions. Before work begins, confirm word count, source requirements, language, deadline, revision count, exclusions, delivery format and payment terms. Do not promise professional legal/medical/financial advice or unsupported research certainty.

## 5. Seven-day execution scorecard
Track actual counts daily:
- Official prospects researched (with source URL): target 10/day.
- Personalised messages reviewed and actually sent: target 5/day, only where contact details and outreach rules permit.
- Replies, qualified enquiries, quotes, accepted jobs, deliveries and payments: record actual values; no fabricated targets presented as results.
- Sample quality checks passed: target 3 before promoting a service.
- Broken public links / misleading capability claims: target zero.
- Enquiries acknowledged within one working day: target at least 90%, if operationally feasible.

Conversion formulas:
- Reply rate = replies ÷ messages actually sent.
- Qualified enquiry rate = qualified enquiries ÷ replies.
- Quote acceptance rate = accepted quotes ÷ quotes sent.
- Paid conversion = payments confirmed ÷ qualified enquiries.
- Collected revenue = payments actually received, less refunds; never count proposals or invoices as cash.

## 6. Outreach controls
Use official institutional/company contact pages and role-relevant recipients. Personalise each message, include a clear opt-out, respect applicable anti-spam/privacy rules, and keep a suppression list. Do not bulk-send or scrape personal contact data. Drafting a message is not sending it; log the sent timestamp and delivery/reply evidence only after the action occurs.

## 7. Security and continuity
- Keep secrets in repository/environment secret stores; never commit tokens or private customer data.
- Use least-privilege workflow permissions and pin third-party actions where practical.
- Preserve a known-good deploy; changes should be small, reviewable and reversible.
- Treat CI success as workflow evidence only, not independent security certification.
- Back up customer records only with consent and an explicit retention policy.
- Do not delete filters, security gates or quality checks merely because they block a release. Fix false positives or document a safe exception with an owner and expiry; never bypass protection that prevents customer harm or data loss.

## 8. Heart-View / HeyGen evidence chain
Track each stage separately:
1. Approved original photo and voice permissions.
2. Script and factual/evidence labels.
3. Voice generation/provider job and source asset.
4. Actual video/MP4 URL or file.
5. Lip-sync review against the rendered video.
6. Spoken test questions and recorded responses.
7. End-to-end timestamp, browser/device, reviewer and defects.
8. Release approval.

A planning page, animated placeholder, generated script, or provider link alone does not prove live Q&A, correct lip-sync, or a completed end-to-end test.

## 9. Release acceptance checklist
- [ ] Homepage tells a first-time visitor what is available in 10 seconds.
- [ ] “View samples” and “Request a quote” work on mobile.
- [ ] Every listed product has a valid route and honest readiness label.
- [ ] Enquiry can be copied/exported without claiming it was sent.
- [ ] Prices and payment status are explicitly marked proposed/unpaid/paid as appropriate.
- [ ] No unsupported live AI, voice, lip-sync, livestream, account, checkout, or security claims.
- [ ] Automated tests and a manual mobile pass are recorded.
- [ ] Rollback target and reviewer are recorded.
