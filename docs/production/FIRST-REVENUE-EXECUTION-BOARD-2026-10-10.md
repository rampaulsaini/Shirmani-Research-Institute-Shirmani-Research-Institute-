## Sellable service menu (proposed, confirm scope before quoting)

| Offer | Deliverable | Proposed starting reference | Boundary |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Hindi/English short writing | One short article, page or polished draft | ₹999 | Final price depends on brief and word count |
| Research summary | Structured summary with source links, limitations and key findings | ₹1,999 | Not a substitute for professional/legal/medical advice |
| Website/blog content pack | Up to three agreed content pieces | ₹2,999 | Scope, revisions and schedule confirmed in writing |

These are proposed pilot references, not binding prices, guaranteed offers, completed orders or market-tested rates. Agree deliverables, word count, sources, revisions, deadline, price and payment terms in writing before work begins.

## Four-stage operating chain

1. **Institute / Intake:** record client objective, audience, language, word count, source links, deadline and budget.
2. **Factory / Production:** prepare outline, draft, source ledger, limitations and deliverable files.
3. **QC / Release gate:** check scope match, language, citations, originality, factual uncertainty, privacy and requested format. Record defects and corrections.
4. **Showroom / Delivery & sale:** publish only approved sample assets; send the proposal manually after human review; mark order/payment only from actual records.

## Measurable funnel

Track counts, not inflated claims:

- prospects researched
- official contact routes found
- personalized drafts prepared
- drafts approved
- messages actually sent
- replies received
- qualified enquiries
- proposals sent
- orders confirmed
- payments received
- deliverables accepted
- revision/complaint count
- repeat/referral enquiries

Conversion rate = next-stage count / prior-stage count × 100, only when the denominator is non-zero. Revenue = payments actually received, not quoted prices or proposals.

## Outreach safety and quality

- Use only official, publicly listed contact routes and messages relevant to the recipient's role.
- Do not scrape private personal details, guess addresses, bulk-spam, or claim an email/WhatsApp message was sent unless it was actually sent.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable local laws/platform terms.
- Never put secrets, credentials, private client data or unlicensed source material in public records.
- Keep prospect tracking local/private until a proper access-controlled storage layer exists.
- No AI voice, image, MP4 rendering, payment, checkout or automated outbound integration is represented as live unless separately implemented and tested.
