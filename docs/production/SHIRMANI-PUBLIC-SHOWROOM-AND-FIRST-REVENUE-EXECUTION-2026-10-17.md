## Product truth rule
Use only four public readiness labels:
1. **Live demo** — link opens and the named behavior was tested.
2. **Prototype** — a local/browser tool works within its stated limits.
3. **Planned** — requirements exist but implementation is not demonstrated.
4. **Verified integration** — real provider/API call plus dated test evidence and a reproducible acceptance result.

Do not mark an item “verified” because a specification, mock-up, sample video, README, or checklist exists.

## Public homepage information architecture
Present one clear path from top to bottom:
1. **What this is:** one-sentence explanation of Shirmani Research Institute.
2. **Start here:** three prominent choices — Browse tools, Hire writing/research, Explore Heart-View.
3. **Featured paid service:** Hindi/English editing, article/web copy, source-based research brief; proposed prices must remain labelled “starting proposal”.
4. **Browse by category:** Writing & Research; AI/ML & learning; Creator/voice/video; Nature & sustainability; Education/library; Commerce & institution tools; Security & verification.
5. **Each product card:** name, product ID, plain-language purpose, who it helps, real demo link, readiness label, price only when approved, last-tested date, limitations, enquiry/order route.
6. **How purchase works:** choose → try demo → define scope → approve written quote → deliver → settle payment → feedback/upgrade loop.
7. **Trust and evidence:** source/capability notes, privacy and consent, accessibility, security reporting, change log.
8. **Account features:** show sign-up/login only if a working authenticated backend exists; otherwise mark “planned” and never imply accounts are active.
9. **Footer:** contact, terms, privacy, accessibility, repository and version/status.

## First-revenue sequence (no spend required to validate demand)
### Offer A — Editing / Proofreading
- Proposed starting rate: ₹499, up to 700 words, one edited version, one in-scope revision.
- Deliverable: edited document plus short change notes.
### Offer B — Article / Website copy
- Proposed starting rate: ₹999, up to 1,000 words, headings and one in-scope revision.
- Deliverable: final copy plus title/meta suggestion where relevant.
### Offer C — Source-based research brief
- Proposed starting rate: ₹1,499, approximately 800–1,200 words, 3–5 credible sources where available.
- Deliverable: summary, source ledger, limitations and next steps.
Prices are proposals, not validated market rates. Confirm scope, timeline, revisions, rights, and payment terms in writing before starting. Do not promise outcomes or fabricate references.

## Seven-day measurable experiment
- Day 1: check every enquiry/contact link, mobile layout, service scope and sample output.
- Day 2: prepare three short, tailored offers using public official contact sources; verify recipient and relevance manually.
- Days 3–5: send a small, respectful batch only after human review; obey provider rules, applicable law, opt-outs and anti-spam requirements. No scraping or bulk unsolicited messaging.
- Day 6: follow up once only where appropriate; record replies and objections.
- Day 7: review funnel and adjust offer, not inflate results.

Track actual values:
- qualified prospects identified
- contact details verified from official sources
- messages manually reviewed and sent
- delivery/bounce status
- replies
- qualified enquiries
- written quotes accepted
- paid orders
- cash actually received
- delivery time, revisions, refunds and client feedback

Baseline is **unknown / not yet measured** until evidence is entered. Targets are operational targets, not guarantees. Revenue equals confirmed payments received, not prospects, sent messages, clicks, or quotes.

## Layered security and release checks
- Secrets: no API keys in browser code, repository files or logs.
- Identity: least privilege, authenticated access for write/admin operations.
- Application: input validation, output escaping, rate limits and abuse handling.
- Supply chain: dependency review, pinned actions where practical, automated checks.
- Data: minimize collection, define retention and deletion paths, protect backups.
- Recovery: documented restore test and incident contact.
- Release: links, mobile layout, keyboard access, privacy notice, factual capability labels, and test evidence checked before publishing.
Security checklists are not equivalent to an independent penetration test or certification.

## Acceptance criteria
- A first-time visitor can identify the three primary paths within 10 seconds on mobile.
- No product card claims a capability that its linked demo does not perform.
- All catalogue entries remain discoverable by category/search; no existing materials are silently deleted.
- Every paid service shows deliverable, scope, proposed price status, revision terms and a clear enquiry route.
- Contact links open correctly and do not falsely claim a message was sent.
- A test enquiry can be copied/exported; real submission/payment is labelled unavailable until connected and tested.
- Revenue dashboard separates prospect, enquiry, quote, order and confirmed payment.
- Heart-View media statuses are evidence-backed and independent from one another.
- Test results and failures are recorded with date, reviewer and next action.
