# SRI — Immediate Revenue & Public Showroom Execution Plan
**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Execution plan; not proof of sales, outreach, deployment, security certification, or live integrations.

## Decision
The repository already contains multiple browser-local enquiry builders, research tools, product catalogues, production boards, and Heart-View/voice specifications. Stop creating duplicate planning surfaces. Consolidate the existing assets into one understandable public journey, and focus first on the only currently offerable service: Hindi/English writing and research.

## 1. First paid offer
**Hindi/English Writing & Research Pilot**
- Option A: 700–1,000 word research summary with dated source ledger and limitations.
- Option B: five-section website/blog copy pack, CTA, and basic SEO description.
- Required brief: topic, audience, language, word limit, deadline, intended use, source links, budget.
- Suggested test prices (proposals, not verified market rates): ₹1,500 small writing pack; ₹2,500 source-led summary; ₹4,000 combined pilot.
- Before work starts, agree scope, deliverables, date, revisions, price, and payment terms in writing.
- No claim of income until money is actually received. No claim of research or verification not actually performed.

## 2. Seven-day client acquisition test
Day 1: publish one clear service page and one illustrative sample; test the enquiry route on mobile and desktop.  
Days 2–3: identify 20 relevant organisations using official websites; record fit and official contact route.  
Days 3–5: send up to 5 individually tailored, human-reviewed messages daily where an official contact route is published. No bulk spam, invented addresses, or unapproved automation.  
Days 4–7: follow up once where appropriate, qualify replies, send written quotes, and start only after written acceptance and agreed payment terms.

Record actual counts: prospects researched, official routes verified, messages sent, replies, qualified enquiries, quotes accepted, payments received, delivery time, revisions, feedback. Initial activity targets—not forecasts: 20 researched prospects, 10 tailored messages, 2 qualified conversations, 1 paid pilot. Report the actual result even if zero.

## 3. Public showroom information architecture
1. **Hero:** one-sentence explanation; “Explore products” and “Request a writing/research quote”.
2. **Start here:** current writing/research service, sample, scope, proposed starting prices, exclusions, enquiry link.
3. **Try before buying:** working browser demos grouped by category, with clear local-prototype limitations.
4. **Categories:** Research & Evidence; Writing & Learning; Presentation & Media; Product/Factory; Security & Operations; Future Concepts.
5. **Readiness states:** Prototype, Tested, Published, Available to buy. A published page is not automatically a tested product.
6. **Trust:** source policy, privacy, accessibility, contact, version/date, known limitations, actual test evidence.
7. **Accounts:** show sign-up/login only after real server-backed authentication, recovery, sessions, and access controls exist. Never present a fake login or imply local browser data syncs to a server.
8. **Footer:** simple navigation, terms, privacy, accessibility, contact.

Preserve all existing materials. Categorise or archive future-stage work rather than deleting it. Remove only confirmed dead duplicates or unsafe content after checking references.

## 4. Heart-View / HeyGen evidence chain
Keep the already-supplied HeyGen reference; do not repeatedly ask the owner to resend the photo/video. Use the authorised original portrait and voice; do not synthesise a replacement identity.
- **Voice:** actual asset/provider ID, authorization status, date, playable reference.
- **Content:** versioned script and evidence state.
- **Avatar:** actual provider job/result ID and rendered video URL/file.
- **Lip-sync:** inspect actual output at beginning, middle, end, and difficult phonemes; record reviewer and issues.
- **Q&A:** at least 10 fixed questions, exact prompts/responses, timestamps, expected evidence state, failure cases.
- **End-to-end:** record one test from question through spoken response to actual avatar output. Distinguish prerecorded video from live interactive Q&A.
- **Release:** publish only when evidence exists and is reviewed; otherwise label prototype/integration unverified.

A CSS mouth animation or browser text-to-speech does not prove accurate lip-sync, cloned voice, real avatar video, or live provider integration.

## 5. Layered security and continuity
1. Protect secrets; never commit keys, tokens, or customer personal data.
2. Use least-privilege repository/provider permissions; review connected integrations.
3. Validate inputs and uploads; escape outputs; update dependencies.
4. Before public accounts/payments, implement server-side authentication, authorization, rate limits, abuse reporting, and validation.
5. Back up data, test restoration, monitor failures, keep incident logs and rollback.
6. Store product-specific QA evidence; independently review high-risk claims.
7. Do not delete/disable a filter simply because it blocks a workflow. Reproduce the false positive, narrow the rule, run regression tests, and preserve protection against real threats. Security testing must be authorized.

## 6. Definition of done
A task is complete only when the changed file/deployment is identified, a reproducible test passes, evidence is stored, the public route is linked, and limitations are visible. Designed, planned, generated, published, tested, integrated, and sold are separate states.

## 7. Implementation order
**P0:** audit and simplify the existing public landing/showroom without deleting materials.  
**P0:** make writing/research enquiry the clearest conversion route.  
**P0:** test visible links, mobile layout, keyboard navigation, and empty/error states.  
**P1:** consolidate catalogue labels and readiness states from the existing manifest.  
**P1:** gather actual Heart-View/HeyGen end-to-end evidence.  
**P2:** build server accounts, checkout, automated outreach, social integrations, or live AI only after credentials, architecture, consent, budget, and end-to-end tests exist.
