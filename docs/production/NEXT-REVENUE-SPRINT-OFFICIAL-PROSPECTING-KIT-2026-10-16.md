# Next Revenue Sprint — Official Prospecting & Proposal Kit

**Status:** operating plan only; not evidence of outreach, a sale, or income.  
**Current sellable scope:** Hindi/English writing, source-linked research summaries, and website/blog content.

## 1. Priority

Sell the service that can be delivered now before expanding into the full AI/media/social platform. Existing browser-local tools are drafting aids; do not claim they automatically research, email prospects, accept payments, or prove delivery.

## 3. Intake and start gate

Record client objective, audience, topic, language, format, word count, source links/restrictions, deadline/time zone, budget, revisions, payment schedule, confidentiality, attribution, and publication permissions.

**Do not start work until the client approves deliverables, price, timeline, revision terms, and payment conditions in writing.**

## 4. Seven-day activity sprint

Targets are activity goals, not forecasts.

| Day | Activity | Evidence |

Verify contact routes from official websites. Never guess addresses, scrape private details, bulk-spam, or claim a message was sent without a record.

## 7. Funnel ledger and measurable results

Track one row per prospect: internal ID, organisation and official URL, fit reason, official contact route, draft status, actual outreach status, replies, proposal status, order status, reconciled payment status, delivery status, next action/date. Keep client and prospect records private and access-controlled, not in public issues.

- Prospects researched = verified prospect records.
- Messages sent = actual sent records only.
- Reply rate = replies ÷ messages sent × 100.
- Qualified enquiry rate = qualified enquiries ÷ replies × 100.
- Proposal acceptance rate = accepted proposals ÷ proposals sent × 100.
- Revenue = payments actually received and reconciled, never quoted prices.
- Delivery quality = accepted work, revisions, complaints, and on-time rate.

Show N/A when a denominator is zero. Proposed prices, drafts, and proposals are not income.

## 8. Product truth and release gates

Browser-local tools may create structured drafts, checklists, and exportable text where implemented. Do not claim connected external research, AI, email/WhatsApp, payment, publishing, voice, lip-sync, avatar, live Q&A, or video rendering unless a real end-to-end test artefact proves the specific integration. Do not promise one-hour custom product delivery until it has been validated in repeated tests.

## 9. Security minimums

Use least privilege, MFA where available, secret scanning, dependency updates, backups, and incident-response procedures. Never commit credentials, private client files, or personal contact lists. Ethical security testing must be authorised and scoped. Do not claim the platform is fully secure.

## Definition of done

The sprint is complete only when samples are reviewed, the enquiry route is tested, prospects are sourced, drafts are approved, actual sends are logged truthfully, and funnel metrics are reported. **No sales or income are claimed by this document.**
