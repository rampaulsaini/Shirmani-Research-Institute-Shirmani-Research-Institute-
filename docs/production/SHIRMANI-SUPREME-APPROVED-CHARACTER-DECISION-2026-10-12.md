## Evidence states
- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED:** a specific claim has adequate evidence and a documented independent test/review.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** sources are named, but independent verification is incomplete.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE:** identity, values, worldview or interpretation.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** explicitly disclose the limitation.

A workflow run is not proof that a product works. Never invent research, citations, scientific formulas, statistics, testimonials, customers, sales, outreach or test results.

## Product cover and showroom contract
Every product/version must have:
- a unique product ID, approved name, version and dedicated product page;
- the original portrait/logo only when the real authorised asset is available, placed upper-left and unaltered except for proportional scaling;
- a unique QR at upper-right, linking to that product's own detailed passport/usage page;
- a short, readable on-image description;
- the full English identity line in a legible footer or responsive alternate layout;
- a real demo video, or the label **STORYBOARD — NOT RECORDED**;
- a real VIP screenshot, or the label **ILLUSTRATIVE MOCK-UP**;
- supported uses, inputs/outputs, limitations, usage guide, price/quote basis, enquiry path, last-tested date and QC status.

Never reuse another product's QR. Scan-test each QR on a mobile device and verify its destination before marking it tested. A storyboard is not a video; a mock-up is not a real screenshot; a QR preview is not proof of a deployed destination.

## Implementation sequence and release gates
1. Verify original photo and audio asset availability/provenance; do not fabricate replacements.
2. Improve the character brief and interaction scripts with simple, layered explanations.
3. Test evidence-state classification, respectful language, uncertainty handling and comprehension.
4. Implement the product-specific cover/passport/QR pipeline, with honest missing-media states.
5. Add authorised voice, avatar, lip-sync and live integrations only after configuration and consent.
6. Test end-to-end media on mobile; record reproducible evidence and known limitations.
7. Publish only after critical gates pass; keep failed or untested capabilities labelled **NOT VERIFIED**.

### Acceptance metrics
- At least 4 of 5 test readers can paraphrase the core message accurately.
- Unsupported prompts trigger the exact insufficiency notice.
- No insulting or stereotyping outputs in the defined regression test set.
- Original-photo fidelity is checked against the supplied source.
- Each product QR resolves to its own page and is scan-tested.
- Video, screenshot, voice, lip-sync and live states are supported by actual artefact URLs and dated test records.
- Critical gate failure blocks production release.
