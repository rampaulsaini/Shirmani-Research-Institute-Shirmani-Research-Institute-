# SRI-THINK-002 — Simple-to-Scientific Explainer Studio

**Status:** Browser-local working prototype, v1.0  
**Public tool:** https://rampaulsaini.github.io/Shirmani-Research-Institute-Shirmani-Research-Institute-/showroom/simple-to-scientific-explainer-studio.html

## Outputs
- Plain-language explanation scaffold and analogy with limits.
- Technical breakdown into testable claims.
- Evidence/uncertainty boundary and research questions.
- Research-paper outline and release checklist.
- Copy and TXT export.

## Honest capability boundary
No connected LLM/API, internet retrieval, independent verification, peer review, equation derivation from validated data, email/WhatsApp outreach, checkout, payment processing, or sales are integrated. It must not infer VERIFIED from a workflow run or a URL.

## Next production layers
1. Add allowlisted source retrieval and provenance.
2. Map individual factual claims to sources.
3. Test for invented citations, numbers, equations and unsupported certainty.
4. Human editorial review, versioning and changelog.
5. Consent-based feedback loop.
6. Integrate an AI provider only after credentials, privacy controls, rate limits and real end-to-end tests exist.
