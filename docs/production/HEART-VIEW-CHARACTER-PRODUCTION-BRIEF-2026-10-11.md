## Evidence labels
- **VERIFIED:** independently checked against adequate evidence and a recorded method.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** supported by named sources, but not independently verified by this system.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** creator's view, value or self-description.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** say “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
