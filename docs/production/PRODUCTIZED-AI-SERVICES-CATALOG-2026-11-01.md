# Omniverse — Productized AI Services Catalogue

Date: 2026-11-01
Status: proposed sellable service specifications; not a claim that integrations are deployed or customers have purchased.

## Commercial rule

Sell a clearly bounded business outcome, not access to a collection of tool names. Every engagement must have:
- a written scope, exclusions, timeline, and customer-approved price;
- customer-approved access and data-processing permissions;
- a working demonstration using synthetic or customer-authorized data;
- acceptance criteria and a handover;
- a support/maintenance boundary;
- a manual payment-verification step until a compliant provider integration is independently implemented and tested.

Do not advertise guaranteed sales, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed lead volumes, or fully autonomous operation.

## Service portfolio

### 1. Retail AI Voice Agent

**Best for:** retail shops, clinics, restaurants, service desks, and appointment-based businesses.

**Customer outcome:** handle routine inbound questions and capture enquiries when staff are busy.

**Starter deliverables**
- business-specific FAQ and product/service knowledge base;
- call greeting, intent routing, and approved answers;
- lead/appointment capture with consent;
- escalation to a human for complaints, sensitive questions, uncertain answers, and transactions;
- call summary and enquiry log;
- test report covering common questions, fallback, and handoff.

**Acceptance checks:** approved FAQ answers, correct business hours/contact details, reliable human handoff, consent notice, and no fabricated stock/pricing or booking confirmations.

**Not included by default:** outbound robocalling, payment collection, emergency dispatch, medical/legal advice, or promises that a booking/order is confirmed without checking the business system.

**Dependencies:** selected telephony/voice provider, business-approved knowledge, API hosting, secure storage, and customer consent wording. Provider and usage charges are separate.

### 2. Instantly AI — B2B Outreach Setup

**Best for:** businesses with a legitimate B2B offer and a defined target market.

**Customer outcome:** a controlled, measurable outbound email workflow.

**Starter deliverables**
- ideal-customer profile and approved prospect criteria;
- compliant contact-source and suppression rules;
- sender-domain and deliverability readiness checklist;
- two or three customer-approved email sequences;
- reply classification and human follow-up workflow;
- campaign dashboard with sends, delivery, replies, positive replies, and opt-outs.

**Acceptance checks:** test messages, unsubscribe/suppression handling, sender identity, campaign limits, and sample reporting.

**Guardrails:** no purchased/scraped unlawful lists, deceptive sender identities, spam evasion, or unapproved mass sending. Follow applicable privacy, anti-spam, and platform requirements in the target country. Instantly account and sending costs are customer/provider costs.

### 3. HubSpot AI CRM & Sales Pipeline

**Best for:** SMEs and service providers who lose track of enquiries and follow-ups.

**Customer outcome:** one visible process from new lead to quote, sale, and delivery.

**Starter deliverables**
- CRM pipeline stages and required fields;
- enquiry capture form or approved import;
- deduplication and ownership rules;
- task reminders and approved email templates;
- dashboard for lead source, stage, response time, and conversion;
- staff handover guide.

**Acceptance checks:** sample lead travels through every agreed stage; permissions are tested; reports reconcile with sample records.

**Guardrails:** no uploading personal data without authority/notice; AI-generated messages require review where appropriate. HubSpot plan, seats, and AI feature availability vary and are not included unless stated in the quote.

### 4. AI Uplift — Business Workflow Improvement

**Naming note:** “AI Uplift” is treated here as the proposed service-line name, not a claim of affiliation with a third-party vendor.

**Best for:** small teams spending repetitive time on email, documents, support, research, or reporting.

**Customer outcome:** reduce a measured, agreed manual task through a supervised AI workflow.

**Starter deliverables**
- one workflow discovery and baseline;
- one automation prototype;
- approved prompts, templates, and human-review checkpoints;
- error/fallback path and audit log;
- before/after time and quality measurement;
- staff handover.

**Acceptance checks:** test cases agreed in advance; outputs reviewed by the customer; no autonomous external action without explicit approval.

### 5. AI SEO & Content Improvement

**Best for:** local businesses and small websites seeking better technical quality and discoverability.

**Customer outcome:** a prioritized, evidence-based improvement plan and implemented on-page fixes within scope.

**Starter deliverables**
- technical and on-page SEO audit;
- search-intent and keyword map;
- page titles, descriptions, headings, internal-link recommendations;
- local business content and structured-data review where appropriate;
- content brief or a defined number of revised pages;
- baseline and follow-up reporting using available analytics/search data.

**Acceptance checks:** changes deployed or delivered as a reviewable document; URLs and tracked metrics listed; technical checks recorded.

**Guardrails:** no guaranteed first-page ranking, fake backlinks, doorway pages, fabricated reviews, or keyword stuffing. Search performance depends on competition, site history, and search engines.

### 6. Claude AI Business Assistant

**Best for:** teams needing help with long documents, research synthesis, drafting, and internal knowledge workflows.

**Customer outcome:** a controlled assistant for one defined business use case.

**Starter deliverables**
- use-case and data-permission review;
- curated customer-approved source documents;
- prompt/workflow design and citation or source-link behavior where supported;
- test set with expected answers and refusal/escalation cases;
- staff usage guide and data-retention rules.

**Acceptance checks:** representative questions tested; source-grounding and uncertainty behavior reviewed; sensitive data access restricted.

**Dependencies:** customer-provided Claude plan/API access where needed. No Anthropic partnership is implied. Model availability, pricing, and data controls depend on the customer's selected plan and current terms.

### 7. Google Maps & Local Presence Optimization

**Best for:** shops and service businesses serving a defined geographic area.

**Customer outcome:** accurate, complete, useful local business information and a repeatable customer-contact path.

**Starter deliverables**
- Google Business Profile eligibility and completeness review;
- business name, address, phone, hours, categories, services, and photos checklist;
- local landing-page and map/contact link review;
- review-response templates and owner workflow;
- local visibility baseline using permitted data;
- monthly change log and reporting option.

**Acceptance checks:** business owner approves edits; listing details match real-world records; contact and direction links tested.

**Guardrails:** no fake reviews, incentivized review manipulation, duplicate listings, misleading locations, or guaranteed map-pack placement. Google account access remains with the owner; comply with Google Business Profile and Maps Platform terms. API usage may incur charges.

## Sales and delivery workflow

1. Publish a service page with a short demo and clear “Request a consultation” form.
2. Record the enquiry in the private CRM/database; obtain consent and collect only necessary information.
3. Confirm customer problem, current process, users, systems, geography, and expected result.
4. Send a written fixed-scope proposal with exclusions, timeline, price, provider costs, and acceptance criteria.
5. Start only after customer approval and verified payment under the agreed terms.
6. Deliver demo, test evidence, handover, and customer acceptance.
7. Offer maintenance only with a separate scope and renewal terms.

## Public claims and payment safety

- Show only working demos that have been tested and label prototypes clearly.
- Do not claim vendor certification, partnership, or official integration unless documented.
- Paytm/UPI and PayPal may be displayed as manual payment methods only if the owner confirms account details and tests the payee display. A displayed ID or QR does not prove receipt.
- Mark an invoice/order as paid only after checking the actual provider transaction/account record.
- Do not place API keys, CRM tokens, telephony credentials, customer lists, or payment secrets in public website code or Git.
- Keep enquiry and CRM data private with access controls and retention limits.

## First execution priorities

1. Build one Retail AI Voice Agent demo using synthetic retail FAQs and a visible human handoff.
2. Build one CRM enquiry-to-quote pipeline with test records.
3. Create one SEO audit sample for a consenting business or a clearly labelled fictional example.
4. Offer a paid, tightly scoped pilot to one real business.
5. Record the first verified enquiry, accepted proposal, payment evidence, delivery, and acceptance separately.
