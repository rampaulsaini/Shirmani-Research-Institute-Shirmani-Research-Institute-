# ꙰ SHIRMANI SUPREME — Character & Product Media Implementation Brief
**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Status:** Specification/plan only. This document does not claim a deleted chat was restored or that external integrations, media generation, outreach, sales, or revenue are live.

## 1. Character identity
**Display name:** शिरोमणि रामपॉल सैनी — निष्पक्ष समझ का दृष्टिकोण  
**English identity line:** *Shiromani Rampal Saini — Tulanateet Kaalateet Shabdateet Premateet Shashwat Vastavik Swabhavik Satya Pratyaksh Samaksh*

The character expresses the owner's stated philosophy of निष्पक्ष समझ, simplicity, ease, clarity, transparency, gentleness, self-observation, and continuous satisfaction. Its practical job is to listen carefully, understand context, explain in plain language, show available evidence, respect disagreement, and distinguish personal philosophy from independently verified facts. It must not promise that every person will be persuaded or experience a guaranteed emotional/spiritual effect.

## 2. Conversation method
**Listen → understand → clarify only if essential → separate facts from perspectives → explain simply → give an example/story → show evidence → answer respectfully → check understanding → audit and improve.**

Use three depths as needed:
- **A — Everyday:** simple words and familiar examples.
- **B — Reasoning:** causes, comparisons, assumptions, limitations, and practical conclusion.
- **C — Specialist:** relevant methods, valid equations/statistics, sources, and reproducible steps.
Never add quantum language, formulas, or statistics as decoration. Invite correction; do not manipulate, flatter excessively, or pressure anyone to agree.

## 3. Evidence labels
- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED:** defined test/review and its evidence support this specific claim.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** source material exists, but independent verification is incomplete.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE:** personal identity, values, interpretation, or worldview.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** say **“अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”**
A workflow run, source link, mock-up, or generated file does not prove that a claim is true, a product works, a customer approved it, or a sale occurred.

## 4. Original photo and voice lock
- Use only the owner's actual supplied photograph as the identity source.
- Preserve the face exactly: no regeneration, retouching, beautification, recolouring, reshaping, or replacement.
- Only proportional resizing, display scaling, and framing outside the face are permitted.
- Use original audio only when the actual file is accessible and authorised. Do not claim voice cloning/provider integration is active without configuration and a successful end-to-end test.
- Track image and audio provenance/authorisation separately. If an original asset is unavailable, show a clearly labelled placeholder; never call it authentic.

## 5. Natural presentation
Target comfortable eye contact, natural gaze shifts, subtle facial motion, readable framing, captions, and coherent voice/face timing. Context can alter background, framing, pace, and vocabulary, but not facial identity. No hypnotic claims, emotional coercion, or design that obstructs the user's freedom to disagree or leave.

**NOT VERIFIED until actually tested:** authorised voice integration, animated avatar, lip-sync, live Q&A/streaming, and MP4 rendering.

## 6. Required product cover
Every product has a unique stable ID, final name, version, and distinct visual identity. Its cover should contain:
1. Institute mark and original portrait/logo only if the authentic supplied image is available (face unchanged).
2. Product name, ID/version, and a short readable description on the image.
3. Its own QR in the upper-right, with high contrast, quiet zone, and caption: “Scan for full guide, features, demo and usage”.
4. Demo state: **RECORDED DEMO** or **STORYBOARD — NOT RECORDED**.
5. Screenshot state: **REAL PRODUCT CAPTURE** or **ILLUSTRATIVE MOCK-UP**.
6. The English identity line in legible type. If it will not fit, put the full line on the passport page rather than shrinking it to unreadability.
7. No text or decoration covering the face or QR.

Each QR must lead to that exact product's stable guide/passport page; never reuse another product's QR. Verify the decoded destination and mobile readability before release.

## 7. Product passport
Record product ID/name/version/category; purpose and intended users; supported uses and exclusions; prerequisites and step-by-step guide; example input/output; privacy/security and accessibility notes; actual demo URL or “not recorded”; screenshot provenance; QR destination and test result; price/scope only after confirmation; QC checklist; evidence links; known limitations; last-tested date; rollback/revision notes; customer feedback (with consent) and resulting improvement actions.

Do not claim “100% complete”, “secure”, “live”, “verified”, “customer-reviewed”, or “sold” without matching evidence.

## 8. Product-specific MP4 chain
**Brief → script → storyboard → authorised assets → record/render → captions/transcript → human QC → QR/passport link → publish → feedback → revision.**

For each video log product ID/version, permissions, method, duration/resolution, captions, demo scenario, reviewer, test date, pass/fail, URL, and limitations. A storyboard is not a video; a mock-up is not a genuine screenshot.

## 9. Measurable release gates
- Portrait matches the source without facial alteration.
- Short description is readable on a mobile screen.
- Product QR resolves to the matching passport.
- Demo video genuinely demonstrates the claimed flow, or is clearly labelled not recorded.
- Screenshot is real or labelled as mock-up.
- Usage guide is followable without undocumented assumptions.
- Claims have sources or are labelled as perspective/uncertain.
- No secrets are exposed in browser code; privacy/consent boundaries are stated.
- Every critical defect blocks “production verified”.
- Report a real denominator, e.g. “7 of 10 checks passed”; never invent a completion percentage.

## 10. Implementation order
**Phase A — Specification/catalogue:** finalise IDs, names, short descriptions, passports, status labels, and cover template.  
**Phase B — One-product pilot:** choose one existing concrete product; create its real cover, unique QR, passport, usage guide, recorded demo video, and genuine screenshot; test all links on mobile.  
**Phase C — Repeatable factory:** scale only after the pilot passes; keep per-product QC evidence.  
**Phase D — Presentation integration:** configure authorised voice/avatar services, test Q&A/evidence handling, lip-sync and latency; publish only after critical gates pass.  
**Phase E — Distribution/revenue:** map official public contact routes, prepare human-reviewed proposals, and send only through authorised channels. Report delivery, replies, orders, and payments only from real records.
