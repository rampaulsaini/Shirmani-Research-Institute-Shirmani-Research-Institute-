## One clear initial offer

**Hindi/English Research Summary Pilot**

For a client who provides a topic and source links:
- agreed brief and scope;
- concise summary in the agreed language;
- source ledger with links and access dates where available;
- key findings, limitations, and questions needing further verification;
- one consolidated revision round;
- editable document or agreed text format.

Exclude legal/medical/investment advice, fabricated citations, guaranteed outcomes, and claims that sources were independently verified unless verification was actually performed and recorded.

### Proposed pilot price (not market-validated)

- Small: ₹1,500 — up to 800 words, up to 5 client-provided or agreed sources, delivery target 3 working days after scope and source receipt.
- Standard: ₹3,000 — up to 1,500 words, up to 10 agreed sources, delivery target 5 working days.
- Bilingual: ₹4,500 — up to 1,000 words in each of Hindi and English, up to 8 agreed sources, delivery target 5 working days.

These are initial test prices, not established market rates or a promise that a customer will accept them. Confirm complexity, source availability, tax/fee treatment, deadline, deliverables, revision limit, and payment terms in writing before accepting work. For a first pilot, agree a written advance (suggested 50%) and balance on delivery only if both parties accept those terms. Do not start paid work until the scope and payment arrangement are confirmed.

## Seven-day execution checklist

### Day 1 — Make the offer easy to inspect
- Open the existing public sample portfolio and enquiry builder.
- Check all links, spelling, mobile layout, sample quality, and price labels.
- Label every sample as illustrative; label prices as proposed.
- Prepare one sample research summary using clearly identified public sources and a visible limitation note.

### Day 2 — Select relevant prospects
- Identify 10 organisations or professionals whose published work indicates a plausible need for bilingual writing or research summaries.
- Record organisation, relevant need, official public contact page, source URL, date checked, and why the offer fits.
- Prefer research groups, education providers, nonprofits, and small businesses with an evident content need. Do not guess private email addresses or scrape personal contact data.

### Days 3–4 — Send carefully reviewed proposals
- Prepare up to 5 individually tailored messages based on the official contact route published by each prospect.
- Human-review every message. Send manually through the stated official channel; this repository tool does not send messages.
- Be transparent that this is an introductory pilot. Do not claim prior clients, outcomes, credentials, or testimonials without evidence.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy, anti-spam, and platform rules.

### Days 5–6 — Respond and scope
- Reply to genuine enquiries.
- Confirm the problem, audience, language, source material, word limit, deadline, revision count, price, and payment terms.
- Send a written scope/quote. Record accepted, declined, and pending outcomes accurately.

### Day 7 — Review evidence
- Count prospects researched, messages actually sent, replies, qualified enquiries, quotes, accepted scopes, paid advances, delivered work, and collected balance.
- Record dates and evidence for every state transition.
- If there is no qualified response, review the offer, sample, prospect fit, and message before increasing volume.
