# First-Client Outreach Kit — Shirmani Research Institute
**Prepared:** 2026-10-18  
**Purpose:** turn the existing Hindi/English writing and research service into a small, measurable, human-approved outreach sprint.

> This is an execution asset, not evidence that any prospect was contacted, a proposal accepted, or income received. No message is sent automatically. Review every recipient and message, use only an official public contact route, and record a send only after it actually happens.

## 1. Start with one offer, not the entire platform

For the first conversation, offer one bounded pilot:
- **Short writing/editing:** one agreed short page/article or polished draft; proposed reference from ₹999.
- **Source-linked research summary:** a concise summary, source links, key findings and limitations; proposed reference from ₹1,999.
- **Three-piece website/blog pack:** up to three agreed content pieces; proposed reference from ₹2,999.

These are proposed starting references, not validated market rates or binding quotes. Before work begins, confirm audience, topic, word count, language, source requirements, deliverables, revision count, due date, final price, and payment terms in writing. Do not begin unpaid speculative work beyond a small illustrative sample.

## 2. Prioritise prospects by fit

Choose organisations only when a visible content need can be named. Research the organisation's own website and official contact page; capture the exact URL and date. Do not guess email addresses or harvest private personal data.

| Segment | Observable fit signal | Best first offer | Evidence required before contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education / training provider | Public course or learner page needs clearer Hindi/English explanations | One-page course or learner guide | Official website + specific page URL |
| Research / civil-society organisation | Public report, programme or project needs a plain-language summary | Source-linked research summary | Official report/programme URL |
| Small business / service provider | Service pages are sparse, unclear or available in only one language | One service page or small website-copy pack | Official site + named page gap |

Avoid mass messages. If no specific, relevant need is visible, do not contact that prospect.

## 5. Proposal acceptance checklist

Before sending a quote, fill every applicable item:
- [ ] Client's requested outcome and intended audience
- [ ] Deliverable format and exact count/word range
- [ ] Language(s), tone and accessibility needs
- [ ] Client-provided sources and independent-source expectations
- [ ] What will not be covered; evidence limitations
- [ ] Delivery date and dependencies on client responses
- [ ] Included revision rounds and what counts as a new request
- [ ] Final fee, currency, taxes if applicable, and payment schedule
- [ ] Confidentiality, permission to use supplied material, and data handling
- [ ] Written client approval before starting

## 6. Minimal private pipeline tracker

Keep prospect details in a private, access-controlled sheet—not in a public repository. Suggested columns:

`research_date, organisation, segment, specific_need, official_website, official_contact_page, source_url, fit_reason, draft_status, human_review, sent_at, reply_status, enquiry_status, proposal_amount, order_confirmed_at, payment_received_amount, payment_evidence_ref, delivery_status, next_action, opt_out`

Allowed status values:
- Draft: `RESEARCHED`, `DRAFTED`, `NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW`, `APPROVED_TO_SEND`
- Contact: `SENT` only after actual sending; `REPLIED`, `NO_REPLY`, `OPTED_OUT`
- Commercial: `ENQUIRY`, `PROPOSAL_SENT`, `ACCEPTED`, `DECLINED`, `DELIVERED`
- Money: record amount received only against actual payment evidence; a quote is not revenue.

Do not store credentials, payment details, sensitive personal information, or confidential client briefs in the public repository.

## 7. 48-hour action sprint

### First working block
1. Review the public writing/research service page and illustrative samples; correct anything not ready for public use.
2. Select one service and define the maximum scope you can deliver reliably.
3. Find 5 organisations with a specific, visible fit using official pages. Record source URLs and why each is relevant.
4. Draft 5 individually tailored messages using the templates above.

### Second working block
1. Human-review each draft for relevance, accuracy, respectful tone and correct link.
2. Send only approved messages through appropriate official contact routes; no bulk sending.
3. Record actual send time and follow-up date.
4. Prepare a written scope/quote promptly for any qualified reply.
5. Review outcomes after 48 hours; do not label no-replies as failures or invent conversion rates.

### Metrics to report
- Prospects researched with official-source evidence
- Drafts prepared / human-approved / actually sent
- Replies / qualified enquiries / proposals sent
- Orders confirmed / payments received / deliverables accepted
- Opt-outs, complaints, revisions and lessons

For each conversion rate, show numerator and denominator and calculate only when the denominator is non-zero. **Revenue = payment actually received**, not a proposed price, quote, order intention or workflow success.
