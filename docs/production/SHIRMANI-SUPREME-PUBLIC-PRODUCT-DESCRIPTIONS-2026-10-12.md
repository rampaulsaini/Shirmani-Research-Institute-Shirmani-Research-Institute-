# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Public Product Descriptions v1
**Updated:** 2026-10-12

These descriptions cover documented browser-local prototypes. A storyboard is not a recorded MP4; a cover is not a captured VIP screenshot; a QR preview is not a deployed QR asset. Nothing here proves sales, revenue, independent verification, or a live AI/voice integration.

## SRI-PRES-001 — Heart-View Character Studio
**Short description:** Create a clear, respectful presentation-character brief with evidence boundaries and a practical rehearsal checklist.
**Useful for:** Educators, researchers, presenters, and creators who need consistent, understandable communication.
**What it does:** Lets a user define audience and context, set tone and evidence labels, prepare a structured brief, and review measurable rehearsal checks.
**Output:** Browser-local brief and checklist, copyable or saveable as text.
**Not included:** Live AI avatar, voice clone, lip-sync engine, MP4 factory, independent scientific verification, or server persistence.

## SRI-THINK-001 — Heart-View Explainer & Research Studio
**Short description:** Turn a topic into a plain-language explanation, everyday analogy, evidence questions, and research-paper outline.
**Useful for:** Learners, teachers, writers, and researchers who need a clear first structure for a difficult idea.
**Output:** Editable explanation outline, analogy, source-ledger prompts, evidence-boundary notes, and research outline.
**Not included:** Independent web research, fact-checking, or connected AI model; a human must verify claims and citations.

## SRI-MEDIA-001 — Product Demo & Visual Brief Studio
**Short description:** Plan a product-specific demo video, VIP screenshot composition, QR destination, and release checklist.
**Output:** Visual/demo brief for the production team.
**Not included:** Recorded MP4, real screenshot, generated QR image, published listing, or independent QC result.

## SRI-MEDIA-002 — Product Media Launch Kit
**Short description:** Assemble a draft product launch pack with cover, five-scene demo storyboard, usage guide, passport, and QR destination preview.
**Output:** Draft launch assets supported by the browser tool.
**Not included:** Rendered video, real running-product screenshot, published store listing, payment processing, or certified quality.

## SRI-MEDIA-003 — Product Release Pack Builder
**Short description:** Prepare a product-specific release pack with QR destination, cover SVG, passport JSON, usage guide, and demo storyboard.
**Output:** Browser-generated draft release assets for review/export.
**Not included:** Recorded MP4, published listing, outreach, payment processing, or proof of sale. QR generation depends on a public CDN.

## SRI-WRITE-006 — First Client Deliverable Studio
**Short description:** Organise a Hindi/English writing or research brief into a reviewable first-draft delivery pack.
**Useful for:** Freelancers, small businesses, educators, and organisations needing website/blog copy, research summaries, explanatory articles, translation/editing, or proposal drafts.
**Inputs:** Goal, topic, audience, language, word limit, deadline, source links, budget, constraints, and revision allowance.
**Output:** Structured draft scaffold, scope/terms checklist, source notes, and copy/TXT/print options.
**Not included:** Completed paid client work, connected research, independent fact-checking, email sending, CRM, checkout, or payment processing. Confirm scope, price, timeline, rights, and payment terms in writing before starting.

## SRI-WRITE-007 — Bilingual Website Copy Pack Studio
**Short description:** Draft a starter website copy pack in Hindi, English, or both, including homepage, about, services, call-to-action, and SEO description.
**Useful for:** New websites, small organisations, independent creators, and service providers.
**Output:** Editable page-copy drafts and quality-control notes, with copy/TXT/print export.
**Not included:** Website publishing, independent verification, message sending, or payment processing.

## Required standard for every product release
1. Unique product ID/name and a one-sentence on-image description readable on mobile.
2. Use the owner's exact original portrait/logo only when the supplied file is accessible; allow proportional resizing only—no regeneration, retouching, recolouring, reshaping, or substitution.
3. Use the owner's original audio only when the exact file is accessible and authorised. Do not claim voice integration without a recorded end-to-end test.
4. A product-specific QR must point to that product's own long description/usage page and be tested after deployment.
5. Attach an actual product-specific demo MP4; otherwise label it **“demo storyboard — video not recorded.”**
6. Attach an actual screenshot captured from the running product; otherwise label it **“visual concept — screenshot not captured.”**
7. Include a passport and usage guide listing inputs, outputs, privacy, limitations, version, test status, and support/enquiry route.
8. Advance release state only with evidence: Draft → Built → Tested → Published.
9. Feed consented customer feedback into a versioned issue/fix/regression log. Never invent reviews or ratings.
10. State price, deliverables, revisions, deadline, rights, and payment terms before accepting paid work.

## Release checklist
- [ ] Description reviewed for plain language and accurate scope.
- [ ] Correct original portrait/logo used if available; identity not altered.
- [ ] QR opens the correct product-specific long description on mobile and desktop.
- [ ] MP4 actually recorded and checked, or clearly marked pending.
- [ ] Screenshot actually captured, or clearly marked as a concept.
- [ ] Passport and usage guide match the current version.
- [ ] Mobile, accessibility, privacy, and evidence-boundary tests pass.
- [ ] Commercial scope and payment terms agreed in writing.
