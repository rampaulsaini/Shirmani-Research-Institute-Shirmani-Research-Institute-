# Omniverse AI Products & Business Automation Catalogue

Date: 2026-11-01
Status: proposed service catalogue and integration plan. These entries are not claims of completed deployments, vendor partnerships, or customer results.

## 1. Retail AI Voice Agent

**Category:** Omniverse-buildable service / retail automation

**Customer problem:** Retail shops lose enquiries when staff are busy or unavailable. Customers need quick answers about opening hours, stock, prices, product details, order status, returns, and pickup.

**Proposed deliverable:**
- Inbound voice agent with clear disclosure that the caller is speaking with an AI assistant.
- Product-catalogue knowledge base with owner-approved stock and price data.
- Hindi and English first; add further languages only after voice-quality testing.
- Escalation to a human for uncertain answers, complaints, refunds, payment disputes, or sensitive requests.
- Optional call summary and lead capture, with consent and data minimization.
- Retailer dashboard for unanswered questions, call outcomes, and human follow-up.

**Acceptance tests:** correct answers from a test catalogue; no invented stock/prices; human handoff; consent notice; call-log privacy; latency and availability measurement.

**Status:** proposed build; no live phone number, telephony account, production agent, or retail customer is evidenced here.

## 2. Instantly AI

**Category:** third-party sales engagement / lead prospecting integration

Official site: https://instantly.ai/
Official AI Sales Agent documentation: https://help.instantly.ai/en/articles/14299661-ai-sales-agent
Official product overview: https://help.instantly.ai/en/articles/6221655-what-is-instantly

**Verified product scope from vendor materials:** B2B lead discovery, enrichment, personalized cold-email campaigns, follow-ups, AI sales/reply agents, and campaign analytics. Vendor documentation describes a review/approval workflow; autonomous sending is a configurable mode and should not be enabled without careful review.

**Omniverse use case:** find relevant institutional prospects for a clearly scoped Omniverse service; draft tailored outreach; require human approval before sending; record replies and qualified opportunities in the private enquiry/CRM pipeline.

**Controls:** comply with applicable email/privacy laws, use relevant business contacts, provide opt-out, suppress complaints/bounces, protect sender-domain reputation, and never fabricate prior relationships or results.

**Status:** external tool reference only; no paid account, API key, connected mailbox, integration, campaign, or vendor partnership is evidenced.

## 5. AI SEO

**Category:** Omniverse-buildable digital visibility service

**Proposed deliverable:**
- Technical SEO audit: crawlability, indexability, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots directives, mobile usability, page speed, and structured data.
- Search-intent and keyword mapping for each genuine service page.
- Helpful original page copy, titles, descriptions, headings, internal links, and image alt text.
- Local SEO basics where the customer has a real eligible business location.
- Measurement plan using Search Console and analytics when the owner grants access.
- Monthly report of impressions, clicks, indexed pages, qualified enquiries, and conversion events.

**Controls:** no guaranteed rankings, fake reviews, doorway pages, keyword stuffing, mass-generated low-value pages, or misleading backlinks. Distinguish Google organic search from AI answer-engine visibility; report measured outcomes only.

**Status:** proposed service; no customer website audit, Search Console connection, or ranking result is evidenced.

## Commercial packaging — initial pilot

Start with one narrow paid pilot instead of claiming all services are production-ready:
1. Choose one retail shop or institution and one service.
2. Agree written scope, data access, exclusions, delivery date, and acceptance criteria.
3. Demonstrate a working prototype with test data.
4. Send a written quote; do not start paid delivery until payment is verified in the actual provider account.
5. Deliver, obtain customer acceptance, and record evidence with private data protected.

Suggested first pilot candidates:
- Retail Voice Agent: one shop, one product catalogue, inbound FAQs and human handoff.
- AI SEO: one website, technical audit and prioritized fixes.
- Lead pipeline: a manually approved outreach sequence connected to a CRM.

No pricing is set in this catalogue. Quote only after scope and delivery cost are known.
