# First Client Outreach Kit — Shirmani Research Institute
**Prepared:** 2026-10-10  
**Purpose:** Convert the existing Hindi/English writing and research service into a small, measurable, respectful client-acquisition sprint.  
**Status:** Outreach assets prepared; no prospect has been contacted and no sale or income is claimed by this document.

## 1. Lead offer
Start with one clear offer: **source-linked Hindi/English research summaries or website/blog writing.**

Deliver only the agreed scope: writing/research summary, relevant source links, stated limitations, and agreed revisions. Do not promise legal/medical advice, guaranteed rankings, guaranteed results, or AI/video/payment features that are not actually connected.

### Proposed pilot price references (not binding quotes)
- Short writing/editing: from ₹999
- Source-linked research summary: from ₹1,999
- Website/blog pack of up to three agreed pieces: from ₹2,999

Before work begins, confirm topic, audience, language, word count, format, source requirements, deadline, revisions, final price and payment terms in writing.

## 2. First prospect cohort: research, don't blast
Build a list of **10 qualified prospects** across:
1. Education/training organisations publishing Hindi/English material.
2. Research centres or non-profits needing public-facing summaries.
3. Small businesses with outdated or thin website/blog content.
4. Science/environment organisations publishing public education resources.
5. Publishers, educators or independent creators with a visible content need.

These are categories, not verified leads. For each real prospect, record its name, official website, evidence of a relevant need, official contact page/email, date checked, and fit score. Use public official channels only. Do not guess email addresses, scrape private details, or send mass unsolicited messages.

### Qualification score (0–2 per item)
- Public evidence of a specific writing/research need.
- Official contact route is available.
- Service is relevant to the organisation's audience.
- A plausible small pilot can be scoped.
- Contact terms and applicable law/platform rules permit the approach.

Prioritise scores 8–10 for human review. This score is an internal aid, not proof that a prospect wants or will buy the service.

## 3. First-contact email draft
**Subject:** Hindi/English writing and source-linked research support

Hello [Name/Team],

I’m reaching out from Shirmani Research Institute. We are introducing a focused service for Hindi/English writing, research summaries, and website/blog content.

I noticed [specific, factual observation from the organisation's official website]. If a concise article or source-linked summary would be useful, I can review a short brief and propose the exact scope, timeline, revision terms, and price for your approval before starting.

A sample and service outline are available here: [public service/showroom URL].

If this is not relevant to your work, please disregard this message. I will respect any request not to follow up.

Regards,  
Shirmani Research Institute  
[Public service URL]  
[Official reply/contact route]

**Personalisation rule:** Replace the bracketed observation with a real, checked fact. If no specific fit can be identified, do not send this message.

## 5. Follow-up policy
- Day 0: send only after human review through an appropriate official channel; log the actual send reference.
- Day 5–7: at most one polite follow-up if permitted and there has been no opt-out.
- After no reply or an opt-out: stop. Do not add recipients to recurring campaigns without an appropriate basis.

**Follow-up draft:**  
Hello [Name/Team], I’m following up once on my note about scoped Hindi/English writing and source-linked research support. If this is not relevant, no reply is needed and I will not follow up again. Thank you.

## 6. Prospect ledger fields
Keep this in a private, access-controlled tracker:
- Prospect ID; organisation name; official website URL; official contact URL/published generic business email.
- Specific public evidence of need (short note + source URL); fit score (0–10); date checked; proposed service.
- Draft status: NOT_STARTED / DRAFTED / HUMAN_REVIEW / APPROVED / REJECTED.
- Outreach status: NOT_SENT / SENT / REPLIED / OPT_OUT / CLOSED.
- Actual send timestamp/reference (only after sending); reply summary and next action.
- Enquiry / proposal / order / payment status, each supported by an actual record.
- Data retention/deletion note.

Do not put private contact lists, credentials, customer data or email secrets in public repository files.

## 8. Weekly metrics
Report exact counts: prospects researched; official contact routes found; drafts prepared; drafts approved; messages actually sent; replies; qualified enquiries; proposals; confirmed orders; payments received; accepted deliverables; revisions/complaints; opt-outs.

Conversion rate = next-stage count ÷ previous-stage count × 100 only when the previous-stage count is greater than zero. Revenue means payments actually received, not proposed prices, quoted value, clicks, or messages sent.

## 9. Production / QC / showroom loop
1. **Institute:** source the client brief and define scope.
2. **Factory:** create the deliverable and preserve source notes.
3. **QC / Gate:** review accuracy, citations, language, originality, privacy, scope and format; log corrections.
4. **Showroom / Delivery:** show samples and proposed service prices; deliver agreed work; record feedback with permission.
5. **Improvement:** aggregate feedback, remove personal data, and improve templates or quality checks.

Do not describe a draft, browser-local prototype, planned integration, or scheduled workflow as a finished live service unless its actual behaviour has been implemented and tested.
