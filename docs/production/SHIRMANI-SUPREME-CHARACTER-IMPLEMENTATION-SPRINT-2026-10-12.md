## Acceptance tests
1. A simple question receives a short, plain-language answer.
2. A complex question receives a simple explanation, example, then deeper reasoning.
3. A source-based factual answer includes a usable source link.
4. A philosophical statement is labelled as a personal/philosophical perspective.
5. Insufficient evidence returns exactly: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
6. The system never invents a citation, review, sale, contact attempt, test result or capability.
7. The original portrait remains unaltered apart from proportional display scaling.
8. Voice playback, facial presentation and lip-sync are verified only after an actual end-to-end test with results recorded.
9. Product QR opens that exact product's detailed page/passport; each product's QR and media record are distinct.
10. Any failed critical gate blocks a “production-ready” claim.
