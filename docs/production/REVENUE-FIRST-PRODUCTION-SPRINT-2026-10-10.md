# Revenue-First Production Sprint — 2026-10-10

## Mission
Turn existing browser-local writing/research tools into a clearly described, customer-ready service workflow. Do not confuse workflow activity, catalogue identities, drafts, or QC records with completed customer deliverables, sales, or income.

## Current offer (proposed, not a confirmed sale)
- ₹499 — Hindi/English writing polish (up to 500 words; one revision).
- ₹999 — source-based research summary (up to 1,000 words; up to five client-approved sources; one revision).
- ₹1,499 — website/blog draft (up to 1,000 words; one revision).
These are pilot price hypotheses, not market-validated rates. Confirm scope, delivery date, source access, revision policy and payment terms in writing before starting. Do not begin paid work until both parties agree.

## Production chain
1. **Institute / intake:** capture client objective, audience, language, word limit, deadline, source URLs, budget and permitted use.
2. **Factory / draft:** produce a first deliverable with source notes, assumptions, open questions and a change log.
3. **QC / release gate:** check scope match, spelling, structure, source traceability, quotations, privacy, originality and factual uncertainty. Mark each check PASS / FAIL / NOT CHECKED; no blanket “verified” label.
4. **Showroom / delivery:** publish only a sample or service description that contains no client-confidential material. Deliver the agreed file through an agreed channel after payment terms are met.
5. **Feedback loop:** request optional customer feedback; record consent, rating, issue, corrective action and release version. Never fabricate reviews.

## Seven-day execution targets
| Metric | Target | Counting rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevant prospects researched | 30 | Unique organisations/persons with a relevant public-facing contact route |
| Personalised drafts prepared | 15 | Draft tailored to a prospect; not counted as sent |
| Outreach messages actually sent | 10 | Count only after a real authorised send action |
| Replies | 2 | Count actual replies only |
| Qualified briefs | 1 | Need, scope and budget/timeline discussed |
| Paid orders | 1 | Count only after explicit acceptance; payment recorded separately |
| Delivered work | 1 | Customer-confirmed receipt, not merely file generation |

Targets are activity goals, not predictions or promises. If no reply/order occurs, report the observed number without implying revenue.

## Prospecting and outreach safeguards
- Prioritise organisations with a clear fit for Hindi/English writing, research summaries or website/blog content.
- Use official websites and published contact routes; comply with site terms and applicable anti-spam/privacy rules.
- Personalise each message, identify the sender honestly, explain relevance, provide a simple opt-out, and stop follow-ups after an opt-out.
- No bulk scraping, guessed personal emails, impersonation, deceptive claims, or automatic sending without an authorised integration and human approval.
- Maintain separate states: DISCOVERED → CONTACT_ROUTE_FOUND → DRAFT_READY → HUMAN_APPROVED → SENT (evidence required) → REPLIED → BRIEF_CONFIRMED → PAID (payment evidence) → DELIVERED → FEEDBACK_RECEIVED.

## Public service enquiry fields
Service; client objective; topic; language; word limit; deadline; source links; budget; intended audience; preferred delivery format; contact route; consent to reply.

## Daily operating loop
- 30 min: find 5 relevant prospects from official public sources.
- 30 min: create up to 3 tailored drafts.
- 60–120 min: improve one portfolio sample or fulfil an agreed brief.
- 15 min: update the pipeline and record evidence.
- End of day: report counts for prospects, drafts, sent messages, replies, briefs, paid orders, delivered work and lessons.

## Security and privacy gates
- Never commit API keys, passwords, client personal data, private briefs or payment details.
- Use least-privilege repository/workflow permissions and review third-party actions.
- Keep client source material only as long as necessary and only with permission.
- Do not claim a penetration test, AI-provider integration, email/WhatsApp sending, checkout, persistent backend or continuous live service unless that capability is actually connected and tested.

## Completion definition
The sprint is complete only when there is evidence of (a) a public offer page, (b) an intake brief, (c) a QC checklist, (d) at least one manually reviewed outreach draft, and (e) a truthful activity report. A sale or income is reported only when independently evidenced by the actual transaction record.
