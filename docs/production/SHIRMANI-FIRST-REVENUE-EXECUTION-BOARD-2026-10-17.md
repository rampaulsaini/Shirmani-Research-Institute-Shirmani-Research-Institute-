## First-client workflow
1. Select one narrow service and one relevant prospective client.
2. Review the organisation's public website and confirm the service plausibly fits its stated needs.
3. Prepare an individually tailored proposal with one useful sample or small scope suggestion; do not imply prior client work or results that are not evidenced.
4. Human-review the recipient, facts, price and message.
5. Send only through a legitimate public business contact channel and record the actual send timestamp. Drafting is not sending.
6. Record replies accurately; a reply is not an accepted quote.
7. Confirm scope, deliverables, deadline, revisions and payment terms in writing.
8. Start only after the agreed start condition is met. Deliver the file and record client acceptance/revisions.
9. Mark revenue as received only after verifying the actual payment transaction.
