# Shirmani Research Institute — Next Execution Plan
Date: 2026-10-17
Status: Proposed execution plan; no deployment, sales, or external verification is claimed by this document.

## Immediate objective
Make the public interface understandable and create the first verifiable revenue opportunity without disrupting existing work.

## Public interface: one clear journey
1. Hero: one-sentence purpose, language switch, and sign-in/create-account only when real authentication exists.
2. Start here: search, categories, featured working demos.
3. Available now: Hindi/English writing and research service with a clear enquiry action.
4. Categorized catalogue: Research & Education; AI/ML/NLP; Creator & Media Studio; Nature & Public Good; Commerce & Institutions; Social Platform.
5. Every product card: stable ID, plain-language value, accurate status (Available / Demo / Prototype / Planned / Blocked), demo, guide, version, price or quote-required, limitations, support and feedback.
6. Heart-View Preview and character studio; separate script, generated media, authorized voice, lip-sync, and live-Q&A evidence.
7. Research library, institutional tools, trust/safety, privacy, accessibility, contact and sitemap.

Preserve existing routes. Do not delete or hide project work permanently to make the homepage simpler; group it in navigation, check links, and redirect replaced URLs. A filter may hide an item from a view but must not delete its source or history.

## First paid service
Use existing routes: `writing-research-enquiry.html` and `products/concrete/SRI-WR-001-writing-research-enquiry-studio.html`.
Offer: Hindi/English writing and editing; source-based research summaries; website/blog drafts; institutional briefs and literature scans.
Flow: enquiry → scope confirmation → written quote → acceptance and payment terms → delivery → client acceptance → feedback.
Never treat an enquiry as an order, a sent message as a reply, a quote as revenue, or an unpaid order as income.

### Pilot prices to test (indicative, not confirmed quotes)
- Short edit / up to 500 words: ₹499–₹999.
- Website/blog draft / up to 1,000 words: ₹1,499–₹2,999.
- Source-based research summary / up to 1,500 words: ₹2,499–₹5,999.
Confirm complexity, sources, revisions, timeline, and payment/tax terms before accepting a job.

## Measurable prospecting experiment
Prepare 10 individually tailored proposals for relevant organisations or businesses. Send only after human review using legitimate public business contact channels; no scraped lists, bulk spam, fabricated endorsements, or unapproved automatic sending.
Track separately: qualified prospects, proposals drafted, messages actually sent, replies, quotes accepted, paid orders verified, deliverables sent, client acceptance, revisions, cash received with evidence, hours spent and net margin. If response is low, revise offer and targeting; do not claim results without records.

## Product lifecycle and anti-regression
Stable product ID → owner/source → status → demo → test cases → security/privacy review → price/terms → release notes → feedback → next version.
States: IDEA, SPECIFIED, PROTOTYPE, DEMO-VERIFIED, SECURITY-REVIEWED, RELEASED, BLOCKED, DEPRECATED.
Archive rather than delete unless there is a documented, approved reason. Keep source material and history.

## Human-presentation verification chain
Voice input → authorized voice asset → script/transcript → portrait/avatar render → measured lip-sync → playable presentation → live Q&A integration → audit.
Evidence must be recorded separately for every stage. A completed sample video alone does not prove voice identity, portrait fidelity, lip-sync accuracy, live Q&A or production integration. Reuse existing referenced assets; do not ask the owner to resend them. Report only genuinely inaccessible assets or missing authorization.

## Security baseline
- Never expose API keys or private media in client code/public commits.
- Enforce server-side authorization, least privilege, rate limits, input validation, secure sessions and dependency scanning.
- Back up before broad changes; keep changes small and reversible.
- Test mobile/desktop, keyboard navigation, contrast, forms, links and errors.
- No real customer data in fixtures. Do not advertise sign-up/login without a working authentication backend and privacy notice.
