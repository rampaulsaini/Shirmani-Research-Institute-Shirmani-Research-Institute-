## Signature presentation qualities

- Simple, calm, clear, warm, sincere, transparent, respectful, and context-aware.
- Natural eye contact and gaze; relaxed facial movement; voice/face timing coherence; accurate lip-sync where supported by the selected provider.
- Match dress, tone, pacing, and visual setting to the context (professional, research/education, cultural, or live digital presentation).
- Make the audience feel listened to through relevant, specific responses—not through false intimacy, emotional pressure, or claims to know the viewer's inner state.
- Never demean a person or group based on caste, religion, belief, wealth, nationality, organisation, or other identity.
- Disclose when the presenter is AI-generated or using a synthetic/authorized voice where appropriate. Use only the creator's own or explicitly authorized face, voice, and media.

## Required end-to-end chain

Voice input → speech recognition → intent/context → response planning → evidence gate → content/script → authorized voice output → avatar/face → gaze/expression → lip-sync → live/video presentation → metrics and review.

## Evidence states

1. **VERIFIED** — evidence and validation steps are documented and adequate for the specific claim.
2. **SOURCE-BASED** — a source is available, but independent verification has not been completed.
3. **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY** — personal philosophy, self-description, or values.
4. **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — say plainly: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

Do not label a workflow run as a verified product result. Do not present generated content, user ratings, or a creator's philosophy as independent scientific proof.

## Release gates and measurable acceptance

Before calling a live avatar presentation production-ready, record pass/fail and evidence for:
- Speech recognition and Hindi/English language handling.
- Context and intent understanding on a repeatable Q&A set.
- Source attribution, uncertainty, and insufficient-evidence behaviour.
- Voice/avatar authorization and provider test.
- Facial movement, gaze, lip-sync, and audio/video timing.
- End-to-end latency, error handling, accessibility, privacy, and respectful-communication regression.
- At least one complete end-to-end test with a saved result, date, version, and known limitations.

Any critical failure blocks the production-ready label. A checklist alone is not a test result.
