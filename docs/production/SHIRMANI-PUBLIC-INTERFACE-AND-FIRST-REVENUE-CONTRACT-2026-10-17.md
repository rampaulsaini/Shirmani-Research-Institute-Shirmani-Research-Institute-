# SHIRMANI Public Interface & First-Revenue Execution Contract
**Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** implementation contract; not a claim of deployment, sales, or independent verification.

## 1. Immediate objective
Make the public entry page understandable and make the existing Hindi/English writing and research service easy to enquire about, scope, deliver, and measure. Preserve existing research, products, character, media, and automation work. Reorganising the front page must not delete less-prominent work.

## 2. Public interface: one clear vertical journey
1. **Hero:** “Shirmani Research Institute”, one-sentence purpose, two actions: “Explore products” and “Request writing/research”.
2. **Start here:** three routes—need a service; try a product demo; explore research and mission.
3. **Services available now:** Hindi/English writing polish, source-based research summaries, website/blog content. Each card states audience, deliverables, required inputs, indicative-price status, scope-dependent timeline, and enquiry route.
4. **Try before you buy:** searchable categories—Writing & Research; Heart-View & Presentation; Product Media; Production & Security; Learning & Evidence; Social Platform. Each product card states what it does, intended user, demo, readiness, limitations, version, and next action.
5. **Order journey:** Brief → written scope/quote → customer approval → agreed payment terms → work → quality review → delivery → feedback. Do not imply checkout exists unless tested.
6. **Research & evidence:** papers, source ledgers, methods, limitations, and explicit status labels.
7. **Institute mission:** environment, education, health, shelter, livelihood, and human dignity as goals; distinguish goals from measured outcomes.
8. **Creators & presentation:** Heart-View, authorized photo/voice, presentation workflow, media demos, and actual provider/test status.
9. **Institutional collaboration:** labs, education, public-interest organisations, creators, businesses; enquiry route, not unverified bulk-email claims.
10. **Community/social roadmap:** account creation, login, moderation, privacy, reporting, accessibility, and live-program status. A browser-local feed must be labelled prototype, not a global network.
11. **Footer:** contact/enquiry, privacy, terms, accessibility, readiness definitions, and change log.

## 3. Visual and usability rules
- One primary title, clear headings, no duplicated banners/catalogue sections.
- Mobile-first layout, readable Hindi/English, sufficient contrast, keyboard focus, accessible labels, no horizontal overflow.
- Keep technical branding separate from evidence-based capability claims; don't use “quantum”, “infinity”, or “supreme” as proof.
- Never label a product live/connected unless its external integration was actually tested and recorded.
- Existing assets remain reachable through category pages, search, or archive.

## 4. First-revenue offer
**A. Hindi/English writing polish:** one agreed document edited for clarity, structure, grammar, and tone, plus a short change note. Inputs: source text, audience, purpose, language, word count, deadline, style guide.

**B. Source-based research summary:** concise summary, source ledger with URLs/dates, key findings, limitations, and open questions. Inputs: question, reader, date range, allowed sources, length, citation style.

**C. Website/blog draft:** agreed page or post, headline options, structure, draft, editorial checklist. Inputs: audience, offer, source facts, desired action, language, length.

Repository price references are proposed anchors, not validated market rates. Confirm exact scope, quote, deadline, revision count, and payment terms in writing before work begins. High-stakes content needs qualified review.

## 5. Outreach: no fabricated contacts or mass spam
1. Find a relevant organisation on its official public website.
2. Log organisation, official URL, public contact route, relevance, date checked, and opt-out status.
3. Draft one tailored message with a specific deliverable and low-risk pilot.
4. Human-review recipient, claims, attachments, and applicable consent/legal requirements.
5. Send manually through an authorised account and log only messages actually sent.
6. Follow up once where appropriate; stop on opt-out or rejection.
7. Track reply, qualified lead, proposal, accepted scope, delivery, invoice, and payment separately.

Do not enable email/WhatsApp automation without credentials, consent/legal review, rate limits, suppression list, audit trail, and successful end-to-end tests.

## 6. Seven-day measurable test
Activity targets, not promised outcomes:
- Research 20 relevant organisations using official sources.
- Prepare 10 individually reviewed outreach drafts; send only those passing human review.
- Log 100% of messages actually sent with date and source.
- Track replies, positive replies, calls, proposals, accepted pilots, completed work, invoices, and payments.
- Daily record: time spent, blockers, next action, and any offer/segment adjustment.

A sent message is not a lead; a proposal is not a sale; an invoice is not paid income.

## 7. Readiness and evidence gates
- **Concept:** idea or written specification.
- **Prototype:** local demo; backend/provider may be absent.
- **Tested:** named steps passed on a stated environment, with date and evidence.
- **Provider-connected:** configuration and a real integration test exist.
- **Independently verified:** separate reviewer or reproducible external evidence supports the claim.
- **Commercially available:** scope, price, support, privacy, terms, delivery, and payment route are defined and tested.

Voice/presentation chain: authorized source asset → provider configured → generated audio artifact → transcript/voice review → lip-sync render → audio/visual review → live Q&A test → recorded evidence. Each is a separate gate.

## 8. Security and preservation
- Keep secrets out of source code, public HTML, logs, and issues.
- Apply least-privilege CI permissions, dependency checks, and a reviewed backup/restore plan.
- No destructive cleanup, mass email, public posting, billing, or production credential changes without approval.
- Archive rather than delete during reorganisation; keep a route map and broken-link check.
- Automations should create proposals/tickets first; production changes require tests and authorised review.

## 9. Acceptance checklist
- [ ] First-time visitor understands the institute, available service, and next action within 10 seconds.
- [ ] Existing product links remain discoverable.
- [ ] Each product card shows readiness and limitations.
- [ ] Enquiry fields create a copyable/downloadable brief without claiming automatic delivery.
- [ ] Mobile, keyboard, accessibility, and internal-link checks pass.
- [ ] At least one sample deliverable is visible and marked illustrative.
- [ ] Outreach/income metrics use actual recorded evidence only.
- [ ] Voice, lip-sync, and live features remain unverified until their own tests and artifacts exist.

## 10. Non-claims
This document does not itself redesign or deploy the homepage, send messages, create accounts, connect services, pass tests, secure infrastructure, or generate income. Update those statuses only after implementation and evidence.
