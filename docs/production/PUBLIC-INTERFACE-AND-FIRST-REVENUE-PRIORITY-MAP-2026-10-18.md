# SHIRMANI — Public Interface and First-Revenue Priority Map

**Status:** implementation plan; not proof of completed work, sales, income, security certification, or live integrations.  
**Purpose:** reduce public-facing confusion while preserving the full repository and direct effort toward the first verifiable paid writing/research engagement.

## 1. Non-destructive operating rules

1. Do not delete, rename, or hide project materials merely because they are not on the homepage. The public landing page is a curated index; the repository/archive remains the source collection.
2. Before any cleanup, inventory the path, references, workflow usage, unique content, and rollback route. Prefer de-duplication in navigation over deleting source files.
3. Keep prototype, planned, externally connected, tested, and production states distinct. Never infer that a link, mock-up, generated image, draft, or green workflow proves a customer-facing integration works.
4. Do not send mass unsolicited messages or imply a relationship with an institution. Start with individually researched, publicly listed official contact routes; respect consent, applicable law, provider rules, opt-outs, and rate limits.
5. Keep the owner's approved photo unchanged except proportional scaling, and use only an explicitly authorized voice. Do not claim HeyGen voice, lip-sync, live Q&A, or streaming is end-to-end verified without test evidence.

## 2. Public landing page: one clear path from top to bottom

The first screen should answer: **What is this? What can I use now? What does it cost? How do I start?**

Recommended order:

1. **Hero:** “Shirmani Research Institute — Hindi/English Writing, Research Summaries & Website/Blog Content.” One short plain-language sentence; two primary links: “View services and sample” and “Start an enquiry”.
2. **Available services:** only currently deliverable services, with a sample, scope, turnaround target, price status, revision terms, and limitations.
3. **Try a sample:** link to the existing bilingual sample portfolio and digital-literacy research brief demo; label samples illustrative, not paid client work.
4. **How it works:** enquiry → written scope/quote → agreement on delivery/payment → source review → draft → quality check → delivery → agreed revision/feedback.
5. **Products and tools:** grouped into Writing & Research, Presentation/Voice, Product Creation, Research & Learning, Production/Security. Each card should say “Try demo”, “Specification”, or “Planned” truthfully.
6. **Evidence and readiness:** separate prototype, tested, externally integrated, and production-ready states; show the date and link to evidence for claims.
7. **Presentation / Heart-View:** a dedicated section for the supplied HeyGen asset and the measurable pipeline below. Do not let this obscure the immediate writing/research offer.
8. **Research archive and library:** searchable categories and topic pages, not a giant unfiltered list on the landing screen. Preserve all materials and provide a full catalogue/search route.
9. **Customer account:** show sign-up/login only after a real secure server-side identity, session, privacy, recovery, and authorization implementation is present. A visual form alone is not an account system.
10. **Footer:** contact, privacy/data handling, terms, refund/cancellation policy, accessibility, and truthful capability boundaries.

## 3. Immediate income path: sell a small, clearly scoped service first

Repository assets already include a Writing & Research Service page, Writing & Research Enquiry, client proposal/scope tools, bilingual sample portfolio, and research-summary demos. Reuse and link these before creating another overlapping tool.

### Proposed pilot (not a confirmed sale)

- **Service:** one Hindi or English research summary, website page, or blog article.
- **Deliverable:** 800–1,200 words or an equivalent agreed brief; editable text; headings and short summary; source list where sources are used; clear separation between sourced facts and interpretation.
- **Indicative price:** ₹999 pilot reference already shown in the service-page draft. It is not a binding quote; confirm final scope, currency, timeline, rights, revisions, cancellation/refund terms, and payment method in writing.
- **Turnaround target:** two business days after the brief, source availability, scope, and payment terms are agreed; confirm feasibility before accepting.
- **Revision:** one revision against the agreed brief.
- **Boundary:** specialist legal/medical/financial advice, extensive original fieldwork, translation beyond the agreed language, urgent work, and independent scientific certification are out of scope unless separately agreed.

### First-client sequence

1. Check that the public service page, sample links, and enquiry mailto address work on mobile and desktop.
2. Prepare one genuinely useful sample for a clearly defined customer type; label it as a sample.
3. Build a short list of 10 relevant organisations or independent professionals from official public websites. Record official source URL, fit, contact route, date checked, and why the offer is relevant.
4. Human-review each message; send individually from the owner's chosen account. The repository's draft generators do not send messages automatically.
5. Follow up once only where appropriate and permitted; record opt-outs and do not re-contact opted-out prospects.
6. For every positive reply, confirm scope, deliverables, deadline, final price, revision, rights, and payment in writing before starting.
7. Deliver one small paid pilot, request permission before using it as a portfolio sample or testimonial, and record the real outcome.

## 4. Weekly measurement board

Record actual counts, not aspirations presented as results.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Service-page check | Mobile + desktop load and link checks passed / attempted |
| Qualified prospects | Prospects with a checked official source and documented fit |
| Messages sent | Individually reviewed messages actually sent |
| Replies | Human replies received |
| Qualified enquiries | Replies with a real need, scope, and contact route |
| Quotes issued | Written quotes actually sent |
| Paid orders | Payment confirmed and scope accepted |
| Revenue received | Cleared amount actually received, with date and currency |
| Delivery quality | Deliverables accepted / revision requests / on-time delivery |
| Opt-outs | Opt-outs recorded and respected |

**Initial seven-day activity target:** verify the service page and sample links; prepare one sample; research 10 suitable prospects; send up to 5 individually reviewed, relevant messages if contact rules permit; record replies and outcomes. This is a target, not a forecast or promise of income. Zero replies or sales must be reported honestly.

## 5. Measurable voice-to-presentation chain

Track each stage independently and attach a timestamped artefact or test result:

1. **Source asset:** record the canonical supplied HeyGen video URL and access/authorization status.
2. **Voice authorization:** record permission and the exact provider/account configuration used; do not store credentials or secrets in the repository.
3. **Content:** keep the approved script, language, version, and factual source ledger.
4. **Voice output:** save a real test output and record provider, timestamp, format, and human listening result.
5. **Photo/avatar and lip-sync:** test the approved original image and a short script; record observable synchronization review and limitations.
6. **Q&A:** run a fixed set of at least five known questions, including one answer that must say “not verified”; save prompts, responses, and reviewer notes.
7. **Live presentation:** separately test an actual session, connectivity, latency, failure handling, and consent. A prerecorded video does not prove live Q&A.
8. **Release gate:** mark each stage not-started, blocked, passed, or failed; only label end-to-end ready when all required gates pass with evidence.

The supplied URL is a reference asset, not by itself proof of ownership, a connected integration, real-time interaction, or successful end-to-end testing.

## 6. Security and reliability gates before broad launch

- Verify least-privilege GitHub Actions permissions and branch protection.
- Run tests and inspect workflow failures; retry failed jobs rather than repeatedly rerunning successful work without reason.
- Check for secrets in tracked files and workflow logs; rotate any exposed secret through its provider.
- Test forms for validation, accessible labels, mobile layout, safe output escaping, and clear failure states.
- Treat user enquiries as personal data: collect only necessary fields, state how they are used, limit retention, and do not expose them in public logs.
- Add account creation, payment, email sending, shared review aggregation, or automated outreach only with real backend/provider integrations and end-to-end tests.

## 7. Definition of done for this priority pass

- One primary landing route clearly explains the Institute and current services.
- A visitor can reach a sample and create a copyable enquiry in no more than two obvious clicks from the hero.
- All other tools remain discoverable by category or search; no source materials are deleted as part of visual cleanup.
- Prototype and live-integration claims are accurately labeled.
- Service scope and proposed price are clearly marked as subject to written confirmation.
- A weekly activity ledger distinguishes prospects, messages, enquiries, orders, and cleared revenue.
- Voice, lip-sync, Q&A, and live states are independently evidenced rather than assumed.
