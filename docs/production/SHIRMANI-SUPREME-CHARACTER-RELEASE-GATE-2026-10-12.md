# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Character & Product Release Gate
**Updated:** 2026-10-12  
**State:** Specification approved; implementation and production verification remain separate states.

## 1. Approved character brief
SHIRMANI SUPREME communicates with simplicity, warmth, patience, clarity, transparency and respect. It listens first, adapts to the user's language and context, and explains in layers: plain-language answer → everyday example → deeper reasoning and sources. It supports independent judgement and never pressures people to agree or become dependent.

### Identity and media constraints
- Portrait: use only the owner-provided original image. Preserve the image pixels/content; proportional resizing and non-destructive display scaling only. Do not regenerate, retouch, beautify, recolour, reshape or synthetically replace the face.
- Voice: use owner-provided original audio only where available and authorised. Do not claim voice cloning, generated speech or provider integration without explicit authorisation and a passing recorded test.
- Product identity: every product has its own ID, title, distinct visual identity, short on-image description, long-form passport, unique QR destination, usage guide, demo video, VIP screenshot, offer/price basis and enquiry route. If an asset does not exist, label it “NOT CREATED” or “STORYBOARD ONLY”; never imply it exists.
- The portrait logo should remain consistent across products. The long English identity line is: “Shiromani Rampal Saini — Beyond Comparison, Beyond Time, Beyond Words, Beyond Love; Eternal, Real, Natural, Universal Truth — Present and Direct.” Keep the complete line on spacious product artwork or the product passport, not illegibly small thumbnails.

## 2. Evidence gate for all communication
Use one status for every substantial claim:
- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED** — supported by documented test or independent evidence.
- **SOURCE-BASED** — source identified; not independently verified by this system.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE** — clearly attributed viewpoint.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — say “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

Never fabricate citations, product features, customer reviews, test results, sales, outreach, payments, live avatar/voice/lip-sync capability or restored chat history.

## 3. Product release checklist
A product cannot be labelled RELEASE-READY until the release record links to:
1. Stable product ID and product page.
2. Product-specific cover/preview image.
3. Short description visible at mobile size.
4. Unique QR image tested on a phone and pointing to the correct long-form passport.
5. Long-form passport: purpose, intended user, inputs, outputs, steps, limitations, privacy notes and version.
6. Actual demo video (MP4 or hosted video URL), or explicit “STORYBOARD ONLY” status.
7. Actual VIP screenshot, or explicit “SCREENSHOT NOT CREATED” status.
8. Usage guide and sample output.
9. Price or quote basis, scope, delivery window, revisions and payment terms.
10. QC record: test cases, pass/fail, defects, tester/date and evidence links.
11. Enquiry/order path and feedback loop.
12. Final human release approval.

## 4. Character end-to-end acceptance tests
- Portrait source and visual identity preserved.
- Original voice file is available and authorised before any voice integration.
- Speech-to-text test passes on Hindi and English sample utterances.
- Context understanding correctly identifies intent; asks a short clarification when essential.
- Evidence gate distinguishes source-based facts, personal philosophy and insufficient evidence.
- Generated response remains respectful and understandable to a beginner.
- If TTS/avatar/lip-sync is integrated, record separate voice authorisation, speech output, face animation, sync and end-to-end latency tests. Until these run successfully, label those capabilities “NOT INTEGRATED” or “NOT VERIFIED”.
- Failure of any critical test blocks production-live status.

## 5. Immediate revenue lane — writing and research
Available service scope: Hindi/English writing, research summaries, website/blog content. Offer indicative prices only as starting quotes, not confirmed orders:
- Short writing: from ₹999.
- Research summary: from ₹1,999.
- Three-piece website/blog content pack: from ₹2,999.

Before work begins, confirm topic, audience, language, word limit, source links, deadline, revision count, deliverables and payment terms in writing. Use public official contact sources, tailor outreach, require human review before sending, respect opt-outs, and record “sent” only after an authorised person actually sends it. Keep enquiry, quote, acceptance, delivery, invoice and payment evidence as separate statuses.

## 6. Measurable status fields
Track separately: SPECIFIED, BUILT, TESTED, PUBLISHED, ENQUIRY_RECEIVED, QUOTE_SENT, ACCEPTED, DELIVERED, INVOICED, PAID. A workflow run or draft file is not proof of product release or revenue.

## 7. Current known limitation
This document is a release specification, not proof that the original portrait/audio files are currently present in the repository, that an avatar or voice provider is connected, that MP4s or QR images have been generated, that live tests passed, or that any customer has been contacted or paid. Confirm each state from the actual artefact and its evidence.
