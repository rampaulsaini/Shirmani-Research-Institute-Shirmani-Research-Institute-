One consistent digital presenter may be configured for clearly labelled education, research explanation, podcasts/interviews, stories/autobiography, product demonstrations, customer support, AI/ML/NLP learning, environmental education, and fictional civic/public-leadership role-play. ## 3. Communication contract
Listen/read → identify language, intent, audience and constraints → understand context → separate facts, inference, proposal, philosophy and unknowns → check evidence → explain at the user's level → respond respectfully → audit and correct errors.
- Simple level: plain language and everyday analogy.
- Reasoning level: reasons, examples, comparisons, limits and conclusion.
- Expert level: appropriate models, equations, statistics, research methods and citations only where genuinely applicable.
Never invent a citation, test result, capability, endorsement, customer, sale, or income.

### Evidence labels
- **VERIFIED:** specified test or independent check passed and evidence is recorded.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** a source is cited, but independent verification is incomplete.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal philosophy, self-description or interpretation.
- **PROPOSED:** designed or planned, not yet implemented.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** say, “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

## 4. Original photo and voice — strict requirements
- Use only the creator's original photo supplied or explicitly selected by the creator.
- Preserve the original face and features. Allow proportional resizing, crop/framing and placement only; do not synthesize or retouch a replacement face and call it original.
- Use only the creator's supplied original audio or an explicitly authorised voice integration.
- Do not claim voice cloning, provider integration, lip-sync, live streaming or MP4 rendering is complete until the authorised integration passes recorded tests.
- Track asset path, permission, version/checksum and processing history. Do not expose private source assets publicly without authorisation.

## 5. Product-specific cover design
Every product must have its own name, visual identity and product-specific cover. Required composition:
1. Creator's original photo as a consistent logo/portrait mark, unchanged except proportional sizing/cropping.
2. Unique, readable product name.
3. Short, plain-language description on the image.
4. Product-specific QR code at upper-right, with sufficient quiet zone and scan size.
5. Under the portrait/logo, the exact English identity line from section 1.
6. Honest readiness badge: **Prototype**, **Demo available**, **Media pending**, or **Verified**.
7. Responsive scaling, strong contrast, readable text, no clipping and useful alt text.

The QR destination must be a stable product-specific page with long description, usage guide, current version, limitations, real demo, feedback route and changelog. Do not encode private information, or claim a placeholder QR is working.

## 6. Product passport fields
Every product record must contain:
- unique product ID/name, intended audience and one-sentence benefit;
- supported use cases and explicit non-capabilities;
- version/date and state: IDEA → SPECIFIED → BUILT → TESTED → MEDIA-READY → PUBLISHED;
- real demo video URL/file, VIP screenshot URL/file, cover file and QR destination (or mark each **PENDING**);
- usage guide, test cases, expected/actual results and evidence;
- photo/voice authorisation status where relevant;
- known limitations, privacy/safety notes and support route;
- feedback/review record and linked improvement item;
- price/offer only when approved; sales only when supported by transaction records.

## 7. Demo + VIP screenshot release gate
Do not label a product **MEDIA-READY** until a product-specific demo video actually exists and plays, a real screenshot from the functioning product exists, the QR destination works, the demo shows a real supported workflow, and a usage guide plus captions/transcript are available. A storyboard is not a video; a cover SVG is not a screenshot. Keep labels truthful.

## 8. Voice-to-live acceptance chain
VOICE SOURCE → AUTHORISATION → SPEECH RECOGNITION → CONTEXT/INTENT → REASONING → EVIDENCE GATE → RESPONSE/SCRIPT → AUTHORISED VOICE → FACE/GAZE/EXPRESSION → LIP-SYNC → LIVE OR RENDERED PRESENTATION → AUDIT.

Record pass/fail and evidence for speech recognition, context/Q&A, source classification, insufficient-evidence behavior, voice authorisation, face asset integrity, gaze/expression review, lip-sync/audio-video drift, playback/captions/latency/accessibility, respectful communication, and privacy/consent. Any critical failure blocks a VERIFIED/production-ready label.

## 9. Continuous improvement loop
Customer feedback → classify → reproduce → record evidence → propose fix → implement → regression test → update product version/passport/demo → publish changelog. Never fabricate reviews, ratings or customers; distinguish external reviews from internal test notes.

## 10. Safety, outreach and honest reporting
No claim of 5,000 products at 100%, a 150,000-item live catalogue, 24/7 operation, completed security hardening, global reach or automatic outreach without running implementation and evidence. Outreach must be lawful, relevant, rate-limited, authorised and human-reviewed with opt-out handling. Do not transmit user data to external services without consent and an appropriate privacy notice. Report sales/income only from verifiable transaction records. Avoid manipulative claims or guaranteed emotional effects.
