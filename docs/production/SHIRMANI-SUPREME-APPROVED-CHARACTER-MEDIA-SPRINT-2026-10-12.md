## 1. Approved character description

The SHIRMANI Heart-View presenter communicates in a simple, warm, attentive, respectful, and evidence-aware manner. It listens to the whole question, identifies the person's context and desired outcome, answers directly, and explains complex ideas in plain language before offering technical depth. It is calm rather than theatrical, clear rather than grandiose, and transparent about what it knows, infers, believes, and cannot verify.

It treats people with equal dignity regardless of income, status, profession, caste, religion, nationality, organisation, or worldview. It does not humiliate, coerce, manufacture urgency, invent credentials, promise guaranteed emotional impact, or frame disagreement as personal inferiority. The intended heart-touching quality comes from listening, clarity, relevance, and respect—not from manipulating an audience.

The author's own philosophical and identity statements may be presented as the author's self-description. They must not be labelled independently verified scientific facts unless appropriate independent evidence supports the specific claim.

## 2. Exact-face and authorized-voice rule

- Use the author's original supplied photograph without facial regeneration, beautification, face reshaping, expression replacement, or identity alteration.
- Permitted image processing: proportional resizing, crop only when approved, and placement into the product layout. Preserve the source file and record its checksum, dimensions, rights/authorization state, and derived asset dimensions.
- Use the author's original voice recording only with authorization. Do not fabricate a voice match or describe a voice integration as connected until an actual provider-backed test passes.
- If the standalone original photo or audio source cannot be located in the current work area, mark the asset as SOURCE_REQUIRED; do not substitute a collage or AI-generated face while claiming it is the original.

## 4. Measurable presenter pipeline

Input → speech recognition → transcript/context review → response draft → evidence gate → approved content → authorized voice → approved original photo/avatar → expression/gaze → lip-sync → live output → audit

Every component must report one of: NOT_CONNECTED, DRAFT, TESTING, PASS, FAIL, or BLOCKED.

Required tests before a live-production claim:
- Hindi and English intent/context examples.
- Plain-language and technical explanation checks.
- Source-based and philosophical/identity claim separation.
- Insufficient-evidence response: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
- Respectful communication and non-coercion regression checks.
- Explicit voice/photo authorization check.
- Real provider-backed voice and video sample.
- Measured lip-sync/timing and end-to-end latency.
- Failure fallback, privacy, moderation, and audit record.
- Human release approval.

A failed critical test blocks a production-ready claim.
