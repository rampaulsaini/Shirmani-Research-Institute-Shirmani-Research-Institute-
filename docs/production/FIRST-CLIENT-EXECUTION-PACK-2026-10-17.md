## 3. Three simple pilot offers

These are **proposed starting references**, not market-validated prices or a final quote. Quote only after confirming scope.

### A. Short writing / editing — reference ₹999
- One short Hindi or English article, page, or polished draft.
- Before quoting: topic, target reader, approximate word count, source needs, deadline and revision count.
- Deliver: editable text, title/headings, and a short handover note.

### B. Source-linked research summary — reference ₹1,999
- A concise summary of a defined question using traceable sources.
- Deliver: question/scope, key findings, source links, date accessed where practical, uncertainties and limitations.
- No claim of peer review, exhaustive coverage, or professional legal/medical/financial advice unless separately qualified and agreed.

### C. Website/blog starter pack — reference ₹2,999
- Up to three agreed content pieces, such as homepage/about/service copy or blog posts.
- Deliver: agreed copy, suggested headings and basic metadata.
- Excludes website development, publishing, design, SEO ranking guarantees and unlimited revisions.

**Every quote must specify:** deliverables, word count/format, sources, revision limit, due date, total price, payment schedule, cancellation/refund terms and client approval. Do not begin paid work until the scope and payment terms are accepted in writing. Do not describe a proposed price as an order or income.

## 6. Prospect qualification score

Score each candidate 0–2 on each item (0 = absent, 1 = plausible, 2 = evidenced):
1. Official website/contact route is verifiable.
2. A current, specific writing/research need is visible.
3. The service matches the recipient's role or department.
4. The organisation appears able to commission external work.
5. A relevant sample can demonstrate the fit.

Prioritise scores of 7–10 for manual review. This is a sorting aid, not proof the organisation will buy. Record the exact page supporting the need-fit; do not invent contact names, email addresses, budgets or demand.

## 8. Funnel definitions and success criteria

- **Researched prospect:** official source and specific fit reason recorded.
- **Message sent:** actual sent record exists; a drafted message does not count.
- **Qualified enquiry:** recipient has stated a relevant need and enough detail to scope it.
- **Proposal sent:** written deliverables, deadline, revisions and price were actually shared.
- **Order accepted:** client accepted the written scope/terms.
- **Revenue received:** payment actually cleared/was received and can be reconciled to a record.
- **Completed delivery:** files were delivered and acceptance or revision status recorded.

Report weekly counts separately. Revenue is the sum of actual received payments, not proposed prices. Conversion rates are calculated only where the previous-stage denominator is greater than zero.

## 9. Blockers and release gates

1. **Public URL:** verify the canonical deployed page before using it in outreach; a GitHub source URL is not proof of a working deployed public page.
2. **Samples:** review factual claims, source links, language quality and publication rights before sharing.
3. **Contact:** human reviews every message; sending remains manual until an authorised, tested email workflow exists.
4. **Pricing:** treat listed figures as starting references; no automatic quote or payment collection is implemented by this pack.
5. **Claims:** no claims of customers, sales, global institutional reach, independent certification or income without records.
6. **Security/privacy:** minimise lead data, honour opt-outs and follow applicable law and platform rules.
