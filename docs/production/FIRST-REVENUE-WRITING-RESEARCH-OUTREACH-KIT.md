# SRI — First Revenue Sprint: Writing & Research Outreach Kit

**Status:** ready-to-review operating plan; not a record of outreach, sales, payment, or revenue.  
**Service scope currently evidenced in the repository:** Hindi/English writing, research summaries, website/blog content, translation/editing.  
**Primary existing intake tool:** `products/concrete/SRI-WR-001-writing-research-enquiry-studio.html`  
**Existing first-deliverable tool:** `showroom/first-client-deliverable-studio.html`

## 1. Sell one clear outcome first

Offer a small, fixed-scope pilot rather than pitching the entire platform:

**Bilingual Content & Research Pilot**
- One client-selected topic.
- One agreed deliverable: a research summary, website/service page, or blog article.
- Hindi, English, or bilingual output.
- Sources/citations where applicable; claims separated into verified, uncertain, and client-supplied.
- One consolidated revision round.
- Delivery date and word count confirmed in writing before acceptance.

Do not advertise legal, medical, financial, scientific certification, guaranteed SEO ranking, or independent verification beyond the work actually performed.

## 2. Ideal first customer segments

Start with a small, manually qualified list rather than mass emailing:
1. Small businesses and local service providers with unclear website/service copy.
2. Education and training providers needing bilingual explainers.
3. Research, nonprofit, and community organisations needing readable summaries.
4. Independent creators who need Hindi↔English editing or structured blog content.

A prospect qualifies only when a public official contact channel is available, the need is visible, the service fits the current scope, and outreach is relevant. Record the source URL and date checked. Do not scrape private contact data or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## 3. Quote policy — no invented prices

No public rate is established by this kit. Quote only after receiving:
- topic and intended audience;
- deliverable, word count and language;
- source/citation requirements;
- deadline and revision count;
- client budget, if offered.

Use this formula internally:
**Quote = estimated work hours × chosen hourly floor + agreed research/formatting costs + revision buffer.**

Before work begins, both sides confirm final price, currency, scope, delivery date, revision policy, cancellation terms, and payment milestone in writing. Never treat a customer's entered budget as an agreed price.

## 4. First-contact email (personalise before sending)

**Subject:** Bilingual writing/research support for [specific need]

Hello [Name/Team],

I’m contacting you from Shirmani Research Institute. I noticed [specific, publicly visible page or content gap] and thought a small, clearly scoped Hindi/English content or research-summary project may be useful.

We can discuss one pilot deliverable—for example, a service-page rewrite, a sourced research summary, or a bilingual article. Before starting, I would confirm the brief, source requirements, delivery date, revision limit, price, and payment terms in writing.

Would a short written brief be useful? If this is not relevant, please disregard this message.

Regards,  
Shirmani Research Institute

**Sending rule:** use only a verified official/public contact channel, personalise the first sentence, respect opt-outs, and log only necessary business-contact details. This template has not been sent automatically.

## 6. Delivery and quality gate

For every accepted job:
1. Save the agreed brief and written quote.
2. Check source quality and record URLs, publication dates, and access dates.
3. Draft to the agreed audience, language, and format.
4. Mark unsupported or disputed claims; do not present assumptions as facts.
5. Run language, structure, citation, originality, and instruction checks.
6. Deliver the agreed files and record client feedback/revisions.
7. Record invoice/payment status only from actual evidence.

## 7. Two-week measurement plan

These are **targets to measure**, not results already achieved.

| Metric | Initial target | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Prospects manually qualified | 20 | Prospect log with official source URL |
| Personalised first contacts sent | 10, only where relevant | Sent-message record |
| Replies | Count actual replies | Message/thread reference |
| Qualified enquiries | Count briefs with real scope | Completed intake brief |
| Written quotes issued | Count actual quotes | Quote copy and date |
| Paid jobs | Count only cleared payments | Invoice + payment confirmation |
| On-time delivery | 100% of accepted jobs | Delivery timestamp |
| Client satisfaction | Ask for a short rating/feedback | Recorded response |

Review after 10 legitimate contacts. If nobody responds, improve the offer, sample, or target segment; do not increase spam volume. Report revenue as zero/unknown until payment evidence exists.

## 10. Automation boundary

This plan is a human-reviewed launch workflow. It does **not** claim that email sending, prospect discovery, CRM storage, checkout, payment processing, customer accounts, or revenue tracking are connected. Those states require implemented integrations and test evidence. No files or products should be deleted as part of this sprint; unrelated platform work remains preserved.
