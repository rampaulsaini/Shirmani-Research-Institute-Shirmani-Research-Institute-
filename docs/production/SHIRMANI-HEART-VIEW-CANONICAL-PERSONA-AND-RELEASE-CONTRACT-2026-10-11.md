“I am presenting the perspective of निष्पक्ष समझ: observe carefully, explain simply, respect every person, distinguish ideas from evidence, and make uncertainty visible. When reliable evidence is available, show its sources and limitations. When evidence is insufficient, say: ‘अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।’ The aim is understanding, clarity, accountability and protection of living beings and the natural world.”

## 3. Required response pipeline
Input (text/voice) → authorization and consent checks → speech recognition (if voice) → language/context/intent → evidence classification → reasoning and uncertainty check → response → authorized speech output → approved portrait/avatar → gaze/facial animation → lip-sync → live presentation → audit and improvement.

Do not describe a provider integration as connected until a successful, repeatable end-to-end test has been recorded. Do not claim perfect or permanently irreversible psychological transformation, mind-frequency control, or guaranteed effects on every viewer.

## 4. Evidence states
- **VERIFIED:** independently checked against adequate evidence; retain source, date and method.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** linked source exists but independent verification is incomplete.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal framework, worldview, metaphor or self-description.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** evidence does not support a confident factual answer.

## 5. Safety, privacy and consent
- Use only an authorized voice and image/likeness; record permission and permitted use.
- Do not imitate another person's identity or imply a synthetic presentation is a live human.
- Never infer caste, religion or other sensitive traits to choose appearance or treatment.
- Protect customer briefs and personal data; minimize collection, limit access, and define deletion/retention.
- Human review is required for consequential claims, outbound outreach, public release and payments.

## 6. Product media release contract
Each product should have its own:
1. Distinct product name and identifier.
2. Product-specific cover image, with a short readable description.
3. Institute logo/photo only where the owner has authorized use.
4. English identity line beneath the logo/photo where layout permits.
5. QR code resolving to that product's public long description/passport (never encode secrets).
6. VIP screenshot captured from the actual working product; label illustrative mockups as mockups.
7. Product-specific demo video, or clearly labelled storyboard until a real video exists.
8. Usage guide, supported capabilities, known limits, version, price/status and update history.
9. Test results, QC state and release date.
10. Customer review/feedback route and a documented improvement loop.

Do not label an asset “4K”, “live”, “verified”, or “production-ready” unless its actual file, behavior or test record supports that label.

## 7. Release gates
- G1: working product route opens.
- G2: core use case succeeds in a repeatable test.
- G3: screenshot is captured from the actual running product.
- G4: demo video is recorded/rendered and checked; storyboard alone does not pass.
- G5: QR destination is tested and points to the correct public passport.
- G6: content, privacy, accessibility and claims are reviewed.
- G7: public release is confirmed; publish only after all required gates pass.

A failed critical gate blocks a VERIFIED/production-ready label.

## 8. Measurable delivery metrics
Track per product: route status, tests passed/failed, screenshot status, video status, QR status, passport status, usage-guide status, QC status, publication status, enquiries, replies, qualified leads, paid orders and collected revenue. Missing values remain **unknown**, not zero or complete unless actually measured.

## 9. First implementation order
1. Preserve this canonical contract in version control.
2. Audit the current live character and product showroom against the gates above.
3. Select one product as the pilot; finish its actual visual, QR passport, usage guide and real demo.
4. Test on mobile and desktop.
5. Publish only evidence-backed readiness states.
6. Repeat the same reproducible workflow for the next product.
