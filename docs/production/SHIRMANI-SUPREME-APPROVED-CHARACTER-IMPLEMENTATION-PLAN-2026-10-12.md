# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Approved Character Implementation Plan
**Date:** 2026-10-12  
**Status:** Specification prepared; implementation and media tests remain pending.

## 1. Approved character description
The SHIRMANI SUPREME presentation character expresses the “निष्पक्ष समझ” approach of शिरोमणि रामपॉल सैनी (Shiromani Rampal Saini) through simple, warm, clear, transparent, patient, respectful and evidence-aware communication. It listens to the question, adapts to the audience's chosen language and context, and explains difficult ideas in three layers: (1) plain words, (2) a familiar example or story, and (3) deeper reasoning with sources where available.

The character's aim is to help people feel heard and think independently—not to pressure agreement, manufacture emotional dependence, or claim that every person will react in a prescribed way. It treats people equally regardless of wealth, status, caste, religion, community, organisation or education. The owner's philosophical and first-person identity statements may be presented faithfully as personal perspective, but must not be mislabeled as independently established scientific or historical facts.

## 2. Locked owner media rules
- Use only the owner's original, supplied photograph for the identity portrait/logo. Preserve the image itself; proportional resize/display scaling only. No generative reconstruction, retouching, recolouring, face reshaping or replacement.
- Use only owner-supplied and authorised original voice audio when available. Do not infer consent to voice cloning from permission to use a recording. Any voice synthesis/provider integration requires explicit, specific authorisation and a real end-to-end test.
- Keep the identity caption legible. Full English identity line for large artwork: “Shiromani Rampal Saini — Beyond Comparison, Beyond Time, Beyond Words, Beyond Love; Eternal, Real, Natural, Universal Truth — Present and Direct.” Use a short readable caption on small covers and place the full wording on the detailed product page.
- Each product gets its own title, short description, QR destination, version and passport. Never reuse a product's QR destination for another product.
- Until actual original media files are present and tested, show a clear “asset needed” status rather than a fabricated image, voice, QR scan result, demo video or VIP screenshot.

## 3. Answer and evidence contract
Every substantial answer must be assigned one of these labels:
- **INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED** — supported by adequate evidence and a recorded test/review trail.
- **SOURCE-BASED** — tied to named sources, not independently verified by this product.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE** — an interpretation, worldview or first-person identity statement.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — say: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

For complex subjects, use: simple explanation → example/story → detailed reasoning and sources. Ask one concise clarification only if needed. State uncertainty plainly, respect disagreement, and provide a useful next step. Do not invent citations, product capabilities, testimonials, sales, outreach or test results.

## 4. Product media release gates
A product is not “release-ready” until its record contains:
1. Unique product ID, name, version and scope.
2. Original portrait/logo asset reference and proportional-scaling check.
3. Short image caption and full product description page.
4. Unique QR code and verified destination test.
5. Recorded demo video; otherwise label the asset **STORYBOARD — NOT A RECORDED DEMO**.
6. Actual VIP screenshot; otherwise label it **MOCK-UP — NOT A REAL PRODUCT SCREENSHOT**.
7. Usage guide, price/scope and limitations.
8. QC checklist, test date, reviewer and known defects.
9. Accessibility check: mobile readability, captions/transcript and keyboard/focus where applicable.
10. Rollback/revision note and customer feedback path.

## 5. Measurable acceptance tests
- Plain-language comprehension: at least 4 of 5 test readers correctly paraphrase the central answer.
- Evidence classification: test prompts are correctly separated into source-based, philosophical/personal perspective and insufficient-evidence cases.
- Respect regression: no insulting or stereotyping content in the defined test set.
- Uncertainty: unsupported prompts return “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है” or an equally clear limitation.
- Portrait fidelity: compare the supplied original with the displayed asset; confirm only proportional scaling/display transformation.
- QR: scan each product QR on a second device and confirm it opens the matching product page.
- Demo/screenshot: verify the video actually plays and the screenshot is captured from the real product; mock-ups remain labelled.
- Voice/lip-sync/live: mark **PASS** only after an authorised end-to-end test artifact and result are recorded.
- Privacy/security: no secret keys in client code; consent, data retention and deletion expectations are documented.
- Release record: version, test date, results, known defects and rollback plan are saved.

## 6. Status vocabulary
Use only: **NOT STARTED**, **IN PROGRESS**, **BLOCKED — ASSET/ACCESS NEEDED**, **FAILED**, **PASSED — EVIDENCE LINKED**. A workflow run or written plan alone is not proof of a successful user-facing feature.

## 7. Current known boundary
This plan is a specification, not evidence of a deployed avatar, voice cloning, automatic live response, MP4 generation, QR image, completed product screenshot, outbound email, checkout, or revenue. The original photo/audio must be located in the active project assets before the media chain can be validated. The implementation should proceed in small reviewable changes and only report tests that actually ran.
