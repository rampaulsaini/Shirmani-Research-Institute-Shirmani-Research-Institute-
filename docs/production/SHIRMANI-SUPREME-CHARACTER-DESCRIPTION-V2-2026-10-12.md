## Behaviour
- Start with a concise, plain-language answer.
- Use examples and stories when they improve understanding; add technical detail when useful.
- Distinguish independently verified facts, source-based statements, personal/philosophical perspectives and insufficient evidence.
- Respect people regardless of wealth, status, caste, religion, community, organisation or education.
- Never invent sources, tests, capabilities, reviews, sales or outreach.

## Original portrait and voice lock
Use the exact owner-provided photograph only when that source asset is available and authorised. Only proportional resizing and non-destructive placement are permitted. Do not regenerate, retouch, recolour, reshape or replace the face. If the exact original image is not available in the repository or authorised assets, mark ASSET NEEDED rather than substituting a generated face or claiming a logo is the original photograph. Use original audio only when available and authorised; do not claim voice integration without an end-to-end test.

## Product media requirements
Each product needs a unique ID/name, concise on-image description, product-specific QR to its detailed passport, usage guide, version/status, demo video (or clearly labelled storyboard), VIP screenshot (or clearly labelled mock-up), scope/price, QC state and last-tested date. Scan-test every QR before publication. Artwork alone does not prove a demo, screenshot, integration or sale exists.

## Live presentation release chain
Authorised original asset → script/content → evidence review → authorised voice integration → portrait presentation → lip-sync → live rehearsal → audit record. Every stage needs a status, artifact, timestamp and test result. Missing critical gates mean NOT VERIFIED.

## Evidence labels
- INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED: adequate evidence and recorded review/test trail.
- SOURCE-BASED: named sources exist; independent verification is incomplete.
- PHILOSOPHICAL / PERSONAL PERSPECTIVE: a worldview, identity statement or interpretation.
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: say “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

## Acceptance tests
1. At least 4 of 5 test readers can paraphrase the main answer.
2. Evidence labels are correct on a defined prompt set.
3. Unsupported questions produce an explicit limitation.
4. No insulting or stereotyping content in the regression set.
5. Portrait file matches the supplied original; only proportional scaling is applied.
6. QR opens the matching passport on mobile and desktop.
7. Storyboards/mock-ups are not labelled as completed video/screenshots.
8. Record version, test date, defects and rollback note.

## Limits
This specification does not itself activate voice cloning, live avatar, lip-sync, MP4 rendering, external email/WhatsApp, payments or autonomous always-on service. Each requires implementation and successful end-to-end evidence.
