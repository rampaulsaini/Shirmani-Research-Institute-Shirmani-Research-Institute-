# SHIRMANI — Clear Public Entry & First-Revenue Execution

Date: 2026-10-18  
Status: Implementation plan + public-entry improvement; sales, outreach, authentication and payment remain unverified until real records exist.

## What changed in this increment

The public showroom now begins with a **Start Here** route before the large product marketplace. It directs visitors to:
1. Hindi/English writing and research enquiry (the currently declared commercial service).
2. Existing browser demos/test-drive hub.
3. Product catalogue and machine-readable catalogue data.
4. Heart-View rehearsal and voice-to-presentation planning.
5. Production progress and customer feedback.
6. Project guide and a clear note that account, checkout and provider integrations must not be assumed live.

The change is additive. It does not delete or hide existing tools, alter product records, change pricing, or claim that a listed tool is production-certified. Existing marketplace and production telemetry remain in place.

## First-revenue offer — scope before work

**Service menu**
- Hindi/English writing or editing.
- Source-led research summary with citations and limitations.
- Website/blog copy.

**Enquiry fields**
Customer objective; topic; language; length; audience; required sources/links; deadline; budget; desired deliverables.

**Acceptance before starting**
- Confirm exact deliverables and excluded work.
- Agree a written quote, deadline, revision count, and payment terms.
- Confirm payment through an actually available authorized method before starting, if that is the agreed condition.
- Deliver the artifact and record acceptance; do not call a draft or enquiry a sale.

Prices shown elsewhere in the repository may be indicative. The final quote is confirmed only after scope review.

## Seven-day measurable execution loop

These are targets for a human-reviewed sprint, not results already achieved.

| Day | Action | Evidence to retain | Metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open and test the public enquiry flow; create one clean bilingual service sample | Enquiry export + sample file + test checklist | 1 tested flow, 1 sample |
| 2 | Prepare three narrowly scoped offers: short writing/edit, source-based summary, website/blog pack | Offer sheet with deliverables, quote basis and exclusions | 3 offers reviewed |
| 3 | Build a list of 10 plausible organizations from their own official public pages; record why each may need the service | Prospect ledger with official URL, fit rationale and date checked | 10 researched prospects |
| 4 | Draft individual, relevant messages; manually check facts, recipient and contact policy | 10 reviewed drafts; no send claim unless actually sent | 10 drafts reviewed |
| 5 | Send only approved messages using an authorized channel; log actual send status and date | Sent record / message ID where available | Count actually sent |
| 6 | Reply to real responses; clarify scope and issue written proposals | Response log + proposal copies | Responses, qualified leads, proposals |
| 7 | Review funnel and choose next action; never infer revenue from clicks or replies | Weekly report | Sent → replies → qualified leads → proposals → paid orders |

### Funnel definitions

- **Prospect researched:** an organization and fit rationale are recorded from an official public source.
- **Message sent:** actual sending evidence exists; a draft does not count.
- **Qualified lead:** the contact confirms a relevant need and next step.
- **Proposal sent:** a dated proposal was actually delivered.
- **Paid order:** authorized payment/order evidence exists.
- **Revenue:** only the amount recorded in the payment/order record; no estimate is counted as realized income.

## Product / platform readiness rules

Keep the existing multi-stage chain:

Research need → specification → concrete artifact → product-specific demo/guide → functional and safety QC → public listing → enquiry/order → actual fulfilment → feedback → versioned improvement.

Use PENDING, BLOCKED, or NOT CONNECTED where evidence is absent. A scheduled workflow, catalogue row, QR link, mock button, draft, screenshot, or provider URL is not proof of successful end-to-end operation.

For the requested HeyGen path, test and record separately:
1. Authorized source photo/voice and permission.
2. Actual generated video / provider job URL.
3. Audio playback and intelligibility.
4. Lip-sync observation against the same clip.
5. Scripted question-and-answer test, with expected/actual result.
6. Error/timeout behavior and timestamped evidence.
7. Human release decision.

Do not claim live voice, live Q&A, lip-sync, account login/signup, automatic email/WhatsApp, payment checkout, or 195-country sales capability without a working integration and a recorded test.

## Safe filtering and maintenance

- Do not bulk-delete or mass-hide materials based on names, age, or apparent duplication alone.
- First identify exact duplicates/obsolete entries and their references; check inbound links, manifests, workflows, tests and dependencies.
- Prefer redirects, archive labels, or a reviewed change set over irreversible deletion.
- Keep a rollback path and run relevant checks after each bounded change.
- A work item is deprioritized only when its relationship to the user-visible outcome is documented; preserving work is the default.
