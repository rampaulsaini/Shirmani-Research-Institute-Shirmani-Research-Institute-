# SHIRMANI Research Institute — Revenue-First Execution Plan
**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Purpose:** Turn the existing Hindi/English writing and research capability into a small, measurable, honest customer-acquisition workflow before expanding the wider platform.

## 1. Current evidence boundary
The repository contains enquiry builders, a first-paid-pilot offer, a revenue-first service desk, an institutional outreach workbench, public showroom pages, product demos, and a revenue execution runbook. These are useful assets, but a page or prototype is not evidence of a sale, a delivered service, an integrated payment system, or recurring income.

**Baseline:** confirmed leads = not measured here; outreach sent = not measured here; replies = not measured here; paid orders = not evidenced here; revenue = not evidenced here. Do not fill these with estimates or fabricated success.

## 2. Sell one service first
Keep the initial offer narrow:
1. Hindi/English writing polish and editing.
2. Source-based research summary with links/citations and a plain-language explanation.
3. Website/blog draft.

The repository's proposed starter anchors are ₹499, ₹999, and ₹1,499 respectively. They are proposal-only, not guaranteed market rates or confirmed quotes. Before accepting work, confirm the exact deliverables, source expectations, word count, revisions, deadline, final price, payment method and payment timing in writing. If a task is larger than the offer, quote it separately.

## 3. Public interface: one clear path
The public showroom should lead with these choices, in this order:
1. **What can I get?** Three service cards, each with audience, deliverables, exclusions, indicative price and turnaround only where confirmed.
2. **See a sample.** Label the water-conservation research sample as a demonstration, not client work or independent scientific certification.
3. **Request a quote.** One short enquiry form, accessible on mobile; avoid forcing visitors through multiple duplicate forms.
4. **What happens next?** Scope review → written quote and deadline → written acceptance → agreed payment → delivery → revision/feedback.
5. **Other products and long-term vision.** Keep the large research, AI, media, education, nature and social-platform roadmap in clearly separated catalogue sections so it does not bury the immediate service.

Do not delete older work merely to simplify navigation. Prefer linking and grouping existing pages; archive or remove only after checking inbound links, unique content and dependencies.

## 4. Minimum viable operating funnel
Record one row per prospect with: organisation/person, official source URL, relevant need, contact channel, source verification date, draft status, human approval, actual send date, reply, proposal amount, acceptance, delivery status, payment confirmation and next action.

Funnel definitions:
- Researched prospect: relevant need and official contact source recorded.
- Contacted: message actually sent by an authorised person; a draft is not a contact.
- Qualified: recipient replies and confirms a relevant need.
- Proposal: scope, price and deadline sent in writing.
- Won: customer explicitly accepts the proposal.
- Paid: payment is actually confirmed.
- Delivered: agreed deliverables sent and recorded.

Never scrape or guess private addresses, auto-send mass messages, fabricate endorsements, or claim sales that are not evidenced. Use official public contact channels and tailor outreach; follow applicable privacy and anti-spam requirements.

## 5. Seven-day measurement plan
Day 1: choose one canonical service page and remove duplicate navigation links without deleting content.
Day 2: verify that the enquiry form works on mobile, generates a complete brief, and provides a usable copy/download path.
Day 3: publish one labelled sample with source links and a clear “request a quote” route.
Day 4: identify 10 relevant prospects from official public sources; record why each might need the service.
Day 5: manually review and send up to 5 individually tailored messages through permitted official channels.
Day 6: respond to replies; prepare proposals only for confirmed needs.
Day 7: report the actual counts below and fix the largest observed drop-off.

Daily scoreboard:
- Relevant prospects researched: __
- Official contacts verified: __
- Messages actually sent: __
- Replies received: __
- Qualified needs: __
- Proposals sent: __
- Accepted orders: __
- Payments confirmed (INR): __
- Deliveries completed: __
- Customer-requested fixes: __

A zero in any field is a valid result and a prompt to diagnose the next step, not a reason to invent success.

## 6. Public product release gate
A product may be labelled **demo**, **pilot**, or **production-ready** only according to its evidence:
- Demo: a reproducible local/browser demonstration with limitations shown.
- Pilot: one real end-to-end use case tested with consent and logged defects.
- Production-ready: deployed service, documented support and privacy terms, tested failure handling, accessibility checks, and any required provider/payment integration actually verified.

For voice/avatar presentation, record separate evidence for: authorised image/voice assets, generated audio, video output, lip-sync quality, question-and-answer behaviour, latency, error handling and provider status. A link or specification alone does not prove all these layers work.

## 7. Security and continuity
Keep secrets out of source control; use least-privilege permissions; review dependency/action versions; preserve backups and audit logs. Automated checks should report findings and create reviewable tasks. Do not disable security filters or delete files just because a gate blocks progress. Fix false positives narrowly with evidence, and keep a rollback path.

## 8. Next implementation acceptance criteria
- One primary public entry point is obvious on a mobile screen.
- Writing/research service, sample, enquiry and process are reachable in no more than two clear navigation steps.
- Form output includes the scope and written-acceptance reminder.
- No unsupported “live”, “verified”, “secure”, “independent”, sales or income claims.
- Outreach dashboard distinguishes drafts from messages sent and proposals from accepted orders.
- Every release has a dated change note and test evidence.

## 9. Explicit non-claims
This plan does not assert that any outreach was sent, any customer was contacted, any order was accepted, any payment was received, any HeyGen/voice/lip-sync chain passed end-to-end testing, or that the global social platform is deployed. Those states require their own evidence.
