# Revenue-first delivery plan — Writing & Research
**Status:** operational plan; not evidence of sales, client outreach, or revenue.

## 1. Sell what can be delivered now
Shirmani Research Institute currently offers:
- Hindi ↔ English writing, editing, and rewriting
- Research summaries based on supplied or publicly accessible sources
- Website and blog content
- Clear, source-linked briefs and explainers

Do not advertise unbuilt AI tools, live HeyGen Q&A, verified lip-sync, a global social network, or enterprise automation as available products until each is implemented and independently tested.

## 2. Simple service menu
Prices are **quote-based**, not published as fixed prices until delivery effort and customer requirements are known.

| Package | Deliverable | Scope agreed before payment |
|---|---|---|
| Writing starter | One short article, page, or rewrite | Topic, language, word count, tone, one revision |
| Research brief | Structured summary with source links and uncertainty notes | Research question, source limits, length, deadline |
| Website content pack | Agreed set of landing-page/about/service copy | Pages, audience, tone, review rounds |
| Custom bilingual project | Hindi and English deliverables | Exact formats, word count, source material, schedule |

For every enquiry, confirm in writing: scope, deliverables, price/currency, deadline, revision count, payment method, and cancellation/refund terms. Never promise unsupported outcomes.

## 3. Lead-to-payment workflow
1. Publish this service menu and a clear enquiry form.
2. Record every lead with a unique ID and consented contact details.
3. Qualify the request: purpose, audience, topic, language, length, source links, deadline, budget.
4. Send a tailored written quote with sample structure and delivery date.
5. Wait for explicit customer acceptance and agreed payment/milestone before beginning paid work.
6. Deliver a clearly labelled draft/final with sources where relevant.
7. Request acceptance and permission before using the work as a public case study.
8. Log actual payment only after it is received and reconciled.

## 5. Outreach rules
- Contact only relevant organisations or people using publicly listed professional contact channels.
- Personalise each message; do not send bulk unsolicited messages or scrape private contact data.
- Identify the institute honestly; do not imply an existing partnership, endorsement, or client result without evidence.
- Do not claim a recipient has been contacted until an actual sent message or delivery record exists.
- Follow applicable privacy, anti-spam, and platform rules. Honour opt-outs.

## 6. Weekly measurement dashboard
Track actual counts; begin at zero until evidence is recorded.

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Qualified leads | Enquiries with a clear need, scope, and reachable contact |
| Proposals sent | Written quotes actually sent |
| Replies | Responses received |
| Accepted proposals | Explicit written acceptance |
| Paid orders | Accepted orders with payment received |
| Revenue received | Cleared amount received, net of refunds; record currency |
| On-time delivery rate | Deliveries on or before agreed deadline / deliveries due |
| Revision rate | Orders requiring revisions / delivered orders |
| Customer satisfaction | Voluntary feedback, reported with sample size |

**First 7-day test:** prepare one polished sample, publish the service menu and enquiry route, identify 20 relevant prospects from legitimate public business sources, send individually tailored messages where permitted, and record every outcome. These are targets, not completed actions or guaranteed results. Review replies and conversion at day 7; adjust the offer based on evidence.

## 7. Evidence and release gates
- **Public website:** verify links, mobile layout, accessibility, and enquiry submission end-to-end.
- **Payment:** test in sandbox first; never store card data directly; reconcile provider confirmation before marking paid.
- **Customer data:** collect only what is necessary, restrict access, define retention/deletion, and publish a privacy notice before collecting real leads.
- **Claims:** publish only features that exist and have passed a documented test.
- **Voice/video:** keep voice authorization, generated content, audio quality, lip-sync, and live question-answering as separate test results. A video URL alone does not prove the whole pipeline works.
- **Platform roadmap:** public interface, authentication, catalogue, checkout, creator tools, social feeds, research library, and automation are separate workstreams. Do not label the whole platform complete based on a plan or repository document.

## 8. Completion definition for this workstream
This revenue workstream is ready for a first controlled launch when:
- The service menu is visible and readable on mobile.
- The enquiry form can be submitted and a lead is actually recorded.
- A test quote can be sent and accepted.
- Payment terms and delivery workflow are documented.
- Metrics can be updated from real records.
- No unverified sales, income, client, security, or product claims appear publicly.
