# Shirmani Nishpaksh AI — Universal Human Understanding & Assessment Platform

**Status:** Proposal / prototype specification. Not a deployed app, credential, government service, or scientifically validated assessment.

## Purpose
A multilingual, accessible, user-controlled platform for reflection, learning, skills evidence, and role-specific assessment. It expresses the creator's Yatharth Siddhant as a clearly attributed philosophical framework while distinguishing personal reflection from independently verifiable facts.

## Product principles
- Human dignity is unconditional. No person must pass an app to be considered human.
- Self-reflection is voluntary and non-diagnostic; the app does not claim to reveal a person's permanent or metaphysical identity.
- Assessments are scoped to a declared purpose and published rubric. A learning assessment, job competency assessment, and public-office disclosure are different products.
- No automated eligibility, hiring, appointment, election, ministerial, judicial, or government decision. AI may assist with practice and evidence organization; authorized humans make consequential decisions under applicable law.
- No universal “truth score,” personality verdict, morality score, or certificate of being human.
- Every result shows rubric, evidence, date, uncertainty, limitations, appeal route, and human reviewer where applicable.

## Assessment pathways
1. Self-reflection: values, needs, emotional vocabulary, goals, and optional journaling. Not a mental-health diagnosis.
2. Education: curriculum-aligned knowledge checks, accessible practice, feedback, and learning plan.
3. Employment / role skills: job-specific work samples, transparent competency rubrics, accommodations, and reviewer feedback.
4. Public accountability: voluntary or legally authorized disclosure of public records and policy commitments. The app must not certify whether a person is fit to be PM, CM, minister, judge, president, or any elected/appointed office-holder.
5. Human-rights and civic learning: knowledge of rights, duties, nonviolence, environmental stewardship, and public-service principles.

## Evidence and assessment lifecycle
Purpose selected → consent and accessibility → rubric disclosed → user submits responses/evidence → AI-assisted organization (if enabled) → qualified human review for consequential outcomes → result with provenance and uncertainty → appeal/correction → retention or deletion per policy.

Statuses: DRAFT, IN_PROGRESS, EVIDENCE_PENDING, REVIEW_REQUIRED, VERIFIED_FOR_SCOPE, NOT_VERIFIED, DISPUTED, EXPIRED, WITHDRAWN. VERIFIED_FOR_SCOPE means only that specified evidence met a named rubric at a given time; it is not a universal truth or guarantee.

## AI/ML/NLP capabilities (bounded)
- Multilingual transcription, translation, summarization, and semantic search with source text retained.
- Explainable rubric mapping that quotes the relevant user-provided evidence.
- Bias and quality monitoring, confidence/abstention, versioned models, reproducible evaluations, and human override.
- Practice questions and personalized learning suggestions based on user-selected goals.
- No emotion, honesty, morality, intelligence, or political-fitness inference from face, voice, gait, eye movement, or biometrics.

## Biometrics and sensitive data
Finger-vein, iris, face, voiceprint, and other biometric sensing are excluded from the MVP and must never be used to infer character, truthfulness, worth, or suitability. If identity verification is later legally necessary, use a separate, purpose-limited, opt-in flow with data minimization, explicit consent, a non-biometric alternative, encryption, deletion controls, independent security/privacy review, and jurisdiction-specific legal review. Never store raw biometric templates in the assessment record.

## Privacy, safety, accessibility
- Collect only data needed for the selected pathway; private by default.
- Explicit consent before recording, AI processing, sharing, or publication; no public profile by default.
- User access, export, correction, withdrawal, and deletion controls.
- Encrypt data in transit and at rest; role-based access, audit logs, retention limits, incident response, and security testing.
- Support screen readers, keyboard navigation, captions, low-bandwidth mode, regional languages, and reasonable accommodations.
- No public leaderboard or comparison of people. Do not expose minors' data; child use requires age-appropriate safeguards and lawful guardian processes.

## Subscription and livelihood
Free access should include basic self-reflection, essential safety information, and account/data controls. Paid plans may offer expanded learning tools, storage, or professional review, with transparent pricing, cancellation, and no pay-to-pass mechanism. Subscription access must not influence assessment outcomes. Payment and subscription are handled through the main platform; the app should receive only a minimal entitlement token, never payment-card credentials. No income, user count, or market outcome is guaranteed.

## Google Play and release gates
The Android app is a planned client, not yet published. Before release: privacy policy and data-safety disclosures; consent UX; security and accessibility testing; age rating; account deletion path; billing-policy review; content moderation; independent assessment validation; and legal review for each assessment use. Government and regulated-profession pathways remain informational until authorized and legally validated.

## MVP milestones
1. Static responsive prototype and published product charter.
2. Consent-first account, purpose selection, local reflection journal, and rubric-based learning demo.
3. Evidence upload with provenance, human review queue, appeal/correction, and audit trail.
4. Privacy/security threat model, accessibility and bias evaluation.
5. Android packaging, closed testing, and Play compliance review.
6. Optional subscription integration only after free core and outcome-independence tests pass.

## Success measures
Measure accessibility, completion, user comprehension, correction/appeal resolution, privacy incidents, rubric reliability, language coverage, and learning progress. Do not optimize for coercive adoption, biometric collection, or unvalidated “human certification.”
