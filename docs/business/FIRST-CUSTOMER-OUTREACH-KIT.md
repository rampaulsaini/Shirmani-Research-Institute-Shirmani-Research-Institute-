# SRI First-Customer Outreach Kit
**Status:** Ready-to-adapt templates. No message has been sent and no customer response is claimed.

## Best first audience
Start narrowly with small businesses, independent educators, NGOs, research groups, and local service providers who need bilingual website copy, articles, or a research summary. Do not attempt mass outreach to all 195 countries before validating one offer.

## Email subject options
- Hindi/English website content support for [Organisation]
- Research summary and bilingual writing support
- A small, clearly scoped content pilot for [Organisation]

## First-contact email
Hello [Name/Team],

I’m contacting you from Shirmani Research Institute. We provide Hindi/English writing and research-support deliverables, including website copy, blog/article drafts, and source-linked research summaries.

For a small pilot, we can agree in writing on the topic, audience, language, length, sources, deadline, revision count, and fixed price before work begins. A sample deliverable can be shared as a clearly labelled demonstration, not as previous client work.

Would a short content or research task be useful to your team at present? If so, please share the topic and expected deadline, and we can send a scoped quote.

Regards,  
Shirmani Research Institute  
[Official contact details]

## Outreach safeguards
- Use only legitimate, publicly listed business contact channels and comply with applicable law and platform terms.
- Personalise each message; do not scrape or bulk-spam personal addresses.
- Respect opt-outs immediately and record them.
- Never invent a relationship, endorsement, customer, testimonial, institutional affiliation, or performance result.
- A sent email is not a lead conversion. A quote is not revenue. Revenue is recorded only after payment is confirmed.
