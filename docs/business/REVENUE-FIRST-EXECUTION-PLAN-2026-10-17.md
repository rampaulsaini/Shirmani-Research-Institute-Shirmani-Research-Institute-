# Shirmani Research Institute — Revenue-First Execution Plan
**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** Proposed execution plan; no sales, client outreach, payment, live integrations, or income are claimed by this document.

## 1. Immediate priority: sell a service that can actually be delivered
Start with the currently deliverable offer: Hindi/English writing and research support. Do not market browser-local prototypes as connected AI, verified research, live avatar, or production SaaS.

### Starter offers (INR; quote is a proposed test price, not a market guarantee)
1. **Research brief** — ₹1,499 pilot: one clearly scoped topic, 800–1,200 words, up to 5 supplied/public sources, citations/links, uncertainty notes, one revision; target turnaround 2 business days after scope and payment confirmation.
2. **Bilingual website copy starter** — ₹2,499 pilot: homepage, about, one service page, 3 calls-to-action, Hindi and English, fact-check checklist, one revision; target 3 business days.
3. **Article/blog draft** — ₹1,499 pilot: 800–1,000 words in one language, source links where factual claims require them, one revision; target 2 business days.
4. **Institutional research summary** — from ₹4,999 after written scoping; timeline and source volume agreed before payment.

These are validation prices. Adjust after 5–10 qualified enquiries based on effort, customer feedback, and conversion. Do not promise outcomes outside the institute's control.

## 2. Required intake and release gate
Use the existing “First Client Deliverable Studio” and enquiry template. Capture:
- Customer/contact and organisation
- Objective, topic, intended audience, language
- Word/page limit, deadline, source links/files, budget
- Deliverables, exclusions, revision count, price, schedule
- Payment terms and customer approval in writing

**No work starts until scope, price, deliverables, deadline, and payment terms are confirmed in writing.** Never fabricate sources, credentials, customer testimonials, client logos, or sales.

## 3. Simple public showroom information architecture
Make the public page understandable from top to bottom:
1. **Hero:** what Shirmani Research Institute does, for whom, and a clear “Request a quote” action.
2. **Services:** Writing & Research first; list deliverables, starting prices, turnaround ranges, and limits.
3. **How it works:** Enquiry → scope/quote → written approval/payment → draft → human fact/source review → delivery/revision.
4. **Sample work:** clearly marked demonstration samples; do not imply a sample is client work.
5. **Product showroom:** group existing prototypes into Writing & Research, Presentation/Rehearsal, Product Media, Production Control, and Research/Explainer. Label each as “browser-local prototype” where appropriate.
6. **Trust and evidence:** distinguish “implemented”, “prototype”, “planned”, and “externally verified”. Show actual evidence links only.
7. **FAQ and contact:** response expectations, privacy, revisions, refunds/cancellation, accessibility, and contact method.

Keep advanced vision (social platform, global library, studio, AI/ML/NLP, NVIDIA/GitHub integrations) in a clearly marked roadmap section until working implementations can be demonstrated. Do not bury the immediate service offer under aspirational claims.

## 4. Product truth and integration evidence
Use the README inventory as the source of truth. Existing browser-local tools can generate drafts, cue sheets, catalogues, or export files, but they do not by themselves prove external AI research, independent fact-checking, email sending, payments, avatar generation, live streaming, or sales.

For HeyGen/media, track separate evidence states:
- Original portrait asset and explicit usage permission
- Authorised voice asset and permission
- Generated video URL/file
- Audio intelligibility review
- Lip-sync review with recorded test result
- Live Q&A test prompts and recorded responses
- Failure/retry behaviour and release approval

Never mark an integration “verified” from a URL alone. Do not request material already supplied; request only a genuinely missing required asset once, with its exact name.

## 5. Security and anti-copying: practical baseline
- Keep API keys, provider tokens, payment secrets, and customer private files out of Git.
- Add secret scanning and dependency/security checks to CI; resolve findings before release.
- Validate inputs, escape output, use least privilege, rate-limit public forms, and protect against spam.
- Do not store sensitive customer information in browser-local prototypes as if it were a secure server database.
- Add a privacy notice, retention/deletion process, consent records, and access controls before collecting production customer data.
- Treat “cannot be copied” as a goal, not a guarantee. Protect brand and source code, use licences/terms, watermark optional previews, and keep differentiating value in quality, provenance, and service.

## 6. NVIDIA and GitHub integration rule
Use tools only where they solve a verified need. GitHub: issues, pull requests, CI, code scanning, dependency review, release tags. NVIDIA: evaluate specific APIs/SDKs only after a working use case, cost, licensing, hardware/runtime requirements, and benchmark plan are documented. No premium infrastructure purchase is required for the first service sale.

## 7. 14-day measurable validation cycle
Daily, record counts rather than claims:
- 5 tailored, relevant prospects researched
- 3 individually written outreach messages sent only where a legitimate public business contact is available and applicable rules permit it
- 1 follow-up batch for prior contacts, respecting opt-outs
- Every lead gets a status: researched / contacted / replied / qualified / quoted / won / lost
- Record time spent, quote value, paid amount received, delivery status, and reason lost

**Success criteria after 14 days:** at least 30 legitimate prospects researched, 15 tailored contacts sent, 3 qualified replies, 2 written quotes, and at least 1 paid pilot. These are targets, not promises. If targets are missed, inspect offer fit, audience, samples, price, and outreach quality before scaling. Income counts only when payment is confirmed.

## 8. Weekly dashboard
Track:
- Leads researched; contacts sent; replies; qualified enquiries
- Quotes sent; quote-to-win rate
- Confirmed payments (INR); refunds; outstanding balances
- Delivery on time; revision hours; customer satisfaction
- Product defects; accessibility issues; evidence gates passed/blocked
