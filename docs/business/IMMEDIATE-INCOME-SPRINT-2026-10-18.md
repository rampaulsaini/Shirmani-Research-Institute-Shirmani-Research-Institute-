# SHIRMANI RESEARCH INSTITUTE — Immediate Income Sprint
**Prepared:** 2026-10-18 · **Status:** proposal and operating plan, not evidence of sales or income.

## One clear service to sell
### Hindi–English Writing & Research Pack
For small businesses, educators, researchers, creators, NGOs and teams.

**Starter deliverable:** one 800-word article, explainer, website page or research summary; agreed language; one revision; editable final file; source list when research is included.

**Test prices (validate demand; not market-rate claims):**
- Starter — ₹1,500: up to 800 words, one language, client brief/sources, one revision.
- Research Plus — ₹3,500: up to 1,200 words, source-led summary and source ledger, one revision.
- Bilingual Web Pack — ₹5,000: up to three short website sections in Hindi and English, one revision.

Confirm scope, source access, deadline, revision count, payment terms and any applicable taxes in writing before starting. No guaranteed outcomes, fabricated citations, unlimited revisions or unpaid custom work.

## Delivery controls
1. Log the enquiry, public contact source, date and opt-out status.
2. Confirm scope, quote, deadline and revision limit in writing.
3. Agree payment terms before starting; use a legitimate payment method and issue an appropriate receipt/invoice.
4. Draft and map factual claims to sources; flag unsupported claims.
5. Check language, links, originality and deliverables.
6. Deliver and request acceptance in writing.
7. Record invoice, actual payment, costs, hours and feedback. Count revenue only when payment evidence exists.

## Seven-day validation sprint
- Day 1: publish service page and test enquiry form on mobile/desktop.
- Day 2: prepare three sample pieces clearly labelled as samples, not client work.
- Days 3–5: identify 10–15 relevant organisations from official public contact pages; personalise messages; respect opt-outs and terms; no scraping or bulk spam.
- Day 6: one appropriate follow-up; record replies and objections.
- Day 7: review results and adjust niche, samples, price or offer.

Track qualified prospects, replies, calls, quotes, paid orders, cash received, delivery time, revisions and satisfaction. This is a process target, not a sales guarantee.

## Public interface
Create a clean “Hire us / Writing & Research” section with service cards, clear prices or quote labels, sample work, enquiry form, privacy note and statuses: Draft / Ready for review / Published / Paid. No invented testimonials, client logos, credentials, sales counts or revenue.

## Automation backlog
**First:** enquiry capture, lead-status tracking, CSV export, quote template, delivery checklist and evidence log.  
**Next:** email sending with preview and human approval; invoice/payment integration; analytics.  
**Later:** CRM sync and scheduled follow-ups with opt-out suppression.

Never auto-send bulk messages, invent official contacts, bypass spam controls or mark a lead as a sale without payment evidence. Minimise customer data and restrict access.

## Acceptance criteria
- A visitor understands service and scope in under a minute.
- Enquiry form works on mobile and validates required fields.
- Quote, deadline and revisions are explicit.
- Research includes a source ledger or clearly states source limitations.
- Outreach has a legitimate source and opt-out status.
- Dashboard separates prospects, quotes, orders, invoices and payments.
- Every income claim has matching payment evidence.
