# SHIRMANI Supreme NLP — Measurable Accuracy Layer

The control layer now measures implementation behaviour instead of declaring universal accuracy.

## Metrics

- **status accuracy** — contract-state classification;
- **state accuracy** — known synthetic signal-pattern classification;
- **reproducibility** — identical inputs produce identical outcomes;
- **governance boundary** — signal interpretation is not converted into proof of subjective experience;
- **latency** — measured benchmark execution time.

A single aggregate score must not hide a critical failure.

## Five-minute Automission

The benchmark participates in the continuous loop:

**Observe → Collect → Normalize → Analyze → Reason → Execute → Test → Verify → Audit → Learn → Improve**

Every cycle produces a machine-readable regression report and artifact.

## ML/NLP expansion contract

Future ML models may replace feature extraction, anomaly detection, classification, multimodal fusion or language generation, but each adapter must preserve:

- model/version;
- preprocessing version;
- input/output schema;
- confidence and calibration metadata;
- provenance;
- evaluation metrics;
- known failure modes;
- abstention/uncertainty state.

The deterministic control spine remains responsible for evidence boundaries and verification.

## Biological / plant / non-living signals

Observable electrical, vibration, environmental and other signals may be translated into simple language. A detected pattern is not automatically evidence of feeling, consciousness, intention or subjective experience. Such claims require operational definitions, labelled data, controlled experiments and independent replication.

## Engineering target

Increase measured performance, reproducibility and robustness while reducing error and latency. Absolute or universal accuracy is not declared without appropriate benchmark evidence.
