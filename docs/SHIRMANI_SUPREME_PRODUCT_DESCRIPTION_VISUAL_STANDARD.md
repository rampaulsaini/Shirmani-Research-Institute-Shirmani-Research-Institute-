# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Product Description & Visual Standard

**Status:** Specification drafted; production assets and end-to-end rendering are not yet verified.

## 1. Purpose
Every Shirmani Research Institute tool or digital product must help visitors understand what it does, who it helps, how to try it, what it costs, and what evidence supports its claims. Use plain Hindi and English for beginners while preserving technical detail for expert readers.

## 2. Required product-card visual
1. **Original identity photo:** Use the user's supplied “मेरी निष्पक्ष समझ” photograph unchanged as the logo. Do not redraw, beautify, or generate a substitute. Preserve aspect ratio and resize proportionally only.
2. **Identity line beneath the photo:**  
   *Shiromani Rampal Saini — Tulanateet Kaalateet Shabdateet Premateet Shashwat Vastavik Swabhavik Satya Pratyaksh Samaksh*
3. **Unique QR code:** Place at the upper-right with strong contrast and quiet space. Each product/version must have its own QR destination opening its long-form passport, not a generic home page.
4. **Product title and short description:** Show a specific name, version, main use, and intended user directly on the image. Avoid unsupported “world's best,” guaranteed-outcome, or “impossible to copy” claims.
5. **VIP screenshot:** Include a real screenshot of the actual product interface. Never label a mock-up as a live product screenshot.
6. **Demo video:** Provide a product-specific MP4 showing problem → steps → output → limitations. Until recorded and checked, label it “Demo pending.”
7. **Full-view scaling:** Use a responsive 16:9 or product-appropriate layout, readable type, high contrast, and mobile-safe margins. Never stretch the original photo.

## 3. Product passport (QR destination)
Each passport should include:
- Product name, stable ID, version, release date, and status
- Capabilities, limitations, intended users, use cases, requirements, and step-by-step guide
- Actual screenshots, demo MP4, sample input/output, and known limitations
- Price, offer terms, payment conditions, support route, and refund/cancellation terms where applicable
- Evidence/source links, test results, last-tested date, changelog, accessibility/security notes
- Customer feedback and documented improvement history

## 4. Evidence and release states
- **VERIFIED:** supported by recorded tests or independently checkable evidence
- **SOURCE-BASED:** a linked source exists; independent verification is not claimed
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal philosophy or self-description, not presented as scientific proof
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** say “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
- **PLANNED / IN PROGRESS / DEMO PENDING:** not yet delivered or tested

A workflow run, generated file, or draft alone does not prove production readiness, a sale, successful outreach, or income.

## 5. Voice and human-presentation policy
Use only the user's authorized original voice asset for their platform. Keep authorization separate from speech recognition, response generation, avatar animation, and lip-sync. Test each stage and the complete chain before marking it live. Do not claim exact voice reproduction or successful lip-sync until a real test passes.

## 6. Quality gates before publishing
- [ ] Original photo asset available and checked against the supplied original
- [ ] Authorized voice asset available and permission recorded, where applicable
- [ ] Unique product name, ID, and version
- [ ] Accurate, mobile-readable on-image short description
- [ ] QR scans and opens the correct passport
- [ ] Screenshot is from the actual product/version
- [ ] Product-specific MP4 plays and demonstrates the stated workflow
- [ ] Usage guide and limitations are present
- [ ] Price and offer terms are explicit; no invented discounts or sales claims
- [ ] Evidence, test date, and release state are recorded
- [ ] Accessibility, privacy, security, and respectful-language checks pass

**Release rule:** Do not mark a product complete until its required gates have evidence. If a source asset is unavailable, mark that gate blocked and request the exact asset instead of substituting a generated likeness or another person's voice.
