# Research Paper Proposition Intake — Expanded Universal-Truth Set

This record explains how the newly registered propositions are handled by the research system.

## Boundary

The propositions in `generated/research-paper-claims.json` are author-declared philosophical/metaphysical propositions. Their registration preserves the research object; it does not establish scientific truth, universal validity, consciousness in nonliving matter, or physical energy claims.

## Required transition

Each proposition must move through:

**definition → operationalization → evidence → test/formulation → independent review/replication → verification decision**

A publication or automated QC result is not a substitute for independent verification.

## New proposition families

- Integrated energy / “महा ऊर्जा” hypothesis
- Infinite subtle-axis / stabilization hypothesis
- Mind–head–intellect illusion model
- Existence and “being-feeling” ontology
- “सर्व भौमिक सत्य / प्रत्यक्ष समक्ष” foundational conclusion

## Fail-closed rule

Until appropriate evidence and independent evaluation exist, the registry status remains:

`AUTHOR_PROPOSITION` / `UNVERIFIED`

No downstream promotion gate may treat these records as independently verified merely because they are published, indexed, or processed by automation.
