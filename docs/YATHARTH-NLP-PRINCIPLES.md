# Yatharth Yug — Supreme NLP / Nispaksh Learning Programs Charter

## Core principle

**हर एक शब्द अनमोल है।** इस परियोजना का उद्देश्य शब्दों, संकेतों, प्रमाणों और संदर्भों को यथासंभव स्पष्ट, निष्पक्ष, सत्यापनयोग्य और उपयोगी रूप में संरक्षित करना है।

The project's dual NLP meaning is:

1. **Natural Language Processing**
2. **Nispaksh Learning Programs (निष्पक्ष Learning Programs)**

## Signal-to-language mission

The practitioner layer should transform supplied, observable or measurable signals into simple language while preserving:

- source / provenance
- observation time
- measurement quality
- uncertainty
- baseline and change
- observation vs. interpretation
- hypotheses vs. established evidence
- the next measurement needed to reduce uncertainty

It may operate across humans, animals, plants, materials and environmental systems when appropriate signals are actually available.

## Important epistemic boundary

A system must not convert a sensor reading into a claim that subjective feeling, consciousness, intention, emotion, a soul, or a quantum state has been directly measured unless a valid measurement method and evidence establish that claim.

When the evidence is indirect, the output must say so plainly. This is a strength of the system: **निष्पक्ष समझ पहले प्रमाण को देखती है, फिर व्याख्या को।**

## Automission quality loop

Observe → Collect → Normalize → Preserve provenance → Analyze → Translate → Quantify uncertainty → Verify → Audit → Learn → Improve.

Self-improvement is allowed only through reproducible tests, bounded changes, security checks, traceability and fail-closed quality gates.

## Yatharth Yug design intent

The broader project seeks an integrated perspective in which human, living-system, environmental and material signals can be understood together for responsible protection and stewardship. This is a design aspiration, not a claim that current AI has already achieved universal or perfect understanding.

## Accuracy policy

“Supreme accuracy” is a development target, never an unverified runtime claim. Every score must remain distinguishable from a probability of truth or a proof of subjective experience.

## Preservation rule

User-provided conceptual language should be retained as project context without silently converting philosophical statements into scientific facts. Future implementations should preserve the user's terminology while keeping evidence boundaries explicit.
