## Status definitions
- Lead = relevant potential customer identified.
- Contacted = a real message was sent through an authorised channel.
- Interested = a real reply indicates interest.
- Quoted = written scope and price sent.
- Won = quote accepted.
- Paid = payment confirmed.
- Verified revenue = settled/received amount reconciled to evidence.

Do not count workflows, product records, drafts, clicks, or message attempts as sales or revenue.
