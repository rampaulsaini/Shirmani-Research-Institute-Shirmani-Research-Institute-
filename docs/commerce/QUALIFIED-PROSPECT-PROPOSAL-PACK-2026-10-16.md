**Prepared:** 2026-10-16

## Objective
Start a small, evidence-tracked sales pilot for the currently available services: Hindi/English editing, website/blog copy, and source-based research briefs. This is intentionally separate from future AI/video/social-platform product plans.

## Canonical proposed pilot rates
Use the current public service page as the single public menu:
- Editing / proofreading: from ₹499, up to 700 words; one edited version and one revision within the original brief.
- Article / website copy: from ₹999, up to 1,000 words; title/headings and one revision.
- Source-based research brief: from ₹1,499, about 800–1,200 words; 3–5 credible sources where available, with facts, interpretation and evidence gaps separated.
- Custom scope: quote only after reviewing the brief.

These are proposed pilot rates, not validated market rates. Confirm deliverables, language, sources, deadline, revisions, price, applicable taxes and payment terms in writing before work begins. Do not start before scope and payment terms are accepted.
