# Income-first: first-customer outreach kit

**Purpose:** turn the existing Writing & Research Service Desk into a real, trackable attempt to win a first paid pilot. This is an execution aid, not proof of customers or income.

## Offer to lead with

Start with one simple, bounded service: **Hindi/English editing and proofreading, up to 700 words, proposed ₹499**. The public sales desk lists this as a proposed price. Confirm scope, deadline, revision count, delivery format and payment terms in writing before accepting work. Do not start unpaid custom work or promise unsupported results.

Sales desk: https://github.com/rampaulsaini/Shirmani-Research-Institute-Shirmani-Research-Institute-/blob/main/showroom/global-product-sales-desk.html

## Who to contact first

Prioritise people who already publish text and can decide quickly:
- Local coaching centres and tutors: course descriptions, notices, brochures.
- Small shops and service businesses: WhatsApp catalogue text, service descriptions, short website copy.
- Independent consultants and community organisations: reports, announcements, bilingual notices.
- Small web/design/marketing agencies: overflow proofreading and bilingual copy support.

Use publicly listed business contact channels. Personalise each message; do not bulk-spam, scrape private data, or claim a prior relationship.

## First message — English

Hello, I’m contacting you from Shirmani Research Institute. We offer a small Hindi/English editing and proofreading pilot for text up to 700 words. The proposed starting price is ₹499, subject to agreeing the exact scope and deadline first.

If you have a notice, service description, brochure, or web page that needs clearer language, I can send the scope and delivery terms for review. No obligation. Would this be useful to you?

## Follow-up (send once, after 3–5 business days)

Hello, just following up once on my message about the small editing/proofreading pilot. If this is not relevant, no reply is needed and I won't follow up again. If useful, please share the approximate word count and deadline so I can confirm scope and price before any work begins.

## Before accepting a paid order

1. Confirm the customer's identity and requested deliverable.
2. Agree price, deadline, format, revision limit, source/rights responsibilities and payment terms in writing.
3. Send an invoice or written payment request with the agreed details.
4. Verify the payment in the actual bank/payment account; do not rely on screenshots alone.
5. Deliver the agreed work and ask the customer to confirm receipt.
6. Record the order and payment privately; publish only consented testimonials or customer details.

## Minimal lead tracker

Track one row per real prospect:
- Date
- Business / organisation
- Public contact channel
- Why the offer is relevant
- First message sent (yes/no)
- Reply
- Scope agreed (yes/no)
- Amount agreed
- Payment verified (yes/no; date)
- Delivery confirmed (yes/no; date)
- Next action / stop-contact note

Do not put passwords, bank details, sensitive personal data, or unpublished customer material in a public repository.

## Honest conversion metrics

- Prospects contacted: count only messages actually sent.
- Replies: count actual replies.
- Qualified enquiries: count only real requests with a stated need.
- Proposals accepted: count written acceptance.
- Paid orders: count only when payment is verified.
- Delivered orders: count only after delivery confirmation.
- Revenue: count verified receipts, net of refunds where applicable.

Do not show targets as achievements. A GitHub commit, PR, page view, draft enquiry or promised payment is not income.
