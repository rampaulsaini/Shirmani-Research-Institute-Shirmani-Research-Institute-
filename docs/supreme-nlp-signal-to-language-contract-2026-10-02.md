# SHIRMANI Supreme NLP Signal → Plain-Language Contract

## Purpose

Define the canonical translation boundary for measurable signals from text, speech, instruments, plants, living systems, environments and non-living systems.

## Required separation

Every interpretation must preserve these distinct layers:

1. **Measured signal** — the observed/instrument-recorded input.
2. **Detected pattern** — a reproducible feature or pattern extracted from the signal.
3. **Model inference** — the bounded ML/NLP prediction or classification.
4. **Plain-language interpretation** — the simplest faithful explanation of the inference.
5. **Confidence / uncertainty** — calibrated or explicitly qualified uncertainty.
6. **Evidence / provenance** — source, instrument, dataset, model and version where applicable.
7. **Alternative interpretation** — a materially plausible competing explanation.
8. **Unresolved unknowns** — what the system cannot establish.

## Living / plant / environmental boundary

The system may describe measurable signal patterns. It must not convert those patterns into a claim of subjective feeling, consciousness, intention or inner experience unless a separate evidence protocol establishes such a claim.

## Output principle

The simplest explanation is preferred only when it remains faithful to the measured data and model output. Simplification must never remove material uncertainty or provenance.

## Verification states

REGISTERED → UNVERIFIED → REVIEW → VERIFIED

BLOCKED remains blocked until the blocking condition is resolved.

## Accuracy

No universal accuracy claim is permitted. Performance is reported only for the defined task, population, dataset, metric and evaluation protocol.
