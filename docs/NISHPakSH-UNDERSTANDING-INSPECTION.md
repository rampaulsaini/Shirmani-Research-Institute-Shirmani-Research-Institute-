# Nishpaksh Understanding Inspection

This is the design specification for a purpose-first, evidence-first inspection layer.

## Purpose
A participant first declares what is being inspected: self-reflection, human-foundation understanding, education, a work/role competency set, public-service responsibilities, or a custom auditable purpose.

## Principle
**Purpose → Consent → Scope → Evidence → Analysis → Human Review → User Review → Verification → Record**

AI/ML/NLP agents can assist with question generation, normalization, evidence organization, summaries, contradiction detection and audit trails. They do not autonomously decide human worth, legal status, employment, education admission, political participation, public-office eligibility, health, personality or similar high-impact outcomes.

## Multimodal boundary
Text, audio, video and documents may be used when necessary and consented. Biometric signals such as eye/finger-vein data are restricted to explicit identity/liveness/access-control purposes where lawful and necessary; they are not converted into personality, emotion, health, intelligence, political preference or suitability scores.

## Subscription
Subscription controls access to the service. It does not create verification, qualification, employment, income, authority or accreditation.

## Certificate
A certificate is a provenance-bearing record of the declared inspection scope and evidence state. It is not automatically academic, professional, governmental or scientific accreditation.

## Scale
The architecture is intended to be scale-ready for a potential population on the order of 850 crore people, with tenant isolation, localization, accessibility, auditability, model/version tracking, privacy controls, independent verification adapters, appeals and disaster recovery. This is an architectural target, not an adoption prediction.
