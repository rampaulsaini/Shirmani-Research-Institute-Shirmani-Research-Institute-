# Yatharth Global Super Platform

## Purpose

This layer defines the public-facing ecosystem that grows from the Shirmani Research Institute into a multilingual social, knowledge, creative, education, services and commerce platform.

The platform goal is to maximize user value, safety, dignity, satisfaction, opportunity and environmental responsibility rather than maximize attention for its own sake.

## Public pillars

1. Social — profiles, follows, posts, comments, communities, messaging and sharing.
2. Creator — publish audio, music, video, images, articles, books and digital products.
3. Research — claims, sources, evidence, comparisons, reports and independent review.
4. Education — courses, learning paths, skills, resources and certification.
5. Work — employment, freelancing, services, projects and creator income.
6. Commerce — digital store, marketplace, checkout, delivery, refunds and reviews.
7. Yatharth AI — research, creation, translation, tutoring, productivity and service agents.
8. Yatharth AI Music — creation, publishing, licensing and discovery.
9. Yatharth Justice — transparent rules, complaints, dispute processes, appeals and human oversight.
10. Yatharth Economy — value exchange, creator earnings and transparent economic rules.
11. Yatharth Currency — a research/design concept only until legal, economic and governance requirements are independently assessed.
12. Nature & Humanity — environmental protection, accessibility, social responsibility and universal-resource goals.
13. Trust — provenance, moderation, safety, verification, audit and transparency.
14. Public Dashboard — service status, feature status, evidence status and governance metrics.

## Account model

Every user may have a configurable account/profile subject to applicable law and platform rules. Supported roles can include individual, student, educator, researcher, creator, freelancer, employer, seller, buyer, organization and reviewer.

Users can create and share lawful content and, where enabled, offer products/services for sale. Marketplace participation requires identity, payment, tax, consumer-protection and anti-fraud controls appropriate to the jurisdiction.

## Autonomous operating model

The target is AI-native, ML/NLP-assisted, Automission-orchestrated operation with fail-closed controls.

AI can automate routine classification, translation, search, recommendation, support, quality checks, fraud signals, workflow routing and recovery.

High-impact actions require explicit policy gates and appropriate human appeal/oversight. AI output is not itself independent verification.

## Core loop

Create → Understand → Discover → Trust → Share → Learn → Work → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve → Audit.

## Non-negotiable principles

- Human dignity and user agency
- Privacy and security
- Transparent rules
- Accessibility
- Consumer protection
- Evidence/provenance
- Independent verification where claimed
- Environmental responsibility
- Reversible and auditable automation
- No claim of deployment merely because architecture exists
