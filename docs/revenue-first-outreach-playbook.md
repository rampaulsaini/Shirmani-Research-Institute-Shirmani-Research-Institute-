## 1. Offer only what can be delivered now

1. Hindi/English editing and proofreading.
2. Article, blog, and website copy.
3. Source-based research brief / summary.
4. Bilingual adaptation of supplied material.

The current site describes pilot starting rates of **₹499** for editing/proofreading, **₹999** for article/website copy, and **₹1,499** for a source-based research brief. These are indicative pilot rates, not independently market-validated prices or a final quote. Confirm scope, source availability, word count, deadline, revisions, and payment terms in writing before accepting work.

Do not advertise unverified AI products, live voice/lip-sync, institutional partnerships, guaranteed outcomes, or capabilities that do not have a working demo and evidence.

## 2. Initial customer segments

Start with a small, relevant test rather than mass-mailing 195 countries.

- **Local/regional businesses:** website copy, product descriptions, bilingual service pages.
- **Independent researchers, educators, and NGOs:** source-based summaries, explainers, bilingual research communication.
- **Small agencies and creators:** editing, blog drafts, scripts, and translation/adaptation.
- **Labs and institutions:** only offer literature summaries, research communication, or web copy within the actual brief; do not imply official affiliation.

Find each prospect through its official website and use only a publicly listed business contact channel. Respect opt-outs and applicable anti-spam/privacy rules. Do not scrape personal addresses or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## 5. Qualification and payment gate

Before accepting an order, record:
- Customer/organization and a verified contact route
- Desired outcome and intended audience
- Service type, language, word/page limit, and source links
- Deliverables and explicit exclusions
- Price and currency, deadline, revision count
- Payment method, due date, refund/cancellation terms
- Permission to use supplied materials and any confidentiality needs

**Start work only after written acceptance of the scope, quote, deadline, revision policy, and payment terms.** Never ask for passwords, one-time codes, or unnecessary personal data.

## 6. Simple outreach pipeline

Use one row per organization in a private tracker. Do not commit private contact details or client information to this public repository.

| Stage | Definition | Count |
|---|---|---:|
| Researched | Fit and official contact verified | 0 |
| Personalized draft | Message tailored and checked | 0 |
| Sent | Message actually sent by an authorized person | 0 |
| Reply received | Human response received | 0 |
| Qualified | Need, scope, and timing established | 0 |
| Quote sent | Written quote delivered | 0 |
| Paid order | Payment confirmed | 0 |
| Delivered | Agreed deliverable submitted | 0 |
| Repeat/referral | Subsequent order or referral confirmed | 0 |

Start with **10 carefully researched prospects over 5 working days**, not a mass campaign. These are targets, not completed actions or guaranteed results.

### Weekly measures
- Prospects researched = count of verified, relevant organizations
- Response rate = replies ÷ messages actually sent
- Qualified-lead rate = qualified leads ÷ replies
- Quote conversion = paid orders ÷ quotes sent
- Revenue = payments actually received, excluding unpaid quotes
- Delivery reliability = on-time deliveries ÷ deliveries due

Report zero where there is no evidence; never fill counts with estimates. Review results after the first 10 personalized contacts and adjust the offer based on actual replies.

## 7. 14-day execution sequence

- **Days 1–2:** check the public service page, test all links, prepare two clearly labelled sample pieces from public/licensed material, and ensure samples do not disclose private information.
- **Days 3–7:** research and personalize 10 suitable prospects; record each contact only in a private tracker; send individually through verified official channels.
- **Days 8–10:** answer replies, qualify needs, and send written quotes only after scope is clear.
- **Days 11–14:** fulfil any accepted and paid work; request permission before using a client result as a case study; calculate actual response, quote, payment, and delivery metrics.

## 8. Truth and safety gates

- A generated draft is not a sent message.
- A sent message is not a lead; a lead is not a sale.
- A quote is not revenue; only confirmed payment is revenue.
- A demo is not independent verification; report what was actually tested.
- Do not delete repository materials merely because they are not currently visible on the public landing page. First inventory, classify, and verify links/dependencies.
- Do not claim a 195-country campaign, 850-crore-user capacity, unique/un-copyable technology, or end-to-end HeyGen voice/lip-sync/live-Q&A success without verifiable evidence.
