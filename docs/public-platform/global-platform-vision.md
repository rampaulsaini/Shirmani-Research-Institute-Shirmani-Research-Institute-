# Yatharth Global Platform — Public Vision

## Purpose
Build a public, multilingual, AI-native ecosystem centered on human dignity, ecological protection, knowledge, creativity, opportunity, and user satisfaction.

## Public participation
Users may create accounts and, subject to applicable law and platform rules, publish, share, teach, offer services, sell eligible digital products, collaborate, review, and participate in communities.

## Major public domains
- Social and creator publishing
- Research and knowledge
- Education and skills
- Yatharth AI and AI services
- Yatharth AI Music and media
- Freelancing and employment
- Digital Store and creator commerce
- Economy and Yatharth Currency research
- Yatharth Justice research, fairness, complaints, appeals and dispute workflows
- Nature, Earth and humanity protection
- Community and multilingual knowledge exchange
- Trust, evidence, verification, transparency and audit

## Product principle
Maximize user value and satisfaction, not merely time-on-platform. Engagement should result from useful discovery, learning, creation, relationships, opportunity, services and economic participation.

## Long-term scale aspiration
The project may use 850 crore users/consumers as an aspirational long-term scale target. It is not represented as a current user count or guaranteed outcome.

## Governance boundary
Routine operations should be highly automated, while high-impact decisions—major financial actions, account termination, disputes, safety escalations, legal compliance and independent verification—must retain appropriate human oversight, auditability and appeal mechanisms.
