# Yatharth Global Platform — Public Vision

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem for the Shirmani Research Institute as a human-value, knowledge, creation, commerce, education, and community platform.

## Public principles
- Human dignity, safety, accessibility, transparency, and user agency.
- Users may create accounts, publish permitted content, offer services, sell digital products, learn, teach, collaborate, and participate in communities.
- Customer satisfaction is measured through usefulness, quality, safety, fair value, service quality, complaint resolution, and repeat voluntary use.
- The stated long-term aspiration of reaching 850 crore users/consumers is a vision target, not a current achievement or forecast.
- Nature, Earth, biodiversity, and long-term human welfare are first-class mission domains.

## Public domains
1. Social and creator profiles
2. Research and knowledge
3. Education and skills
4. AI/ML/NLP services
5. Yatharth AI Music and media
6. Freelancing and employment
7. Digital store and creator commerce
8. Business and professional services
9. Yatharth economic concepts
10. Yatharth justice, trust, complaints, and appeals
11. Nature/Earth/humanity initiatives
12. Community and collaboration
13. Public verification and evidence
14. Multilingual access
15. Public status, transparency, privacy, and security

## Value loop
Create -> publish -> discover -> learn/connect -> transact where applicable -> deliver -> review -> improve.

The platform must never imply that architecture, automation, or AI output is equivalent to independent scientific, legal, financial, or human verification.
