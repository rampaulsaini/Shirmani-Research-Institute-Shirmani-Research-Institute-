# Public Service and Planetary Protection Metrics

The platform should measure outcomes instead of relying on slogans.

## Human services

Suggested indicators:
- people with reliable food access;
- people with safe shelter;
- essential healthcare access;
- education access and completion;
- clean water/sanitation access;
- disability/accessibility coverage;
- emergency response time;
- unresolved public-service complaints;
- appeal resolution time.

## Planetary and living-system indicators

Suggested indicators:
- protected habitat area;
- biodiversity trends;
- water quality;
- air quality;
- soil health;
- restoration progress;
- waste/recycling rates;
- disaster-risk exposure;
- animal/plant protection incidents.

## Interpretation

Metrics are evidence for policy evaluation, not proof of “complete satisfaction.” Definitions, measurement methods, uncertainty and population coverage must be shown with each metric.

AI may help collect and analyze metrics, but the measurement methodology must remain inspectable and independently auditable.
