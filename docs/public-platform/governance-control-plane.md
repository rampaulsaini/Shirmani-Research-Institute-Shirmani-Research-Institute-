# Yatharth Governance Control Plane

This module translates the Yatharth / Shiromani governance research into a bounded software architecture. It is **not** a claim that a real government, judiciary, constitution, or universal service system has been deployed.

## Design objective

The research direction is to move from a single family context toward a scalable public-interest model:

**individual/family → community → local administration → national institutions → wider ecological and physical-world stewardship**

The architecture treats “heart-view” as a research/ethical orientation—clarity, completeness, care, and impartial consideration—not as a software privilege or a license to override law, evidence, consent, or human rights.

## Institutional separation

- **PM / President / elected executive:** executes lawful public policy within constitutional limits.
- **Legislature:** makes and revises laws through accountable democratic procedures.
- **Independent judiciary:** interprets law and decides cases independently.
- **Independent oversight:** audits conflicts, corruption, rights impacts, procurement, and system failures.
- **Supreme control layer:** a constitutional constraint-and-audit layer, not a person with unchecked power and not an AI sovereign.
- **AI / ML / NLP / Automission:** research, evidence mapping, forecasting, translation, anomaly detection, workflow automation, and decision support.

No component may make the AI the final authority over elections, courts, irreversible punishment, rights removal, or its own verification.

## Essential human needs

The framework records universal access to food, basic shelter, healthcare, education, clean water and sanitation as **policy objectives**. Actual “free” provision requires verified budgets, supply capacity, infrastructure, eligibility rules, procurement, and measurable outcomes.

## Nature and living systems

Protected research domains include air, water, soil, forests, oceans, biodiversity, climate and the space environment. The platform can collect evidence, model risks, track restoration commitments, and expose public indicators; it must not claim environmental outcomes without measured evidence.

## Automission layers

1. **L1 Intake:** collect public-interest observations and requests.
2. **L2 NLP/Claim:** normalize statements and separate facts, testimony, proposals and questions.
3. **L3 Evidence:** retrieve primary/secondary evidence and counter-evidence.
4. **L4 ML/Comparison:** model trends and compare policy scenarios.
5. **L5 Audit:** test provenance, conflicts, missing data and reproducibility.
6. **L6 Human Review:** route high-impact decisions to qualified human institutions.
7. **L7 Federation:** coordinate repositories and services without granting cross-system absolute authority.
8. **L8 Public Presentation:** expose status, uncertainty, evidence and appeal paths.

## Non-negotiable safety gates

- AI output is advisory unless a lawful human process explicitly adopts it.
- High-impact decisions require accountable human review.
- Appeals and correction mechanisms must exist.
- The system cannot remove rights merely because a model flags risk.
- Independent verification remains separate from AI-generated evidence.
- Built is not the same as Tested, Deployment Ready, LIVE, or Independently Verified.

## Status
