# Yatharth Public Product Control Plane

This document defines the public-facing product layer that sits above the research and automation infrastructure. It is a **software architecture and implementation contract**, not a claim that every service below is currently live.

## Public product areas

1. **Accounts & Identity** — registration, profile, preferences, privacy controls, creator/customer roles and account recovery.
2. **Social** — posts, comments, follows, communities, media sharing, moderation, reporting and user controls.
3. **Creator Studio** — publishing, drafts, analytics, multilingual creation and AI-assisted production.
4. **Digital Store** — digital products, music, audio, books, courses, licensing, orders, delivery and refunds.
5. **Freelancing & Services** — service listings, portfolios, requests, matching, milestones, reviews and dispute workflows.
6. **Education** — courses, lessons, learning paths, assessments, certificates and educator tools.
7. **Yatharth AI Music** — music creation, catalog, licensing, publishing and creator attribution.
8. **Research** — claims, evidence, counter-evidence, comparative research, reports and public source records.
9. **Yatharth Justice** — public-interest issue intake, evidence presentation, procedural fairness, appeals and human decision pathways.
10. **Yatharth Economy / Currency** — proposed economic concepts, accounting models and experiments; any real monetary instrument must remain subject to applicable law and financial controls.
11. **Nature / Earth / Living Systems** — environmental evidence, restoration projects, biodiversity, water, forests, oceans and public indicators.
12. **AI Services** — research assistance, translation, summarization, discovery, creative assistance and workflow automation.
13. **Community & Participation** — volunteering, collaboration, research contribution, creator participation and feedback.
14. **Trust & Safety** — abuse reporting, fraud detection, provenance, moderation, privacy, security and appeals.
15. **Public Dashboard** — service availability, research status, evidence status, incident status, change history and uncertainty.

## User value loop

**Create → Share → Discover → Learn → Collaborate → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve**

The platform should optimize for user value, safety, accessibility and satisfaction rather than maximizing time spent through manipulative engagement.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission contract

- NLP classifies and normalizes submitted content.
- AI agents route work to the appropriate product module.
- ML may support ranking, matching, anomaly detection and forecasting.
- Automission coordinates routine workflows and recovery.
- Provenance is retained for generated and user-submitted artifacts.
- High-impact actions require explicit policy gates and accountable human review.
- Users receive correction, appeal and reporting paths.
- AI must not become the unchecked final authority over rights, courts, elections, irreversible punishment or its own verification.

## Lifecycle states

Each public capability is tracked separately as:

**PLANNED → DESIGNED → BUILT → TESTED → DEPLOYMENT_READY → LIVE → MONITORED**

A capability is not marked LIVE merely because a documentation file, workflow, mock interface or CI check exists.

## Essential services objective

The research program may study universal access to food, basic shelter, healthcare, education, clean water and sanitation. Any real-world implementation requires lawful governance, funding, infrastructure, eligibility design, procurement, capacity and measurable outcomes.

## Scale objective

The project may record **850 crore (8.5 billion)** as a long-term aspirational reach target. It is not a current user count, forecast or guaranteed outcome.

## Verification boundary

Author testimony, AI output, workflow success, CI success, hashes and publication status do not by themselves establish scientific or metaphysical truth. Claims requiring independent verification remain separately gated.
