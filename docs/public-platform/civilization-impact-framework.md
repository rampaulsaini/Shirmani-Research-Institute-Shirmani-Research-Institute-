# Yatharth Civilization Impact Framework

## Scope
Translate the stated Yatharth mission into measurable service outcomes without treating philosophical propositions as independently established facts.

## Nested scope
Family -> community -> city -> region -> country -> global ecosystem.

Each scope retains individual rights, local context, lawful authority, accessibility and appeal.

## Human domains
- Food and nutrition access
- Clothing and essential goods
- Safe housing
- Healthcare access
- Education and lifelong learning
- Employment and freelancing
- Digital inclusion
- Social connection and dignity
- Safety and complaint resolution

## Earth domains
- Air and water quality awareness
- Forest and biodiversity protection
- Ocean and marine ecosystem protection
- Soil and agricultural sustainability
- Waste and pollution reduction
- Restoration and conservation participation
- Environmental education
- Space-environment stewardship

## Measurement contract
For each initiative record baseline, target, population, geography, time period, data source, uncertainty, cost/resource model, legal constraints and independent review status.

Core metrics may include reach, accessibility, affordability, completion, satisfaction, complaint resolution time, environmental impact indicators, safety incidents and independently reviewed outcomes.

## Governance boundary
AI may coordinate information, workflows, service routing, monitoring and analysis. It must not replace constitutional government, courts, elected institutions or accountable human decision-makers.

## Mission principle
The platform should seek to improve human and environmental outcomes through evidence, service quality, transparency and voluntary participation. It must not claim that any single philosophy, algorithm or platform can guarantee universal happiness or eliminate all suffering.
