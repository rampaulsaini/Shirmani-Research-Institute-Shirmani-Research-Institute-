# Yatharth Public Experience Contract

## Purpose
Define the public-facing experience for the Yatharth global platform while keeping the deployed state distinguishable from the long-term target architecture.

## Public capabilities

A visitor may:
- browse research, education, media, music and knowledge;
- create an account and profile when the deployed identity service supports it;
- publish eligible text, audio, image, video and other permitted content;
- offer eligible digital products and services;
- discover education, employment and freelancing opportunities;
- use marketplace, creator and community features where enabled;
- receive multilingual AI-assisted discovery and support;
- report content, transactions or disputes and use an appeal path.

## User rights

The platform should provide:
1. understandable terms and policies;
2. privacy and data controls;
3. content and account export where technically feasible;
4. transparent moderation reasons;
5. appeal and dispute mechanisms;
6. transaction receipts and status;
7. accessible customer support;
8. protection against fraud, spam and abuse.

## AI/ML/NLP operating contract

AI systems may assist with:
- classification and semantic understanding;
- translation and accessibility;
- search and recommendation;
- creator assistance;
- research organization;
- fraud and abuse detection;
- customer support;
- quality and continuity checks.

AI output is not automatically evidence of truth, legality, ownership, authorship or independent verification.

## High-impact boundary

Account termination, significant financial restrictions, unresolved disputes, legal/compliance decisions, and independent verification must have auditable rules, a human-review/appeal path where required, and reversible safeguards where feasible.

## Marketplace integrity

Seller and buyer workflows should maintain:
- product/service identity;
- price and transaction state;
- delivery evidence where applicable;
- refund/dispute state;
- reviews with anti-manipulation controls;
- prohibited-content controls.

## Nature and humanity objective

The platform may present the author's Yatharth framework and its stated objectives concerning human welfare and protection of nature, Earth, living beings and resources. These are presented as framework goals and proposals unless independently supported by evidence.

## Status vocabulary

Every public capability should expose one of:
- PLANNED
- IN_DEVELOPMENT
- TESTING
- LIVE
- LIMITED
- PAUSED
- RETIRED

Target architecture must never be displayed as though it were already deployed.

## Success measures

Public reporting should distinguish:
- active users;
- creator participation;
- customer satisfaction;
- successful transactions;
- dispute resolution;
- safety incidents;
- accessibility;
- platform reliability;
- environmental/community initiatives.

Growth targets are aspirations, not guaranteed outcomes.
