# Yatharth Human–Earth Continuity Charter

## Purpose
This charter records the author's public mission: to develop a platform that places human dignity, nature, Earth, other living beings, knowledge, creativity and long-term continuity at the center of its design.

These statements are preserved as an author mission and design objective, not as independently established scientific facts.

## Design objectives
- Encourage reflection, balance, empathy, responsibility and evidence-aware decision making.
- Make useful education, basic information, health resources, opportunity and digital participation broadly accessible, subject to real-world costs, law and safety.
- Treat forests, oceans, atmosphere, biodiversity and other environmental systems as first-class protection concerns.
- Provide transparent evidence, provenance, appeal and audit mechanisms.
- Let people create, share, learn, collaborate, offer services and sell permitted digital goods.
- Optimize for user value and satisfaction rather than addictive engagement alone.

## Family-to-global scope
1. Individual and family needs.
2. Community and local participation.
3. National-scale public services and knowledge.
4. International collaboration.
5. Long-term human–Earth continuity.

## Governance boundary
AI agents may automate routine operations, detection, routing, translation, quality checks and recommendations. High-impact decisions require defined human oversight, appeal and audit paths. No AI layer may silently convert an author's philosophical or metaphysical statement into an independently verified fact.

## Core lifecycle
Observe → Preserve → Understand → Compare → Test → Assist → Review → Audit → Improve

## Success measures
- user-reported satisfaction and successful task completion;
- accessibility and reliability;
- safety and abuse-response performance;
- evidence traceability;
- environmental-impact indicators where measurable;
- creator and worker opportunity;
- education outcomes;
- independent review and verification status.

## Author source
The author's continuing source material is expected to extend this charter. New material should be preserved as source records before normalization or implementation.
