# Yatharth Global Super Platform — Public & Autonomous Systems Blueprint

## Purpose

This blueprint extends the Yatharth public-platform architecture from a research website into a global, multilingual, user-participatory digital ecosystem.

The platform vision is to combine knowledge, education, social participation, creative work, commerce, freelancing, research, AI services, environmental stewardship, trust, and public-interest tools in one coherent interface.

The stated aspiration is to maximize human benefit, customer satisfaction, accessibility, dignity, ecological protection, and opportunities for learning and livelihood. User claims and philosophical propositions remain author-source material until independently evidenced.

## Public experience

A visitor can:

- browse public knowledge, research, articles, books, audio, music, video and educational material;
- create an account and profile;
- publish permitted text, images, audio, video, research and creative work;
- follow creators, researchers, educators and communities;
- create communities and collaborative projects;
- offer services and freelance work;
- list eligible digital products in the store;
- create and sell courses and educational material;
- discover jobs, services, research opportunities and collaborations;
- use multilingual AI assistance;
- submit reviews, complaints and support requests;
- access public evidence, provenance, verification and audit information.

## Account and creator layer

Every eligible user account gets:

- profile and identity controls;
- privacy controls;
- content library;
- creator dashboard;
- store/service listings;
- earnings and transaction records where applicable;
- education progress;
- reputation/review history with safeguards;
- moderation and appeal status;
- export/delete controls;
- security and session management.

The platform must not require users to surrender unnecessary personal information.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission operating model

### L1 — Intake Agent
Receives content, account events, research material and marketplace events.

### L2 — NLP Understanding Agent
Language detection, transcription, translation, semantic parsing, entity/concept extraction and classification.

### L3 — Safety & Policy Agent
Detects spam, fraud, abuse, prohibited content, privacy risks and other policy violations.

### L4 — Knowledge & Research Agent
Links claims to sources, provenance, evidence, counter-evidence and comparative material.

### L5 — Recommendation & Discovery Agent
Provides relevance-based discovery with user controls and transparency. Avoid manipulative engagement optimization.

### L6 — Marketplace Agent
Matches buyers, sellers, students, educators, clients, freelancers and services.

### L7 — Creator Agent
Assists drafting, translation, metadata, accessibility, publishing and product preparation while preserving user ownership and attribution.

### L8 — Education Agent
Builds learning paths, explanations, practice and progress tracking.

### L9 — Music & Media Agent
Supports Yatharth AI Music and media workflows with rights/provenance checks.

### L10 — Trust & Verification Agent
Maintains evidence status and prevents AI-generated confidence from being mistaken for independent verification.

### L11 — Customer Support Agent
Handles routine support, triage, multilingual assistance and escalation.

### L12 — Audit Agent
Continuously checks contracts, logs, permissions, data integrity and service health.

### L13 — Automission Supervisor
Coordinates agents, retries bounded failures, creates receipts, escalates exceptions and stops unsafe loops.

### L14 — Federation Agent
Coordinates authorized repositories/services using least-privilege credentials and explicit delivery receipts.

## Fail-closed governance

Automation must not silently convert an author claim into an independently verified fact.

The following remain separate states:

AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → COMPARATIVE_REVIEWED → READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW → INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED → VERIFIED

Possible non-verified outcomes include PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, INCONCLUSIVE and NOT_VERIFIED.

## Human oversight

“Highly autonomous” means routine operations can be automated; it does not mean that every consequential decision is delegated to an AI.

Human review and appeal must remain available for:

- permanent account termination;
- major financial disputes;
- serious safety incidents;
- high-impact marketplace disputes;
- justice/dispute-resolution decisions;
- independent scientific or scholarly verification;
- exceptional access-control decisions.

The governance layer must be neutral, transparent, auditable and subject to applicable law and rights protections.

## Yatharth Justice

The public justice layer is a platform mechanism, not a replacement for lawful courts or constitutional institutions.

It should provide:

- clear rules;
- complaint intake;
- evidence submission;
- case tracking;
- mediation/dispute-resolution pathways where legally permitted;
- reasoned decisions;
- appeal mechanisms;
- conflict-of-interest controls;
- audit trails;
- public aggregate transparency without exposing private case data.

No political office, executive authority, AI agent or platform operator should receive an unbounded “supreme control” capability.

## Universal basic-needs vision

The project may research and prototype pathways toward universal access to essential needs such as food, shelter, healthcare and education. Such proposals must be treated as policy/economic research and tested for feasibility, funding, legal compatibility, sustainability and unintended effects rather than represented as already implemented.

## Customer satisfaction

The primary product metric should be user value and satisfaction, not raw time spent.

Track:

- task success;
- content quality;
- search relevance;
- service reliability;
- complaint resolution;
- accessibility;
- safety;
- trust;
- fair pricing;
- retention by genuine value;
- creator and buyer outcomes.

The system must avoid dark patterns and deliberate addictive-design mechanisms.

## Implementation gates

Every public feature follows:

DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → SECURITY REVIEW → ACCESSIBILITY REVIEW → CONTRACT CHECK → PRODUCTION READINESS → DEPLOY → OBSERVE → AUDIT → IMPROVE

A feature is not labelled LIVE merely because its documentation or workflow exists.

## Current status principle

GitHub Actions, research automation, QC, public-platform contracts and continuity checks demonstrate engineering activity. They do not by themselves prove that the complete global social, marketplace, education, finance, justice or AI ecosystem is deployed.

Each module therefore requires an explicit status:

PLANNED | DESIGNED | BUILT | TESTED | LIVE | AUTONOMOUS | INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED

This status registry is the authoritative public-facing implementation contract.
