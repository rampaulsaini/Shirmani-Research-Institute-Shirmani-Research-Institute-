# Yatharth Global Public Super-Platform — Capability Registry

## Status contract
PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → MONITORED → AUTONOMOUS (where appropriate). Documentation is never deployment evidence.

## Public capability domains
1. Identity: accounts, profiles, preferences, creator profiles, secure sessions, ownership, privacy, accessibility.
2. Social: feed/discovery, posts, comments, reactions, sharing, communities, audio, video, live/media, multilingual publishing.
3. Creator economy: creator studio, digital products, Digital Store, music/audio store, publications, courses, licensing, earnings, reviews.
4. Work: Freelancing, services marketplace, Employment, jobs, matching, skills, portfolios, Research-as-a-Service, AI services.
5. Education: Yatharth Education, courses, learning paths, skills, research library, articles, publications, knowledge search, AI/ML/NLP learning, certificates.
6. Research: canonical source archive, claim registry, definitions, evidence, counter-evidence, testing, comparison, independent review, verification, public dashboard.
7. AI/creative: AI research assistant, NLP, semantic search, translation, creator/education assistance, discovery, Yatharth AI Music, AI Creative Studio, AI agents.
8. Economy: economic research, creator economy, marketplace payments, lawful free/subsidized access models, sustainability, Yatharth Currency research/model.
9. Justice/trust: fair-process information, evidence presentation, complaint intake, dispute support, appeals, transparency, accountability/audit. The platform does not replace courts or lawful public institutions.
10. Nature/Earth/humanity: conservation, biodiversity knowledge, community participation, welfare, sustainability education, resource protection.
11. Automission control plane: intake, semantic/NLP, classification, search, translation, creator, education, marketplace matching, support, safety/fraud/spam, research/evidence, comparison, QC, audit, supervisor, recovery, federation, public-status agents.
12. Governance: safety, privacy, security, moderation, reporting, appeals, audit trails, human review, responsible AI, machine-readable status.

## Core loop
Create → Share → Discover → Learn → Collaborate → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve.

The objective is user value and satisfaction, not engagement for its own sake.

## Bounded autonomy
Routine reversible operations may be automated. Fail closed for account termination, significant financial actions, legal/justice decisions, safety-critical actions, and independent verification decisions; provide appropriate human review and appeal.

## Yatharth objective
The platform may support the author's stated objectives concerning human wellbeing, environmental protection, social cooperation and balanced development. These remain framework objectives/claims unless independently verified.

## Important economic note
Yatharth Currency remains a research/design proposal until legal, technical, monetary and regulatory requirements are independently established.
