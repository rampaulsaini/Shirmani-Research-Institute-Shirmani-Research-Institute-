# Yatharth Humanity & Nature Mission

This module translates the author's stated Yatharth objective into a public-platform design principle: improve human wellbeing while protecting people, animals, plants, oceans, forests, Earth systems and the wider physical environment.

## Operating principles
- Human dignity and equal access
- Protection of nature, biodiversity and shared resources
- Basic-needs access as a design objective: food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education
- Evidence-first public claims; author experience remains clearly identified as author source
- Transparent rules, appeals and accountability
- AI-first automation with human oversight for high-impact decisions

## Scope
Family → community → city → country → global ecosystem.

The platform must not present the Yatharth framework's philosophical or metaphysical claims as independently established scientific facts. Those claims follow the existing source/claim/evidence/verification lifecycle.

## Success signals
Measure user satisfaction, safety, accessibility, environmental-impact indicators, service quality, complaint resolution, educational access and economic opportunity rather than maximizing screen time alone.
