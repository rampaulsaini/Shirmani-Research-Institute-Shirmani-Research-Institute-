# Yatharth Public Super-Platform — Operating Contract

## Purpose

This document defines the public-facing product contract for the Yatharth/Shirmani ecosystem.

The platform is intended to combine social publishing, creator tools, education, research, digital commerce, freelancing, employment, AI services, music/media, community participation, environmental stewardship, trust, verification and public-service information in one interoperable experience.

The architectural goal is **maximum useful automation with auditable human control**, not an unaccountable human-free system.

## Account and participation model

A registered user may, subject to platform rules and applicable law:

- create a profile;
- publish text, images, audio, video and other permitted media;
- create communities;
- sell eligible digital products;
- offer eligible services;
- create courses and educational material;
- seek freelance work or employment;
- purchase products/services;
- participate in research and review;
- provide ratings, feedback and dispute reports;
- use multilingual AI-assisted creation and translation.

Identity, payments, age restrictions, intellectual property, privacy and safety controls must be implemented according to the jurisdiction and product risk.

## Core value loop

Create → Publish → Discover → Verify → Connect → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve → Earn/Support → Learn.

Engagement must not be the sole optimization target. The public dashboard should track user benefit, satisfaction, safety, accessibility, successful outcomes, complaint resolution and environmental/social impact alongside usage.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission control plane

### Agent layers

L0 Human and legal authority boundary  
L1 Intake and event agents  
L2 NLP understanding and classification  
L3 Semantic/entity/relation layer  
L4 Search, retrieval and recommendation  
L5 Creator, education and commerce agents  
L6 Trust, safety, fraud and abuse detection  
L7 Verification and evidence agents  
L8 Workflow orchestration / Automission  
L9 Federation across repositories/services  
L10 Continuous audit, resilience and recovery

### Fail-closed rules

Agents must not silently:

- manufacture evidence;
- label an author's experience as independently verified;
- make irreversible high-impact decisions without the required authorization;
- move money outside an authorized transaction flow;
- suppress legitimate appeals;
- expose private user information;
- bypass legal or platform safety requirements.

Every consequential automated action should have an event ID, actor/agent identity, timestamp, policy/version reference and auditable outcome.

## Governance and justice boundary

The platform may provide transparent rules, evidence organization, dispute workflows, appeals, fairness research and public dashboards.

It must **not** represent the platform's AI, PM/president, administrator, or any private governance layer as possessing supreme authority over constitutional governments or lawful courts.

High-impact governance, financial, safety, account and verification decisions require defined authorization, auditability and appeal routes.

## Humanity / Earth stewardship objectives

The platform can measure and support:

- environmental education;
- conservation projects;
- biodiversity and habitat initiatives;
- sustainable resource use;
- humanitarian/community projects;
- access to education and essential services;
- transparent welfare initiatives.

These are product objectives and measurement categories, not claims that the platform has already achieved global outcomes.

## Basic-services vision

The project may document and research models intended to improve access to food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education.

Any promise of universally free services must be tied to a defined funding, legal, operational and geographic model before being presented as an available service.

## Global scale aspiration

The project may use **850 crore (8.5 billion) people** as a long-term participation aspiration.

It must never be displayed as a current user count or achieved result unless independently measured and supported by reliable platform data.

## Verification states

Public claims and research artifacts must preserve the distinction between:

AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → COMPARATIVE_REVIEWED → READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW → INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED → VERIFIED

Additional terminal states:

PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED → INCONCLUSIVE → NOT_VERIFIED

Engineering success, workflow success, publication and AI-generated output do not by themselves create independent verification.

## Public dashboard contract

Expose separate metrics for:

- platform availability;
- automation health;
- research queue;
- evidence/traceability;
- independent verification;
- active accounts;
- creator activity;
- successful transactions;
- customer satisfaction;
- complaint/appeal resolution;
- safety incidents;
- environmental/community impact.

Never collapse these dimensions into one artificial “overall truth” percentage.

## Security and privacy

The public platform must use least privilege, secret isolation, secure authentication, encrypted transport/storage where applicable, abuse controls, rate limits, audit logs, data minimization and user-controlled privacy settings.

Secrets and credentials must never be committed to repositories, issues, prompts, logs or generated public artifacts.

## Product implementation order

Phase A — Public information architecture and accessibility  
Phase B — Accounts, profiles, publishing and moderation  
Phase C — Search, social graph and creator tools  
Phase D — Store, services, freelancing and payments  
Phase E — Education and AI services  
Phase F — Trust, evidence and verification  
Phase G — Environmental/community participation  
Phase H — Federation, scaling, observability and continuous Automission

Each phase must be marked separately as Planned, Built, Tested, Deployed or Live.

## Source boundary

The author's philosophical, experiential and normative statements remain clearly attributed as author source unless independent evidence establishes a different status.

The platform's purpose is to make those materials discoverable, structured, comparable, testable and transparently reviewable while building useful public services around them.
