# Humanity and Planet Mission Contract

## Mission

The platform may carry the author's stated mission of encouraging a shift from a narrowly self-preservation-oriented viewpoint toward a more balanced relationship among individual wellbeing, society, other living beings and the physical environment.

This is an author-defined philosophical and design objective. It is not presented as an independently established scientific conclusion.

## Scope

The public platform can support:

- environmental education;
- conservation information;
- responsible consumption;
- nature and biodiversity projects;
- open research and evidence sharing;
- education and skills access;
- lawful livelihood opportunities;
- community support;
- accessibility and inclusion;
- transparent public-interest projects.

## Basic-needs vision

The author's vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. The platform may support this through information, education, service discovery, donations, lawful marketplaces and partnerships.

The platform must not represent universal free provision as already achieved unless a concrete program, funding mechanism and delivery system actually exist.

## Governance principle

No individual—including an administrator, founder, PM, President, court-like module or AI agent—should receive an undefined “supreme” power over users.

For high-impact public decisions, the architecture should instead use:

1. clear rules;
2. evidence and provenance;
3. separation of duties;
4. audit logs;
5. conflict-of-interest controls;
6. appeal/review;
7. lawful human authority where required;
8. bounded AI assistance.

## Measurement

Mission progress should be measured with transparent indicators such as:

- access to educational resources;
- completed learning outcomes;
- creator/service opportunities;
- customer satisfaction;
- resolved support cases;
- environmental project participation;
- verified research records;
- accessibility metrics;
- safety incidents and remediation;
- platform availability.

No single composite “humanity score” should be used to declare that the mission has been achieved.

## Source boundary

Statements about Yatharth, Shirmani, the “heart-view” and related philosophical propositions remain attributable to their author unless independently supported. The platform should preserve the source while making room for evidence, counter-evidence and independent review.
