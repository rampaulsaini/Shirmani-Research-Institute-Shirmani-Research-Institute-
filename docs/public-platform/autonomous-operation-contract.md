# Autonomous Operation Contract

The platform is AI-native but fail-closed.

## Agent responsibilities
1. Intake and classification
2. NLP understanding and semantic indexing
3. Content quality and policy checks
4. Search and recommendation
5. Marketplace matching
6. Education assistance
7. Creator assistance
8. Customer support
9. Translation and accessibility
10. Research/evidence assistance
11. Fraud, spam and abuse detection
12. Monitoring, recovery and continuous audit

## Safety boundaries
AI agents must not silently convert an author's experience into verified fact. Independent verification remains a separate gate.

AI agents must not make irreversible high-impact decisions without the governance path required by the platform policy. Users need notice, records and an appeal route where applicable.

Financial, legal, privacy and identity-sensitive functions must use explicit controls, least privilege, audit logs and applicable compliance requirements.

## Runtime lifecycle
Observe → Understand → Decide within policy → Act → Record → Audit → Learn from approved feedback → Escalate when uncertain.
