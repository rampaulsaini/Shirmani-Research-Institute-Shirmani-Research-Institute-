# Yatharth Neutral Governance Architecture

## Purpose

This document translates the author's Yatharth framework into a bounded, testable public-governance architecture. It is a design proposal, not a claim that a new government, court, currency, or public-service system currently exists.

The objective is to protect human dignity, nature, Earth, biodiversity, and future generations while preserving impartial decision-making, due process, transparency, accountability, and individual rights.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Human dignity and equal civic standing.
2. Impartiality: decisions use published rules and evidence, not personal loyalty, wealth, identity, or access.
3. Separation of powers: executive administration, legislature, courts, election administration, audit, and AI infrastructure remain independently governed.
4. Human constitutional authority: AI advises, detects, summarizes, tests, and monitors; it does not become sovereign.
5. Due process: consequential decisions require notice, reasons, opportunity to respond, appeal, and human-authorized final decisions where law requires.
6. Evidence before status: AI output is not independent verification.
7. Auditability: material decisions retain provenance, versioning, and appeal trails.
8. Least privilege: every agent receives only the permissions required.
9. No single-point supreme AI: one model, operator, or workflow cannot control the whole system.
10. Public-interest testing for policies affecting essential services, nature, or vulnerable populations.

## Constitutional-style control plane

### Layer 0 — Rights and constraints
Record applicable law, fundamental rights, privacy requirements, environmental obligations, and non-discrimination rules.

### Layer 1 — Democratic institutions
Elected and constitutionally authorized institutions retain lawful authority over legislation, administration, budgets, and policy.

### Layer 2 — Independent justice
Courts and legally authorized adjudicators remain independent. AI may assist with research, retrieval, scheduling, and evidence organization, but must not silently replace judicial judgment.

### Layer 3 — Independent elections and oversight
Election administration, public audit, complaints mechanisms, and independent oversight remain separately controlled.

### Layer 4 — AI/ML/NLP Automission
Bounded agents perform document retrieval, NLP rule extraction, provenance, contradiction detection, impact analysis, forecasting, accessibility, translation, audit preparation, and incident detection.

No agent may grant itself authority, alter constitutional constraints, suppress appeals, or change its own permissions.

### Layer 5 — Public service delivery
Essential services are measured through transparent service indicators, lawful eligibility rules, grievance handling, and human escalation.

## Institutional role matrix

| Function | Human authority | AI/Automission role | Final authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Executive administration | Authorized government | planning, monitoring, forecasting | lawful public authority |
| Legislature | Elected/authorized body | research and impact analysis | legislature |
| Courts | Independent judiciary | research, retrieval, consistency tools | court/judge |
| Elections | Independent election authority | logistics and anomaly detection | election authority |
| Public audit | Independent auditors | continuous checks and evidence packaging | auditor |
| Essential services | Public institutions | demand forecasting and routing | lawful service authority |
| Environmental protection | Competent public bodies | sensing, modelling, alerts | authorized authority |
| AI governance | Independent oversight | model tests and audit evidence | oversight body |

## Supreme-control safety rule

Here, supreme control means supreme impartial constraints, not unrestricted command power.

Extraordinary actions use a dual-key rule:
- a lawful human authority; and
- an independent oversight/audit control.

AI cannot satisfy both keys by itself.

Emergency powers must be legally defined, time-limited, logged, reviewable, and returned to ordinary governance after the emergency period.

## Universal basic services design

The proposal for free basic facilities is represented as a policy objective, not as a claim that unlimited free provision is currently economically feasible.

Candidate essential-service domains:
- nutritious food security;
- basic clothing and shelter;
- primary and emergency healthcare;
- foundational education;
- clean drinking water and sanitation;
- basic energy access;
- essential digital/public-information access;
- disability and elder support;
- disaster and climate resilience.

For each service publish:
- legal basis;
- eligibility or universal-access rule;
- capacity and funding model;
- current availability;
- queue/wait time where applicable;
- quality and safety indicators;
- complaint and appeal path;
- independent audit status.

## Nature and all-life protection

Use environmental and biodiversity indicators as public-interest signals without claiming that software can literally sense every form of suffering.

Practical chain:

Sensor/data -> validation -> environmental model -> risk threshold -> human/agency alert -> lawful intervention -> outcome audit

Potential data include air and water quality, temperature and climate indicators, forest cover, biodiversity observations, soil/agricultural indicators, disaster signals, and lawful privacy-preserving public-health/environment interfaces.

## Super-senses: bounded interpretation

Super senses is treated as a research term for multi-modal observation, not supernatural perception.

AI/ML/NLP may combine text, images, audio, satellite/environmental data, public statistics, and sensor streams.

Outputs must carry confidence, source provenance, timestamp, model version, and human-review status.

## Anti-abuse and anti-capture controls

Continuously test for political or institutional capture, discriminatory outcomes, hidden rule changes, model drift, fabricated evidence, prompt injection, privilege escalation, secret leakage, complaint suppression, unexplained service denial, automated retaliation, and single-operator concentration of power.

A critical alert creates an incident and escalation path; it does not automatically punish a person.

## Verification states

DESIGN_ONLY -> BUILT -> TESTED -> DEPLOYMENT_READY -> LIVE -> INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED

VERIFIED is reserved for claims satisfying the repository's independent-verification contract. CI success, AI output, generated reports, publication, or author testimony do not establish independent verification by themselves.

## Implementation boundary

This architecture does not authorize a private platform to supersede a country's constitution, government, legislature, courts, election authority, or lawful institutions. It is a research and software blueprint for impartial decision support, public transparency, essential-service coordination, environmental protection, and accountable AI.
