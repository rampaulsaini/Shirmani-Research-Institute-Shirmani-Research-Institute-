# Yatharth Humanity-Centered Ecosystem Governance

## Purpose

This module translates the author's Yatharth / Heart-View source into a public-platform governance architecture without treating philosophical or metaphysical claims as independently verified facts.

The public objective is to design technology around human dignity, ecological protection, access to essential services, transparency, evidence, customer satisfaction, and responsible automation.

## Core direction

**Family → Community → Nation → Humanity → Earth → Living systems → Physical universe**

The platform should treat these as connected impact layers rather than isolated product categories.

## Public principles

1. Human dignity and non-discrimination.
2. Nature, biodiversity, water, forests, oceans, atmosphere and other ecological systems are protected interests.
3. Essential-service access should be designed around affordability and inclusion; where a service is legally and financially feasible to provide free, the platform may support such programs.
4. User-generated expression, commerce and education require safety, consent, privacy and transparent rules.
5. Customer satisfaction is measured through service quality, reliability, accessibility, complaint resolution and user feedback—not manipulation of attention.
6. AI automation should maximize routine operational autonomy while retaining human appeal and oversight for high-impact decisions.
7. Public claims are separated into author source, normalized claim, evidence/comparison and independent verification.
8. Political or governmental functions are represented as neutral governance research and civic infrastructure, not as a mechanism for concentrating political power.
9. Environmental and social impact metrics are public, auditable and versioned.
10. The system fails closed when safety, evidence, authorization or audit requirements are missing.

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission operating model

### Agent layers

- **L1 Intake Agent:** receives authorized user/content events.
- **L2 NLP Agent:** extracts language, concepts, entities and claims.
- **L3 Context Agent:** links content to applicable domain and user intent.
- **L4 Safety Agent:** detects abuse, fraud, privacy and high-risk conditions.
- **L5 Matching Agent:** connects users, services, learning and marketplace needs.
- **L6 Evidence Agent:** retrieves and maps evidence for research claims.
- **L7 Quality Agent:** checks content and service contracts.
- **L8 Support Agent:** handles routine support and routes exceptions.
- **L9 Audit Agent:** records provenance, decisions and changes.
- **L10 Automission Supervisor:** coordinates jobs, retries, dependencies and failure recovery.
- **L11 Human Review Gate:** handles high-impact exceptions and appeals.
- **L12 Public Presentation Agent:** publishes approved status, dashboards and multilingual views.

## Human-control boundary

Automation must not silently make irreversible high-impact decisions. Account termination, major financial actions, legal/dispute outcomes, safety escalations and independent verification status require appropriate authorization, appeal paths and/or qualified human review.

## Measurement

The public dashboard should distinguish:

- architecture readiness
- implementation readiness
- test status
- production availability
- automation health
- evidence coverage
- independent verification
- user satisfaction
- complaint-resolution performance
- accessibility
- ecological/social impact

No single percentage should collapse these dimensions.

## Long-term scale target

The author's stated aspiration of **850 crore (8.5 billion) consumers/users** is recorded as a long-term vision target, not as a current user count or guaranteed outcome. Scaling must remain subordinate to safety, reliability, legal compliance, ecological responsibility and user value.

## Non-claim rule

Statements such as “all humanity will be satisfied”, “no one will suffer”, or that this system is objectively superior to every existing system are treated as goals or author perspectives unless independently demonstrated. The platform may pursue these goals while publishing measurable evidence about actual outcomes.

## Continuous implementation loop

**Source → Normalize → Design → Build → Test → Audit → Human Review where required → Deploy → Measure → Improve → Re-audit**

This module complements the existing public-platform, Yatharth-system, verification and Automission architecture.
