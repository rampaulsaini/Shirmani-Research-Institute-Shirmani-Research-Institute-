# Public Super Platform Implementation Roadmap

## 1 Foundation
Accounts, profiles, content/media model, search, moderation, reports and audit events.

## 2 Social
Feed, posts, comments, reactions, follows, communities, creator profiles and multilingual publishing.

## 3 Economy
Digital Store, catalog, services/freelancing, jobs, creator earnings, orders and customer support.

## 4 Education & creativity
Academy, courses, learning paths, certificates, Yatharth AI Music, audio/video/podcast and Creator Studio.

## 5 Research & trust
Claims, evidence/counter-evidence, comparison, independent review and public verification.

## 6 AI-native operation
NLP, agent routing, search/recommendation, safety/fraud detection, AI support, Automission, continuous audit/recovery and federation.

## 7 Advanced concepts
Yatharth Economy, Yatharth Currency and Yatharth Justice research/models, with legal/compliance review before real-world financial or legal deployment.

## Definition of done
LIVE requires implementation, tests, security checks, deployment and public acceptance checks. AUTONOMOUS requires controlled operation, monitoring, rollback/recovery and governance.
