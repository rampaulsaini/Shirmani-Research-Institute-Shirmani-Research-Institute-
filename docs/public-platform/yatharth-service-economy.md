## User value loop
Learn -> Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Collaborate -> Buy/Sell -> Earn -> Review -> Improve

The product objective is not simply maximum screen time. Engagement should arise from useful services, meaningful participation, learning, creativity, relationships and economic opportunity.

## Economic safeguards
Clearly distinguish:
- real legal tender
- platform credits
- loyalty points
- proposed Yatharth Currency
- donations
- marketplace balances

A proposed currency cannot be represented as legal tender unless applicable law and licensing requirements are satisfied.

## Customer satisfaction
Track service quality, successful completion, refund/dispute rates, support response time, accessibility, voluntary repeat use and user-reported satisfaction.

Do not manipulate users into excessive use. Provide notification controls, privacy controls and account/data controls.

## 8.5-billion aspiration
A goal of 850 crore users/consumers may be recorded as a long-term aspiration. It must not be presented as a current user count, guaranteed outcome, or forecast.

## Deployment stages
### Stage A - Public information
Research, articles, education, media and transparent source archive.

### Stage B - Participation
Accounts, profiles, publishing, community and creator tools.

### Stage C - Marketplace
Digital products, services, freelancing, courses and creator income.

### Stage D - AI-native operation
Agents, ML/NLP, recommendation, support, trust/safety and Automission.

### Stage E - Global federation
Multilingual scaling, interoperability, ecological/public-good integrations and independent audits.

Every stage has its own release gate; architecture does not imply deployment.
