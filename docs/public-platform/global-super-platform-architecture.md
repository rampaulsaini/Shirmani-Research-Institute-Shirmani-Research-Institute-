# Yatharth Global Super Platform — Public Architecture

## Purpose

This defines the public-facing platform layer. It is an architecture contract, not a claim that every capability is already live.

## Public pillars

1. Social — accounts, profiles, posts, media, communities and discovery.
2. Research — source archive, claims, evidence, comparisons and reports.
3. Education — courses, learning paths, skills, certification and knowledge library.
4. AI — research, creator, multilingual and bounded agent services.
5. Music & Media — Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast, video and creative studio.
6. Freelancing & Employment — profiles, services, jobs, matching and workspaces.
7. Digital Store — digital products, books, audio, music, courses and licences.
8. Economy — creator income, marketplace transactions and the separately defined Yatharth Currency concept.
9. Yatharth Justice — fairness, evidence, complaints, disputes, appeals, transparency and accountability.
10. Nature & Humanity — environmental protection and practical participation.
11. Trust & Verification — provenance, reviews, evidence and independent review.
12. Community & Governance — rules, moderation, safety, privacy and appeals.
13. Public Dashboard — system health, research progress, verification status and change history.

## Account model

Every user may have a profile and, where legally and technically supported, may publish, share, offer services, sell eligible digital products, teach, participate in communities and use AI-assisted creation and research tools.

The platform must provide privacy controls, reporting, appeals, consent, age-appropriate safeguards and protection against fraud, spam and abuse.

## AI-native operating layer

AI Agents → ML → NLP → retrieval/evidence → policy/safety checks → workflow orchestration → audit → human escalation.

Specialized agents include Intake, Profile, NLP Understanding, Content Classification, Search/Discovery, Recommendation, Marketplace Matching, Education, Research, Evidence, Verification Coordination, Creator/Music, Customer Support, Trust & Safety, Fraud Detection, Translation, Transaction Monitoring, Audit, Automission Supervisor and Federation.

## Human oversight

Autonomous means routine operations can be automated; it does not remove legal safeguards or appeal routes. High-impact decisions such as account termination, substantial financial actions, disputes, justice decisions and independent verification require appropriate human review and/or appeal.

## Value principle

Optimize for utility, safety, quality, transparency and satisfaction rather than engagement time alone.

## Scale objective

The project may define a long-term aspiration of serving up to 8.5 billion people. This is a target, not a present user count or guaranteed outcome. The architecture should be multilingual, accessible, mobile-first, privacy-conscious and globally scalable.

## Core lifecycle

Create → Understand → Verify → Discover → Share → Learn → Work → Trade → Review → Improve.

Every capability is tracked as PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → AUTONOMOUS.

A workflow run, generated artifact or CI success must never by itself be represented as independent verification of a substantive claim.
