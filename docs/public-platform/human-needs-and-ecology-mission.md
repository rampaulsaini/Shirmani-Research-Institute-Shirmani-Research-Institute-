# Human Needs and Ecology Mission

The platform's stated mission includes improving access to knowledge, education, opportunity, essential services and constructive participation while supporting protection of nature, Earth, biodiversity and human dignity.

The project may research models for broad access to essential needs such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. Such proposals must remain subject to feasibility, funding, law, public policy, evidence and implementation review; the platform must not represent universal free provision as an already achieved fact.

## Core design test
For every major feature ask:
- Does it increase meaningful human capability?
- Does it reduce avoidable harm?
- Does it improve transparency and fairness?
- Does it protect privacy and dignity?
- Does it reduce ecological harm where feasible?
- Can users understand, challenge and appeal consequential decisions?
