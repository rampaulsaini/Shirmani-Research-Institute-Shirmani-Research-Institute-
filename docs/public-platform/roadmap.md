# Public Platform Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation
- Public navigation and capability registry
- Account/profile architecture
- Content and product schemas
- Trust, safety and audit model

## Phase 2 — Participation
- User publishing
- Creator profiles
- Digital products
- Store workflows
- Freelancing and service listings
- Education/course publishing

## Phase 3 — AI-native operation
- NLP content understanding
- semantic discovery
- recommendation
- marketplace matching
- customer support
- translation
- automated QC
- Automission supervision

## Phase 4 — Economy and civic layers
- Creator economy
- employment/services
- Yatharth Economy
- Yatharth Currency research/definition layer
- Yatharth Justice research and dispute pathways

## Phase 5 — Global scale
- multilingual operation
- accessibility
- low-bandwidth experience
- federation
- observability
- resilience
- continuous independent review

## Success measures
Track separately: active users, satisfaction, successful transactions, creator earnings, learning outcomes, support resolution, safety incidents, uptime, automation health, evidence coverage, and independently reviewed claims.
