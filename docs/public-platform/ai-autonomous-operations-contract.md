# AI / ML / NLP / Automission Autonomous Operations Contract

## Objective
Provide a single operating contract for high-automation public-platform services while keeping high-impact decisions accountable to people and lawful institutions.

## Agent layers
L0 Control and policy: permissions, rate limits, audit requirements and emergency stop controls.

L1 Intake: accounts, posts, products, services, research submissions, reports, support requests and public observations.

L2 NLP understanding: language detection, transcription, translation, entity extraction, topic classification, claim extraction and semantic normalization.

L3 ML intelligence: recommendation, search ranking, matching, anomaly detection, risk signals, forecasting and quality prediction.

L4 Evidence and trust: provenance, evidence retrieval, counter-evidence, duplicate detection, source quality checks and verification routing.

L5 Marketplace and service automation: listings, discovery, matching, order state, delivery state, creator tools, learning paths and support workflows.

L6 Safety and moderation: spam, fraud, abuse, harmful-content signals and privacy risks. Automated signals must not by themselves impose irreversible high-impact penalties.

L7 Automission orchestration: scheduling, dependency management, retries, health checks, workflow federation, rollback and incident routing.

L8 Human review: qualified human review for rights-impacting, legal, financial, judicial, safety-critical and independent-verification decisions.

L9 Public transparency: service status, evidence state, uncertainty, correction channels, appeals and relevant audit summaries.

## Autonomous operation requirements
A capability may be labelled AUTONOMOUS only after implementation, automated tests, production-like tests, monitoring, rollback, security review, privacy review, abuse/fraud testing, documented human escalation and demonstrated safe operation.

## Fail-closed rules
- AI cannot become sovereign over people.
- AI cannot verify its own scientific or metaphysical claims.
- AI cannot silently change governance rules.
- AI cannot permanently remove rights based solely on model output.
- Financial transfers require explicit authorization and auditable records.
- Justice decisions require lawful human institutions and appeal.
- Emergency stop and recovery paths must remain available.

## Continuity objective
Routine operations should continue automatically when dependencies fail where safe to do so, while high-risk actions pause and escalate.

## Status vocabulary
PLANNED -> BUILT -> TESTED -> DEPLOYMENT_READY -> LIVE -> AUTONOMOUS

INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED remains a separate evidence status and must never be inferred from automation success.
