# Autonomous AI/ML/NLP Operating Model

## Objective
Provide a machine-readable architecture for high automation while preserving human control over high-impact decisions.

## Agent layers
- L1 Intake Agent: account/content/service/event intake.
- L2 NLP Agent: language detection, extraction, normalization, translation, semantic indexing.
- L3 Trust & Safety Agent: spam, abuse, fraud, harmful-content and policy signals.
- L4 Knowledge Agent: retrieval, evidence linking, provenance and claim mapping.
- L5 Recommendation Agent: user-controlled discovery and relevance signals.
- L6 Commerce Agent: catalog, matching, order/service workflow and fulfillment signals.
- L7 Education Agent: learning paths, assessment support and resource discovery.
- L8 Creator Agent: drafting, metadata, localization and production assistance.
- L9 Research Agent: comparative research, packet generation and audit preparation.
- L10 Verification Gate: fail-closed status transitions; never upgrades claims merely because AI generated them.
- L11 Automission Supervisor: schedules, dependencies, retries, health checks and escalation.
- L12 Federation Agent: coordinates approved repositories/services with explicit trust boundaries.
- L13 Public Presentation Agent: dashboards, multilingual summaries and user-facing status.
- L14 Continuity/Recovery Agent: backup, anomaly detection, recovery and incident escalation.

## Human control gates
Human review/appeal remains required or available for high-impact account actions, substantial financial disputes, legal/justice decisions, safety escalations, independent verification, and irreversible actions.

## Non-equivalence rule
A successful workflow run proves execution of the workflow, not truth of a philosophical claim, scientific claim, legal conclusion, or market outcome.
