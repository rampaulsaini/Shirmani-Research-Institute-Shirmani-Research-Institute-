# Autonomous Operations Boundary

The platform targets highly automated AI/ML/NLP/Automission operations, while keeping high-impact decisions auditable and appealable.

## Autonomous domain
Routine ingestion, classification, indexing, translation, recommendation, content assistance, monitoring, testing, reporting, workflow routing and recovery may be automated subject to policy and audit gates.

## Human-governed domain
Account termination appeals, major financial actions, legal disputes, justice-related decisions, safety escalations and independent verification require defined human oversight or an independently accountable review path.

## Fail-closed rule
Automation success, workflow completion or AI output does not itself establish truth, legal validity, scientific verification or justice. Such status requires the relevant evidence and review contract.

## Required telemetry
Each autonomous action should retain provenance, actor/agent identity, policy version, input/output references, decision reason, confidence where meaningful, timestamp and appeal/review state.
