# Essential Services and Earth Protection

## Public-interest objective
The Yatharth platform research direction places basic human needs and protection of living systems at the center of its service model.

Long-term policy/service objectives include nutritious food, basic shelter, essential clothing, healthcare, education, clean water, sanitation, safe living conditions, livelihood opportunities and accessible knowledge.

## Earth protection domains
The platform can support evidence collection, monitoring, education, coordination and public accountability for forests, soil, rivers and groundwater, oceans and seas, air quality, biodiversity, wildlife, climate, waste and pollution, sustainable resource use and space-environment stewardship.

## Evidence-first operation
Environmental or social improvement must be measured with appropriate data. A dashboard must distinguish:
proposal -> activity -> output -> measured outcome -> independently reviewed outcome

No AI score, workflow success, publication, hash or author statement is sufficient by itself to establish an environmental or social outcome.

## Protection feedback loop
Observe -> Measure -> Analyze -> Prioritize -> Act -> Monitor -> Audit -> Correct -> Publish

## Human rights and dignity
Essential-service design must preserve equality before the law, non-discrimination, privacy, informed participation, accessibility and meaningful appeal.

## Long-term research question
The platform may investigate whether a shift from predominantly survival-oriented decision-making toward the author's proposed heart-view orientation can produce measurable changes in cooperation, resource protection, wellbeing and environmental outcomes. Such claims require operational definitions and independent evidence.
