# Yatharth Civilization Service Model

## Purpose
This document translates the user's stated direction from family-scale wellbeing to a public-interest architecture spanning community, institutions, society, nature, Earth and the wider physical environment.

It is a design and research specification, not a claim that such a civilization-scale system currently exists.

## Core orientation
The author's "heart-view" concept is treated as an ethical/research orientation: impartial consideration, clarity, completeness, care, balance and continuity of wellbeing. It does not override law, evidence, consent, democratic institutions or human rights.

## Service domains
1. Food and nutrition
2. Basic shelter
3. Clothing and essential goods
4. Healthcare
5. Education and lifelong learning
6. Clean water and sanitation
7. Employment and livelihoods
8. Freelancing and creator work
9. Digital commerce
10. Research and knowledge
11. Justice, dispute resolution and appeals
12. Community participation
13. Environmental protection and restoration
14. Biodiversity and animal welfare research
15. Ocean and freshwater stewardship
16. Forest protection and restoration
17. Climate and pollution monitoring
18. Space-environment stewardship
19. Digital identity, privacy and security
20. Accessible public information

## Family-to-civilization progression
Person -> Family -> Community -> Local systems -> National institutions -> International cooperation -> Ecological stewardship -> Planetary perspective

At every level, the system should preserve individual dignity, consent, due process, privacy and the ability to appeal.

## User value loop
Learn -> Create -> Share -> Collaborate -> Work -> Trade -> Earn -> Contribute -> Review -> Improve

The objective is durable user value and satisfaction, not maximizing screen time through manipulative engagement.

## Measurement
Public dashboards should distinguish service availability, utilization, quality, affordability, accessibility, user-reported satisfaction, verified outcomes, environmental indicators, unresolved complaints and appeal outcomes.
