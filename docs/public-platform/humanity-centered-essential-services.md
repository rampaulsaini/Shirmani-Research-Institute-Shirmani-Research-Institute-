# Yatharth Humanity-Centered Public Service Layer

## Purpose
This module defines a public-facing service layer for the Yatharth Global Super Platform. It translates the stated humanity, planetary-protection, education, health, housing, food and livelihood goals into explicit product capabilities without presenting aspirations as existing services.

## Core principle
Every public feature should be evaluated against:
1. Human dignity
2. Equal access
3. Safety and privacy
4. Environmental impact
5. Transparency
6. Accessibility
7. Affordability
8. User control and appeal
9. Evidence and measurable outcomes
10. Long-term sustainability

## Essential-service capability map
- Food and nutrition information, access and support discovery
- Clothing and essential-goods marketplace
- Housing information, assistance discovery and verified service providers
- Health information, care navigation and emergency-resource discovery
- Education, open learning and skills development
- Employment and freelancing
- Digital products and creator commerce
- Community and social participation
- Nature, forest, water, ocean and biodiversity protection information
- Research, evidence and public knowledge
- Accessibility and multilingual services

The platform must not claim that these services are free or universally available until a real funding, eligibility, provider and delivery mechanism exists.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission role
AI agents may assist with:
- multilingual understanding and translation
- content classification and routing
- service discovery and matching
- fraud, spam and abuse detection
- accessibility transformation
- research and evidence retrieval
- quality checks
- workflow orchestration
- monitoring and incident detection
- continuous audit and recovery

AI must not silently make irreversible high-impact decisions about a person's essential services, account, money, legal status, health care, housing eligibility or access to justice.

## Human oversight
High-impact actions require:
- explainable decision records
- notification to the affected user
- an appeal or correction route
- human review where required
- audit logs
- conflict-of-interest controls
- privacy and security protections

Political institutions, courts, public officials and other authorities may be represented as governance interfaces or research subjects, but the platform does not replace lawful public institutions or claim sovereign authority.

## Planetary protection layer
Environmental claims and interventions should use measurable indicators where possible:
- biodiversity
- forest cover
- water quality
- ocean health
- pollution
- resource consumption
- carbon and other emissions
- restoration activity

The system should distinguish user-authored values and proposals from independently established environmental evidence.

## Customer satisfaction
Track satisfaction through transparent measures such as:
- task completion
- service quality
- response time
- complaint resolution
- accessibility
- repeat voluntary use
- refund/dispute outcomes
- user-reported trust

The platform should optimize for durable user value and satisfaction, not merely maximum time spent.

## Implementation state
This document is an architecture specification. A capability becomes LIVE only after its real interface, backend/service dependency, security controls, testing and operational owner are present.

Suggested states:
IDEA -> DESIGNED -> IMPLEMENTED -> TESTED -> LIVE -> MONITORED

For AI autonomy:
ASSISTED -> SUPERVISED_AUTOMATION -> BOUNDED_AUTONOMOUS -> CONTINUOUSLY_AUDITED

No state transition may be inferred from a successful documentation or CI workflow alone.
