# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Operating Model

## Purpose

Define the public-facing operating model for a global social, knowledge, creator, education, marketplace and research platform.

The platform goal is to maximize **user value, safety, satisfaction, opportunity and ecological responsibility**, not merely time spent.

## Public capabilities

- account and profile creation
- social publishing and sharing
- creator pages and communities
- digital products and store listings
- freelancing and service listings
- employment and opportunity discovery
- education, courses and learning resources
- Yatharth AI and Yatharth AI Music
- research and knowledge discovery
- multilingual publishing and translation
- customer support and dispute/appeal channels
- trust, evidence and public status information

## Economic loop

Create → Publish → Discover → Trust → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Earn → Improve.

No financial feature is considered LIVE merely because its architecture exists.

## User value loop

Learn → Create → Connect → Contribute → Earn/Exchange → Receive support → Give feedback → Improve.

## Autonomous operating principle

AI/ML/NLP/Automission may perform routine classification, routing, moderation assistance, recommendations, testing, monitoring, support and recovery.

High-impact actions require bounded authority, audit trails, appeal paths and human review where law, safety, rights, money or independent verification are materially affected.

## Truth boundary

Architecture, workflow success, generated content and AI output are not independent verification.

Feature state must be reported from the machine-readable status registry.

## Ecological mission

Nature, Earth, biodiversity and human well-being are first-class public-interest objectives. Any future economy or recommendation system should expose measurable sustainability constraints rather than treating ecological protection as marketing copy.

## Scale aspiration

A long-term aspiration of serving up to 850 crore people may be recorded as a target. It is not a current user count, forecast, guarantee or evidence of adoption.

## Release rule

Every public feature must pass:

Implementation → Tests → Security/Privacy → Monitoring → User-facing status → Rollback/Appeal.

Until then its state remains ARCHITECTURE, BETA, RESEARCH or REVIEW_REQUIRED as appropriate.
