# AI-Native Public Platform Operating Model

## Goal
Define the target operating model for a public social, knowledge, education, creative and economic platform using AI agents, ML, NLP and Automission.

This is an implementation target, not a claim that every component is already deployed.

## Agent layers
1. Intake: account events, posts, media, products, jobs, learning materials and support requests enter typed queues.
2. NLP understanding: language detection, transcription, entity and concept extraction, semantic classification, summarization and multilingual normalization.
3. Safety and trust: spam, fraud, abuse, impersonation, unsafe content, privacy risks and marketplace anomalies are detected and routed by explicit policy.
4. Personalization: search, recommendations, learning paths, creator discovery and service matching use user controls and explainable signals where practical.
5. Domain agents: research, education, social content, commerce, freelancing, music/media, support, environmental information and verification.
6. Automission orchestration: bounded tasks, typed artifacts, retries, provenance and exception escalation.
7. Audit and governance: contracts, permissions, data lineage, policy compliance, release gates and health checks.
8. Human review: high-impact, disputed or ambiguous cases go to authorized human review with appeal and audit records.

## Non-negotiable controls
- least-privilege credentials;
- explicit consent for sensitive actions;
- no secrets in prompts, issues or logs;
- provenance for important decisions;
- rollback and recovery;
- rate limits and abuse controls;
- user export/delete controls where legally applicable;
- human appeal for consequential account, financial and justice decisions.

## Public capabilities targeted
Accounts, profiles, social publishing, communities, education, research, digital products, store, freelancing, jobs, creator tools, AI services, music/media, support, verification and public status.

## Status discipline
Every capability must be marked independently as: PLANNED, IN_BUILD, TESTED, LIVE, or AUTONOMOUS_OPERATION.

A workflow success is not evidence that the entire public capability is live.
