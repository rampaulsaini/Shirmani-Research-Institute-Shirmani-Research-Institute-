# Yatharth Autonomous Operations Contract

## उद्देश्य
Public platform के routine संचालन को AI/ML/NLP/Automission से अधिकतम स्वचालित बनाना, जबकि मानव अधिकार, कानून, privacy, safety, appeals और independent review की सीमाएँ स्पष्ट रखना।

## Operating loop
Intake → Understand → Classify → Match → Execute bounded workflow → Monitor → Audit → Learn → Improve → Escalate.

## AI/ML/NLP responsibilities
- NLP: भाषा, intent, entities, claims और content structure समझना।
- ML: ranking, matching, anomaly/risk signals और forecasting में सहायता।
- AI agents: research, support, translation, moderation assistance, marketplace assistance और workflow execution।
- Automission: schedules, dependencies, retries, federation, observability और recovery।

## Human boundary
AI final authority नहीं है for:
- irreversible punishment;
- rights/access removal without accountable process;
- elections/civic decisions;
- court override;
- high-impact medical or public-benefit decisions;
- environmental harm authorization;
- its own verification.

## Public transparency
हर capability को LIVE, BETA, ARCHITECTURE, RESEARCH, REVIEW_REQUIRED या DISABLED जैसे status में दिखाया जाए। Architecture को deployment न माना जाए।

## Customer/user value
Success metrics केवल time-on-platform नहीं होंगे। Quality, safety, usefulness, accessibility, satisfaction, complaint resolution, creator outcomes, learning outcomes और ecological/public-interest outcomes भी measure किए जाएंगे।

## Economic vision boundary
Freelancing, digital store, creator income, employment, Yatharth economy और Yatharth mudra को अलग capability domains के रूप में विकसित किया जा सकता है। किसी proposed currency/economic mechanism को वास्तविक legal tender या guaranteed income तब तक न कहा जाए जब तक लागू कानून, issuer, reserves/settlement, compliance और operational evidence स्पष्ट न हों।

## Status
Architecture contract; implementation and production deployment remain capability-specific.
