# Yatharth Global Platform — Complete Module Map

This map turns the public-platform vision into explicit capability modules while preserving the distinction between architecture, tested implementation, production deployment, and independent verification.

## Identity & participation
- account registration/login/recovery
- profile, creator, researcher, student, freelancer, seller, buyer and business capabilities
- privacy, consent, data export and deletion
- notifications, follows, communities and user settings

## Social & media
- posts, articles, comments, reactions and sharing
- audio, music, podcast, video and live publishing
- multilingual discovery, accessibility and moderation
- creator studio and content provenance

## Work & opportunity
- freelancer profiles and service listings
- client requests, proposals, milestones and fulfilment
- jobs, applications and employment exchange
- business profiles, offers and AI-assisted matching

## Commerce
- digital products, books, courses, audio/music and services
- cart/order lifecycle, entitlement and delivery
- seller dashboard, buyer dashboard, refunds and support
- payment/payout provider integration is deployment-gated

## Education
- courses, learning paths, lessons, assessments and certificates
- AI/ML/NLP skills and practical training
- learner progress, instructor tools and accessibility

## Research & knowledge
- canonical source archive
- claim registry and evidence map
- comparative research
- evidence/counter-evidence
- independent review and verification gate
- public provenance and audit trail

## AI/ML/NLP + Automission
- language detection and translation
- semantic classification and search
- recommendation and matching
- content quality checks
- support automation
- research extraction and evidence routing
- workflow supervision, retries, health checks and recovery
- human approval gates for high-impact actions

## Trust, safety & justice
- reporting and complaints
- moderation and abuse prevention
- appeals
- dispute resolution
- transparent case/audit records
- independent verification workflow
- high-impact decisions require human review

## Economy
- creator/freelancer income surfaces
- value exchange
- Yatharth Economy research
- Yatharth Currency remains a research/design concept unless separately deployed and legally compliant

## Nature & humanity
- environmental knowledge and projects
- community initiatives
- nature/Earth protection economy concepts
- multilingual global knowledge exchange

## Platform operations
- observability, backups, incident response and disaster recovery
- security and secret management
- API/version contracts
- federation and repository integrations
- capability-status dashboard

## Truth boundary
A module may be documented before it is deployed. Architecture, MVP, TESTED, LIVE, AUTOMATED, and INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED are separate states. Automation health does not establish scientific or human verification.
