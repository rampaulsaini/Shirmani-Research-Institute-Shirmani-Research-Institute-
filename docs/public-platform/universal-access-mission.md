# Universal Access Mission

## Goal
Design the platform around the user's stated aspiration that basic human needs and opportunities should be more accessible, while treating implementation as a policy and engineering research problem rather than an unsupported guarantee.

## Research domains
- Food security and nutrition
- Clothing and essential goods
- Safe housing
- Healthcare access
- Education and lifelong learning
- Skills and employment
- Digital access
- Accessibility for people with disabilities
- Environmental protection
- Emergency and community support

## Platform role
The platform can provide knowledge, discovery, education, services, commerce, community coordination, fundraising where lawful, and AI-assisted access to resources. It cannot by itself guarantee that every material need is free or universally available.

## Design requirement
Every proposed social-benefit feature should record:
1. objective,
2. eligible population,
3. funding/resource model,
4. legal constraints,
5. evidence,
6. risks,
7. measurable outcomes,
8. appeal and accountability mechanism.
