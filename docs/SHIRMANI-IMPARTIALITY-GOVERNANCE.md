# SHIRMANI Impartiality Governance — v2

## Objective

The Automission system must remain impartial by construction: it should not convert a user's framework, a model preference, popularity, authority, or repeated assertion into evidence.

The user's **Heart-View / Shamikaran Yatharth Siddhant / Yatharth Yug** terminology may be represented as a user-defined framework or research hypothesis. The evidence layer must separately identify what is measured, what is sourced, what is inferred, and what remains unverified.

## Mandatory pipeline

**Observe → Collect → Normalize → Analyze → Reason → Classify Claim → Compare Evidence → Check Counter-Evidence → Estimate Uncertainty → Translate → Independently Verify → Audit → Improve**

## Claim discipline

Every substantive output receives one of:

- OBSERVED_SIGNAL
- MEASURED_RESULT
- SOURCE_REPORTED_CLAIM
- MODEL_INFERENCE
- HYPOTHESIS
- UNVERIFIED_ASSERTION

No class may silently be promoted to measured fact.

## Living / plant / non-living signal interpretation

The system may translate observable sensor, audio, visual, electrical, chemical, movement, environmental, or other measurable signals into plain language.

It must **not** automatically label a signal as subjective feeling, consciousness, intention, or emotion. Such interpretations require validated evidence and must remain explicitly classified.

## Accuracy

The system does not declare "perfect" or "100% accuracy". Accuracy is measured on defined benchmarks, with confidence intervals/error rates where applicable. Unknown or unsupported cases should trigger abstention rather than fabricated certainty.

## Fail-closed rules

- Missing evidence → abstain or label unverified.
- Conflicting evidence → report the conflict.
- Low confidence → abstain or escalate.
- Failed independent verification → do not promote to VERIFIED.
- Governance violation → fail closed.
- Scheduled production-code mutation → blocked.

## Human agency

Automission may generate tests, analyses, candidate patches, and improvement proposals. Production-impacting changes remain behind explicit verification and repository controls.
