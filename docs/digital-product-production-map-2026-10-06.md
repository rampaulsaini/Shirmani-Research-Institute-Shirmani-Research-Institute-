## Product must become five things

Every commercial digital product should move through:

**IDENTITY → REAL ARTIFACT → PRODUCT PAGE → QC/GATE → ORDER/DELIVERY**

Only after these states are evidenced should the product be represented as fully commercialized.

### A. Identity
Unique product ID, family, engine, version and description.

### B. Real artifact
A usable browser tool, document, audio file, image/creative pack, research edition, template, dataset, or other customer-visible digital deliverable.

### C. Product page
Clear buyer-facing name, use case, format, scope, price or rate, preview, limitations and acquisition route.

### D. QC/Gate
Deterministic functional checks, provenance, version, content integrity and dispatch metadata.

### E. Order/Delivery
Actual checkout/order/payment and delivery evidence. Repository status must not invent a sale or income.

## Production priorities

Convert the strongest existing assets into explicit offers:
1. Audio / MP3 editions
2. Research PDF / knowledge editions
3. Creator/audio packs
4. Research & evidence support packages
5. AI/automation service packages
6. Digital publishing packages
7. Marketing/SEO production packs
8. Browser micro-tools with clear use cases

### Priority 2 — Productize the 25 engines
Each engine should have a genuinely differentiated buyer use case, not merely a renamed copy. Repeated catalog variants remain identities until their artifact and purpose are differentiated.

### Priority 3 — Batch production
Produce the remaining 916 identities in auditable batches. Each batch should create:
- product IDs
- artifact routes
- product metadata
- QC result
- production timestamp
- version
- dispatch state

### Priority 4 — Commercial readiness
For products intended for sale:
**PRODUCT → PREVIEW → PRICE → CHECKOUT ROUTE → DELIVERY → CUSTOMER FEEDBACK**

No fake sales, fake reviews, guaranteed-income claims or unsupported verification claims.

## Definition of “work complete”

The 1,016 target is complete only when each product identity has:
- a distinct or intentionally reusable engine configuration,
- a customer-visible artifact,
- a clear product description,
- a QC record,
- a stable product ID/version,
- an acquisition or free-access route,
- and an explicit state showing whether it is produced, sale-ready, delivered, or still pending.

Independent research/scientific verification remains a separate downstream state.

## Operating loop

**DISCOVER → SPECIFY → PRODUCE → QC → PACKAGE → PUBLISH → OFFER → ORDER → DELIVER → FEEDBACK → IMPROVE → ARCHIVE**

The goal is not to inflate the number of catalog entries. The goal is to turn catalog identities into **real, useful, clear and auditable digital products**.
