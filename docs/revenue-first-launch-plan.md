**Status:** Proposed execution plan; no customer, payment, or revenue is claimed.  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-18  
**Purpose:** Turn the already-present Hindi/English writing and research offer into a small, measurable, honest sales process before expanding to the much larger platform.

## 2. One offer to sell first
**Offer:** Hindi/English writing, editing, source-linked research summaries, and website/blog content.

Pilot reference prices already shown in repository material (not market-validated and not binding quotes):
- Short writing/editing, up to 500 words: ₹499 reference.
- Source-linked summary, up to three agreed sources: ₹999 reference.
- Website/blog draft, up to 1,000 words: ₹1,499 reference.

Before accepting any job, confirm the brief, source access, exact deliverables, word limit, delivery date, revision count, price, payment method, and refund/cancellation terms in writing. Do not promise legal, medical, financial, or other specialist conclusions beyond the agreed research scope.

## 3. First-customer workflow
1. Open the public service page and enquiry form on mobile and desktop.
2. Submit a test enquiry using clearly marked test data; verify what actually happens after submit.
3. Confirm the portfolio/demo opens and all links work.
4. Prepare one tailored sample for a real target segment (for example, a small business needing bilingual website copy, a creator needing a researched script, or an educator needing a source-linked explainer). Mark speculative samples as samples.
5. Build a small list of 10 relevant, publicly listed organisations or prospective clients. Record source URL, fit, public contact route, date contacted, consent/opt-out status, and next action. Do not scrape private contact data or mass-spam.
6. Send individual, relevant outreach through each organisation's published business contact route. No bulk automation until deliverability, privacy, and opt-out handling are tested.
7. For each reply, issue a written quote and scope; start only after acceptance and agreed payment terms.
8. Deliver the agreed work, ask for factual feedback, and request permission before publishing the work or testimonial.

## 5. Seven-day scorecard
Track actual counts; use zero until evidence exists.

| Metric | Target for first 7 days | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Public service page and enquiry flow manually checked | 1 complete pass | Dated test notes + screenshots/logs |
| Relevant prospects researched | 10 | Source URL and relevance note per prospect |
| Individual, relevant messages sent | Up to 10, only after contact details and page are verified | Sent record; do not count drafts |
| Replies received | Measure, do not forecast | Message/thread record |
| Qualified enquiries | Measure, do not forecast | Completed brief |
| Written quotes accepted | Measure, do not forecast | Client acceptance |
| Paid jobs | Measure, do not forecast | Payment-provider/bank confirmation |
| Revenue | Actual cleared amount only | Transaction record, excluding uncollected quotes |

A target is not a result. No revenue is booked from page views, workflow runs, drafts, or sent messages.

## 6. Funnel definitions
- **Prospect:** relevant organisation/person with a recorded public source.
- **Contacted:** a message was actually sent via an appropriate published channel.
- **Reply:** an actual response exists.
- **Qualified enquiry:** scope, language, deadline, and budget are sufficiently clear to quote.
- **Quote accepted:** written acceptance of scope and price.
- **Paid:** payment is confirmed as received/cleared.
- **Delivered:** agreed deliverables have been sent.
- **Verified outcome:** delivery and payment evidence can be independently checked.

Keep these stages separate. Do not infer one stage from another.

## 7. Technical launch gates before scaling
- Test enquiry submission, validation, success/error feedback, and mobile layout.
- Identify whether the form only creates a local draft or actually submits to a backend; label it honestly.
- Check every CTA and canonical URL; remove dead or misleading links only after confirming dependencies.
- Add privacy notice, data-minimisation, retention, and contact/opt-out handling before collecting leads centrally.
- Never store payment secrets, API keys, or private customer data in repository files.
- Use a real payment provider and verify payment server-side before marking an order paid.
- Keep an auditable order record: enquiry ID, accepted scope/version, quote, payment status, delivery link, and timestamps.
- Do not automate bulk email/WhatsApp outreach or claim access to 195 countries' official contacts without verified sources, legal basis, and a tested opt-out process.

## 8. Decision after day seven
- If enquiries are zero: review audience fit, page clarity, sample quality, and contact relevance; do not merely increase volume.
- If replies exist but no qualified enquiries: refine the offer and qualification questions.
- If qualified enquiries exist but quotes are not accepted: ask for feedback and adjust scope/pricing based on evidence.
- If paid jobs exist: deliver reliably, record actual time/cost, and use client-approved feedback to improve the offer.
- Expand to digital tools, institutional sales, and the broader public interface only after this small revenue loop is evidenced and repeatable.

## 9. Completion rule
This plan is ready for review; execution is not considered complete until real tests and outreach records exist. The document itself creates no leads, sends no email, accepts no payments, and does not prove income.
