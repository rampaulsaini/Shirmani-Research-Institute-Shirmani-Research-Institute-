# SHIRMANI Research Institute — First Paid Pilot Playbook

**Status:** Operating plan and copy-ready drafts; not evidence of outreach, customers, sales, or income.  
**Current service scope:** Hindi/English writing and editing, source-linked research summaries, and website/blog content.  
**Owner:** Shirmani Research Institute  
**Review rule:** Confirm the deliverable, final price, deadline, revision count, source/citation requirements, payment method, and cancellation/refund terms in writing before work starts.

## 1. The first objective

Obtain one legitimate, clearly scoped paid pilot, deliver it well, and use the result to improve the service. Do not start by advertising the whole future platform as if every planned feature were production-ready.

## 2. Best first customer groups

Start with a small, relevant, manually reviewed list rather than emailing hundreds of unrelated organisations.

1. **Local businesses and independent professionals:** website copy, service descriptions, FAQs, and bilingual edits.
2. **Small agencies and web designers:** overflow blog writing, landing-page copy, and Hindi/English localisation.
3. **Researchers, educators, and NGOs:** source-linked literature summaries, plain-language explainers, and bilingual material.
4. **Creators and community organisations:** scripts, descriptions, captions, and editorial polishing.

For each prospect, record a public business contact source and one specific reason the service is relevant. Use only contact channels published for business enquiries. Respect opt-outs and applicable anti-spam, privacy, and platform rules. Do not scrape private addresses or send automated bulk messages.

## 7. Weekly measurement and decision rules

Record actual counts; never backfill imagined activity.

- Relevant prospects reviewed
- Contacts approved for outreach
- Messages actually sent
- Replies received
- Qualified briefs received
- Quotes sent
- Paid orders confirmed
- Deliverables accepted
- Revenue actually received (INR), refunds, and direct costs
- On-time delivery rate and revision/defect rate

**Formulas**
- Reply rate = replies received ÷ messages actually sent.
- Qualified enquiry rate = qualified briefs ÷ replies received.
- Quote conversion = paid orders ÷ quotes sent.
- Realised revenue = cleared client payments minus refunds; do not count proposals or unpaid invoices as revenue.
- On-time delivery rate = accepted deliverables delivered by the agreed deadline ÷ deliverables due.

If no messages have been sent, report the rates as “not yet measurable,” not 0% success or failure. After the first 10 relevant, individually reviewed contacts, inspect responses and revise the offer. Ten contacts is a small learning sample, not a statistically reliable market estimate.

## 8. Quality-control gate for every delivery

1. Match the written scope and output format.
2. Check spelling, readability, and language consistency.
3. Trace factual claims to the agreed sources; identify gaps and uncertainty.
4. Verify links and quotations; respect copyright and source attribution.
5. Check privacy, permissions, and confidential material.
6. Apply the agreed revision count and record changes.
7. Obtain client acceptance where agreed and log delivery evidence.
8. Ask for optional, honest feedback; never fabricate a testimonial or case study.

## 9. Current automation boundaries

The existing browser enquiry tools create drafts locally. They do **not** automatically send email/WhatsApp, authenticate a customer, accept a payment, create a binding order, or prove that a client was contacted. Those capabilities require a properly configured provider integration, consent, access controls, test coverage, and an auditable record before being advertised as live.
