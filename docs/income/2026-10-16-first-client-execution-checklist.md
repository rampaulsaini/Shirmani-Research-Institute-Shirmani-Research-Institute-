## Outreach rules
1. Contact only relevant recipients using an official, publicly listed organizational contact channel.
2. Personalize the message to a verifiable need; do not invent a prior relationship, customer, testimonial, partnership, result, or claim of institutional endorsement.
3. Keep this document as a draft workflow. A drafted message is not a sent message. Log a sent event only after a human actually sends it through an authorized account.
4. No bulk unsolicited messaging. Respect channel terms, consent, opt-outs, and applicable law.
5. Never put credentials, private banking details, or unnecessary personal data in the lead ledger.
