# First-client outreach pilot — 16 October 2026

## Status and guardrails

- **Purpose:** prepare a small, measurable first-client experiment for the currently deliverable services: Hindi/English writing, editing, research summaries, and website/blog content.
- **Status:** drafts only. No message has been sent; no recipient or active need is independently verified by this document; no customer, order, payment, or revenue is claimed.
- **Pricing gate:** do not quote or publish a price from these drafts until the owner approves one canonical service menu and the conflicting menus are reconciled under Issue #2462. The current public service page labels its rates as proposed; this is not market validation.
- **Before sending:** open the organisation's official website, verify the current department/team and contact channel, identify a specific current public need, confirm that the recipient accepts this kind of enquiry, personalise the message, and obtain owner approval. Do not guess email addresses, scrape contacts, or bulk-send.
- **Scope gate:** agree deliverables, word count, language, sources, deadline, revision limit, usage rights, final price, and payment terms in writing before work begins.
- **Evidence gate:** only mark a message SENT after it is actually sent; only mark an order ACCEPTED after written acceptance; only mark PAID after the actual transaction receipt is checked.

## Seven-day experiment and measurement

Targets are planning targets, not achieved results or revenue forecasts.

| Metric | Seven-day target | Evidence required |
|---|---:|---|
| Official-source prospect records | 10 | URL, page title/date, relevant need, checked-on date |
| Qualified prospects | 3 | A specific match between a public need and an available service |
| Personalised drafts approved by owner | 3 | Approval record/version |
| Messages actually sent | Up to 3, only after approval | Sent-mail record or authorised channel receipt |
| Replies | Record actual count; no minimum promised | Message/thread evidence |
| Qualified enquiries | Record actual count | Customer's stated objective and scope |
| Written quotes | Only after scope and pricing approval | Quote copy and approval |
| Accepted jobs | Record actual count | Written acceptance |
| Payments received | Record only confirmed receipts | Transaction evidence; amount and date |
| On-time deliveries | Record actual count | Delivery record and agreed deadline |

**Core funnel:** researched → qualified → draft approved → sent → reply → qualified enquiry → quote → accepted → paid → delivered → accepted/feedback.

Report conversion rates only when the denominator is non-zero. Realised revenue equals confirmed payments received, not quoted or promised amounts. Keep gross receipts separate from costs and refunds.

## Automission controls

1. **Discover:** find public official sources and capture URLs/dates.
2. **Qualify:** match an evidenced need to an existing deliverable service; reject speculative or irrelevant leads.
3. **Draft:** create a personalised draft from verified details; never fabricate relationships, outcomes, credentials, or recipient addresses.
4. **Human approval:** require explicit owner approval before external contact, quote, discount, or public price change.
5. **Send:** use only an authorised channel; respect applicable privacy/anti-spam rules and opt-outs. Sending must be a separate, auditable action.
6. **Record:** append timestamped evidence for actual sends, replies, quotes, acceptance, receipts, delivery, and complaints.
7. **Deliver:** start only after written scope and payment terms are agreed.
8. **Quality-check:** check factual claims, source links, language, scope compliance, and revisions.
9. **Learn:** use anonymised aggregate outcomes to improve templates; do not silently reuse confidential customer content or personal data.
10. **Audit/stop:** stop outreach if source verification, consent/compliance, price approval, payment verification, or record integrity fails.

## Scope boundary

This pilot does **not** build or claim a production global social network, 8.5-billion-user capacity, a proprietary email platform, automatic WhatsApp/email sending, payment processing, a live HeyGen-equivalent avatar, or scientific validation of philosophical percentages. Those are separate product tracks requiring requirements, architecture, budget, privacy/security review, and acceptance tests. The immediate income experiment stays limited to services that can presently be delivered and evidenced.
