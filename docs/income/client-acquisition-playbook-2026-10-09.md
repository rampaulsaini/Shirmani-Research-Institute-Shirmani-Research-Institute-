**Scope:** Hindi/English writing, source-based research summaries, website/blog content

## 1. First revenue lane — sell a service that can be delivered now

Do not wait for the global showroom, AI avatar, social network, or payment automation to be complete. Begin with services already described in the repository and accept only work whose scope can be delivered with available tools.

| Offer | Suggested starting quote (INR) | Included | Must confirm before accepting |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Clear writing / proofreading | ₹499–₹999 | Short copy, rewrite, or proofreading | Word count, source material, one revision |
| Research summary | ₹999–₹2,499 | Structured summary, source links, limitations | Topic, acceptable sources, length, deadline |
| Website/blog content | ₹1,499–₹3,999 | Page or article draft and FAQ if scoped | Audience, length, factual review, revisions |

These are proposed test prices, not researched market averages, guaranteed prices, or confirmed orders. Quote a fixed amount only after clarifying the brief. For a new client, agree in writing on deposit/payment milestones, deliverables, deadline, revision limit, and cancellation terms. Never claim a paid order until payment evidence exists.

## 2. Seven-day measurable execution

| Day | Production action | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare one Hindi and one English writing sample; prepare one source-based research-summary sample | Sample links/files; completion date |
| 2 | Identify 10 relevant organizations through their official websites and published business contact routes | Organization, official URL, contact page, relevance note |
| 3 | Personalize and send up to 5 relevant enquiries manually | Date, channel, exact message version, delivery evidence if available |
| 4 | Identify 5 more relevant prospects; tailor each message | Prospect records and drafts |
| 5 | Follow up once where appropriate; answer real replies | Reply and follow-up log |
| 6 | Prepare scope/quote proposals for qualified leads | Proposal sent, amount proposed, deadline |
| 7 | Review results and adjust offer/message | Sent, replies, qualified leads, proposals, paid orders, money received |

Targets are activity goals, not predictions. Do not send bulk spam, scrape private contact details, bypass opt-outs, or misrepresent an affiliation. Use only relevant official/public business contact channels and respect each recipient's communication preferences.

## 4. Prospect qualification checklist

Before writing a proposal, answer:
- What concrete problem is the client paying to solve?
- Who is the audience and what format is required?
- Which language(s), word count, sources and output files are required?
- What is the deadline and how many revision rounds are included?
- Is any specialist expertise, copyrighted material, confidential data or regulated advice involved?
- What amount and payment schedule have both sides accepted?
- What will count as delivery and client acceptance?

Decline or narrow a task when it needs unsupported claims, plagiarism, deceptive reviews, unauthorized personal data, or specialist advice beyond the service scope.

## 5. Simple funnel and truthful reporting

Record the following separately each week:

1. Prospects researched
2. Relevant enquiries sent
3. Replies received
4. Qualified enquiries
5. Written proposals sent
6. Proposals accepted / orders confirmed
7. Work delivered and accepted
8. Invoice/payment requested
9. Actual money received (INR) with date and evidence
10. Refunds, fees, or unpaid balance

**Conversion formulas**
- Reply rate = replies ÷ enquiries sent × 100
- Qualified-lead rate = qualified enquiries ÷ replies × 100
- Proposal acceptance = accepted proposals ÷ proposals sent × 100
- Realized revenue = payments actually received − refunds (report platform/bank fees separately)

If a denominator is zero, report “N/A”, not 0% or 100%. Do not count GitHub workflow runs, catalog entries, page views, drafts, proposed quotes, or unverified payment screenshots as revenue.

## 6. Product/showroom production is a separate lane

The broader digital-product plan remains staged:
1. **Institute:** research and define one specific customer problem.
2. **Factory:** build a concrete, usable product—not only a name, schema, workflow, or placeholder.
3. **QC / release gate:** test core user tasks, errors, accessibility, privacy, and limitations; issue a product-specific passport and demo.
4. **Showroom / sale:** publish a usable demo, clear price/terms, support route, and real payment/order integration before claiming transactions.

For each product, track separate fields for catalog identity, concrete asset, demo route, product-specific video, visual asset, passport, QC state, published state, order state, payment state, and independent verification state. A generated browser animation is not a rendered product-specific MP4. A QR URL is not proof that a QR image was generated. A listing is not a sale. Do not promote an item as ready for purchase if the deliverable, price, license, support, or payment/fulfilment route is missing.
