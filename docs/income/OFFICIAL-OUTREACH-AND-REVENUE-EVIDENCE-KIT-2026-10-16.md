## Lead qualification rules
Only add a prospect after checking its official website or another reliable official source.
Record only business contact details needed for the enquiry. Do not guess email addresses, scrape personal contact details, or treat a directory listing as verified without checking it.
Personalise the message to a genuine fit; obey applicable laws, channel rules, and opt-outs. Do not send bulk unsolicited messages.
Do not mark an email as sent unless a real sent-message record exists.

## Quote acceptance checklist
- Customer objective, audience, topic and intended use
- Language and word/page limit

- Exclusions and known limitations
- Price, taxes if applicable, and payment/advance terms
- Deadline and time zone
- Included revision count and feedback window
- Written customer acceptance before production begins

## Daily measurable targets (targets, not forecasts)
- Research 4 relevant organisations per working day; record official source and fit reason.
- Prepare up to 5 personalised messages per day only after contact details and channel are verified and sender is authorised.
- Count sent messages only from actual sent records; count replies only from real message threads.
- Track qualified leads, replies, quotes, accepted jobs, delivered work, revision load, hours spent, and cleared payments separately.
- Review after 7 days. Do not infer sales or revenue from visits, drafts, prospects, sent quotes, or verbal interest.

## Evidence states
- VERIFIED: evidence directly inspected and adequate for the specific claim.
- SOURCE-BASED: a source is linked, but independent verification is incomplete.
- PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY: a viewpoint or personal description, not an independently established scientific claim.
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: say so plainly and do not guess.
