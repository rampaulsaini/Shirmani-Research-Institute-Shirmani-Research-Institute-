## 5. Sample deliverable structure
1. Title and decision question
2. Executive summary (plain language)
3. Scope and method (what was and was not checked)
4. Findings table: claim | source | date | what it supports | caveat
5. Points of agreement and disagreement across sources
6. Evidence gaps and uncertainty
7. Practical next steps
8. Source list with working URLs and access dates

Never invent sources, citations, customer outcomes, or scientific certainty. Label illustrative examples as samples, not client work.
