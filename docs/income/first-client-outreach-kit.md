# SHIRMANI RESEARCH INSTITUTE — First Client Outreach Kit

**Purpose:** turn Hindi/English writing and research-summary services into a measurable, honest client-acquisition process.

**Status rule:** this kit is not evidence of a customer, sent email, order, payment, or revenue. Record each only after it actually happens.

## 1. Clearly scoped starter offers

Prices below are **proposed launch prices in INR**, not verified market rates. Confirm scope and price in writing before starting.

| Offer | Deliverable | Proposed price |
|---|---|---:|
| Quick Research Summary | 1,000–1,500 words, source links, key findings and limitations | ₹799 |
| Hindi/English Website or Blog Draft | One 800–1,200-word draft, title, headings, meta description, one revision | ₹999 |
| Bilingual Content Pack | One core article plus Hindi/English adaptation, source notes, one revision | ₹1,799 |
| Custom Research & Writing | Price after reviewing the brief | Quote first |

Do not promise search rankings or guaranteed outcomes. Do not present unsupported claims as facts. Specialist legal, medical, financial, or other high-stakes advice is excluded unless appropriately qualified review is arranged.

## 2. Client enquiry brief

Collect the goal and audience; topic and language; word count and format; required sources; deadline and time zone; budget; confidentiality requirements; and permission for any portfolio use.

## 5. Pipeline tracker fields

One row per real prospect: date added; organization; public contact source; reason for relevance; channel; status (researched / draft ready / sent / replied / brief received / proposal sent / won / lost / opted out); next action/date; proposed value; agreed value; payment received/date; evidence note.

Never mark a message sent unless it was sent through an actual channel.

## 6. Weekly measurable results

Track qualified prospects, personalized messages actually sent, replies, complete briefs, proposals sent, work agreed, deliverables completed, payments received, hours spent, effective hourly earnings, and reasons for lost opportunities.

Keep proposed value, agreed value, invoiced value, and cash received separate. Calculate conversion rates only when the denominator is non-zero.

## 7. Delivery and payment sequence

1. Confirm the brief and scope.
2. Agree deliverables, deadline, revisions, price, and payment schedule in writing.
3. Use a payment method available to both parties; verify receipt in the provider account.
4. Produce the agreed work and cite sources when appropriate.
5. Deliver through the agreed channel.
6. Record feedback and permitted portfolio use.
7. Request an honest review only after real delivery; never fabricate testimonials or ratings.
