## Delivery quality gate
Before sending any paid deliverable, check:
- The result matches the agreed scope and audience.
- Hindi/English grammar, tone, readability and accessibility are reviewed.
- Research claims link to traceable sources; uncertainty and limitations are explicit.
- No fabricated citations, credentials, testimonials, data or client results.
- Originality, client permissions, confidentiality and usage rights are respected.
- Deliverable format, revision count and delivery date match the quote.
- Record client acceptance and payment separately.
