## 3. Minimum client offer
Offer only work currently supportable:
- Hindi/English writing, editing and adaptation;
- source-linked research summaries;
- website/blog content.

Every quote must specify objective and audience; exact deliverable, language and word limit; source/citation expectations and exclusions; deadline, revision count and acceptance criteria; final price/currency, payment schedule and cancellation/refund terms; and permitted use of supplied material and final output.

No work begins until scope and price are accepted in writing and payment/milestone terms are met.

## 4. Lead-to-cash evidence states
| State | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| RESEARCHED | Official website/contact source, date checked, specific fit hypothesis |
| DRAFTED | Saved personalised message tied to a real public page or need |
| APPROVED | Owner approval to contact, where required |
| SENT | Actual sent copy, timestamp and channel |
| REPLIED | Actual received reply |
| QUALIFIED | Complete scope, audience, deadline and budget/quote route |
| QUOTED | Written proposal with deliverables, price and terms |
| ACCEPTED | Client's written acceptance |
| PAID | Payment receipt/processor record reconciled securely |
| DELIVERED | Dated handoff and agreed deliverables |
| ACCEPTED_DELIVERY | Client acceptance or recorded revision request |

A draft is not a sent message. A sent message is not a sale. An accepted quote is not payment. A payment is not proof of accepted delivery. Never store credentials, OTPs or sensitive payment details in public issues or repository files.
