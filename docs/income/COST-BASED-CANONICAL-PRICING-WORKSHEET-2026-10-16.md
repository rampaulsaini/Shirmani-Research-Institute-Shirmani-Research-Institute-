## 3. Costing formula

Use one consistent method for all packages.

- **Direct labour cost** = actual expected hours × approved loaded hourly cost.
- **Base job cost** = direct labour + research/source costs + job-specific software/production cost + allocated overhead + revision allowance + contingency.
- If the payment fee is a percentage of the pre-tax quote and the target contribution margin is expressed as a fraction of that quote, a simplified minimum pre-tax quote is:

  `(Base job cost + fixed payment fee) / (1 - payment fee rate - target contribution margin)`

  This formula is valid only when the denominator is greater than zero and the fee assumptions match the provider's actual fee basis. Recalculate if fees apply to tax-inclusive amounts or use a different basis.
- Add applicable taxes only after the relevant tax treatment is verified. Do not describe a tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive amount ambiguously.
- Compare the computed quote with actual observed delivery time, scope, client demand, and defensible like-for-like market references. A lower price is not automatically competitive if it cannot cover delivery and support.

## 4. Service boundaries to preserve

- Editing: up to 700 words; one edited version; one revision within the original brief.
- Article / web copy: up to 1,000 words; title/headings; one revision within the original brief.
- Research brief: about 800–1,200 words; 3–5 credible sources where available; clearly separate sourced facts, interpretation, and evidence gaps.
- Additional words, extra sources, rush delivery, extra revisions, translation, design, or specialist review require a written custom quote.
- Confirm scope, deliverables, deadline, revision limit, source expectations, payment terms, and applicable tax treatment in writing before work starts.
- Do not accept work beyond demonstrated capacity or promise an untested turnaround.
