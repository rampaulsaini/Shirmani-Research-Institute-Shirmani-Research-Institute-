## Quote and acceptance gate

Before starting, record:

1. Customer and authorised contact
2. Objective, intended audience and use
3. Topic, language and word/deliverable limit
4. Agreed source/citation requirements and exclusions
5. Final price and applicable taxes
6. Delivery date and revision count
7. Payment terms and any agreed advance
8. Customer's written acceptance

## Evidence definitions

- **Prospect researched:** official website and fit reason recorded.
- **Outreach sent:** sent-message record exists. A draft is not outreach.
- **Quote sent:** actual quote copy and timestamp exist.
- **Order accepted:** customer has accepted the scope and terms in writing.
- **Delivered:** agreed files were sent and delivery is recorded.
- **Paid:** receipt or payment-provider evidence confirms funds received. An invoice is not payment.
- **Public sample/testimonial:** permission has been obtained.
