## Immediate offer
**Source-linked research summary or Hindi/English web content.** Deliverables can include:
1. One agreed article, page, or research brief.
2. Source links and a short note separating source claims from independently checked findings.
3. One revision if included in the written quote.
4. A defined word limit, format, deadline, and usage rights.
5. A final handoff link/file.

No invented citations, unearned credentials, guaranteed outcomes, or claims that AI/avatar/live-video functions are available unless independently tested.

## Price and payment control
Until the conflicting site prices are reconciled, quote **after reviewing scope**. Before work starts, both sides must confirm in writing:
- client objective and intended audience;
- topic, language, word count, source expectations and deliverable format;
- deadline and revision count;
- final price and currency;
- payment schedule and refund/cancellation terms;
- permission to use client-provided material and intended usage rights.

For a small fixed-scope pilot, request an agreed advance before starting; for larger jobs use a written agreement or protected milestone. Never pay a fee to receive work, and never request/share OTPs or passwords.
