## 4. Required order-to-payment chain
1. Receive enquiry through an actually working channel.
2. Confirm customer objective, audience, topic, language, word limit, sources, deadline, budget and intended use.
3. Send a written quote with deliverables, exclusions, revision count, deadline, source/citation standard and payment terms.
4. Obtain written customer acceptance and any agreed advance payment before work begins.
5. Produce the draft; verify links, quotations, factual claims and language. Label claims that cannot be adequately supported.
6. Deliver the agreed files and request written acceptance or a consolidated revision list.
7. Record invoice/payment evidence only when actually received; never infer payment from a sent invoice.
8. Ask permission before using the work as a public sample or testimonial.

No order is accepted, no email is sent, and no payment is received merely because a form or local tool generated a brief.
