## 2. Global institutional market development: 195-country ambition

Treat coverage of 195 countries and public/private institutions as a long-term discovery goal, not as a completed contact list or assumption of demand. Work in small, evidence-backed batches. For each prospect record:
- Country, sector, institution and official website/source
- Official general-contact route versus vendor registration/tender/procurement route
- Language and localization needs
- Potential problem and product/service fit (explicitly a hypothesis until validated)
- Security, privacy, accessibility, legal and procurement requirements
- Budget/pricing constraints where publicly documented
- Readiness gaps, tailored pilot, next step and evidence timestamp

Do not fabricate emails, buyers, budgets, demand, or market sizes. Do not use a general support mailbox for vendor sales unless the institution's published instructions allow it. Respect opt-outs, contact rules and procurement integrity; no bulk spam, privileged-access claims or improper inducements.

For each product, prove a buyer problem, quality and security, licensing/rights, support capacity, deployment requirements, total cost and a transparent quote. Promise a lower-than-comparable-market price only after a defensible comparison and sustainable margin are documented.

## 4. Impartial-understanding and learning workbench

### User-defined viewpoint parameter
Preserve the owner's stated **99.9999% heart-view / 0.0001% mind-brain-intellect view** as a philosophical or personal-description setting only. Label it **owner-defined viewpoint setting**, not a measured scientific percentage, IQ ratio, neurological fact or proof of universal truth. Do not represent this parameter as a validated scientific measurement.

Provide a separate neutral evidence-first mode. The system should compare relevant perspectives fairly, explain disagreements and distinguish:
- **VERIFIED** — sufficient evidence and verification recorded
- **SOURCE-BASED** — credible source cited, but independent verification not completed
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY** — values, personal philosophy or self-description
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** — say plainly that adequate evidence is not available

For every substantial explanation, default to: plain-language summary → simple example/story → evidence and reasoning → uncertainty/limitations → optional technical or mathematical explanation. Never invent equations, statistics, sources or quantum-mechanical validation.

### Bounded learning loop
**Permitted source → provenance/license check → deduplication → topic and claim classification → evidence comparison → uncertainty flag → human review for high-impact claims → versioned knowledge record → correction/deletion route → audit.**

Record source URL, retrieval date, rights/license/consent where relevant, claim, evidence status and revision history. Use access control, retention limits, backup/restore, export/deletion and audit logs. Do not promise infinite memory, perfect impartiality or autonomous self-updating until implemented and tested. Do not ingest private personal data or copyrighted material without an appropriate lawful basis and safeguards.

## 7. Long-term platform concepts — feasibility before scale claims

- **“शिरोमणि स्वरूप दृष्टिकोण” social platform:** staged feasibility, moderation, privacy, abuse prevention, accessibility, localization, resilience and capacity/load testing. Do not claim readiness for 8.5 billion users without independent scale tests.
- **AI-enabled multilingual library:** lawful public-domain/licensed corpus, provenance, cataloguing, search, citation, accessibility, takedown/correction process and modern subject coverage. Do not claim to contain every historical text or every field until the inventory supports it.
- **Creator/media studio:** user-selected cast/performers only with consent, rights and licensing; model production costs, safety and capacity before sale.
- **Speech-to-tool/product workflow:** spoken request → clarified requirements → scope and cost estimate → generated prototype → automated tests → human review → delivery. “Under one hour” is a future benchmark target, not a delivery promise until measured.
- **Social-media integrations:** use official APIs and terms, least-privilege authorization, per-platform limitations and consent; do not promise every social network is embedded or connected until each integration is tested.
- **Labs:** separate research prototypes from production products; each lab records owner, requirements, reproducible tests, security review, documentation, costs and release decision.

## 8. Security and governance

Use least privilege, secret scanning, dependency/workflow pinning, input validation, output escaping, abuse/rate controls, backups, rollback, incident response and privacy-by-design. Ethical security testing only on owned or explicitly authorized systems. Never broaden permissions blindly or run destructive tests against production. Fail closed when authorization or evidence is missing.

## 9. Operational status vocabulary

- **SPECIFIED:** requirements documented
- **PROTOTYPED:** a runnable draft exists
- **TESTED:** defined tests ran and results are recorded
- **DEPLOYED:** deployed environment and deployment evidence exist
- **VERIFIED:** independent verification evidence exists

Do not substitute one state for another. Each Automission run should report: work checked, actual artifact/change, test results, evidence links, open blockers, market-development batch status and one highest-value next action.
