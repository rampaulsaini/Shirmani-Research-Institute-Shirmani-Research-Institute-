## One measurable loop
`OBSERVE → CLASSIFY → CHECK SOURCES → REASON → DRAFT → HUMAN APPROVAL → SEND/DELIVER → MEASURE → AUDIT → IMPROVE`

Automission may gather evidence, draft copy, run non-destructive checks, calculate metrics, and propose improvements. It must not autonomously invent prospects or credentials, send bulk unsolicited messages, accept contractual terms, claim revenue, change prices, publish unreviewed factual claims, expose private client data, or bypass required human approval.

## Evidence labels
- **VERIFIED:** direct, relevant evidence was checked and recorded.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** a named source supports the statement, but independent verification was not completed.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** a person's own stated philosophy or self-description; do not present as a scientific finding.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** missing, conflicting, stale, or inconclusive evidence. Hold the claim or ask for more information.

Every result record should include the claim/task, evidence URL or artifact, timestamp, test method, outcome, limitations, reviewer (where required), and next action. A successful workflow run proves only that the workflow completed—not that a claim is true or a product works end-to-end.

## Heart-View / impartial-understanding parameter
Treat “heart-view” as a **design and evaluation framework**, not a scientifically established percentage or a claim about a person's objective superiority.

Score each reviewed output from 0–5 on:
1. Evidence fidelity and source traceability
2. Fair representation of differing perspectives
3. Clear separation of fact, inference, opinion, and uncertainty
4. Plain-language accessibility
5. Respect, non-manipulation, and consent
6. Practical usefulness and harm reduction
7. Reproducibility and auditability

Report each score, reviewer rationale, sample size, and disagreements. Do not convert the user's proposed 99.9999% / 0.0001% figures into measured facts without a valid, independently validated measurement method. No score overrides safety, consent, source quality, or human review.

## Revenue state machine
`UNQUALIFIED → QUALIFIED → DRAFT_READY → HUMAN_APPROVED → SENT → REPLIED → ENQUIRY_COMPLETE → QUOTED → ACCEPTED → PAYMENT_CONFIRMED → IN_PROGRESS → QC_PASSED → DELIVERED → CLOSED`

- Every transition requires a timestamp and evidence appropriate to that state.
- `DRAFT_READY` is not `SENT`; `SENT` is not a sale.
- A quote is not revenue. Count revenue only when payment is actually received and cleared.
- Do not store bank details, payment credentials, passwords, or OTPs in public issues or repository files.
- Respect opt-outs and applicable contact rules; avoid bulk scraping or repetitive unwanted follow-ups.
- If pricing is inconsistent across pages, **HOLD public price promotion** and quote only after written scope review until the canonical menu is reconciled.
