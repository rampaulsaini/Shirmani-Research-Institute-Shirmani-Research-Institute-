# Signal-to-Language Evidence Contract

The platform may receive measurable signals from biological, environmental,
mechanical, or other systems and convert patterns into plain language.

Every result must distinguish:
1. Measurement — what was observed.
2. Feature — numerical pattern extracted from the observation.
3. Model inference — what a model associates with that pattern.
4. Plain-language interpretation — an accessible description.
5. Confidence — model confidence, not a guarantee of truth.
6. Uncertainty — what the available evidence cannot establish.

Electrical, acoustic, optical, chemical, movement, temperature, vibration, or other
measurable signals can be analyzed when suitable sensors and validated datasets exist.

A signal pattern must not automatically be described as subjective feeling, emotion,
intention, or consciousness. Such claims require independent validation appropriate
to the scientific question.

Automission gate:
Observe → Validate → Extract → Model → Explain → Verify → Audit

A failed validation or verification step must prevent an interpretation from being
promoted to a verified claim.
