# Yatharth Public Platform — Public Interface Contract

## Purpose

Define the public-facing product surface for a global, multilingual, AI-native social, knowledge, education, creative, marketplace and research platform.

## Core user capabilities

A registered user may, subject to platform rules and applicable law:

- create and manage a profile;
- publish text, images, audio, music and video;
- follow, discover, search, save and share content;
- create communities and participate in discussions;
- offer services and freelancing work;
- publish courses and educational resources;
- list permitted digital products in the Digital Store;
- create creator pages and portfolios;
- use supported AI tools for creation, translation, research and productivity;
- purchase permitted products/services;
- review transactions and request support or dispute resolution;
- export or delete their own eligible data.

## Platform principles

1. User value and satisfaction over engagement manipulation.
2. Safety, privacy, accessibility and transparency by design.
3. Author-source material is clearly separated from independent evidence.
4. AI-generated material is labeled where appropriate.
5. Recommendations must not silently become political, financial, medical or legal decisions.
6. Users receive meaningful controls, appeals and dispute pathways.
7. The platform must not promise capabilities that are not deployed.

## Public status labels

Every major capability should expose one of:

- PLANNED
- IN DEVELOPMENT
- TESTING
- LIMITED LIVE
- LIVE
- AUTOMATED
- HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED
- PAUSED

A feature is not considered LIVE merely because its documentation or GitHub workflow exists.

## Economic surfaces

The public platform may contain Digital Store, Freelancing, Jobs, creator monetization, subscriptions, permitted digital services and other lawful commerce. Payment, tax, consumer-protection, KYC/AML and marketplace obligations must be implemented according to the jurisdictions in which the service operates.

## Yatharth Currency

“Yatharth Currency” is treated as a user-defined research/product concept until a lawful, technically implemented monetary system exists. It must not be represented as legal tender or a functioning payment instrument without the required legal, financial and technical infrastructure.

## Yatharth Justice

“Yatharth Justice” is a public research and governance concept emphasizing evidence, fairness, transparency, due process, accountability and appeal. AI may assist with intake, classification, evidence organization and procedural support; final high-impact legal decisions must remain subject to appropriate human/legal authority.

## Basic-needs vision

The author's stated vision includes broad access to food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. This is recorded as a normative/public-policy objective, not as an already achieved condition.

## Scale objective

The author's long-term aspiration is a global user/consumer reach target of 850 crore (8.5 billion). This is a target, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome.

## Accessibility

Design targets include mobile-first delivery, low-bandwidth operation, multilingual access, readable interfaces, assistive-technology compatibility and progressive enhancement.

## Acceptance rule

No public page may describe a planned, simulated, documented or workflow-tested capability as fully deployed.
