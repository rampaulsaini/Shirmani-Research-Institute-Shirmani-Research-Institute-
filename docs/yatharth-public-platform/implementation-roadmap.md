# Yatharth Global Platform — Implementation Roadmap

## North-star architecture

Build one public ecosystem with shared identity, search, content, trust, commerce and AI infrastructure while keeping research verification and high-impact governance independently auditable.

## Phase A — Foundation

- Public information architecture
- Account/profile model
- Content object model
- Search/index contract
- multilingual content model
- privacy and consent model
- moderation and appeals contract
- audit/event schema
- public capability-status registry

## Phase B — Social platform

- registration/login
- profiles and creator pages
- posts and media
- comments/reactions/sharing
- follow/feed/discovery
- communities
- notifications
- reporting and appeals

## Phase C — Knowledge and education

- Yatharth knowledge hub
- research library
- claim/evidence explorer
- courses and learning paths
- creator/teacher publishing
- certificates and learning records

## Phase D — Creative platform

- Yatharth AI Music
- audio/podcast
- video
- creator studio
- multilingual creation
- digital media library

## Phase E — Economic platform

## Phase F — Governance and protection

- Yatharth Justice research layer

- low-bandwidth delivery

- independent audits
- staged geographic/product rollout

## Success measures

Measure product dimensions separately:

- active users and retention;
- creator participation;
- successful transactions;
- customer satisfaction and complaint resolution;
- learning outcomes;
- research evidence coverage;
- independent verification;
- safety incidents;
- system reliability;
- automation health;
- accessibility;
- environmental impact where measurable.

Do not use engagement time alone as the optimization target.

## Current-state rule

Existing GitHub Actions, PRs, workflow runs and documentation are evidence of engineering activity. They do not by themselves establish that the entire public platform is deployed or that its claims are independently verified.

## Vision boundary

The author's broader vision—humanity, nature and Earth protection, balanced cognition and heart-centered living, broad access to basic needs, and a globally useful Yatharth ecosystem—is preserved as the project's stated vision. Implementation claims must remain tied to measurable deployed capabilities.
