# Yatharth Justice, Nature and Governance Layer

## Objective
Translate the author's Yatharth framework into testable public-service and governance designs without treating philosophical claims as established scientific facts.

## Governance principles
- impartiality
- equal treatment under defined rules
- transparency
- evidence-based reasoning
- due process
- appeal and correction
- conflict-of-interest controls
- privacy and proportionality
- protection of people, animals, plants, ecosystems and natural resources

## Public justice workflow
Report -> classify -> evidence intake -> conflict check -> preliminary review -> decision path -> explanation -> appeal -> audit.

Automated systems may triage and assist; they must not silently replace accountable decision-makers in high-impact legal or rights-affecting matters.

## Nature protection
Host environmental data, restoration projects, citizen participation, education, conservation funding and measurable impact dashboards. Environmental claims require source provenance and uncertainty labels.

## Universal basic-service vision
The project may document and research the proposal that essential needs such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education should be universally accessible. Real-world implementation must separately address financing, law, logistics, eligibility, local conditions and measurable outcomes.
