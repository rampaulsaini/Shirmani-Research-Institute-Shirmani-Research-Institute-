# Yatharth Global Platform — Master Integration Contract

## Purpose
This document is the integration contract for the public Yatharth platform and its internal AI/ML/NLP/Automission operating layers.

The architecture must distinguish **target architecture** from **deployed capability**. A module is not considered live merely because a workflow, documentation file, CI check, or automation run exists.

## Public experience
The public platform is intended to provide one coherent account-based ecosystem for:
- Social publishing and community participation
- Research and knowledge
- Education and skills
- AI/ML/NLP services
- Yatharth AI Music and creative media
- Digital products and store
- Freelancing, employment and services
- Business and creator economy
- Economic services and the proposed Yatharth Currency concept
- Yatharth Justice, trust, complaints, appeals and dispute processes
- Nature, Earth and humanity protection initiatives
- Multilingual access
- Personal dashboards, search, discovery and support

Users may create accounts, publish permitted content, offer permitted products/services and participate in communities, subject to applicable law, platform rules, safety controls and appeal mechanisms.

## Agent responsibilities
AI agents may assist with intake, classification, translation, search, recommendations, moderation signals, fraud/spam detection, customer support, research retrieval, evidence mapping, workflow routing, quality checks and operational recovery.

Agents must not silently convert an author's statement into independently verified fact.

High-impact actions require explicit policy gates, audit trails and appropriate human review. Examples include permanent account actions, major financial actions, legal/justice outcomes, disputes and independent verification.

## Evidence state machine
**Author Source → Normalized Claim → Evidence Mapped → Comparative Review → Independent Review → Verified / Partially Supported / Inconclusive / Not Verified**

Automation success is operational evidence only; it is not scientific or human verification.

## Human–Earth service objective
The platform may encode the author's stated objective of improving protection of people, animals, plants, Earth and natural systems as a design objective.

Basic-needs proposals such as food, shelter, healthcare and education must be treated as policy/economic proposals requiring feasibility, legal, fiscal and independent evaluation rather than as automatically established outcomes.

## Satisfaction and value
The product should optimize for durable user value, safety, accessibility, usefulness, trust, fair exchange and satisfaction—not merely maximum time spent.

Recommended public metrics include user satisfaction, resolution time, safety incidents, appeal outcomes, service reliability, content quality signals, marketplace fulfillment, education completion, creator/freelancer outcomes, research verification state and measurable environmental indicators.

## Global-scale target
The stated long-term aspiration of reaching 850 crore users/consumers may be retained as a **vision target**. It must not be presented as a current user count, forecast, guarantee or achieved milestone.

Global-scale design should prioritize multilingual operation, accessibility, low-bandwidth support, privacy, security, interoperability and regional legal compliance.

## Definition of completion
A capability is **LIVE** only when its user-facing implementation exists, its data/storage and security path are configured, automated tests pass, monitoring exists, and the relevant production path is demonstrably usable.

A capability is **AUTOMATED** only when the operational path is actually executed by the intended automation.

A capability is **VERIFIED** only when its defined independent evidence contract has been satisfied.

Status labels:
- PLANNED
- DESIGNED
- BUILT
- TESTED
- LIVE
- AUTOMATED
- INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED
- VERIFIED

These states must never be collapsed into one percentage.

## Integration rule
All future source material supplied by the author should be ingested through the source-preservation and claim-lifecycle system, then mapped to the appropriate public module and internal automation layer without erasing provenance or uncertainty.

The platform should grow by **integration and reuse**, not by creating redundant parallel architectures for the same capability.
