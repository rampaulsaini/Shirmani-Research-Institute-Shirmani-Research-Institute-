# Universal Welfare and Protection Framework

## Vision
The Yatharth framework proposes a human-and-nature-centered direction in which technology is designed to improve protection, access, dignity, learning and sustainable living.

## Public service areas
- food/basic-needs information and assistance pathways
- clothing and shelter resources
- healthcare information and service navigation
- education and open learning
- employment and skills
- environmental protection
- forest, ocean and biodiversity protection
- accessibility and inclusion
- community support
- emergency and crisis-resource navigation

## Principle
These are platform goals and design proposals, not claims that the platform currently provides universal free provision.

## Ecological layer
Measure and promote resource efficiency, environmental impact, restoration, conservation, responsible consumption, biodiversity protection and evidence-based environmental information.

## Family-to-global scope
The proposed model can be mapped from person -> family -> community -> city -> region -> country -> global ecosystem, while lawful public institutions retain their legal authority and individual rights remain protected.

## Justice and fairness
Yatharth Justice is a proposed research/service layer for transparent rules, evidence, complaint handling, mediation and appeal pathways, and accountable decision support. It is not a claim of authority to replace courts or constitutional/legal institutions.
