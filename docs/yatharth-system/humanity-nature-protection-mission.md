# Humanity, Nature and Earth Protection Mission

The Yatharth framework describes a goal of balancing human cognition, lived experience and care for the wider living and physical environment. This repository records those statements as the author's framework; it does not present metaphysical or civilizational claims as independently established scientific facts.

## Practical public objectives
- reduce avoidable environmental harm
- promote conservation of forests, water, oceans, biodiversity and clean air
- improve access to basic services through lawful, sustainable programmes
- support education, health access, food, clothing and housing initiatives where resources and public policy permit
- make environmental and social indicators visible through transparent dashboards
- connect creators, researchers, educators, service providers and communities around measurable projects

## Measurement
Environmental and social outcomes must be measured with defined indicators, periods, sources and uncertainty. Claims of improvement require evidence rather than aspiration alone.
