# Yatharth Global Operating Model

## Purpose
Define the public-facing and autonomous operating model while separating author source, normalized claims, evidence, independent verification, deployment status, and product promises.

## Public ecosystem
Accounts, profiles, social publishing, creator tools, education, research, digital products, music/media, freelancing, employment, services, community, marketplace functions, and multilingual access.

## Core loop
Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Trust -> Share -> Buy/Sell -> Deliver -> Review -> Learn -> Improve.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission
Intake; NLP understanding; semantic search; recommendations; creator assistance; education assistance; research/evidence assistance; marketplace matching; safety/fraud detection; provenance and quality checks; translation/accessibility; customer support; workflow orchestration; federation; continuous audit and recovery.

## Human-control boundary
Routine operations may be automated. Account termination, major financial actions, disputes, appeals, legal/compliance decisions, and independent verification retain appropriate human review and appeal paths.

## Long-term vision
The author's stated aspiration of 850 crore consumers/users is a target, not a current count or guaranteed outcome. Universal access to essential services is a vision-level objective unless actually implemented and independently evaluated.

## Status
PLANNED | DESIGNED | IMPLEMENTED | TESTED | LIVE | AUTOMATED | HUMAN_REVIEW_REQUIRED | INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED

Workflow success does not establish product deployment or independent verification.
