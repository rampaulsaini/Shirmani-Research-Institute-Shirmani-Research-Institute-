# Yatharth Civilization Operating Model

## Purpose
Translate the author's stated Yatharth vision into a public-platform operating model. This is an architecture proposal, not a claim that the proposed social, economic, justice, currency, or planetary outcomes already exist.

## Core separation
1. Author Source — experience, language and philosophy.
2. Normalized Model — software-ready interpretation.
3. Evidence — independent empirical, legal, scientific or historical material.
4. Verification — independent review under a defined evidence contract.
5. Live Capability — actually deployed, tested and available.

## Family-to-planet scope
Family → Community → City → Region → Country → Global public commons → Earth/biosphere → wider scientific/space context.

Public domains:
- Social participation
- Research and knowledge
- Education and skills
- Employment and freelancing
- Digital commerce
- Creator economy
- Yatharth AI Music and media
- Nature/Earth protection
- Justice, fairness, complaints and appeals
- Trust and independent verification
- AI/ML/NLP assistance
- Automated operations and continuous audit

## Basic-needs capability map
The public registry may expose pathways for food/nutrition, clothing/essential goods, housing assistance, healthcare navigation, education, employment/skills, digital access and emergency/help resources.

The platform must not claim that services are universally free unless real funding, provider, legal and operational mechanisms exist.

## Human dignity and satisfaction
Optimize for useful value and satisfaction rather than merely screen time. Measure task completion, quality, accessibility, affordability, safety, complaint resolution, creator/freelancer outcomes and measurable environmental impact.

## Planetary protection
Support biodiversity/habitat information, forest and water stewardship, pollution reporting, climate/environment education, conservation projects, responsible consumption and verified local action. Environmental claims require evidence and geographic/time context.

## Governance boundary
AI may automate routine operations, but high-impact decisions require notice/explanation where appropriate, human review, appeal, audit logs, conflict-of-interest controls, legal compliance and fail-closed behavior. The platform does not replace constitutional governments, courts, elected institutions or lawful public authorities.

## Capability lifecycle
PROPOSED → SPECIFIED → BUILT → TESTED → PILOT → LIVE → MONITORED

Documentation or a successful GitHub workflow never by itself makes a capability LIVE.
