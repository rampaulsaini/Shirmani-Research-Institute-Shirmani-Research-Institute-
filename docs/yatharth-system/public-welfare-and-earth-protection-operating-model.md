# Public Welfare and Earth Protection Operating Model

## Scope
Connect individual wellbeing with family, community, nation, Earth, biodiversity and the wider physical environment while keeping measurable outcomes distinct from philosophical interpretation.

## Service layers
### Basic human needs
- food access
- clothing and essential goods
- safe housing pathways
- healthcare navigation
- education and skills
- clean water and sanitation
- accessibility
- emergency support routing

### Economic participation
- jobs
- freelancing
- creator income
- digital products
- ethical marketplace participation
- education-to-employment pathways

### Environmental protection
- forest protection
- biodiversity
- water and ocean protection
- pollution monitoring
- waste reduction
- sustainable resource use
- climate and ecological indicators

## AI/ML/NLP functions
Use AI for classification, forecasting, matching, anomaly detection, multilingual access, document analysis and workflow automation. Model outputs remain probabilistic and require validation appropriate to their consequences.

## Continuous improvement loop
Observe → measure → identify harm/gap → propose intervention → test → independently evaluate → deploy cautiously → monitor → rollback when required.

## Human agency
The system should increase people's ability to access information, opportunities and services rather than manipulate them into excessive engagement. Engagement metrics must never override safety, autonomy or wellbeing.
