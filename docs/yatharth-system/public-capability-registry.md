# Public Capability Registry

A single public capability registry should tell users what exists, what is being built, and what has independent verification.

## Domains

### Social
Accounts, profiles, posts, follows, messaging, communities, search, discovery, accessibility and multilingual publishing.

### Research
Canonical source, claims, evidence, counter-evidence, comparative research, research packets, independent review and public verification.

### Education
Courses, learning paths, skills, AI/ML/NLP learning, research education, assessments, certificates and open resources.

### Work and income
Employment, freelancing, services, portfolios, client matching, digital work products, creator income and research-as-a-service.

### Commerce
Digital store, listings, payments integration, order lifecycle, licensing, subscriptions, refunds, reviews and buyer/seller support.

### Creative
Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast, video, media publishing, creator studio, multilingual transformation and licensing.

### Economy
Economic education, creator economy, marketplace analytics and the author-defined Yatharth Currency concept. Any currency feature requires precise specification, legal review, security controls and a real settlement mechanism before live financial use.

### Justice and trust
Fairness principles, complaints, disputes, appeals, evidence presentation, moderation review, transparency and independent review. The platform does not impersonate a court.

### Nature and humanity
Conservation information, environmental reporting, projects, volunteering, resource stewardship and measurable impact.

### AI and Automission
AI assistant, NLP understanding, semantic indexing, recommendation, matching, translation, safety detection, fraud signals, research assistance, workflow orchestration, monitoring and recovery.

## Status vocabulary
CONCEPT · SPECIFIED · IN_DEVELOPMENT · TESTING · PILOT · LIVE · RESTRICTED · DEPRECATED

Research verification status remains separate. A workflow SUCCESS does not make a research claim VERIFIED.

## Account roles
Consumer · Creator · Learner · Educator · Researcher · Freelancer · Client · Seller · Buyer · Reviewer · Community Participant

One account may hold multiple roles subject to identity, safety, age, legal and platform requirements.

## Public trust contract
Where applicable expose responsibility, offer/terms, price, provenance/evidence, review signals, complaint route and refund/dispute route, plus whether an important decision was automated or human-reviewed.
