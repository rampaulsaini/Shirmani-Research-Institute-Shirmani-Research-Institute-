# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Ecosystem Architecture

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem as a unified social, knowledge, creative, education, commerce, employment, research and community platform.

## Public pillars
1. Social & profiles — accounts, profiles, follows, communities, posts, comments, sharing and discovery.
2. Creator economy — creators can publish, offer services and sell permitted digital products.
3. Research — Yatharth Research Institute, source archive, claim registry, evidence, comparison and public verification.
4. Education — courses, learning paths, skills, research education, AI/ML/NLP learning and certificates.
5. Creative — Yatharth AI Music, audio, video, podcast, live, books and creative studio.
6. Work — employment, freelancing, service listings, client discovery and research-as-a-service.
7. Commerce — digital store, products, courses, subscriptions, licensing and creator sales.
8. Economy — Yatharth economic concepts and any future currency/payment concepts, subject to legal and financial review.
9. Justice & trust — fairness, complaints, disputes, transparency, accountability, appeals and independent review.
10. Nature & humanity — practical protection, sustainability, conservation and community participation.
11. Global access — multilingual, accessible, mobile-first and low-bandwidth public experience.
12. Public transparency — system status, verification status, audit reports, version history and change logs.

## Account model
Every participating user may have a profile and, subject to platform rules and applicable law, may:
- create and share content;
- follow and communicate with other users;
- create communities;
- offer services;
- publish courses;
- list permitted digital products;
- sell permitted products/services;
- receive reviews and respond to them;
- manage earnings and transactions through supported payment providers.

## Value loop
Create → Publish → Discover → Learn/Interact → Trust → Buy/Book → Deliver → Review → Improve.

The platform should optimize for user value, safety, accessibility and satisfaction rather than engagement time alone.

## AI-native operating layer
The public platform is supported by:
- NLP understanding and classification;
- semantic search and retrieval;
- recommendation and discovery;
- multilingual translation;
- content quality assistance;
- fraud/spam/abuse detection;
- marketplace matching;
- customer-support automation;
- research agents;
- education agents;
- creative assistants;
- continuous monitoring and Automission orchestration.

## Human oversight
Routine operations may be highly automated. High-impact actions such as major financial decisions, account termination, serious disputes, justice-related decisions and independent verification require defined human review, appeals and audit trails.

## Non-claims
Architecture, workflow success, automation coverage or generated records do not establish independent scientific verification of Yatharth claims. Verification remains evidence-gated and fail-closed.
