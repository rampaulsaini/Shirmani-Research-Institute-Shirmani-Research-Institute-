# Humanity and Nature Protection Charter

## Vision
The Yatharth framework identifies protection of people, living beings, vegetation, oceans, forests, Earth systems and the broader physical environment as a major public-interest objective. The author's philosophical interpretation remains author source; factual environmental/social claims require independent evidence.

## Design requirements
Measure environmental impact where possible; monitor resource use; make accessibility and essential-service objectives explicit; provide reporting/escalation for harmful use; avoid intentionally optimizing harmful engagement; distinguish aspiration, implementation, and measured outcomes.

## Essential-services vision
The author's vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. This remains a vision/policy proposition unless implemented and independently evaluated.

## Governance
No individual automatically receives “supreme control”. High-impact governance should be constrained by documented rules, lawful authority, auditability, appeals, and independent oversight.

## Public indicators
Environmental initiatives, resource consumption, accessibility coverage, safety reports/resolution time, customer satisfaction, dispute outcomes, education access, creator/freelancer participation, and independently verified social-impact outcomes.
