# Humanity, Earth and Living Systems Framework

## Purpose
Translate the Yatharth framework's stated aim of balancing human life, nature and the wider physical world into measurable platform programs.

## Scope
- forests and biodiversity;
- oceans and freshwater;
- air and pollution;
- soil and food systems;
- climate and resilience;
- wildlife and vegetation;
- sustainable housing and infrastructure;
- health and education access;
- livelihoods and social support;
- scientific and civic knowledge.

## Principle
The platform can promote the user's stated vision of moving from a narrowly self-preservation-centered viewpoint toward a broader, evidence-aware perspective that considers people, other living systems and the physical environment together.

This is a normative framework proposal, not an established scientific conclusion.

## Program model
Observe -> Measure -> Verify -> Prioritize -> Act -> Audit -> Publish results.

## Basic-needs objective
The platform may host projects supporting access to food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. Availability, funding and delivery must be represented according to actual verified programs and applicable law; the platform must not promise universal free provision unless it has the resources and legal mechanism to deliver it.

## Environmental evidence
Each project should record:
- baseline;
- target;
- location or scope;
- responsible organization;
- resources;
- measurable output;
- independent evidence where feasible;
- current status;
- audit history.

## Public dashboard
Show project counts, measurable outputs, unresolved risks, evidence status and funding/delivery information without implying that a dashboard metric alone proves real-world impact.
