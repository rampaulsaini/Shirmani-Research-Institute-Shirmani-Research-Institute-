# Yatharth Civilization Balance Framework

## Scope

This document records the author's proposed civilizational objective in a form that can be researched, tested, debated, and implemented through lawful, evidence-based systems.

## Author-proposed objective

The framework seeks a shift from a narrowly self-preservation-oriented perspective toward a broader perspective that considers human dignity, social cooperation, nature, Earth systems, other living beings, and long-term planetary continuity.

The author's language about a "heart viewpoint", "Shirmani form", "Yatharth Yug", and continuity of complete satisfaction is treated as an **author-defined philosophical/experiential framework**, not as independently established scientific fact.

## Balance model

The platform should evaluate proposed policies, products, or system changes across multiple dimensions:

1. Human dignity and basic needs
2. Physical and mental safety without speculative diagnosis
3. Education and capability
4. Health and essential services
5. Food, clothing, shelter, and accessibility
6. Economic opportunity and fair participation
7. Environmental and ecological impact
8. Intergenerational impact
9. Treatment of animals and other living systems
10. Transparency and accountability
11. Freedom, privacy, and informed choice
12. Evidence quality and uncertainty
13. Distributional effects
14. Reversibility and unintended consequences

No single dimension is allowed to silently override all others.

## Basic-needs principle

The platform may research and support models intended to expand access to essential needs such as food, shelter, healthcare, and education. Claims that every such service can be universally free require a defined funding model, legal feasibility analysis, operational capacity, and evidence.

## Nature protection

Environmental objectives should be operationalized with measurable indicators such as:

- habitat and forest protection;
- air and water quality;
- waste and pollution reduction;
- biodiversity;
- soil health;
- resource efficiency;
- climate-relevant emissions;
- restoration activity.

## Decision architecture

A public-facing recommendation or governance proposal should expose:

**objective → evidence → assumptions → alternatives → trade-offs → uncertainty → affected groups → review path**

The system must not disguise value judgments as objective scientific conclusions.

## Political neutrality

Government offices, legislatures, courts, public institutions, and political actors may be represented as data and governance entities. The platform must not automatically endorse a party, candidate, government, or political outcome.

For public-policy analysis, the system should present documented positions, evidence, effects, uncertainty, and competing views while leaving political choices to people and lawful institutions.

## Justice architecture

"Yatharth Justice" is a proposed platform concept, not a replacement for courts or lawful authorities.

It can provide:

- evidence organization;
- complaint intake;
- procedural guidance;
- mediation support where lawful;
- transparency dashboards;
- case-status information;
- appeal and human-review pathways;
- research into fairness and access to justice.

It must not impersonate a court or autonomously determine criminal guilt, legal liability, or other high-impact legal outcomes.
