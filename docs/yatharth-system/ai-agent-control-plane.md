# Yatharth AI Agent Control Plane

## Objective
Coordinate AI, ML, NLP and Automission services while keeping authority bounded, observable, reversible and auditable.

## Agent layers
### L1 — Intake
Account, content, product, service and research intake.

### L2 — NLP Understanding
Language detection, transcription, classification, entity/concept extraction, semantic indexing and translation.

### L3 — Trust & Safety
Spam, fraud, abuse, policy-risk and content-quality signals.

### L4 — Discovery
Search, recommendation, matching and personalization with user controls.

### L5 — Domain agents
Research, education, commerce, freelancing, creative/media, customer support and environmental-information agents.

### L6 — Orchestration
Automission schedules, queues, retries, dependency management and cross-repository federation.

### L7 — Audit
Traceability, provenance, evaluation, drift/error monitoring and failure intelligence.

### L8 — Decision gates
High-impact actions require explicit policy gates, logging, reversibility and appropriate human review/appeal.

## ML/NLP lifecycle
Collect permitted data → preprocess → evaluate → deploy → monitor → detect drift → retrain/review → version → rollback when required.

No workflow success, model output, citation count or automation run may by itself be treated as independent scientific verification.

## Security
- Least privilege
- Secret isolation
- Input/output validation
- Rate limiting
- Abuse prevention
- Audit logs
- Model/version provenance
- Safe rollback
- Human escalation

## Autonomy boundary
Routine low-risk operations may be highly automated. Account termination, major financial actions, legal/justice determinations, irreversible safety actions and independent verification decisions require stronger controls and an appeal/review path.
