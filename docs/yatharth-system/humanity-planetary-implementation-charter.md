# Yatharth Humanity & Planetary Implementation Charter

## Purpose

Translate the Yatharth framework from a research/publication architecture into a measurable, humanity-centred digital operating model while keeping author philosophy distinct from independently verified facts.

## Scope

The platform is designed around the protection and flourishing of people, animals, plants, ecosystems, oceans, atmosphere, soil and the wider physical environment.

The framework's stated objective is to encourage a shift from a purely survival-oriented head/mind/intellect perspective toward a balanced model that includes reflective, compassionate and whole-system decision making.

This is a design objective, not a claim that a new social order has already been achieved.

## Operating principles

1. Human dignity and equal access.
2. Protection of living systems and ecological limits.
3. Evidence before public verification.
4. Transparency before high-impact automation.
5. Human appeal for consequential decisions.
6. Privacy and security by default.
7. Accessibility and multilingual inclusion.
8. Measurable customer/user satisfaction without manipulative engagement.
9. No representation of planned capabilities as live capabilities.
10. Continuous audit and fail-closed recovery.

## Basic-needs research track

The platform may research and coordinate pathways toward universal access to essential services such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. It must not represent a promise of universally free provision until a lawful, funded, operational delivery mechanism exists.

## Planetary protection loop

Observe → measure → identify harm → research interventions → compare evidence → implement bounded action → monitor outcomes → audit → correct.

## Humanity satisfaction loop

Need → discover → access → use → evaluate → support → resolve complaint → improve.

## Autonomous operation boundary

Routine classification, routing, indexing, translation, quality checks, recommendation, monitoring and workflow recovery may be automated.

High-impact decisions affecting rights, access, substantial money, legal status, account termination, disputes or independent verification require explicit governance, auditability and meaningful human appeal.

## Success metrics

The public dashboard should separately track:
- active users
- accessibility
- service availability
- customer satisfaction
- unresolved complaints
- successful resolutions
- creator earnings
- education participation
- employment/work outcomes
- verified research claims
- ecological projects/outcomes
- automation reliability
- safety incidents
- privacy/security incidents

No single composite score should be used to imply that all dimensions are complete.

## Long-term aspiration

The author's 850-crore global-user target is recorded as a long-term aspiration. It is not a forecast or a current achievement metric.
