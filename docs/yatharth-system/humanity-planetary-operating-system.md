# Yatharth Humanity & Planetary Operating System

## Purpose
This module defines a public-facing and machine-readable architecture for applying the user's Yatharth framework as a proposal for improving human, ecological, and planetary outcomes.

The repository distinguishes Author Source, Normalized Design, Evidence, and Independent Verification. It does not claim that the proposed social, economic, justice, currency, or planetary system is already implemented in the physical world.

## Core objective
Design a system that seeks to balance human dignity, individual freedom, evidence-based decision making, ecological protection, long-term planetary resilience, accessible education and healthcare, fair opportunities for work and creation, protection of living systems, and continuous measurement and correction.

## Public principles
- Human dignity: no person is reduced to an engagement metric, economic value, political identity, or algorithmic score.
- Ecological stewardship: nature, forests, oceans, air, soil, biodiversity, and the wider environment are first-class concerns.
- Universal access: pursue broad access to essential knowledge, education, safety resources, and participation; actual free provision depends on funding, infrastructure, law, and verified delivery.
- Neutral governance: no individual, AI agent, political office, or institution receives unrestricted supreme control.
- Evidence before verification: AI output, author testimony, workflow success, hashes, publication, and dashboards are not independent verification by themselves.

## System layers
1. Individual — account, identity controls, privacy, learning, work, creation.
2. Family/community — cooperation, support, local resources, dispute pathways.
3. Economic — marketplace, freelancing, digital goods, services, transparent transactions.
4. Education — courses, knowledge library, skills, research literacy.
5. Justice/trust — rules, evidence, complaints, appeals, independent review.
6. Ecological — environmental data, conservation projects, impact reporting.
7. Planetary research — aggregated indicators with uncertainty.
8. AI/ML/NLP — classification, retrieval, translation, matching, anomaly detection, summarization, decision support.
9. Automission — orchestration, monitoring, recovery, provenance, audit.
10. Human oversight — appeal, high-impact review, safety escalation, independent verification.

## Safeguards
- No autonomous agent may become an unreviewable authority.
- High-impact decisions require an appeal path.
- Financial actions require transaction controls and auditability.
- Personal data must be minimized and protected.
- Recommendations must not secretly manipulate users.
- Ecological and social claims expose source, date, uncertainty, and methodology.
- “Super senses” means enhanced sensing, monitoring, and early-warning capabilities, not omniscience.
- The system fails closed when verification requirements are unmet.

## Public outcome dashboard
Track dimensions separately: access, education, employment, creator earnings, customer satisfaction, safety, dispute resolution, ecological indicators, platform reliability, and verification progress.

No single composite score should substitute for these dimensions.

## Long-term vision
The user's stated vision extends from the individual and family to society, nations, nature, Earth, and the wider physical universe. The platform records this as a design vision and research program; implementation and empirical claims remain separately testable.
