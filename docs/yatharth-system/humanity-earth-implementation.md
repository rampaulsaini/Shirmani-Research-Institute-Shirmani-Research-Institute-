# Yatharth Humanity & Earth Implementation Layer

## Purpose
Translate the author's stated objective of a more balanced human, social and ecological order into an explicit public-platform architecture.

This document records a design objective, not a claim that the proposed social order has already been implemented or scientifically validated.

## Core design principle
The platform should treat human welfare, ecological protection, transparency, learning, livelihood and dignity as connected system objectives.

The architecture should move through:

**Observe → Understand → Protect → Provide → Learn → Create → Trade → Review → Improve**

## Public domains
- Social participation and user accounts
- Education and open learning
- Employment and freelancing
- Digital commerce and creator income
- Research and evidence
- AI/ML/NLP services
- Music, media and creative work
- Nature, Earth and biodiversity initiatives
- Justice, dispute resolution and appeals
- Public transparency and audit

## Basic-needs objective
The author's proposal includes universal access to essentials such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. The platform may support research, coordination, information, fundraising, service discovery and partner programs around these goals.

It must not represent universal free provision as already achieved. Availability, funding, law, geography and service capacity must be shown explicitly.

## Ecological objective
The platform can measure and coordinate projects concerning forests, water, oceans, air, biodiversity, waste, energy and habitat restoration. Claims about environmental outcomes require evidence and measurable indicators.

## Human-centered safeguards
AI automation must not remove a person's right to explanation, appeal, privacy, safety or lawful human review in high-impact decisions.

## Success indicators
- User-reported satisfaction
- Resolution time for support requests
- Verified service quality
- Educational completion and learning outcomes
- Employment/service matches
- Creator earnings
- Environmental project outcomes
- Safety incidents and remediation
- Independent review coverage
