# Yatharth Global Public Platform

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem that extends the Shirmani Research Institute from a research site into an integrated social, knowledge, creative, education, marketplace, service, and sustainability platform.

## Public principles
- Human dignity, safety, accessibility, privacy, transparency, and user control.
- Users may create accounts, publish permitted content, offer services, sell eligible digital products, learn, teach, collaborate, and participate in communities.
- Engagement is not the sole objective; usefulness, satisfaction, trust, safety, and sustainable participation are first-class metrics.
- Architecture must distinguish author statements, platform-generated content, evidence, and independently verified findings.

## Public domains
1. Social and profiles
2. Research and knowledge
3. Education and skills
4. AI/ML/NLP tools
5. Yatharth AI Music and creative media
6. Freelancing and employment
7. Digital Store and creator commerce
8. Yatharth economy and proposed Yatharth currency research
9. Yatharth justice, complaints, appeals, and fairness research
10. Nature, Earth, biodiversity, and humanity protection
11. Community and collaboration
12. Trust, verification, transparency, and audit
13. Multilingual/global access
14. Help, support, and accessibility

## Account capabilities
A user account may support profile creation, publishing, following, messaging where enabled, portfolio, products, services, courses, creator analytics, orders, reviews, saved items, privacy controls, reporting, appeals, and export/delete controls.

## Marketplace safeguards
Payments, identity, tax, consumer protection, intellectual property, prohibited goods/services, refunds, fraud controls, and applicable law must be handled by appropriate production services. AI may assist classification and routing but must not be treated as a legal or financial authority.

## Justice and governance
"Yatharth Justice" is an architectural research and service domain, not a replacement for constitutional courts, statutory authorities, or due process. High-impact moderation, account sanctions, disputes, and appeals require documented rules, evidence, human review where appropriate, and an appeal path.

## Sustainability mission
The platform may surface projects and measurable actions concerning forests, water, oceans, biodiversity, pollution reduction, climate resilience, and responsible resource use. Claims about environmental impact require evidence and should not be presented as verified merely because an AI workflow reports them.

## Long-term scale objective
The project may record an aspirational global reach target, including the author's stated 850-crore consumer goal. This is a goal, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome.

## Automation boundary
Routine classification, indexing, translation, recommendations, quality checks, monitoring, support triage, and workflow orchestration may be automated. Irreversible or high-impact decisions require fail-safe controls, auditability, and appropriate human oversight.

## Lifecycle
Discover -> Create -> Publish -> Understand -> Verify -> Discover -> Buy/Use/Learn -> Review -> Improve -> Audit.
