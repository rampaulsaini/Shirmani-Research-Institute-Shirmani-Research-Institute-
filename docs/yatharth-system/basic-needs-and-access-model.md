# Basic Needs & Universal Access Model

## Public-interest objective
The platform may support initiatives intended to improve access to food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education.

## Design
- Discover local/community resources
- Education and skill pathways
- Employment and freelancing opportunities
- Verified service providers
- Community support and volunteering
- Emergency/resource information
- Accessibility and multilingual support

## Guardrails
The platform must not promise that every service is universally free unless a real funding and delivery mechanism exists. “Free” offerings must identify the responsible provider, eligibility, geography, capacity and terms.

## Measurement
Measure availability, uptake, completion, satisfaction, unmet demand and geographic coverage separately.
