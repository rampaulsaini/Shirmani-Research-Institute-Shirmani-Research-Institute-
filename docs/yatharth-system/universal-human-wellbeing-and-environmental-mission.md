# Universal Human Wellbeing and Environmental Mission

## Purpose

This module records the Yatharth platform's proposed mission to connect human wellbeing with protection of nature, Earth and other living systems.

The mission is a normative design objective, not a claim that the platform can guarantee universal satisfaction or eliminate all suffering.

## Core areas

### Basic-needs access research

The platform may support research, education, partnerships and lawful programs concerning:

- food security;
- clothing and essential goods;
- safe housing;
- healthcare access;
- education;
- clean water and sanitation;
- digital access;
- employment and livelihood opportunities.

Any public program promising free provision must identify its actual funding, eligibility, delivery partners, jurisdiction and sustainability model.

### Nature and Earth

A dedicated public layer can track and promote:

- forest protection and restoration;
- biodiversity;
- clean air and water;
- ocean health;
- waste reduction;
- sustainable consumption;
- climate and ecological research;
- local conservation projects;
- citizen participation and transparent impact records.

### Human-centred design

The platform should encourage:

- dignity;
- equal access;
- transparent rules;
- non-discrimination;
- user agency;
- meaningful appeal;
- protection from exploitation;
- accessibility for different abilities and languages.

### Yatharth Justice — research/proposal layer

The public interface may host definitions, comparative research, evidence, proposed procedures and transparent case-management concepts under the label “Yatharth Justice”.

It must not represent a private platform proposal as a replacement for a jurisdiction's courts, constitution, laws or lawful public institutions.

### Yatharth Currency — research/proposal layer

“Yatharth Currency” may be developed as a documented economic concept with:

- explicit definition;
- issuance model;
- accounting rules;
- governance;
- fraud controls;
- consumer protection;
- legal/regulatory analysis;
- pilot/testing criteria;
- independent review.

It must not be represented as legal tender or as an operating currency until all applicable legal and technical requirements are actually satisfied.

## Measurement

Public dashboards should separate:

- stated mission;
- active programs;
- verified outcomes;
- independently verified environmental or social impact;
- funding and resource flows;
- unresolved limitations.

## Long-term vision

The platform can provide a common digital space where people create, learn, collaborate, earn, research, protect nature and participate in transparent communities while automation handles routine work and consequential decisions remain accountable and reviewable.
