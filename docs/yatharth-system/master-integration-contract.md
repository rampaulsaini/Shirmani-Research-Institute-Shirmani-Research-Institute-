# Yatharth Global Platform — Master Integration Contract

## Purpose

This contract consolidates the public-platform, humanity, ecological, governance, research, education, commerce and AI/ML/NLP/Automission architecture into one implementation map.

The Yatharth framework and its philosophical statements remain **author-sourced unless independently verified**. The platform must never present aspiration, authorship, simulation, workflow success, or AI output as independent verification.

## Public experience

The public product is organized around:

- Social and personal accounts
- Creator publishing
- Research and knowledge
- Education and skills
- AI and AI services
- Music, audio, video and podcast
- Freelancing and employment
- Digital Store and digital products
- Business and creator economy
- Yatharth Currency research/proposal surface
- Yatharth Justice, trust and dispute-support surface
- Nature, Earth and humanity stewardship
- Community and participation
- Public verification and transparency
- Help, accessibility, privacy and safety

Every capability has a lifecycle:

**PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → MONITORED → AUTONOMOUS**

No UI may silently imply a later state when only an earlier state exists.

## Account and participation model

A user account may, subject to applicable rules and platform safeguards:

1. create a profile;
2. publish and share content;
3. create products or services;
4. offer or purchase eligible digital goods;
5. teach or learn;
6. participate in communities;
7. use AI-assisted creation and research;
8. receive feedback and reviews;
9. manage consent, privacy and account controls.

Marketplace, payment, identity, copyright, taxation and consumer-protection requirements must be implemented before corresponding production functionality is activated.

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission control plane

### L1 — Intake
Ingest user actions, content, reports and platform events with consent and provenance.

### L2 — NLP / semantic understanding
Classify language, entities, topics, claims, intent, multilingual content and relationships.

### L3 — ML quality and safety
Detect spam, abuse, manipulation, fraud indicators, unsafe content and anomalous activity.

### L4 — Recommendation and matching
Provide search, discovery, education, creator, customer, freelancer and marketplace matching while exposing meaningful user controls.

### L5 — Agent orchestration
Coordinate specialized agents for research, support, commerce, education, media and platform operations.

### L6 — Automission
Run eligible routine operations continuously, with bounded permissions, audit logs, rollback and fail-closed behavior.

### L7 — Verification and audit
Separate AI-generated conclusions from evidence, human review and independent verification.

### L8 — Escalation
High-impact financial, justice, account, safety and verification decisions require appropriate human review, appeal and audit pathways.

## Human and planetary outcome layer

The platform measures more than engagement:

- user satisfaction;
- successful task completion;
- learning outcomes;
- creator/freelancer opportunity;
- customer resolution;
- accessibility;
- safety;
- privacy;
- ecological indicators where measurable;
- essential-service access where the platform can legitimately support it;
- complaint and appeal resolution.

The aspiration for broad participation, including the author's stated 850-crore target, is recorded as a **goal**, never as an achieved metric.

## Governance principles

1. Neutral and auditable rules.
2. Separation of platform operation from independent verification.
3. No AI claim of authority merely because an agent produced it.
4. Explainable status transitions.
5. User appeal and correction mechanisms.
6. Least-privilege agent permissions.
7. Immutable or tamper-evident audit records where appropriate.
8. Privacy-by-design.
9. Protection against automated discrimination and manipulation.
10. Human oversight for high-impact decisions.

## Essential-needs vision

The author's vision includes universal access to basic needs such as food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. These are recorded as a **humanitarian objective/vision**, not as a claim that the current platform can already provide them universally.

Implementation should prioritize measurable programs, partnerships, eligibility rules, funding models, legal compliance and transparent outcomes rather than unsupported promises.

## Research integrity

For every substantive Yatharth proposition:

**Author Source → Normalized Claim → Operational Definition → Evidence → Counter-Evidence → Comparison → Test → Independent Human Review → Verification Status**

Metaphysical, philosophical, historical and civilization-scale claims must remain clearly distinguished from empirically established findings.

## Integration acceptance criteria

A public-platform release is not considered complete merely because workflows pass.

Completion requires, as applicable:

- public interface exists;
- capability is connected to a real backend;
- authentication and authorization work;
- data model and persistence work;
- security controls pass;
- relevant AI/ML/NLP agents are connected;
- Automission permissions are bounded;
- observability and audit work;
- failure and rollback paths are tested;
- accessibility is tested;
- legal/compliance requirements are addressed;
- human escalation exists for high-impact actions;
- live status is honestly represented.

## North-star

The platform should seek to create measurable human value: useful knowledge, education, creative expression, livelihood opportunities, trustworthy commerce, community participation and ecological stewardship.

The user's Yatharth vision may guide the product's stated mission, but claims of universal transformation or superiority remain author claims until independently supported.
