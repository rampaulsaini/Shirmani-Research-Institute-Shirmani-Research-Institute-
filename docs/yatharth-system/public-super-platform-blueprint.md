# Yatharth Global Public Super Platform Blueprint

## Purpose

Define the public-facing ecosystem that connects social participation, research, education, creativity, commerce, work, services, trust, nature protection, and continuous customer satisfaction.

## Public pillars

1. Social — profiles, follows, posts, comments, communities, messaging, sharing.
2. Creator — audio, video, music, writing, images, live, portfolios.
3. Research — claims, sources, evidence, comparisons, reports, public verification status.
4. Education — courses, learning paths, skills, assessments, certificates, knowledge library.
5. Work — jobs, freelancing, services, portfolios, client matching.
6. Commerce — digital store, products, subscriptions, licensing, creator sales.
7. AI — research assistant, creator tools, translation, recommendation, support, agent services.
8. Yatharth AI Music — creation, publishing, discovery, licensing and marketplace workflows.
9. Yatharth Justice — transparent rules, complaints, appeals, dispute processes and human review.
10. Yatharth Economy — value exchange and creator/service income models; any currency concept remains explicitly a proposal until legally and technically defined.
11. Nature & Humanity — conservation projects, education, participation and measurable impact.
12. Trust — identity/account controls, provenance, evidence labels, moderation, safety, audit and appeals.

## Account model

Every eligible user can have a profile and, subject to platform rules:
- publish and share content;
- offer services;
- list digital products;
- teach or learn;
- create communities;
- participate in research;
- receive reviews;
- track orders, earnings and activity.

## Core loop

Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Understand -> Connect -> Buy/Sell/Collaborate -> Deliver -> Review -> Resolve -> Learn -> Improve.

## Experience principle

Optimize for user value, safety, accessibility, satisfaction and meaningful participation rather than engagement time alone.

## Scale target

The 850-crore (8.5-billion) user/consumer figure is recorded as a long-term aspiration, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome.

## Status discipline

A public feature is marked LIVE only when its implementation, deployment, tests and operational dependencies are actually present. Architecture documents do not count as deployment evidence.
