# Public Platform Capability Registry

A capability becomes LIVE only when an actual public implementation is deployed and tested.

## Status vocabulary
- DESIGNED — architecture exists.
- IN_BUILD — implementation is underway.
- TESTED — defined automated/manual tests pass.
- LIVE — publicly deployed and usable.
- BLOCKED — dependency prevents safe release.
- REVIEW_REQUIRED — human/legal/security review is required.

## Capability families

### Identity & Social
Account creation, profiles, follows/connections, posts, sharing, comments, messaging, communities, creator pages, privacy controls, reporting and appeals.

### Creation & Media
Audio, music, video, images, articles, books, podcasts, live, multilingual publishing, AI-assisted creation.

### Economy
Digital Store, digital products, services, freelancing, jobs, creator earnings, marketplace matching, orders/delivery, reviews, refunds and disputes.

### Education
Courses, learning paths, knowledge library, research literacy, AI/ML/NLP learning, skills training, certificates.

### Yatharth Research
Canonical source, claim registry, evidence, counter-evidence, comparative research, claim testing, independent review, verification dashboard.

### Justice & Trust
Rules, complaints, evidence submission, case tracking, appeals, transparency records, independent review, safety escalation.

### Yatharth Currency research
Economic model documentation, currency concept registry, simulation, legal/compliance analysis, risk analysis, independent review. Currency functionality remains research/simulation until legal, financial, security, and operational requirements are independently satisfied.

### Nature & Earth
Environmental knowledge, conservation projects, biodiversity information, pollution reporting, resource stewardship, impact dashboards, ecological research.

### AI / ML / NLP / Automission
NLP ingestion, semantic search, translation, recommendation, matching, evidence discovery, contradiction detection, moderation assistance, fraud/anomaly detection, research packet generation, agent orchestration, workflow monitoring, failure recovery, audit/provenance.

## Release rule
A capability must not be labeled LIVE merely because a workflow, prototype, documentation page, or AI agent exists. Status changes require deployment evidence and test evidence.
