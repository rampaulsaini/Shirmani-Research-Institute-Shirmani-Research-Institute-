# Yatharth Global Public Super Platform — Operating Model

## Purpose

This document defines the public-facing operating model for a unified Yatharth platform combining social publishing, research, education, creative work, commerce, freelancing, services, community participation, environmental stewardship, trust, and transparent governance.

The platform is an architectural proposal. It must not be represented as already deployed or as guaranteeing outcomes.

## Public account capabilities

Every eligible user may have a profile/account and, subject to applicable rules and verification requirements, may:

- publish posts, articles, images, audio, video, music and other permitted media;
- create communities and collaborative projects;
- offer services and freelancing work;
- list digital products, courses, publications, audio and music;
- buy, sell, license or subscribe to permitted digital goods;
- learn through courses, research materials and skills programs;
- participate in research, evidence review and public discussion;
- use multilingual AI-assisted creation and translation;
- view their activity, sales, learning, creator and trust dashboards.

## Value loop

Create → Publish → Discover → Learn/Use → Collaborate → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve → Earn/Share Value.

The platform should optimize for user value, satisfaction, safety and meaningful participation rather than maximizing time spent through manipulative engagement.

## AI-native operating layer

AI/ML/NLP services may support:

1. identity/profile assistance;
2. content understanding and semantic classification;
3. translation and accessibility;
4. search and discovery;
5. recommendation with user controls;
6. spam, fraud and abuse detection;
7. marketplace matching;
8. customer support;
9. creator assistance;
10. education assistance;
11. research/evidence assistance;
12. quality and integrity checks;
13. workflow orchestration;
14. continuous monitoring and recovery.

Automission coordinates bounded tasks and escalates high-impact or uncertain cases.

## Human control

Routine operations can be highly automated, but high-impact decisions must remain reviewable. Account suspension, major financial actions, disputes, safety appeals, legal compliance and independent verification require defined human oversight, audit trails and appeal paths.

## Global-scale design target

The long-term aspiration may target billions of users, including the stated 850-crore goal. This is a target, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome. The architecture should therefore be multilingual, mobile-first, accessible, privacy-conscious, resilient and geographically scalable.

## Success measures

Track separate metrics for:

- user satisfaction;
- successful task completion;
- safety and abuse rates;
- creator earnings;
- marketplace fulfillment;
- education completion;
- research/evidence quality;
- service reliability;
- environmental-impact initiatives;
- independent verification;
- automation health.

No single engagement metric should be treated as the overall quality of the platform.
