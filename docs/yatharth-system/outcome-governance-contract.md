# Yatharth Global Platform — Outcome & Governance Contract

## Purpose

This contract turns the Yatharth global-platform vision into measurable, auditable product requirements. It complements the master integration contract and does not treat philosophical, metaphysical, political, economic or civilization-scale propositions as independently verified facts.

## Public outcome model

The platform should optimize for durable human value rather than raw time-on-site:

- useful participation
- learning and skill development
- creator opportunity
- fair commerce
- successful service delivery
- customer satisfaction and resolution
- accessibility
- privacy and safety
- ecological stewardship where measurable
- transparent governance
- meaningful appeals and corrections

Engagement is an outcome to observe, not the sole objective.

## Account and creator economy

A production account layer should support:

1. registration and authentication;
2. profile and identity controls;
3. publishing and sharing;
4. creator pages;
5. digital products and eligible services;
6. freelancing and employment discovery;
7. education and course delivery;
8. customer purchase and delivery flows;
9. ratings/reviews with abuse controls;
10. earnings, invoices and applicable tax records;
11. privacy, consent, export and deletion controls.

No financial or marketplace feature is considered LIVE until payment, consumer protection, fraud controls, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements are addressed.

## Essential-needs program layer

The stated humanitarian vision includes access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. The platform must present this as a programmatic objective unless a specific service is actually funded, legally available and operational.

For every essential-needs program:

**Need → Eligibility → Funding → Provider → Delivery → Evidence → Outcome → Appeal**

The platform must never imply universal free provision merely because an aspiration exists in documentation.

## Yatharth Justice / civic support

The public interface may provide:

- rights and policy information;
- evidence organization;
- complaint intake;
- mediation/dispute-support information;
- case-status transparency where legally permitted;
- appeal pathways;
- independent-review workflows.

The platform must not impersonate a court or government authority. Legal decisions remain with competent human institutions.

## Political and institutional neutrality

Any civic or governance layer must be neutral toward political parties, candidates and officials.

AI agents may:

- summarize documented positions;
- organize public records;
- compare evidence;
- surface uncertainty;
- explain procedures.

AI agents must not:

- choose a political candidate for users;
- manipulate voting preferences;
- suppress lawful political participation;
- infer political preferences from private behavior;
- present an automated political ranking as objective truth.

## Ecological stewardship

Where reliable measurements are available, the platform may track:

- forest and land-use indicators;
- biodiversity indicators;
- water quality/access indicators;
- pollution measurements;
- waste reduction;
- emissions-related metrics;
- restoration projects;
- verified community actions.

Ecological claims must identify their source, measurement period and uncertainty.

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission

Agents operate under least privilege:

**Observe → Classify → Recommend → Act within bounds → Log → Audit → Escalate**

Required controls:

- explicit permissions;
- provenance for important actions;
- immutable/tamper-evident audit records where appropriate;
- rollback;
- rate limits;
- anomaly detection;
- model/version tracking;
- prompt/input/output logging subject to privacy requirements;
- human escalation for high-impact decisions.

No agent may grant itself additional authority.

## Verification state machine

Every public capability and substantive research claim uses explicit state:

**PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → MONITORED → AUTONOMOUS**

Research claims additionally use:

**AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED → VERIFIED / PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE / NOT_VERIFIED**

Workflow success alone cannot advance a research claim to VERIFIED.

## Public metrics

The dashboard should separately report:

- platform capabilities LIVE;
- automation health;
- agent coverage;
- successful/failed jobs;
- research evidence coverage;
- independent verification status;
- user satisfaction;
- complaint resolution;
- accessibility coverage;
- safety incidents;
- ecological program outcomes.

Never combine these into a single misleading “overall completion” percentage.

## Long-term scale objective

The author's stated aspiration of reaching 850 crore consumers is recorded as a long-term goal. It is not a current user count, forecast or guarantee.

Scaling architecture should prioritize:

- multilingual support;
- mobile-first delivery;
- low-bandwidth operation;
- accessibility;
- regional resilience;
- privacy and security;
- abuse resistance;
- data portability;
- interoperable APIs;
- graceful degradation.

## Acceptance gate

A release may be called production-ready only when the applicable public UI, backend, persistence, authentication, authorization, security, accessibility, observability, AI connections, Automission bounds, rollback, legal/compliance checks and escalation paths are actually tested.

**Mission:** maximize measurable human value, dignity, opportunity, learning, trustworthy exchange and ecological stewardship while preserving user agency and independent verification.
