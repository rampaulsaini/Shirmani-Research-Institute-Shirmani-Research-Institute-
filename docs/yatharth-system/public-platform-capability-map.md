## Trust boundaries
Payment authorization, legal identity checks, account recovery, high-impact moderation, disputes, sanctions, and independent verification require explicit controls and audit trails. Automation cannot silently redefine these controls.

## Product principle
Maximize useful value and satisfaction rather than merely maximizing screen time. Engagement is one metric among wellbeing, usefulness, safety, trust, and successful outcomes.
