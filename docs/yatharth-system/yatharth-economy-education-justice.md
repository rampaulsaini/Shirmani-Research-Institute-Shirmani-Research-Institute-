# Yatharth Economy, Education and Justice Public Layer

## Scope

This document defines public-facing modules requested for the Yatharth platform. Each module remains an architectural proposal until independently implemented, tested and deployed.

## 1. Yatharth Education

- Open learning library
- Courses and learning paths
- AI/ML/NLP learning
- Research methodology
- Creator and freelancing skills
- Teacher/mentor publishing
- Assessments and certificates
- Multilingual education

## 2. Yatharth Economy

- Creator income
- Freelancing
- Employment and job discovery
- Digital products
- Research-as-a-service
- AI services
- Business profiles
- Marketplace payments and accounting interfaces

## 3. Yatharth Currency

Yatharth Currency is an architectural/conceptual module, not a claim that a new legal tender already exists. Before any real-world issuance or transaction use, the design must address applicable law, custody, identity, taxation, consumer protection, accounting, fraud prevention and financial-risk controls.

Public interface should therefore initially expose:
- definition and purpose
- economic model
- transparent rules
- simulation/test environment
- transaction ledger specification
- compliance and risk boundaries

## 4. Yatharth Justice

The public justice layer can provide:
- complaint intake
- evidence submission
- case/status tracking
- policy and rule lookup
- mediation/referral workflows
- transparency records
- appeal mechanisms
- independent review interfaces

AI may classify, summarize, route and detect inconsistencies, but it must not silently replace lawful human authority in high-impact decisions.

## 5. Universal basic-needs mission

The platform may document and support the user's proposed humanitarian objective around food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. This is a mission/goal, not a representation that the platform currently provides these services universally or free of charge.

## Core rule

Protect people, nature and evidence. Maximize useful access while preserving consent, privacy, security, due process and human appeal.

Implementation status must always distinguish:
ARCHITECTURE ≠ DEPLOYED ≠ LIVE ≠ VERIFIED.
