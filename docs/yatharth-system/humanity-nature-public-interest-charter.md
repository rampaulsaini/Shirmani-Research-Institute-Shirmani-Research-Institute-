# Humanity, Nature and Public-Interest Operating Charter

## Purpose

Translate the author's Yatharth vision into testable platform objectives without presenting philosophical or metaphysical claims as established scientific facts.

## Public-interest objectives

- Improve access to useful knowledge and education.
- Support lawful livelihood, freelancing and creator opportunities.
- Promote environmental awareness and conservation projects.
- Provide accessible information and community participation.
- Make evidence, provenance, corrections and uncertainty visible.
- Reduce harmful, deceptive and abusive platform activity.
- Provide complaint, appeal and dispute pathways.
- Measure user-reported satisfaction and service quality.

## Basic-needs vision

The author's vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. The platform can support information, coordination, fundraising, services, education and partnerships around these goals, but cannot claim to guarantee universal free provision unless a real, funded and lawful mechanism exists.

## Governance boundary

The platform is not a replacement for elected governments, courts, constitutional institutions or lawful public administration. “Supreme control” in the software architecture means a bounded policy/control plane with auditability and appeals, not private authority over people or governments.

## Measurement

Track measurable indicators such as educational completion, successful service delivery, customer satisfaction, resolved complaints, conservation participation, accessibility, safety incidents and verified evidence coverage. Avoid a single “humanity improved” score.
