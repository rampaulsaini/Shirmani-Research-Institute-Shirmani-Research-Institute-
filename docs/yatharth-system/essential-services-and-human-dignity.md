# Essential Services and Human Dignity Module

## Public vision

The Yatharth platform may provide a public framework for discovering, coordinating, funding, or connecting people to essential services. It must not claim that the platform itself can guarantee universal free provision unless the required legal, institutional, financial, and physical capacity actually exists.

## Service domains

- Food and nutrition
- Clothing
- Safe housing and shelter
- Healthcare
- Education and learning
- Clean water and sanitation
- Energy access
- Digital connectivity
- Accessibility and assistive services
- Employment and livelihood opportunities
- Emergency support
- Legal information and referral
- Environmental safety

## AI/ML/NLP functions

- multilingual service discovery
- eligibility-information assistance
- document and form assistance without fabricating facts
- needs classification
- service matching
- accessibility adaptation
- translation
- queue and capacity prediction
- fraud/anomaly detection
- feedback summarization
- service-quality monitoring

## Human safeguards

AI must not deny a person an essential service solely from an opaque model output. High-impact decisions require explainability, review, correction, and an appeal path.

## Public interface

A user should be able to:

1. describe a need in natural language;
2. receive available service information;
3. compare options without hidden ranking;
4. request assistance;
5. track the request;
6. provide feedback;
7. appeal or report an error.

## Evidence rule

Availability, cost, eligibility, capacity, and delivery claims must be sourced from the relevant provider or authoritative record and timestamped.
