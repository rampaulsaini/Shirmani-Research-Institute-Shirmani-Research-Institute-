# Sustainability and Humanity Mission

## Scope
A public mission layer for protecting people, animals, plants, forests, water, oceans, atmosphere, biodiversity, and the wider physical environment.

## From viewpoint to implementation
The Yatharth framework can be represented as an author's philosophical proposal for shifting decision-making toward broader, longer-term consideration of human and ecological systems. The platform must distinguish this proposal from independently established scientific or policy conclusions.

## Practical modules
- Environmental knowledge library
- Verified conservation projects
- Biodiversity and habitat information
- Forest and water restoration projects
- Pollution reporting and evidence collection
- Community volunteering
- Educational sustainability courses
- Responsible consumption guidance
- Impact measurement with source provenance
- Public dashboards for documented project outcomes

## Basic-needs research
The platform may host research and projects concerning food, clothing, shelter, healthcare, education, accessibility, and other basic needs. Statements about providing services "free" require a sustainable funding and legal/operational model before being presented as an available service.

## Measurement
Prefer measurable indicators, baseline dates, methodology, source links, uncertainty, and independent review. Never convert an aspiration into a verified impact claim merely through automation.

## Guiding loop
Observe -> understand -> protect -> restore -> measure -> independently review -> improve.
