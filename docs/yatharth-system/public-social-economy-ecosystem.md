# Yatharth Public Social & Economic Ecosystem

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem in which people can create accounts, publish lawful content, learn, offer services, sell digital products, collaborate, and participate in communities.

## Public domains
- Social profiles and feeds
- Creator publishing
- Digital store and marketplace
- Freelancing and services
- Employment and skills
- Education and courses
- Yatharth AI Music and creative media
- Research and knowledge
- Community participation
- Nature, Earth and humanity initiatives
- Trust, safety, complaints and appeals
- Public transparency and verification

## User lifecycle
Discover -> Create account -> Profile -> Create/share -> Discover -> Learn/connect -> Offer or buy -> Deliver -> Review -> Resolve issues -> Earn/support -> Contribute.

## Design principles
1. User value before engagement maximization.
2. Clear ownership, consent, privacy and portability.
3. Multilingual and accessibility-first.
4. Safety, anti-fraud and abuse prevention.
5. Transparent ranking/recommendation signals.
6. Human appeal for high-impact decisions.
7. Independent verification is never substituted by automation.
8. Economic and social claims remain claims until evidence supports them.

## Account capabilities
Subject to applicable law and platform rules, an account can:
- publish posts and media;
- follow and communicate with others;
- create communities;
- list lawful digital products;
- offer lawful services;
- publish educational material;
- build a creator portfolio;
- receive reviews;
- manage orders and earnings;
- request support or appeal moderation decisions.

## Economy
The platform may support digital commerce, freelancing, creator income, subscriptions, courses and services. Any payment, stored-value, currency, financial or regulated activity must be implemented only with appropriate legal, compliance, identity, tax, payment-provider and jurisdictional controls.

## Yatharth Currency
"Yatharth Currency" is maintained as a proposed conceptual/economic module unless and until its definition, legal status, issuance/redemption rules, safeguards and independent review are established. It must not be represented as legal tender by default.

## Yatharth Justice
"Yatharth Justice" is a proposed fairness, dispute-resolution and accountability layer. Automated systems may triage, explain and surface evidence, but high-impact determinations require due process, human review and appeal.

## Environmental mission
The platform can expose measurable programs for nature, biodiversity, forests, oceans, climate, pollution reduction and resource conservation. It should report measurable actions and evidence rather than unsupported claims.

## Success measures
- user satisfaction and successful task completion;
- safety and abuse rates;
- dispute resolution quality and appeal outcomes;
- creator/service-provider earnings;
- learning completion and outcomes;
- marketplace reliability;
- environmental actions with evidence;
- system uptime and automation health.

These metrics must not be collapsed into a single "best" or "complete" score.
