# Impartial Governance and Justice Layer

## Goal
Define a technology-neutral governance model centered on equal treatment, due process, transparency, evidence, accountability and protection of human dignity, nature and living systems.

## Institutional separation
The public platform must not give an AI system unilateral sovereign authority over people. Executive, legislative, judicial and independent oversight functions remain institutionally distinct where required by the applicable legal system.

The platform may provide:
- evidence organization
- case-status information
- procedural guidance
- transparency dashboards
- conflict-of-interest declarations
- audit logs
- complaint and appeal routing
- independent-review workflows

It must not silently replace lawful courts or elected institutions.

## AI justice safeguards
AI systems must:
- disclose when an automated system is involved in a consequential decision
- preserve relevant evidence and decision traces
- provide an understandable reason where legally appropriate
- support appeal and human review
- test for disparate error rates
- fail closed when required evidence is missing
- prevent unauthorized access and tampering

## Neutrality
The system does not select political parties, candidates, officials or ideologies. It presents documented positions, evidence, procedures and competing interpretations so people can make their own decisions.

## Verification
Author-source statements, philosophical propositions, scientific hypotheses, legal claims and independently verified findings must remain separate status classes.
