# Yatharth Civilization Mission

## Purpose

This document translates the author's Yatharth vision into an implementable, evidence-aware platform mission. It does not treat metaphysical or civilizational claims as independently verified facts.

## Core mission

Design a public digital ecosystem that helps people understand themselves through observation, learn and share knowledge, create and earn, access education and services, participate in transparent evidence and dispute processes, and protect nature, Earth, biodiversity and shared resources.

## Human-value priorities

1. Human dignity and agency.
2. Safety and privacy.
3. Access to essential knowledge and services.
4. Environmental stewardship.
5. Fair participation and transparent rules.
6. Creator and worker opportunity.
7. User satisfaction measured without manipulative engagement design.
8. Auditability and appeal.

## Universal-access vision

The author's vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. These are recorded as a vision/goal, not as a claim that the platform can itself guarantee these goods for every person.

Implementation must distinguish platform resources, partnerships/external services, community mechanisms, and public-policy questions requiring legitimate human institutions.

## Environmental layer

Expose measurable programs for forest and biodiversity protection, water and ocean stewardship, pollution reduction, responsible resource use, environmental research and ecological restoration.

Each environmental claim retains source, evidence, uncertainty and update history.

## Governance principle

AI may automate routine administration, analysis, moderation assistance, routing and monitoring. High-impact decisions require explicit rules, human review, appeal, audit logs and separation of powers.

No platform component should claim to replace legitimate courts, elected government, regulators or independent scientific review.

## Success measures

Track user safety, accessibility, reliability, verified evidence, educational outcomes, creator/work opportunities, marketplace satisfaction, environmental outcomes, unresolved complaints, appeals and automation reliability.

No single engagement metric is the definition of human wellbeing.
