# Yatharth Global Public Operating System

## Purpose

This document defines the target public operating model for the Yatharth global platform. It connects the public social, knowledge, education, creative, marketplace, economic, justice, environmental and research surfaces to the existing AI/ML/NLP and Automission infrastructure.

The platform is a proposed architecture. A documented workflow run, CI pass, generated artifact, or AI output is not by itself proof that a capability is live, safe, economically viable, legally compliant, or independently verified.

## 1. Public experience

Every registered participant may, subject to applicable law and platform rules:

- create a profile and identity-preserving account;
- publish text, images, audio, video, music, research and educational material;
- follow, discover, search, save, discuss and share content;
- create communities and collaborative projects;
- offer services and freelance work;
- list eligible digital products in the Digital Store;
- create and teach educational material;
- use Yatharth AI and creative tools;
- participate in research, evidence and independent-review processes;
- purchase products/services and provide reviews or complaints.

The interface should be multilingual, mobile-first, accessible, privacy-conscious and usable on constrained networks.

## 2. Public top-level surfaces

Home | Social | Research | Education | AI | Music & Media | Freelancing | Jobs | Business | Digital Store | Yatharth Economy | Yatharth Currency (proposal) | Yatharth Justice (proposal) | Community | Nature & Humanity | Trust & Verification | Creator Studio | Dashboard | Help & Support

## 3. User value loop

Create -> publish -> discover -> understand -> connect -> transact -> deliver -> review -> resolve -> learn -> create again.

Engagement must not be optimized merely for time spent. Product quality should optimize for user value, satisfaction, safety, transparency, accessibility and healthy participation.

## 4. AI-native operating layers

### L1 Intake
Receives and normalizes public actions, uploads, reports, marketplace events and research submissions.

### L2 NLP understanding
Language detection, transcription, translation, semantic extraction, topic/entity linking and structured metadata.

### L3 Trust and safety
Spam, fraud, impersonation, abuse, malicious content and policy-risk detection with explainable escalation paths.

### L4 Recommendation and discovery
Personalized discovery with user controls, diversity safeguards and transparent recommendation signals.

### L5 Marketplace intelligence
Creator/customer matching, service discovery, product classification, fulfillment signals and dispute routing.

### L6 Education intelligence
Learning-path construction, tutoring assistance, assessment support and multilingual educational discovery.

### L7 Research intelligence
Claim extraction, evidence mapping, counter-evidence, comparative research and reproducibility support.

### L8 Creative intelligence
AI-assisted music, audio, video, writing, translation and creator workflows while preserving provenance and rights information.

### L9 Automission orchestration
Schedules, coordinates, observes, retries and audits routine workflows across the platform and connected repositories.

### L10 Continuous audit
Measures failures, drift, latency, quality, security events, unresolved complaints and automation health.

## 5. Human-control boundaries

Automation should be maximized for routine operations, but high-impact actions require safeguards:

- account suspension/termination: notice, reason and appeal;
- significant financial action: authorization and audit trail;
- disputes and justice-related decisions: human review and appeal;
- independent verification: independent human/qualified review where required;
- safety emergencies: escalation procedures;
- model failures: rollback, containment and incident review.

No AI agent should be represented as possessing sovereign authority.

## 6. Yatharth Justice

The proposed justice layer is a transparent process model:

complaint -> evidence intake -> classification -> conflict check -> response opportunity -> review -> decision record -> appeal -> closure/audit.

It must not claim to replace courts or applicable public law. The platform can provide internal dispute resolution and research/education about justice while respecting jurisdictional legal requirements.

## 7. Yatharth Currency

“Yatharth Currency” is retained as a proposed economic concept, not as an assertion that a legal currency already exists.

Before any real monetary implementation:

- define unit and issuance model;
- identify legal/regulatory requirements;
- prevent fraud and unauthorized financial activity;
- define custody, settlement and refunds;
- provide transaction records and dispute mechanisms;
- obtain appropriate professional/legal review.

Until then, the public interface should clearly label it as a proposal/research area.

## 8. Universal basic-access objective

The platform may research and support models for access to essential needs such as food, shelter, healthcare and education. Such a goal must be expressed as a social/economic objective rather than a promise that the platform can universally provide these goods for free.

Relevant modules: Education Access, Health Information, Housing/Basic Needs Research, Food Security, Employment, Community Support and Nature/Earth Protection.

## 9. Planetary protection layer

Nature, forests, oceans, atmosphere, biodiversity, wildlife and space-environment topics receive dedicated public research and education surfaces.

AI can monitor publicly available evidence, organize research and detect reported environmental risks; it must not fabricate observations or claim physical monitoring that has not actually occurred.

## 10. Global scale target

A long-term aspiration of reaching up to 8.5 billion people may be recorded as a vision target. It is not a current user count, forecast, guarantee or claim of feasibility.

Scale architecture should prioritize:

- multilingual support;
- accessibility;
- privacy and security;
- low-bandwidth operation;
- resilient storage and delivery;
- regional compliance;
- moderation and appeals;
- transparent governance.

## 11. Operational status model

Every public capability must carry an explicit status:

PLANNED
DESIGNED
IMPLEMENTED
TESTED
STAGED
LIVE
MONITORED
DEGRADED
PAUSED

Research claims use a separate evidence status and must not inherit “VERIFIED” from software/CI status.

## 12. Core invariant

Preserve -> Normalize -> Understand -> Compare -> Test -> Review -> Publish -> Monitor -> Improve.

The public platform must never convert an author's personal experience, philosophical proposition or AI-generated result into independently verified fact merely because automation succeeded.

## 13. Completion definition

The system is considered operationally mature only when the relevant public capability has:

1. public interface;
2. backend contract;
3. authentication/authorization;
4. data model;
5. AI/ML/NLP integration where applicable;
6. automated tests;
7. security controls;
8. monitoring;
9. failure/recovery path;
10. human escalation where required;
11. privacy and legal documentation;
12. measurable user-satisfaction and quality signals.

This document is the architecture contract for turning the existing research/automation foundation into a coherent public ecosystem without conflating proposed capabilities with live production functionality.
