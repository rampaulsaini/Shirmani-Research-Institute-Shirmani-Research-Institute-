# Yatharth Implementation Charter — 2026-09-30

## Author-defined direction
The author states a goal of moving from a primarily head/mind/intellect-oriented way of living toward a balance that includes a heart-centered “Shirmani” perspective, with intended practical outcomes of greater clarity, completeness, protection and human/nature well-being.

This is recorded as an author-defined philosophical objective, not as an independently established scientific fact.

## Implementation priorities
1. Protect the author's source.
2. Build a useful public social platform.
3. Give users creation, sharing, learning, work and commerce capabilities.
4. Build trust, safety and dispute/appeal mechanisms.
5. Connect research and evidence to public claims.
6. Support environmental and humanitarian participation.
7. Automate routine operations with bounded AI/ML/NLP/Automission.
8. Keep high-impact decisions auditable and reviewable.
9. Measure actual user value and satisfaction.
10. Never claim a capability is live before deployment and testing.

## Product sequence
Foundation: accounts, profiles, permissions, content publishing, search/discovery, multilingual interface, notifications and accessibility.

Value: education, freelancing, employment, digital store, creator tools, Yatharth AI Music, research services and community.

Trust: moderation, provenance, reviews, complaints, appeals, refunds/disputes and independent verification.

Planet: conservation information, reporting, projects and measurable impact.

Autonomy: AI assistant, agent orchestration, NLP/ML services, Automission, continuous audit and resilience/recovery.

## Long-term aspiration
The author's stated aspiration includes an 8.5-billion-person consumer target. This is retained as an aspiration, not a forecast or present user count.

Optimize for real usefulness and satisfaction rather than manipulative engagement. Time spent is an outcome to observe, not the sole objective.

## Definition of complete
A capability is complete only when its lifecycle state, implementation or documented non-live reason, tests, monitoring, ownership, safety controls and public status are explicit.

New user material should be preserved, normalized, mapped to modules and routed through the appropriate evidence/implementation lifecycle.
