# Public Platform Module Map

## Navigation domains
Home; Social; Profiles and Accounts; Creator Studio; Research; Knowledge Library; Education; AI; Yatharth AI Music; Audio/Video/Podcast; Freelancing; Employment; Business and Services; Digital Store; Digital Products; Yatharth Economy; Yatharth Currency (concept/research unless deployed); Yatharth Justice; Community; Nature/Earth/Humanity; Verification and Trust; Public Dashboard; Help and Customer Support; Governance/Privacy/Security; Multilingual/Accessibility.

## Account capabilities
A registered user may, subject to rules and applicable law, create a profile, publish permitted content, share media, create communities, offer services, list eligible digital products, teach/publish courses, participate in research, buy eligible products/services, review transactions/content, report abuse/disputes, and manage privacy/settings.

## Marketplace integrity
Listings require provenance, pricing, delivery terms, applicable refund rules, safety checks, and fraud controls. Commercial relationships should be transparent.

## Trust signals
Relevant items can expose provenance, verification state, reviews, update history, and limitations.

## Separation rule
Internal workflows, agent prompts, CI machinery, secrets, tokens, private logs, and credentials remain operational infrastructure, not public-interface content.
