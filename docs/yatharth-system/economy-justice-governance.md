# Yatharth Economy, Justice and Governance Architecture

## Economy

The platform may contain research and implementation modules for:
- digital commerce;
- freelancing;
- employment;
- creator income;
- education and services;
- digital products;
- subscriptions;
- payments through lawful providers;
- economic research.

## Yatharth Currency

“Yatharth Currency” is treated as a proposed research/design concept unless and until a lawful, technically implemented and independently reviewed system exists. Any public implementation must define issuer, unit, custody, transfer, redemption, taxation, consumer protection, fraud controls and applicable regulation.

## Yatharth Justice

The justice layer should provide:
- transparent rules;
- complaint intake;
- evidence submission;
- case status;
- conflict-of-interest controls;
- appeal;
- human review for high-impact outcomes;
- audit trails.

It must not claim to replace courts or public law. Any governance model must operate within applicable law and clearly state its scope.

## Governance

Automated systems may monitor compliance and route cases. They must not receive unlimited “supreme control”. Authority boundaries, auditability, human accountability and appeal remain explicit.

## Basic-needs objective

The project's aspiration to improve access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education should be represented as a social objective/research program, with eligibility, funding, delivery, evidence and legal constraints specified before any service is described as guaranteed or free.
