# Human–Earth Outcomes and Metrics

## Purpose

Measure whether the platform is producing useful outcomes rather than merely increasing activity.

## Human metrics

- user-reported satisfaction
- successful support resolution
- learning completion
- employment/freelance opportunities completed
- creator earnings and payout reliability
- accessibility coverage
- privacy and security incidents
- appeal resolution time
- harmful-content incident rate

## Earth and nature metrics

Where reliable data is available:
- conservation projects supported
- restoration activity
- waste-reduction projects
- resource-use efficiency
- biodiversity or habitat initiatives
- verified environmental impact of platform operations

Metrics must identify their source, measurement period and limitations.

## Economic metrics

- transactions completed
- refunds and disputes
- creator/freelancer payouts
- marketplace liquidity
- education access
- assistance delivered through verified partners

## Research metrics

- canonical sources preserved
- claims normalized
- evidence mapped
- counter-evidence recorded
- independent reviews completed
- verified claims, where the evidence contract is satisfied

## Dashboard principle

Do not collapse all dimensions into one “overall truth” or “overall success” percentage. Public dashboards should show separate, auditable dimensions.

## Long-term aspiration

A global user/community target may be displayed as an aspiration or roadmap objective. It must never be represented as current users, guaranteed adoption, or a forecast unless measured data supports that statement.
