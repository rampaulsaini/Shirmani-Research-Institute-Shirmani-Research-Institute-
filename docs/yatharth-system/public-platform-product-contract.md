# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Product & Automation Contract

## Purpose

This document defines the next public-facing implementation layer for the Shirmani Research Institute repository.

The target is a unified public platform in which a person can create an account, learn, publish, collaborate, offer services, sell permitted digital products, discover opportunities, use AI-assisted tools, and participate in research and community activities.

The platform vision is public-interest oriented: useful participation, safety, transparency, customer/user satisfaction, environmental stewardship, and access to knowledge and opportunity.

## Public account and identity

A registered user may, subject to applicable law and platform rules:
- create and maintain a profile;
- publish posts and media;
- follow or collaborate with other users;
- create creator/service/store pages;
- offer permitted services or products;
- purchase permitted products or services;
- participate in education and research;
- review transactions and request support;
- export or request deletion of their eligible data.

Identity, privacy, age/eligibility, payments and moderation requirements must be implemented according to the relevant jurisdiction and risk level.

## User-value loop

Create → Share → Discover → Learn → Collaborate → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve

The system should optimize for useful outcomes and satisfaction, not merely maximum screen time.

Public metrics should include, where measurable:
- successful task completion;
- service/product quality;
- support response time;
- complaint resolution;
- repeat voluntary use;
- learning completion;
- creator earnings;
- marketplace fulfillment;
- safety incidents and resolution;
- environmental/public-interest outcomes.

No metric should be presented as proof of universal satisfaction without representative evidence.

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission contract

### L1 — Intake Agent
Accept structured and unstructured user requests/content.

### L2 — NLP Understanding Agent
Language detection, normalization, semantic extraction, entity/concept linking and intent classification.

### L3 — Safety & Rights Agent
Detect spam, abuse, privacy risks, prohibited content, suspected fraud and rights/consent issues; route uncertain cases.

### L4 — Discovery & Recommendation Agent
Personalize discovery using explicit user controls and safety constraints. Avoid hidden manipulation and provide meaningful controls where required.

### L5 — Marketplace Matching Agent
Match users with jobs, freelance services, courses, products and research opportunities.

### L6 — Research/Evidence Agent
Separate author source, normalized claims, evidence, counter-evidence and independent verification.

### L7 — Transaction/Operations Agent
Coordinate orders, delivery status, service workflows, notifications and support routing.

### L8 — Education Agent
Support learning paths, explanations, translation, practice and progress tracking without fabricating credentials.

### L9 — Creator/Media Agent
Assist with editing, metadata, multilingual transformation and publishing workflows.

### L10 — Audit Agent
Continuously check provenance, contracts, anomalies, failed workflows and status drift.

### L11 — Human Review Router
Route high-impact, ambiguous or appealed cases to accountable human reviewers.

### L12 — Federation Agent
Coordinate approved repositories/services with least-privilege access and explicit delivery receipts.

### L13 — Public Presentation Agent
Expose capability status, limitations, uncertainty, evidence and appeal/support paths.

## High-impact boundary

AI/ML/NLP/Automission must not become an unchecked sovereign authority.

At minimum, accountable human processes remain required for:
- elections and civic rights;
- judicial determinations;
- irreversible punishment or rights removal;
- major financial/regulated decisions;
- identity disputes;
- serious safety incidents;
- independent verification;
- appeals;
- changes to the platform's own verification rules.

## Essential-needs objective

The research vision may study universal access to food, basic shelter, healthcare, education, clean water, sanitation and other essential services.

The software must distinguish policy objective from actual entitlement or delivery. A public promise becomes an operational service only when funding, supply, infrastructure, eligibility, lawful authority, delivery and measurable outcomes exist.

## Nature / Earth protection

The public platform may track forests and biodiversity; air and water quality; soil and land use; oceans and marine systems; climate indicators; waste and pollution; restoration commitments; and space-environment observations.

AI-generated estimates must remain distinguishable from measured observations and independently sourced evidence.

## Release lifecycle

Every public capability follows:
PLANNED → ARCHITECTURE_READY → IMPLEMENTATION_IN_PROGRESS → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → SECURITY_REVIEWED → MONITORED → LIVE

A capability cannot become LIVE merely because documentation, an AI response, a workflow run or a GitHub check succeeded.

## Verification boundary

independent_verified_claims remains a separate counter.

AI output, workflow success, publication, hashes, API success or platform completion do not independently verify philosophical, scientific, historical or metaphysical claims.
