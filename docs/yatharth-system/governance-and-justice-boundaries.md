# Governance, Justice and Neutrality Boundaries

## Purpose

Define how the platform can support fairness, transparency and public accountability without replacing legitimate democratic, legal or judicial institutions.

## Separation of roles

The platform may provide:

- evidence organization;
- public information;
- policy/document comparison;
- complaint intake;
- workflow routing;
- transparency dashboards;
- audit trails;
- lawful dispute-resolution support.

It must not present the platform or its AI agents as a supreme sovereign authority.

## Political and institutional neutrality

Public information about governments, presidents/prime ministers, legislatures, courts and public officials must be descriptive and sourced.

The platform must not use personalization or recommendation systems to covertly manipulate political preferences.

## Justice workflow

Report → classify → acknowledge → evidence collection → lawful routing → human review where required → decision by competent authority → appeal/response → audit record.

AI may assist with administration and evidence organization but should not be represented as a court or as the final legal decision-maker.

## Fairness controls

- consistent published rules;
- explainable decision categories;
- notice to affected users;
- appeal mechanisms;
- anti-discrimination testing;
- audit logs;
- conflict-of-interest controls;
- independent review for high-impact claims.

## Essential-service decisions

Where the platform participates in distributing assistance, eligibility rules must be explicit, auditable and lawful. Automated decisions affecting essential access require human escalation and an appeal path.

## Yatharth Justice

“Yatharth Justice” is maintained as the author's proposed framework for fairness and justice research. Its concepts should be documented, compared with established legal and ethical frameworks, and independently reviewed before being described as validated principles.

## Verification boundary

Independent verification remains separate from AI workflow completion, CI success, publication, popularity or author testimony.
