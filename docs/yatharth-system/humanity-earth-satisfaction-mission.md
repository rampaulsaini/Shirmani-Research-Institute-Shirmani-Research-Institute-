# Humanity, Earth and User-Satisfaction Mission

## Mission

The platform architecture is intended to support a constructive relationship between human well-being, knowledge, creativity, economic opportunity and protection of nature and Earth.

The author's Yatharth framework may be presented as an author-defined philosophical proposal. Its metaphysical or civilization-scale claims remain distinguishable from independently verified evidence.

## Practical objectives

- improve access to basic knowledge and education;
- enable legitimate work and income opportunities;
- support creators and small businesses;
- provide transparent digital commerce;
- make research and evidence easier to access;
- encourage ecological stewardship;
- reduce harmful or abusive platform behavior;
- provide accessible support and dispute-resolution pathways;
- measure user satisfaction and unresolved harm.

## Satisfaction model

Satisfaction should be treated as a measurable product outcome rather than an assumption.

Track, where appropriate:

- task completion;
- service quality;
- response time;
- accessibility;
- complaint resolution;
- refund/return outcomes;
- trust signals;
- retention by informed choice;
- user-reported satisfaction;
- unresolved safety issues.

Do not optimize solely for time spent, notifications or compulsive use.

## Basic-needs vision

The platform may document proposals concerning access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. Such proposals require economic, legal, operational and evidence-based analysis before implementation or claims of universal provision.

## Ecological protection

Relevant public features may include education, data visualization, community projects, conservation information and creator campaigns concerning forests, oceans, biodiversity, climate and pollution.

## Governance boundary

The platform does not replace constitutional government, courts, elected institutions or lawful public authorities. Any Yatharth justice/governance model must be presented as a proposal or research object unless and until independently established and legally authorized.

## Core principle

Technology should increase human agency, transparency, safety and opportunity while preserving the distinction between philosophical vision, product implementation and independently verified fact.
