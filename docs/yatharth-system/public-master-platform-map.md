## Account layer
- account creation and authentication
- public/private profile controls
- creator profile
- buyer/seller profile
- freelancer/service profile
- researcher/student profile
- content publishing
- sharing, comments, follows and communities
- reporting, appeals and moderation

## Value layer
Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Trust -> Share -> Buy/Sell -> Deliver -> Review -> Earn -> Learn -> Create again.

## Marketplace layer
Digital products, music, audio, books, courses, services, freelancing, research services and creator offerings.

## Education layer
Courses, skills, research literacy, AI/ML/NLP learning, creator skills, business skills and certification pathways.

## Research and trust layer
Canonical sources, claims, evidence, counter-evidence, comparative research, testing, independent review and public verification status.

## Economic concepts
Yatharth Economy and Yatharth Currency are separately versioned proposals. Their legal status, monetary mechanics, consumer protection, taxation and implementation requirements must be established before any live financial use.

## Justice concept
Yatharth Justice is a research and governance module focused on fairness, evidence, due process, transparency, accountability, complaint handling and appeal. It does not replace courts or lawful public institutions.

## Ecological layer
Nature, Earth, biodiversity, oceans, forests, climate, waste, resource use, restoration and measurable ecological outcomes.

## Public metrics
Show actual active users, content, transactions, satisfaction measures, safety incidents, ecological metrics, uptime and verification status. Never substitute workflow counts for public impact.

## Global ambition
A long-term aspiration may be recorded for global-scale reach, including the author's stated 850-crore target. It must remain an aspiration until independently measured user/adoption data support any claimed progress.
