# Account, Creator and Marketplace Contract

## Account capabilities
1. Secure registration and authentication.
2. Profile management.
3. Creator, learner, freelancer, researcher, buyer, seller and community roles.
4. Publishing of permitted text, images, audio, video, music, documents and products.
5. Services, courses, digital products and marketplace listings.
6. Orders, delivery and customer communication.
7. Ratings, reviews, disputes and transaction records.
8. Privacy controls and eligible data export/deletion.

## Marketplace lifecycle
Draft -> automated checks -> publish -> discovery -> order -> payment -> delivery -> customer confirmation -> review -> dispute/appeal when needed -> settlement.

AI agents may assist with categorization, translation, search, matching, content quality checks, fraud signals, customer support and routing. High-impact enforcement and financial actions require safeguards, auditability and appeal paths.

Reviews, verification badges and recommendation signals must be traceable to their basis. AI confidence is not independent human verification.
