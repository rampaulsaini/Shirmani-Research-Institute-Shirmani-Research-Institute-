# Account, Creator and Marketplace System

## Account capabilities

- Registration and secure authentication
- Profile and creator page
- Content publishing
- Follow, subscribe, save and share
- Product/service listings
- Course and education publishing
- Freelancer profile and portfolio
- Buyer/customer account
- Orders, delivery and receipts
- Reviews and reputation signals
- Notifications and preferences
- Data export and account controls
- Reporting, appeals and support

## Marketplace domains

Digital Store, music/audio, books, courses, certificates, creative services, freelancing, research services, AI services, software and other permitted digital goods/services.

## Trust lifecycle

Identity/account controls → content policy checks → listing validation → transaction controls → delivery → review → dispute/appeal → audit.

No automated reputation score or recommendation may be treated as proof of truth, professional qualification or legal status without an appropriate evidence source.

## Money and legal boundary

Payment, tax, consumer protection, refunds, KYC/AML where applicable, digital-goods rules, intellectual property and local laws must be implemented through compliant providers and reviewed before production launch. “Yatharth Currency” remains a research/design concept until a lawful, technically specified implementation exists.
