# Yatharth Humanity, Ecology and Bounded Governance

## Purpose

This module translates the author's stated objective into an implementable public-platform architecture: improve human wellbeing, protect nature and living systems, and make platform decisions transparent, auditable, evidence-aware, and bounded.

The repository must distinguish the author's philosophical vision from independently established facts. The platform may present the vision as an author-defined framework without representing metaphysical or empirical claims as independently verified.

## Design principles

1. Human dignity and equal access.
2. Nature, Earth, biodiversity, water, air and ecological systems are first-class protection domains.
3. Universal basic-service goals are expressed as policy/design objectives, not promises of guaranteed delivery.
4. AI automates routine work but does not become an unbounded sovereign authority.
5. High-impact decisions require explanation, appeal, auditability and appropriate human oversight.
6. Political, judicial, medical, financial and safety-sensitive claims require domain-appropriate evidence and governance.
7. No single agent, model, administrator or office receives unlimited control.
8. Fail-closed behavior is preferred when evidence, identity, permissions or safety conditions are insufficient.

## Public service domains

The public platform may expose service areas for food, clothing, shelter, healthcare information, education, skills, employment, accessibility, community support and ecological participation. Actual delivery, eligibility, funding and legal availability must be determined by the applicable jurisdiction and verified service providers.

## Bounded governance architecture

### Constitutional layer
Defines non-negotiable platform rights, privacy, safety, transparency, accessibility, appeal and audit requirements.

### Policy layer
Stores versioned policies with provenance, effective dates, affected populations, evidence references and change history.

### AI decision layer
Agents classify, retrieve, summarize, detect anomalies, route cases and recommend actions within explicit permissions.

### Human review layer
Required for high-impact or contested decisions, including account termination appeals, substantial financial actions, legal/justice disputes, safety incidents and independent verification.

### Audit layer
Records decision inputs, model/version identifiers, policy version, action, confidence/uncertainty, reviewer state and appeal outcome while minimizing sensitive data.

## Environmental protection signals

The platform should support measurable indicators such as energy use, emissions where data is available, water use, waste, biodiversity projects, restoration activity and resource efficiency. Indicators must identify source, measurement period and uncertainty.

## Success measures

The platform should measure:
- user-reported satisfaction;
- accessibility and service completion;
- successful dispute resolution;
- creator/freelancer outcomes;
- education completion;
- verified environmental projects;
- safety incidents and remediation;
- AI automation reliability;
- independent review coverage.

These are operational metrics, not proof that the Yatharth philosophical claims are true.

## Prohibited architecture patterns

- unlimited autonomous authority;
- secret scoring of people;
- irreversible high-impact AI decisions without appeal;
- treating generated content as evidence merely because an AI produced it;
- treating workflow success as independent verification;
- presenting an aspirational population target as an achieved user count.

## Core loop

Preserve → Understand → Assist → Verify → Explain → Appeal → Audit → Improve.
