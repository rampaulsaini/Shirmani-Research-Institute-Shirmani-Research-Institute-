# Yatharth Public Super Platform Roadmap

## Public product vision
A multilingual, accessible platform combining social sharing, research, education, creativity, commerce, freelancing, employment, community and environmental-purpose services.

## Public domains
- Social profiles and user publishing
- Communities and messaging
- Research and knowledge library
- Education and skills
- AI/ML/NLP tools
- Yatharth AI Music and creative studio
- Digital Store and digital products
- Freelancing and service marketplace
- Employment and opportunity discovery
- Creator economy
- Yatharth Economy proposal
- Yatharth Currency proposal
- Yatharth Justice research/proposal
- Nature, Earth and humanity protection
- Evidence and independent verification
- Public transparency dashboard
- Support and dispute/appeal channels

## Account model
A user may have a profile, creator identity, buyer/seller roles, learner/teacher roles, freelancer/client roles, researcher/contributor roles, and community participation—subject to verification, privacy and platform rules.

## Publish/share/sell lifecycle
Create → safety/privacy checks → classify → publish/list → discover → interact → purchase/contract → deliver → review/appeal → audit → improve.

## Customer-value loop
Discover → understand value → use/buy → receive support → provide feedback → resolve problems → return voluntarily.

## Global scalability
Design targets include multilingual support, mobile-first UX, accessibility, low-bandwidth operation, localization, privacy, security, and resilient infrastructure.

## 850-crore target
The stated 850-crore consumer target is recorded as a long-term aspiration, not a guaranteed forecast or current user count.

## Delivery phases
### Phase A — Foundation
Identity, profiles, content, search, moderation, audit, public status.

### Phase B — Creator & commerce
Store, digital products, payments integration, creator tools, freelancing and services.

### Phase C — Education & research
Courses, learning paths, research library, evidence registry, AI research tools.

### Phase D — Creative AI
AI music, audio, video, multilingual creation and creator workflows.

### Phase E — Trust & governance
Independent review, appeals, transparency, justice research, safety and environmental reporting.

### Phase F — Federation
Approved cross-repository Automission, scalable AI services, public APIs and international localization.

Every phase must report actual deployment separately from planned architecture.
