## Account capabilities

Authenticated users should be able to:

- create and manage a profile
- publish permitted text, image, audio and video
- follow, discover, save and share content
- create communities
- list permitted digital products
- offer permitted freelance services
- create educational material
- participate in research
- receive orders or service requests
- manage their own content and transactions
- view reputation, reviews and satisfaction signals
- control privacy and account settings
- submit complaints and appeals

## Marketplace loop

**Create → Publish → Discover → Trust → Buy/Engage → Deliver → Review → Support → Improve**

The platform should not manufacture reviews, fabricate demand, or misrepresent verification.

## AI-native assistance

Users may receive AI assistance for:

- writing and translation
- content organization
- accessibility
- discovery and search
- product/service matching
- learning pathways
- research navigation
- creator workflows
- customer support
- moderation and safety signals

AI recommendations should be transparent enough to distinguish automated assistance from human decisions where relevant.

## Public status

The public interface should show implementation status by feature rather than implying that every planned service is already operational.

Suggested labels:

**Planned | In Build | Testing | Live | Limited Pilot | Temporarily Unavailable**

## Customer satisfaction

Measure satisfaction using multiple signals rather than maximizing time-on-platform alone:

- task completion
- repeat voluntary use
- support resolution
- refunds/disputes
- accessibility
- content quality
- safety reports
- user feedback

The product objective is durable user value and satisfaction, not compulsive engagement.

## Economic participation

The platform can connect creators, educators, researchers, freelancers, businesses and consumers through permitted commerce and service mechanisms.

Any currency, payment, financial product or marketplace mechanism must be implemented only with appropriate legal, compliance and payment infrastructure.

## Protection mission

The public product should make it easy to discover and participate in projects concerning:

- nature
- forests
- oceans
- biodiversity
- pollution reduction
- education
- human wellbeing
- scientific research
- community support

## Non-negotiable separation

Public product claims must not imply:

- universal availability when a service is not deployed
- independent verification where only automation exists
- legal authority that the platform does not possess
- scientific certainty for metaphysical or experiential claims
- guaranteed income, employment, health or housing outcomes

This separation protects users while allowing the Yatharth vision to be developed progressively.
