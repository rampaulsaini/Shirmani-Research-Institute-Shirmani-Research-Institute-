# Yatharth Humanity Operating System

Purpose: define a public-platform architecture centered on human wellbeing, nature, Earth, living beings, evidence, fairness, and sustainable resource use.

## Core loop
Observe -> understand -> compare -> test -> protect -> improve -> audit -> learn -> repeat.

## Scope
- individual and family wellbeing
- community and public services
- education and skills
- employment and freelancing
- creator economy and digital commerce
- research, evidence and independent verification
- nature, forests, oceans, biodiversity and environmental stewardship
- transparent governance and appeal

## AI/ML/NLP role
Agents may classify, translate, retrieve, summarize, detect anomalies, match needs to services, monitor workflows, and propose actions. High-impact decisions require explicit policy, auditability, appeal, and appropriate human oversight.

## Public-service principle
Universal/basic-access ideas are recorded as proposed Yatharth models. They are not represented as currently funded or legally available services until implementation evidence exists.

## Dignity and non-harm
Optimization must not treat engagement time as the sole objective. User welfare, privacy, safety, accessibility, informed choice, and meaningful value are explicit objectives.

## Governance
References to PM, president, courts, or supreme control are treated as governance-design concepts. The platform remains neutral toward political actors and requires applicable law, due process, separation of powers, transparency, and appeal.

## Metrics
Track separately: satisfaction, service completion, safety, verified evidence, environmental outcomes, education outcomes, employment outcomes, accessibility, automation reliability, and independent review status.

Automation success never becomes proof that a substantive social, scientific, environmental, or metaphysical claim is true.
