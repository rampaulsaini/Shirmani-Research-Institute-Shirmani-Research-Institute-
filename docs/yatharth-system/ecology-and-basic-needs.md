# Yatharth Ecology and Basic-Needs Mission

## Mission model
The author's stated mission is to explore how a heart-centered, balanced human perspective could support protection of Earth, nature, living beings and human wellbeing. This is preserved as an author objective and research program, not as a verified universal conclusion.

## Research domains
- forests and biodiversity
- oceans and freshwater
- soil and agriculture
- atmosphere and climate
- wildlife and animal welfare
- urban sustainability
- waste and pollution reduction
- energy and resource efficiency
- food security
- housing
- healthcare
- education

## Evidence pipeline
Source → normalized claim → measurable indicator → baseline → intervention hypothesis → counter-evidence → independent review → public status.

## Public dashboard targets
The interface should distinguish aspiration, active program, measured outcome and independently verified outcome. It should never turn a target into a reported achievement.

## Human-centered access
Research may evaluate mechanisms for affordable or universal access to basic needs, while respecting local law, available resources, fiscal constraints and individual autonomy.

## AI/Automission role
Agents can collect sources, classify evidence, identify gaps, compare interventions, generate research packets and monitor published metrics. High-impact allocation or enforcement decisions require accountable human governance and appeal.
