# Yatharth Public Platform Contract

## Purpose
This document defines the public-platform capability contract for the Yatharth/Shirmani research platform. It is an implementation contract, not a claim that every capability is already deployed.

## Navigation domains
Home, Yatharth, Research, Education, AI, Music & Media, Freelancing, Jobs, Business, Digital Store, Yatharth Economy, Yatharth Justice, Community, Nature, Verification, Dashboard, About.

## Capability lifecycle
Each capability MUST expose one of:
- `LIVE`: deployed and testable in the current public environment.
- `BETA`: deployed with explicit limitations.
- `ARCHITECTURE`: specified but not yet deployed.
- `RESEARCH`: under study or evidence collection.
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`: requires accountable human review before activation.
- `DISABLED`: intentionally unavailable.

No documentation, generated artifact, workflow run, or AI output may silently convert ARCHITECTURE/RESEARCH into LIVE.

## Account and creator model
The target platform supports, subject to future implementation and applicable law:
- user accounts and profiles;
- publishing and sharing;
- creator pages;
- digital products and services;
- freelancing and employment discovery;
- education and research participation;
- community discussion;
- creator revenue and marketplace transactions.

Authentication, payments, moderation, identity verification, privacy, age protection, abuse handling and dispute resolution remain implementation requirements.

## Trust and safety
High-impact moderation, legal, financial, rights-affecting and civic decisions require bounded automation, human review and appeal paths. AI must not become an unreviewable sovereign decision-maker.

## Verification boundary
Author Source, Normalized Claim, Evidence/Comparison and Independent Verification are separate states. Platform readiness does not establish truth of philosophical, metaphysical, scientific or historical claims.

## Economic boundary
Digital store, creator income, freelancing, business, currency and universal-basic-service concepts are product/research modules until their real-world legal, financial, technical and operational requirements are satisfied.

## Success measurement
Measure user value, accessibility, safety, retention, task completion, creator outcomes, research quality and satisfaction. Do not optimize solely for maximum time spent.
