# Human–Earth Service Charter

## Mission

The Yatharth platform is intended to support a shift from narrow self-interest toward a broader perspective that considers the individual, family, society, humanity, living systems and the Earth together.

This is a design objective, not a scientifically established claim about all humans or all historical eras.

## Design priorities

1. Human dignity and basic needs.
2. Protection of nature, biodiversity and ecosystems.
3. Education and accessible knowledge.
4. Health information and access pathways.
5. Food, clothing and shelter information/services where the platform can lawfully and practically assist.
6. Employment, freelancing and creator opportunities.
7. Transparent commerce.
8. Fair complaint, appeal and dispute processes.
9. Responsible AI and privacy.
10. Evidence-based public knowledge.

## Universal-access principle

The platform may provide free or subsidized access to selected information, learning resources, community services or digital tools when resources and applicable law permit.

A future proposal for universally free physical necessities must remain a policy/economic proposal until its funding, logistics, legal authority, safety and sustainability are independently established.

## Nature protection layer

The platform should help users learn about environmental impacts, discover conservation resources, document local environmental observations, participate in lawful restoration projects, compare evidence and sources, track measurable environmental indicators, and identify actions with documented benefits.

AI recommendations must distinguish evidence from opinion and uncertainty.

## Social and economic layer

A person should be able to move through:

Learn → Create → Publish → Serve → Sell → Earn → Support others

The platform should measure whether users actually receive value, not merely how long they remain online.

## Satisfaction principle

The design target is:

usefulness + trust + accessibility + safety + fair value + responsive support + meaningful participation

Longer usage is not itself treated as proof of satisfaction.

## Governance principle

The platform's governance layer should be transparent, reviewable and appealable.

AI may assist public administration workflows, but should not be presented as a substitute for legitimate human institutions or applicable law.

## Scope

This charter guides product and automation design. It does not itself establish a government, legal authority, currency, justice system, medical service, or scientific proof.
