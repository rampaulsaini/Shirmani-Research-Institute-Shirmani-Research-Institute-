## Completion rule

Do not convert an unchecked item into LIVE merely because a document, workflow, generated page, or AI output exists. Promotion requires the applicable implementation, tests, security/privacy controls, monitoring, user-facing status, rollback/appeal path and—where claims are involved—appropriate evidence and independent review.

## Continuity rule

**देखें → समझें → तथ्य/अनुभव अलग करें → प्राथमिकता चुनें → छोटा कार्य करें → परिणाम दर्ज करें → जाँचें → सीखें → सुधारें → दोहराएँ।**

The goal is not to declare everything finished at once. The goal is to keep the next useful, verifiable step visible and continue it safely.
