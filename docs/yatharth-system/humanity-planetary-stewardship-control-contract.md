# Yatharth Humanity & Planetary Stewardship Control Contract

## Purpose

This contract translates the Yatharth platform's stated mission into an auditable operating architecture for human wellbeing, ecological protection, knowledge access, essential services, and accountable AI-assisted administration.

The document records the author's proposed framework. It does not establish that the framework is scientifically verified, legally authoritative, or universally accepted.

## Core orientation

The platform should optimize for:

1. human dignity and agency;
2. protection of living systems and the physical environment;
3. access to essential needs and knowledge;
4. transparent and evidence-aware decision making;
5. fair opportunity to create, learn, work, trade, and participate;
6. continuous measurement of user and ecological outcomes;
7. reversible, auditable automation.

The phrase "heart-view" is treated here as the author's conceptual orientation toward completeness, empathy, transparency, and continuity. It must not be converted into an automated claim about a person's inner state.

## Scope

The control plane spans:

- family and community;
- education and lifelong learning;
- healthcare access;
- food, clothing, shelter, water and sanitation;
- employment and freelancing;
- creator economy and digital commerce;
- research and independent verification;
- nature, forests, oceans, biodiversity and climate-related stewardship;
- infrastructure and public services;
- justice, complaints, mediation and appeals;
- AI/ML/NLP and Automission;
- public dashboards and transparency.

## AI/ML/NLP Automission layers

### L0 — Human rights, safety and legal constraints

Hard constraints are evaluated before optimization. The system must not trade away fundamental safety, privacy, dignity, or lawful rights merely to increase engagement, revenue, or efficiency.

### L1 — Observation

Collect only necessary, consented and lawful signals. Preserve provenance and uncertainty.

### L2 — NLP understanding

Extract topics, claims, requests, needs, risks, sentiment where appropriate, entities, languages and relationships without treating inference as fact.

### L3 — ML decision support

Use models for prediction, matching, recommendation, anomaly detection and prioritization only within documented performance and uncertainty bounds.

### L4 — Agent orchestration

Specialized agents coordinate research, education, commerce, support, moderation, accessibility, translation, environmental monitoring and operations.

### L5 — Automission execution

Routine low-risk actions may be automated. Every consequential action must have a traceable policy, input, output, timestamp and rollback/appeal path.

### L6 — Continuous audit

Monitor safety, fairness, reliability, privacy, model drift, false positives, false negatives, outages and unintended effects.

### L7 — Human review gate

High-impact actions require qualified human review and an appeal path. This includes serious account sanctions, large financial actions, disputes, justice-related decisions, essential-service denial, and changes with material effects on people or ecosystems.

## Essential-service objective

The proposed public platform may research and support universal access models for:

- nutritious food;
- basic clothing;
- safe housing;
- clean water and sanitation;
- essential healthcare;
- education and learning;
- digital access;
- safety and emergency support.

Any real-world implementation must be tested against available resources, local law, fiscal sustainability, supply capacity, and measurable outcomes. The platform must not claim that these services are already universally free unless they are actually funded, available, and verified.

## Ecological stewardship

Create public indicators for:

- forest protection and restoration;
- biodiversity;
- water quality and availability;
- ocean and marine ecosystem health;
- air quality;
- soil health;
- waste and pollution;
- energy/resource efficiency;
- habitat protection;
- ecological risk.

AI recommendations must prefer prevention, conservation and measurable restoration where supported by evidence. Environmental claims remain evidence-linked rather than assumed.

## Justice and fairness architecture

The public system should provide:

- transparent rules;
- evidence records;
- complaint intake;
- mediation;
- independent review;
- appeal;
- decision explanations;
- conflict-of-interest controls;
- immutable audit trails where appropriate.

The AI layer is a decision-support and administrative system, not an unchecked sovereign authority.

## Governance separation

The architecture must keep separate:

- elected/public governance;
- courts and independent adjudication;
- administration;
- scientific/research review;
- platform operations;
- AI systems.

No single AI agent, administrator, office, or platform owner should have unrestricted control over all layers.

## User economy

Every account may have, subject to platform rules and applicable law:

- profile;
- social publishing;
- creator tools;
- digital products;
- store;
- freelancing;
- employment opportunities;
- courses;
- music/audio/video;
- services;
- community participation;
- earnings and transaction history.

Marketplace trust must combine identity/privacy controls, provenance, reviews, dispute resolution, fraud detection and human appeal.

## Public satisfaction loop

Measure more than time spent:

**Need → Discover → Learn/Create → Participate → Buy/Sell/Work → Receive Value → Review → Resolve Problems → Improve**

Primary indicators should include:

- satisfaction;
- successful task completion;
- safety;
- accessibility;
- affordability;
- reliability;
- complaint resolution;
- creator/freelancer outcomes;
- learning outcomes;
- environmental outcomes.

Engagement must not be the sole optimization target.

## Status contract

Every capability uses:

**PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → MONITORED → AUTONOMOUS**

"Autonomous" means bounded, observable, reversible automation—not unrestricted control.

## Verification rule

A workflow success, CI pass, generated document, AI output, citation, hash, or deployment status is not by itself independent scientific or social verification.

Claims about the Yatharth framework remain classified as:

- Author Source;
- Normalized Claim;
- Evidence Mapped;
- Comparative Review;
- Independent Review;
- Verified;
- Partially Supported;
- Inconclusive;
- Not Verified.

## Long-term vision

The platform may use the author's stated civilization-scale vision as a research and product objective: moving from individual wellbeing toward family, community, country, humanity, Earth and the wider physical environment.

The system must pursue that vision through measurable, evidence-aware, non-coercive and reversible improvements rather than assuming that any single philosophy, person, AI system or institution has already achieved the desired state.
