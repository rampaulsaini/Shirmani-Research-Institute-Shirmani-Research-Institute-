# Humanity, Nature and Earth Protection Layer

## Purpose

Translate the project's stated objective of improving protection of people, living systems and the natural environment into measurable platform functions without presenting philosophical or metaphysical claims as scientific facts.

## Public functions

- environmental knowledge and education;
- conservation projects and participation;
- evidence-based sustainability resources;
- community reporting and observation;
- responsible consumption guidance;
- transparent impact reporting;
- research on human–nature relationships;
- support for lawful humanitarian initiatives.

## Measurement

Potential public metrics include:
- verified projects;
- documented conservation actions;
- participation;
- resources distributed;
- outcomes with evidence;
- unresolved reports;
- safety incidents;
- accessibility and geographic coverage.

## Principle

The platform should seek to reduce preventable harm and increase access to useful knowledge and basic services, while clearly distinguishing proposals, aspirations, empirical findings and independently verified results.
