# Public Capability Matrix

Status vocabulary:
- SPECIFIED: public contract exists
- PROTOTYPE: implementation exists but is incomplete
- LIVE: deployed and usable for the stated capability
- AUTOMATED: routine operation is automated
- AUTONOMOUS: bounded autonomous operation has passed its release gate
- VERIFIED: independent verification requirement satisfied where applicable

| Domain | Public capability | Target automation | Release requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social | Account/profile | High | Identity, privacy, abuse controls |
| Social | Post/share/media | High | Moderation, copyright, reporting |
| Community | Groups/following | High | Safety and privacy |
| Research | Claim/evidence library | High | Provenance and traceability |
| Research | Independent verification | Assisted | Independent human review |
| Education | Courses/learning | High | Content quality/accessibility |
| Creative | Music/audio/video | High | Rights and content policy |
| Marketplace | Digital Store | High | Payments, refunds, seller rules |
| Freelancing | Services/jobs | High | Identity, dispute and fraud controls |
| Business | Listings/discovery | High | Trust and reporting |
| Economy | Yatharth economic model | Assisted | Legal/economic review |
| Currency | Yatharth currency concept | Assisted | Legal/compliance review before deployment |
| Justice | Yatharth justice research | Assisted | Due process, appeals, human authority |
| Nature | Earth/biodiversity initiatives | High | Evidence and impact measurement |
| AI | AI assistant/services | High | Safety, privacy, observability |
| AI/ML/NLP | semantic understanding | High | Evaluation benchmark |
| Automission | workflow orchestration | High | Bounded permissions and rollback |
| Trust | fraud/abuse detection | High | False-positive monitoring and appeals |
| Support | customer support | High | Escalation to humans |
| Governance | public status/audit | High | Immutable/auditable records |

The matrix is deliberately conservative: a design or workflow run does not automatically change a feature to LIVE, AUTONOMOUS or VERIFIED.
