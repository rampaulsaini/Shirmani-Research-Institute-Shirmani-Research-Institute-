# Public Platform Completion Roadmap

## Phase A — Foundation
Accounts, profiles, consent, content objects, search, media, notifications and accessibility.

## Phase B — Creator and commerce
Digital Store, digital products, creator profiles, freelancing, employment, service listings, checkout/payment integration and dispute workflows.

## Phase C — Knowledge and education
Research library, evidence registry, education, courses, learning paths, certificates and multilingual AI assistance.

## Phase D — Yatharth AI
AI assistant, NLP claim extraction, semantic search, translation, recommendation, AI Music and creative tooling.

## Phase E — Trust and public-interest services
Verification dashboard, complaints/appeals, justice research, transparency, nature/Earth projects and public-service information.

## Phase F — Autonomous operations
Agent federation, Automission supervisor, continuous audit, resilience, incident response, policy versioning and controlled deployment.

## Gate
Each phase moves from SPECIFIED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → OBSERVED. "Implemented" or "workflow success" alone does not mean LIVE.
