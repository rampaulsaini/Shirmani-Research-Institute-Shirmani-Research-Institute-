# Humanity, Nature and Essential-Welfare Charter

## Purpose

Translate the stated Yatharth mission into measurable platform design requirements without presenting the philosophical vision as an already-established scientific or social result.

## Scope

The platform should consider, where legally and operationally feasible:

- human dignity and equal access;
- food, clothing, shelter, education, and access to essential services;
- healthcare information and navigation;
- environmental protection;
- forests and biodiversity;
- oceans and freshwater;
- air quality and climate-related risk;
- animal welfare;
- responsible use of natural resources.

## Product requirements

### Essential-access layer
Create public information and service-discovery pathways for essential needs. Where the platform itself provides a service, clearly state its actual scope, eligibility, funding, and geographic availability.

### Nature layer
Expose educational resources, conservation projects, verified data, local participation opportunities, and environmental impact metrics.

### Wellbeing layer
Measure satisfaction, accessibility, unresolved complaints, safety incidents, and service quality rather than optimizing only for time-on-platform.

### Opportunity layer
Connect education, skills, freelancing, employment, creators, and permitted commerce.

## Measurement

Suggested public metrics:

- user-reported satisfaction;
- successful task completion;
- accessibility coverage;
- unresolved complaint age;
- verified environmental projects;
- educational completion;
- creator/freelancer successful transactions;
- safety incidents and resolution;
- platform availability;
- model error and escalation rates.

## Important limitation

A platform cannot guarantee that nobody will ever suffer, nor can an AI system independently establish a universal social or moral truth. The system should instead continuously reduce preventable harm, expose uncertainty, and provide accessible correction and appeal mechanisms.

## Governance principle

The phrase “supreme control” is implemented here as a constraint hierarchy: rights, safety, law, evidence, transparency, and appealability take precedence over engagement or commercial optimization.
