# Yatharth Global Platform — Humanity, Nature and Universal Wellbeing Architecture

## Purpose

This document defines the public-facing and autonomous-system architecture for the Yatharth Global Platform.

The platform is designed around the author's stated objective: develop a practical digital ecosystem that connects individual wellbeing, family, society, education, livelihood, justice, research, technology, nature, Earth and humanity while preserving evidence boundaries and user agency.

## Core principle

Preserve → Understand → Compare → Test → Review → Improve → Protect

The platform must not present the author's philosophical or experiential claims as independently established scientific facts unless the evidence and independent review requirements are met.

## Public platform domains

1. Social identity, profiles and user-generated content
2. Knowledge and research
3. Education and skills
4. Employment and freelancing
5. Digital store and creator commerce
6. Yatharth AI Music and creative media
7. Business and services marketplace
8. Yatharth economic models and currency research
9. Yatharth justice, fairness, complaints and appeals
10. Nature, Earth, biodiversity and environmental protection
11. Community and participation
12. Multilingual global access
13. Trust, evidence and independent verification
14. Accessibility and essential-service information
15. Public transparency and system-status dashboard

## User participation

A user may create an account and, subject to applicable law and platform rules, publish posts and research; share text, audio, images and video; create communities; offer services and freelance work; list eligible digital products; publish courses and educational resources; create music and media; buy, sell, subscribe or otherwise transact through supported providers; and provide ratings, reviews, feedback and complaints with correction and appeal pathways.

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission architecture

L1 Intake — capture and normalize user, content, commerce and research events.

L2 NLP Understanding — language detection, transcription, translation, semantic extraction, entity/concept linking and structured metadata.

L3 Safety and Trust — spam, abuse, fraud, privacy, copyright and policy checks with fail-closed handling for high-risk cases.

L4 Personalization — user-controlled discovery, search, recommendations and learning paths with transparent controls.

L5 Marketplace Intelligence — match users with relevant services, products, jobs, courses and opportunities.

L6 Research Intelligence — claim extraction, evidence mapping, counter-evidence, comparison and research-packet generation.

L7 Quality and Verification — traceability, reproducibility checks, independent-review queue and verification gate.

L8 Operations — workflow orchestration, monitoring, failure detection, recovery and continuous audit.

L9 Federation — coordinate approved repositories and services while preserving security boundaries and provenance.

L10 Public Presentation — generate current public status, multilingual summaries, dashboards and accessible interfaces from machine-readable records.

## Human oversight boundary

Automation should handle routine operations wherever safe and appropriate, but high-impact decisions must retain human review and appeal pathways. This includes account termination, major financial actions, legal or justice outcomes, sensitive safety decisions and independent verification.

No AI agent may silently convert an author statement into a verified fact.

## Essential-needs objective

The platform may research and support models concerning food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education access. Any promise of free or universal provision must be represented as a proposal or model until a lawful, funded and operational service actually exists.

## Environmental objective

Nature, Earth, biodiversity and resource protection are first-class platform domains. The system should measure and surface environmental impacts where reliable data exists and encourage evidence-based conservation actions.

## Governance

The governance layer must be evidence-aware, transparent, auditable, appealable, privacy-preserving, non-discriminatory, subject to applicable law, explicit about uncertainty and resistant to automated concentration of power.

The platform architecture does not grant a private AI system sovereign authority over governments, courts, citizens or humanity.

## Public status model

Every major capability must carry an explicit status: PLANNED, ARCHITECTURE_DEFINED, IN_DEVELOPMENT, TESTING, PILOT, LIVE, AUTOMATED, INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED, or VERIFIED.

VERIFIED may be assigned only when the defined verification contract is satisfied. Engineering success, workflow success and independent verification must never be conflated.

## Long-term vision

The long-term vision is a multilingual, accessible, trustworthy and highly automated digital ecosystem in which knowledge, creativity, education, livelihood, commerce, community participation and environmental stewardship reinforce one another.

The stated aspiration of reaching billions of users is a future goal, not a current adoption claim or guaranteed outcome.
