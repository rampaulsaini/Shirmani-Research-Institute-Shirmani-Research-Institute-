# Yatharth Global Operating Model

## Purpose

Define the public-platform operating model for the Yatharth ecosystem while preserving a strict distinction between the author's philosophy, documented evidence, independent verification, and implemented software.

## Human-centered objective

The platform is designed around a stated objective of improving human, ecological, educational, creative, and economic wellbeing through a heart-view / impartiality-oriented framework.

The following are design goals, not claims of already-achieved outcomes:

- protect people, animals, plants, oceans, forests, atmosphere, and the wider environment;
- make essential knowledge, education, health-related information, and opportunity easier to access;
- enable users to create, share, learn, collaborate, offer services, and sell permitted digital goods;
- measure user satisfaction, safety, accessibility, and environmental impact;
- keep consequential decisions explainable, reviewable, and appealable.

## Public operating loop

Create → Understand → Verify → Discover → Learn → Collaborate → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve.

## Platform domains

1. Social and community
2. Research and knowledge
3. Education and skills
4. Creator and media
5. AI services

7. Digital store and marketplace
8. Economic experimentation
9. Yatharth AI Music

11. Nature, Earth, and biodiversity
12. Trust, evidence, and independent verification
13. Multilingual accessibility
14. Public transparency and audit

## Non-negotiable boundary

No automated workflow, AI output, QC pass, hash, publication, or successful software run by itself constitutes scientific, legal, medical, or independent human verification.

## Deployment principle

Architecture is not deployment. A feature is labelled Live only after a reproducible public implementation and its tests demonstrate the required behavior.
