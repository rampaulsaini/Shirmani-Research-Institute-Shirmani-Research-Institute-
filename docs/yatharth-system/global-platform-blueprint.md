# Yatharth Global Platform — Public & Autonomous Operating Blueprint

## Purpose

Define the public-facing and machine-operable architecture for a global platform built around the author's Yatharth framework, with emphasis on human dignity, nature/Earth protection, knowledge, education, creativity, economic opportunity, trust and user satisfaction.

This document is an architecture and implementation blueprint. It does not assert that the proposed social, economic, justice, currency, environmental or metaphysical outcomes have already been achieved or independently verified.

## Public product surface

A user may create an account and, subject to applicable law and platform rules:

- create a profile and identity/preferences
- publish text, images, audio, music and video
- follow, communicate and form communities
- learn and teach
- offer services and freelance work
- create and sell permitted digital products
- discover jobs and opportunities
- use AI research, creative and productivity tools
- participate in research and evidence review
- access multilingual content
- submit feedback, complaints and appeals
- view transparent platform, safety and verification status

## Core value loop

Create → Publish → Discover → Learn → Connect → Transact → Deliver → Review → Improve → Earn/Benefit → Contribute

The platform should optimize for user value, safety, accessibility and satisfaction rather than maximizing time-on-platform at any cost.

Agents must be bounded by explicit permissions, audit logs, rollback paths, rate limits and policy contracts.

## Human oversight boundary

Routine operations may be highly automated. High-impact actions require appropriate human review and appeal mechanisms, including major account sanctions, substantial financial disputes, legal/justice decisions, safety escalations and independent scientific verification.

No AI agent may declare a disputed Yatharth/metaphysical claim scientifically verified merely because an automated workflow succeeds.

## Essential public services

### Universal access goals
The project may research and advocate for broad access to essentials such as food, shelter, healthcare and education. Implementation must distinguish aspiration, policy proposal, funding model, legal feasibility and measurable outcomes.

### Nature/Earth protection
Environmental modules should track measurable indicators, interventions, evidence, outcomes and unintended effects rather than relying only on declarations.

### Justice & trust
Justice features must provide transparent rules, evidence handling, conflict-of-interest controls, appeals, auditability and human review. The platform itself is not a substitute for lawful courts or public institutions.

### Yatharth Currency

"Yatharth Currency" is maintained as a separately specified proposal/module. Before any real-world financial use, its legal status, issuance, custody, accounting, consumer protection, taxation, AML/KYC obligations where applicable, exchange model and risk controls must be independently reviewed.

## Verification states

AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → COMPARATIVE_REVIEWED → READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW → INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED → VERIFIED

Possible non-verification outcomes include PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED, INCONCLUSIVE and NOT_VERIFIED.

## Success metrics

Track separately:

- active users
- successful sessions
- user satisfaction
- accessibility
- content quality
- safety incidents
- complaint resolution
- creator earnings
- transaction success
- education outcomes
- research throughput
- evidence coverage
- independent verification
- environmental outcomes
- system reliability

Never collapse these into a single "everything complete" percentage.

## Global-scale aspiration

A target such as 850 crore users/consumers is treated as a long-term aspiration, not a present fact or guaranteed outcome. Scaling plans must be capacity-tested incrementally.

## Deployment rule

Architecture ≠ implementation ≠ production availability ≠ independent verification.

Every feature must expose a machine-readable status such as:
PLANNED | DESIGNED | IMPLEMENTED | TESTED | DEPLOYED | MONITORED | VERIFIED

## Continuous improvement

All new author source material should enter through:

Preserve → Classify → Normalize → Link → Compare → Test → Audit → Independently Review → Publish status

This blueprint is intended to connect the existing Research Institute automation layer to a future public platform without overstating current capabilities.
