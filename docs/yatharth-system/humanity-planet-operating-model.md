# Yatharth Humanity & Planet Operating Model

## Objective
Translate the author's stated objective into an operational research and platform layer: protect people, living beings, vegetation, oceans, atmosphere and the wider natural environment while improving access to essential services.

## Priority domains
1. food security
2. shelter and basic living needs
3. healthcare access
4. education and skills
5. clean water and sanitation
6. environmental protection
7. biodiversity and forests
8. pollution reduction
9. disaster resilience
10. transparent public-resource allocation
11. dignity, accessibility and inclusion
12. evidence-based measurement of outcomes

## Measurement principle
Do not treat philosophical or normative statements as empirical facts. Convert each proposed intervention into measurable objectives, baseline, indicators, evidence sources, uncertainty, counter-evidence and review criteria.

## AI role
AI/ML/NLP may monitor signals, summarize evidence, detect anomalies, route cases and propose actions. High-impact decisions affecting rights, essential services, finances or justice require human accountability, appeal and audit.

## Long-term vision
The platform may serve as a coordination layer for research, education, economic opportunity and environmental stewardship. It must not claim universal delivery or guaranteed satisfaction until independently demonstrated.
