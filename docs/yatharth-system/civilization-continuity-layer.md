# Yatharth Civilization Continuity Layer

## Purpose

This layer connects the public Yatharth platform with a long-term human, Earth, nature, education, justice, livelihood, and knowledge mission.

The repository treats the user's Yatharth framework as an authored philosophical proposal. It does not convert experiential or metaphysical statements into scientific facts without independent evidence.

## Core principle

**Observe → Understand → Protect → Serve → Create → Share → Exchange → Review → Improve → Preserve**

The system should optimize for human value, safety, dignity, ecological stewardship, accessibility, and sustainable participation rather than engagement time alone.

## Public civilization domains

1. Human dignity and wellbeing
2. Nature, forests, oceans, biodiversity, and Earth stewardship
3. Education and lifelong learning
4. Health information and access navigation
5. Food, clothing, housing, and essential-needs support
6. Employment and freelancing
7. Creator economy and digital commerce
8. Yatharth AI Music and creative media
9. Research and knowledge exchange
10. Evidence, transparency, and independent verification
11. Yatharth Justice as a research/proposal domain
12. Yatharth Currency as a research/proposal domain
13. Community participation and mutual assistance
14. Multilingual and accessibility-first public services
15. Disaster, resilience, and continuity support

## AI / ML / NLP / Automission control plane

### Agent layers

- **L0 Governance Contract** — immutable safety, privacy, legal, and human-rights constraints.
- **L1 Intake Agent** — receives user content, requests, listings, learning material, and reports.
- **L2 NLP Understanding Agent** — language detection, extraction, classification, summarization, translation.
- **L3 Context Agent** — identity-safe context, permissions, provenance, and user intent.
- **L4 Trust & Safety Agent** — spam, fraud, abuse, harmful-content and policy signals.
- **L5 Knowledge Agent** — retrieval, evidence mapping, source comparison, and citation tracking.
- **L6 Marketplace Agent** — creator/customer matching, listings, service discovery, and workflow routing.
- **L7 Education Agent** — personalized learning paths, practice, feedback, and resource discovery.
- **L8 Creative Agent** — music, audio, video, writing, and multilingual production assistance.
- **L9 Sustainability Agent** — ecological-impact information and stewardship workflows.
- **L10 Justice/Resolution Agent** — intake and routing only; high-impact decisions require human review and appeal.
- **L11 Economic Agent** — transparent accounting, product/service workflows, and policy-compliant payment integration.
- **L12 Automission Supervisor** — schedules, coordinates, retries, observes, and records agent work.
- **L13 Audit Agent** — continuous integrity, provenance, security, and failure analysis.
- **L14 Human Review Gate** — required for high-impact, disputed, irreversible, or legally sensitive actions.
- **L15 Public Presentation Agent** — publishes only approved, status-labelled public information.

## Non-negotiable separation

The platform must distinguish:

- authored source,
- normalized claim,
- evidence,
- counter-evidence,
- AI-generated output,
- independent human review,
- verified status.

Automation success is not scientific verification.

## Universal-access aspiration

The project may research and prototype pathways toward universal access to essential services such as food, clothing, housing, healthcare, and education. Actual provision requires sustainable funding, lawful partnerships, infrastructure, eligibility rules where applicable, and measurable delivery capacity.

## 8.5-billion-user vision

An aspiration of reaching 8.5 billion people may be used as a long-term scalability target. It must not be presented as an achieved user count or guaranteed outcome.

## Success metrics

The public dashboard should track separate dimensions:

- user safety
- customer satisfaction
- service quality
- accessibility
- education outcomes
- creator earnings
- successful marketplace transactions
- dispute resolution
- ecological-impact indicators
- system reliability
- automation health
- independent verification
- privacy/security incidents

No single composite percentage should conceal failures in a critical dimension.

## Human agency

AI should assist people rather than become an unaccountable sovereign authority. Public institutions, courts, elected officials, and other lawful governance structures cannot be replaced by an AI workflow merely by architectural declaration. The platform can provide evidence, analysis, simulations, service routing, transparency tools, and auditable decision support.

## Implementation sequence

1. Preserve and classify the canonical source.
2. Define public module contracts.
3. Build account/profile and content primitives.
4. Build creator publishing and marketplace primitives.
5. Add education and research services.
6. Add AI/ML/NLP services with provenance.
7. Add Automission orchestration and observability.
8. Add trust, safety, privacy, and human-review gates.
9. Add economy/payment integrations subject to law and provider requirements.
10. Add ecological and social-impact dashboards.
11. Run continuous tests and independent reviews.
12. Release only features that pass their declared readiness gate.

## Definition of completion

A module is not "complete" merely because its documentation, workflow, or CI check exists. Completion requires an explicit status such as:

**DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → OPERATING → AUDITED**

with evidence for each transition.
