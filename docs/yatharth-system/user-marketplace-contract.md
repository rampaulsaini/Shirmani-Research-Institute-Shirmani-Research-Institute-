# Yatharth User, Marketplace and Trust Contract

## Principle
The public platform should allow people to create, share, learn, collaborate and conduct lawful commerce while preserving user agency, safety, privacy and transparent rules.

## User rights
- clear platform rules
- access to their account data where legally available
- privacy and consent controls
- transparent transaction information
- accessible support
- notice and appeal pathways for consequential moderation actions
- protection against fraud, impersonation and unauthorized transactions

## Seller/creator obligations
- lawful content and products
- accurate descriptions and pricing
- clear ownership/licensing
- fulfillment and refund terms where applicable
- no deliberate fraud, impersonation or harmful abuse

## Buyer/customer protections
- clear product/service information
- transaction receipts
- dispute and support pathways
- fraud monitoring
- review mechanisms with anti-manipulation controls

## AI role
AI agents can assist with classification, matching, translation, customer support, anomaly detection, content assistance and workflow automation.

AI must not silently become the final authority for high-impact account, financial, judicial, rights or independent-verification decisions.

## Trust states
UNREVIEWED -> AUTOMATED_CHECKED -> PUBLISHED -> FLAGGED -> HUMAN_REVIEW -> APPEALED -> RESOLVED.

## Data minimization
Collect only data needed for the stated service, protect it with least-privilege access, retain it according to published policy and provide appropriate user controls.

## Human escalation
High-impact disputes, account termination, major financial actions, safety incidents and rights-affecting decisions require an accountable escalation path.
