# Yatharth Public Platform — User, Creator, Commerce & Trust Contract

## उद्देश्य
Public platform को केवल presentation site नहीं बल्कि account-based participation ecosystem के रूप में विकसित करना:
- user account/profile;
- publishing and sharing;
- creator pages;
- digital products and services;
- freelancing and employment;
- education and courses;
- social/community participation;
- discovery/search/recommendation;
- orders, delivery, reviews and support;
- transparent capability status.

## Capability states
Every public capability MUST expose one state:
LIVE | BETA | PILOT | ARCHITECTURE | RESEARCH | REVIEW_REQUIRED | DISABLED.

No architecture page may imply that a backend, payment rail, authentication system, marketplace or AI service is already production-ready.

## Account model
A user account may support:
1. profile and creator identity;
2. content drafts and publications;
3. products/services;
4. course/learning participation;
5. orders and fulfilment;
6. earnings/settlement records;
7. complaints, appeals and support;
8. privacy and consent controls.

Authentication, authorization, recovery, abuse prevention and deletion/export controls must be implemented by a real backend before accounts are advertised as production functionality.

## Creator and marketplace loop
Create → Review → Publish → Discover → Purchase/Engage → Fulfil → Review → Support → Settle → Improve.

Users may share or sell lawful content/services subject to applicable law, intellectual-property rights, platform rules, safety controls and dispute/appeal mechanisms.

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission
AI can assist with:
- language and intent understanding;
- multilingual translation;
- semantic search;
- content classification;
- creator/customer matching;
- recommendation;
- fraud/spam/anomaly signals;
- customer support;
- moderation assistance;
- product metadata;
- research and evidence organization;
- workflow scheduling, retries, monitoring and recovery.

AI must not silently become final authority for irreversible account punishment, rights removal, legal judgments, financial guarantees, or independent verification.

## Customer satisfaction
Measure more than time-on-platform:
- usefulness;
- accessibility;
- safety;
- quality;
- satisfaction;
- complaint-resolution time;
- creator outcomes;
- learning outcomes;
- successful fulfilment;
- refund/dispute outcomes;
- ecological/public-interest outcomes.

Engagement optimization must not intentionally exploit vulnerable users or misrepresent value.

## Economy and Yatharth Mudra boundary
The platform may research and prototype value-exchange mechanisms. A proposed “Yatharth Mudra” must be clearly labelled as concept/prototype until issuer, legal status, compliance, settlement, custody, reserves/backing where applicable, consumer protection and operational evidence are established.

## Public-interest layer
The platform can expose measurable goals for food, basic shelter, healthcare, education, clean water, sanitation and environmental protection. These remain goals unless actual service capacity and outcome evidence demonstrate delivery.

## Verification boundary
Workflow success, CI success, AI confidence, popularity or user ratings do not equal independent verification.

## Production gates
Before a capability is labelled LIVE:
- backend/API dependency exists;
- authentication/authorization is tested where applicable;
- data/privacy controls exist;
- abuse and security controls exist;
- observability exists;
- recovery path exists;
- accessibility is checked;
- legal/compliance review is identified where required;
- human escalation and appeal path exists for high-impact decisions;
- production smoke/contract tests pass.

## Status
This document is an implementation contract. It does not claim that every listed capability is already deployed.
