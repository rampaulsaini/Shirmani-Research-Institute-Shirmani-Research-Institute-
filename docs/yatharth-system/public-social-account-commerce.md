# Yatharth Public Social, Creator and Commerce Contract

## Purpose

Define the public-facing layer in which a person can create an account, publish content, offer services, sell permitted digital products, learn, teach, collaborate, and participate in communities.

## Public account capabilities

- Account creation and secure authentication
- Profile and creator identity
- Public/private content controls
- Posts, articles, images, audio, video and music
- Following, subscriptions, communities and messaging
- Search, discovery and multilingual presentation
- Creator portfolio
- Service and freelance listings
- Digital-product listings
- Course and educational listings
- Customer reviews and support
- Creator analytics and earnings dashboard

## Commerce lifecycle

Create → publish → discover → purchase/request → deliver → review → support → refund/dispute where applicable → improve.

## AI/ML/NLP assistance

- Content classification and metadata extraction
- Semantic search and recommendation
- Translation and accessibility assistance
- Duplicate/spam/fraud detection
- Marketplace matching
- Customer-support routing
- Quality and policy checks
- Personalization with user controls

AI recommendations must not become hidden manipulation. Users need understandable controls, privacy choices, reporting, appeal and the ability to opt out where applicable.

## Trust boundary

Automated systems may assist with moderation, discovery, commerce and support. High-impact account, financial, legal or dispute decisions require defined escalation and appeal paths.

## Lifecycle states

PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → AUTOMATED

These states describe implementation status only; they do not mean scientific or independent verification.
