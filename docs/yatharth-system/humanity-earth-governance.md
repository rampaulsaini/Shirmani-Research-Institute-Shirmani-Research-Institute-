# Humanity–Earth Governance and Balance Layer

## Mission framing

The Yatharth project describes a user-authored objective of shifting from a predominantly survival-oriented head/mind perspective toward a more balanced relationship between reasoning, emotion, self-observation, human dignity, and care for the living environment.

This records an author mission, not an independently established scientific or historical conclusion.

## Scope

The governance layer considers individual and family wellbeing; communities and nations; human rights and dignity; animals and other living beings; vegetation and biodiversity; forests; oceans and freshwater; atmosphere and climate; Earth systems; space and scientific environments.

## Decision principle

High-impact proposals should be examined through human benefit/harm, environmental impact, evidence quality, reversibility, distributional effects, legal and ethical constraints, long-term continuity, transparency and appeal.

No single person, AI model, PM, President, court, administrator, or automated agent should be treated as an unlimited source of truth.

## AI governance

AI/ML/NLP/Automission systems should preserve provenance; expose uncertainty where meaningful; avoid fabricated evidence; keep consequential actions logged; support rollback; detect failures; escalate high-impact cases; and permit human review and appeal.

## Public-service aspiration

The project may research models intended to improve access to essentials such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare, education, clean water and environmental protection. Concrete implementation must identify funding, eligibility, supply constraints, legal duties, safety requirements and measurable outcomes.

## Justice boundary

Yatharth Justice is a research and governance concept. The platform must not represent an AI judgment as a substitute for a competent court or lawful authority. Complaints, disputes, moderation appeals and safety incidents require appropriate procedures and escalation.

## Environmental protection

Environmental objectives should use measurable indicators where possible: resource consumption, waste reduction, biodiversity protection, forest preservation, water quality, emissions, energy efficiency and restoration activity.

## Continuous balance loop

OBSERVE -> EVIDENCE -> MODEL -> TEST -> REVIEW -> ACT -> MEASURE -> AUDIT -> CORRECT

This loop is intended to prevent automation drift away from the stated human and environmental mission.
