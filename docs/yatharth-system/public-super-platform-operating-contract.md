# Yatharth Public Super-Platform Operating Contract

## Purpose

This document turns the Yatharth/Shirmani public-platform vision into an implementation contract for a globally accessible, AI-native public ecosystem.

The platform is intended to combine social publishing, knowledge, research, education, creative work, commerce, freelancing, employment, community participation, environmental action, and public-value services.

The platform must distinguish **architecture** from **deployed functionality**. A capability is not considered live merely because a workflow, schema, documentation page, or AI-generated artifact exists.

## 1. Public account and identity layer

Every eligible user should be able to:

- create and manage an account;
- maintain a public or private profile;
- publish posts, articles, images, audio, music and video;
- follow, subscribe, communicate and participate in communities;
- create a creator, educator, researcher, freelancer or business profile;
- maintain content, product, service and earnings dashboards;
- export their own data where technically and legally appropriate.

Privacy, consent, security, age-appropriate protections, abuse reporting, account recovery and appeal mechanisms are first-class requirements.

## 2. Public creation and social layer

Core public capabilities:

1. Feed and discovery
2. Search
3. Profiles
4. Posts
5. Comments and discussions
6. Reactions and sharing
7. Communities
8. Messaging
9. Live/media publishing
10. Multilingual presentation
11. Creator analytics
12. Content reporting and appeals

Ranking and recommendation systems must optimize for user value, safety, relevance and healthy participation rather than maximizing time-on-platform at any cost.

## 3. Creator and commerce layer

A user may, subject to applicable law and platform rules:

- sell digital products;
- sell music and audio;
- publish books and educational material;
- offer services;
- offer freelance work;
- create courses;
- accept eligible customer orders;
- manage licensing;
- receive platform-supported earnings.

Required lifecycle:

**Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Trust -> Purchase/Contract -> Deliver -> Review -> Resolve -> Earn -> Audit**

Marketplace functions must include seller disclosures, transaction records, refunds/returns where applicable, fraud controls, dispute handling and customer support.

## 4. Education layer

Public education capabilities should include:

- open learning resources;
- courses;
- structured learning paths;
- skills training;
- AI/ML/NLP education;
- research methodology;
- creator and digital-business skills;
- assessments;
- certificates where a legitimate issuing process exists;
- learner progress dashboards.

Certificates must never be represented as independently accredited unless accreditation actually exists.

## 5. Research and knowledge layer

The research system follows:

**Preserve -> Normalize -> Compare -> Test -> Independently Review -> Verify**

Every substantive claim should be classifiable as:

- AUTHOR_SOURCE
- NORMALIZED
- EVIDENCE_MAPPED
- COMPARATIVE_REVIEWED
- READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW
- INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED
- VERIFIED
- PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED
- INCONCLUSIVE
- NOT_VERIFIED

Author experience and philosophy remain distinguishable from independently established facts.

## 6. Yatharth AI Music and creative layer

The creative system may support:

- AI-assisted music creation;
- lyrics and composition workflows;
- audio production;
- multilingual creative generation;
- creator-owned catalogues;
- publishing;
- licensing;
- storefront integration;
- attribution and provenance;
- rights and takedown workflows.

Generated content must be labelled appropriately where required.

## 7. Economy and Yatharth Currency layer

“Yatharth Currency” is treated as a proposed conceptual/economic module until its legal, technical and economic model is independently specified and applicable regulatory requirements are satisfied.

Before any real-money or transferable monetary deployment:

- define the unit;
- define issuance and supply;
- define custody;
- define conversion;
- define transaction records;
- define consumer protection;
- define fraud controls;
- define taxation/compliance responsibilities;
- obtain required legal/regulatory review.

The architecture must never imply that a conceptual currency is already legal tender or an operational financial product.

## 8. Yatharth Justice and fairness layer

The public platform may provide:

- transparent rules;
- reporting;
- complaint intake;
- evidence submission;
- dispute workflows;
- appeal;
- moderation transparency;
- independent review pathways;
- audit records.

AI may assist with classification, routing, summarization and evidence organization, but high-impact legal, financial, account-termination and justice decisions require appropriate human authority, review and appeal.

No AI agent is the supreme legal authority.

## 9. Humanity, Earth and living systems layer

The platform's public-value mission can support:

- environmental education;
- conservation projects;
- biodiversity information;
- reforestation and restoration initiatives;
- pollution-awareness and reporting;
- resource-efficiency projects;
- humanitarian participation;
- community welfare initiatives.

Claims about environmental or social outcomes must be measured rather than assumed.

## 10. AI/ML/NLP/Automission control plane

The control plane is organized into cooperating functions:

### L1 — Intake Agent
Receives and validates new content, requests and events.

### L2 — NLP Understanding Agent
Extracts language, concepts, entities, topics, relationships and claims.

### L3 — Safety/Policy Agent
Checks applicable platform rules and routes uncertain/high-impact cases.

### L4 — Knowledge/Evidence Agent
Links claims to source records, evidence and counter-evidence.

### L5 — Recommendation/Discovery Agent
Provides relevant discovery while incorporating safety and user controls.

### L6 — Commerce/Service Agent
Supports product, service, job and freelancer matching.

### L7 — Education Agent
Supports learning paths, tutoring assistance and educational discovery.

### L8 — Creative Agent
Supports music, media and creator workflows.

### L9 — Trust/Integrity Agent
Detects spam, coordinated abuse, fraud signals and provenance anomalies.

### L10 — Audit Agent
Continuously checks contracts, traces actions and records failures.

### L11 — Automission Supervisor
Coordinates routine workflows, retries safe failures, escalates exceptions and records mission state.

### L12 — Federation Agent
Coordinates approved actions across repositories and platform services.

### L13 — Human Escalation Gate
Routes high-impact or ambiguous decisions to authorized human review.

## 11. Safety and fail-closed requirements

The system must fail closed for:

- missing authorization;
- uncertain identity/access;
- high-risk financial actions;
- legal/justice decisions;
- irreversible destructive operations;
- insufficient evidence for a verification status;
- suspected credential leakage;
- policy ambiguity;
- unsafe autonomous escalation.

Automation success is not evidence of truth.

Workflow success is not independent verification.

AI confidence is not human authorization.

## 12. Public-value metrics

The public dashboard should separately report:

- active users;
- successful transactions;
- creator earnings;
- learner activity;
- customer satisfaction;
- complaint resolution;
- safety incidents;
- accessibility;
- system availability;
- environmental projects;
- verified research records;
- automation health;
- independent-review status.

The system must not combine these into one misleading “overall success” percentage.

## 13. Scale vision

A long-term aspiration of **850 crore users/consumers** may be retained as a vision target.

It is not a current user count, forecast, guarantee or verified achievement.

Scalability requirements should therefore be tested incrementally through measurable capacity, reliability, security and accessibility milestones.

## 14. Implementation maturity

Every public capability must carry one of these states:

**PROPOSED -> ARCHITECTED -> IMPLEMENTED -> TESTED -> DEPLOYED -> OPERATIONAL -> AUDITED**

A capability may not be labelled OPERATIONAL merely because documentation or a GitHub Actions workflow exists.

## 15. Core operating principle

The public platform should seek to maximize **human value, dignity, safety, learning, creativity, opportunity, trust and customer satisfaction**.

Longer usage is an outcome to be earned through usefulness and trust, not the sole optimization target.

The intended direction is:

**individual -> family -> community -> society -> nation -> humanity -> Earth -> living systems -> wider physical universe**

The platform architecture remains a technical implementation of a stated vision. Its philosophical and metaphysical claims remain subject to the evidence and verification lifecycle defined elsewhere in this repository.
