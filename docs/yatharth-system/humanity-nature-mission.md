# Yatharth Humanity–Nature Mission

## Purpose
Translate the author's Yatharth framework into a public, testable platform mission centered on human dignity, ecological protection, universal access, and balanced decision-making.

## Core principles
- Preserve author source as author source; do not present metaphysical or experiential claims as independently verified facts.
- Prefer evidence, transparency, reversibility, appeal, and measurable outcomes.
- Treat human, animal, plant, ocean, atmospheric, and wider ecological interests as explicit impact dimensions.
- Optimize for user value and satisfaction rather than compulsive engagement or time spent.
- Make essential-service proposals explicit as proposals; implementation depends on resources, law, evidence, and public governance.

## Impact dimensions
1. Human dignity and basic needs
2. Health and education access
3. Economic opportunity
4. Ecological integrity
5. Animal and biodiversity protection
6. Water, air, soil, forest and ocean protection
7. Intergenerational impact
8. Transparency and accountability

## Outcome rule
No AI system may claim that a policy or intervention is universally beneficial merely because an agent scores it highly. Scores are advisory; evidence, affected-party input, lawful governance, and appeal remain necessary.
