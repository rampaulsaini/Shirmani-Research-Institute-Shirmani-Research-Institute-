# Account, Social and Marketplace Contract

## Account layer

Every eligible user may have a profile with configurable privacy, language, creator, seller, learner, worker and community roles.

## Publish layer

Supported content classes can include text, images, audio, music, video, documents, courses, services, research records and digital products, subject to applicable law and platform policy.

## Share layer

Users can publish, share, follow, comment, save, report and control visibility. Recommendation systems must not silently convert engagement optimization into unrestricted manipulation.

## Sell layer

A seller can create a listing, define permitted price/licensing terms, receive orders, deliver digital goods or services, receive reviews, handle refunds where applicable, and access transaction records.

## Work layer

Freelancers can publish skills and service offers; clients can discover, compare, contract and review providers.

## Education layer

Educators can publish learning resources and courses; learners can discover, study, complete assessments where supported, and retain portable completion records.

## Trust layer

Trust signals should distinguish:
- user-provided claims,
- platform moderation decisions,
- automated risk signals,
- community reports,
- independent review,
- verified evidence.

No automated trust score should be presented as an absolute measure of a person's worth or truthfulness.

## Safety and appeals

High-impact moderation, financial restrictions, disputes and account termination require documented reasons and an appeal path. Automation may triage and assist but must remain bounded by policy and review controls.
