# Public User & Marketplace Contract

## Account
A public user may create an account subject to applicable law and platform rules. The account can support a profile, content, products, services, learning material, communities, and transactions.

## Publish
Users can submit text, images, audio, video, music, courses, research material, and other permitted digital content.

Every submission passes through automated intake and policy checks. Automation does not make a submission scientifically verified merely because it passed a technical check.

## Sell and earn
Eligible users may offer digital products, music/audio, courses, research services, creative services, freelance services, software/AI services, and other lawful offerings.

Marketplace systems should provide account safeguards, transparent pricing, transaction records, refunds/disputes where applicable, and abuse detection.

## Trust states
Content and claims should visibly distinguish: Author Source; User Generated; AI Assisted; Evidence Mapped; Independently Reviewed; Verified.

"Verified" is reserved for the project's defined evidence contract; an automated pass is not independent verification.

## Customer satisfaction
The platform should continuously collect voluntary feedback and monitor delivery quality, dispute rates, refund rates, support resolution, repeat use, accessibility, and reported safety problems.

Recommendation systems must not manipulate users into purchases or excessive engagement.

## Automated moderation
AI/ML/NLP may classify spam, abuse, fraud indicators, copyright signals, unsafe material, duplicate content, and routing needs. High-impact actions require an appeal path and, where appropriate, human review.

## Economic integrity
Any proposed "Yatharth Currency" must first have a documented definition, issuance/redemption model, accounting rules, legal analysis, consumer protections, and independent review before being represented as a functioning currency.

## Education
Users may teach and learn through courses, articles, workshops, research resources, and skills pathways. Educational claims should identify their source and evidence level.

## Justice and dispute support
"Yatharth Justice" is a proposed platform framework for fairness, evidence organization, complaint handling, mediation support, transparency, and appeal. It does not replace courts or lawful public institutions.
