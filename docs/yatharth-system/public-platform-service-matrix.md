# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Service Implementation Matrix

This contract turns the public-platform vision into independently testable service domains.

## Public services

| Domain | Public capability | Required state before "Live" |
|---|---|---|
| Social | accounts, profiles, posts, media, follows, communities | auth, moderation, privacy, abuse reporting |
| Creator | publish, portfolio, creator dashboard | ownership, licensing, analytics |
| Marketplace | buy/sell digital goods and services | catalog, checkout, delivery, refunds |
| Freelancing | freelancer profiles, gigs, client requests, matching | contracts, milestones, dispute/appeal |
| Education | courses, learning paths, resources, certificates | content, progress, assessment, accessibility |
| Yatharth AI Music | creation, publishing, discovery, licensing | rights metadata, moderation, attribution |
| Research | claims, evidence, comparisons, reports | provenance and source traceability |
| Verification | independent review and status dashboard | human review and fail-closed status |
| Yatharth Justice | complaints, evidence, review, appeal | due process, human oversight, audit trail |
| Economy | creator/service/product earning dashboards | transparent accounting and applicable compliance |
| Yatharth Currency | proposal/model information only until lawful implementation | legal, economic and technical review |
| Employment | jobs, skills, applications, matching | employer/user verification and privacy |
| AI services | research, writing, translation and automation assistance | safety, quotas, auditability |
| Community | discussion, groups, collaboration | moderation, reporting, appeals |
| Nature & Earth | ecological knowledge, projects, participation | evidence-backed information |
| Public dashboard | service health, verification and transparency | independently reproducible status definitions |

## AI/ML/NLP control plane

1. Intake and identity boundary
2. NLP classification and semantic extraction
3. Retrieval and evidence mapping
4. Recommendation and matching
5. Safety, abuse and fraud detection
6. Quality control
7. Workflow orchestration
8. Continuous audit
9. Failure detection and recovery
10. Human escalation and appeal
11. Independent verification gate
12. Public status publication

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Automation does not equal independent verification.
- Author experience remains distinguishable from externally verified evidence.
- High-impact account, financial, legal, justice and safety decisions require human appeal/oversight.
- No service is marked "Live" merely because its workflow or CI check succeeds.
- Every public capability has explicit states: PLANNED, BUILT, TESTED, PILOT, LIVE, DEGRADED, PAUSED.
- New user source material may extend the registry without overwriting provenance.

## Success measures

Measure separately:
- availability and reliability
- user satisfaction
- successful task completion
- safety incidents and resolution
- evidence/verification coverage
- creator earnings and marketplace activity
- education completion
- ecological/community participation
- automation health

The long-term aspiration may be global-scale access, but user counts and satisfaction must be reported from actual measurements rather than forecasts.
