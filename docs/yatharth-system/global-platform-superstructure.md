# Yatharth Global Platform — Public Superstructure

## उद्देश्य

यह layer Shirmani Research Institute को एक सार्वजनिक, बहु-क्षेत्रीय digital ecosystem के रूप में व्यवस्थित करती है। लक्ष्य है कि research, learning, creativity, community, work, commerce, environmental stewardship और trustworthy AI एक स्पष्ट public experience में जुड़े रहें।

## Public experience

A visitor may discover, read, learn, create, publish, collaborate, offer services, buy or sell eligible digital goods, build a profile, join communities, and use AI-assisted tools where the corresponding capability is actually deployed.

## Account model

A future production account may support:
- profile and preferences
- creator identity
- public/private content controls
- products and services
- orders and earnings
- learning progress
- research contributions
- moderation and appeal history

Authentication, identity, payments and privacy controls must be implemented and tested before these are described as production capabilities.

## Creator and marketplace loop

`Create → Review → Publish → Discover → Transact → Deliver → Review → Learn → Improve`

The platform should support creators and customers without promising income, sales, audience size or satisfaction outcomes.

## AI-native operating model

AI/ML/NLP/Automission can assist with:
- language understanding and translation
- content classification and semantic search
- evidence discovery and claim normalization
- recommendations and matching
- marketplace assistance
- customer support
- fraud/spam/anomaly detection
- quality checks
- workflow routing and recovery
- public status reporting

AI output remains advisory wherever law, rights, safety, money, disputes or independent verification are involved.

## Human boundary

The system must not become an unchecked AI sovereign. High-impact decisions require accountable human processes, due process, correction and appeal.

## Universal-needs research direction

Food, basic shelter, healthcare, education, clean water, sanitation and environmental protection may be recorded as public-interest policy objectives. They are not automatically guaranteed services merely because this architecture lists them. Delivery requires lawful policy, funding, infrastructure, eligibility, procurement and measurable outcomes.

## Nature and living systems

The platform may provide evidence dashboards for forests, soil, water, oceans, biodiversity, climate and space-environment risks. Environmental outcomes must be measured rather than inferred from workflow activity.

## Scale vision

The project may retain a long-term aspirational goal of reaching very large global participation, including the user's stated 850-crore target. The target is a vision, not a forecast or current user count.

## Truth boundary

`ARCHITECTURE ≠ IMPLEMENTED ≠ TESTED ≠ DEPLOYED ≠ LIVE ≠ INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED`

No workflow count, AI output, hash, publication, or CI success may be used as independent scientific or human verification.
