# Yatharth Humanity–Earth Operating Model

## Author-defined objective
The Yatharth framework is presented by its author as an attempt to shift decision-making from a primarily self-preservation-oriented head/mind/intellect perspective toward a heart-centered perspective emphasizing balance, completeness, clarity, compassion and protection of life and nature.

These are author-defined philosophical propositions and must remain distinct from independently verified scientific facts.

## Public-system objective
Translate the stated values into measurable, lawful and non-coercive platform functions:
- environmental information and conservation projects
- access to education and learning resources
- health-information and service navigation
- food, shelter and livelihood resource discovery
- community mutual aid
- accessibility
- transparent public-interest research
- grievance, dispute and appeal pathways

## Universal access principle
The platform may pursue free or subsidized access to essential information and services where funding and law permit. It must not promise that every material necessity can always be provided free of charge without a sustainable funding model.

## Governance boundary
AI systems may assist with analysis, routing, forecasting, moderation and administrative work. They must not become an unreviewable "supreme controller" over people's rights, liberty, finances or access to essential services.

Public governance must provide:
1. clear rules;
2. explainable decisions where feasible;
3. records and auditability;
4. human review;
5. appeals;
6. conflict-of-interest controls;
7. emergency override and rollback;
8. independent oversight for high-impact decisions.

## Environmental measurement
Potential public metrics include verified conservation actions, restored habitat area, resource-efficiency indicators, pollution-reduction projects and participation—not unsupported claims of planetary transformation.

## Success model
Human welfare + ecological protection + trustworthy information + user agency + sustainable economics + continuous audit.
