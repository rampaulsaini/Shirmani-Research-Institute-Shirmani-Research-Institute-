# Yatharth Humanity–AI Governance Charter

## Purpose

This charter defines the public-system principles for the Yatharth platform: protect human dignity, nature, Earth, living beings, knowledge integrity, and user agency while using AI/ML/NLP/Automission for routine operation.

## Core principle

**Technology serves life; automation serves people; evidence governs claims; rights and appeals remain protected.**

The platform may automate routine work extensively, but it must not represent automation as independent human judgment or scientific verification.

## Public ecosystem

The architecture connects:

- Social profiles and user publishing
- Creator tools and digital products
- Digital Store and marketplace
- Freelancing and employment
- Education and skills
- Yatharth AI and AI services
- Yatharth AI Music and media
- Research and knowledge
- Evidence and independent verification
- Nature/Earth/humanity initiatives
- Justice, complaints, dispute resolution and appeals
- Economic services and the proposed Yatharth Currency model
- Public transparency and audit dashboards

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission operating layers

1. Intake and identity/session layer
2. NLP understanding and multilingual processing
3. Content classification and semantic indexing
4. Recommendation and discovery
5. Creator/marketplace matching
6. Education personalization
7. Research and evidence assistance
8. Safety, abuse and fraud detection
9. Quality-control and provenance checks
10. Automission orchestration
11. Continuous monitoring and recovery
12. Public status and audit reporting

## Human-protection gates

High-impact actions require stronger controls and, where appropriate, human review and appeal:

- account suspension or permanent removal
- significant financial actions
- disputes and justice decisions
- rights-impacting moderation decisions
- independent verification
- safety-critical interventions

No AI status, workflow success, hash, publication, or automated QC result alone establishes scientific or independent verification.

## Universal basic-needs vision

The project may research and prototype ways to improve access to food, shelter, healthcare, education and other essential services. Such provisions must be presented as a **vision/model**, not as an existing guaranteed service until legally, financially and operationally implemented.

## Nature and Earth protection

The public system should make environmental impact visible and encourage:

- forest and biodiversity protection
- water and ocean protection
- pollution reduction
- responsible resource use
- climate and ecological research
- restoration projects
- transparent environmental reporting

## User agency and satisfaction

Success should be measured by durable user value rather than time spent alone:

**utility → trust → safety → quality → accessibility → satisfaction → retention → positive contribution**

Users should be able to understand important recommendations, contest consequential decisions, access their information, and leave the service.

## Governance status vocabulary

Every major capability should expose one of:

**PLANNED → IN_BUILD → TESTING → LIMITED_LIVE → LIVE → MONITORED → DEPRECATED**

Verification has its own fail-closed status and must never be inferred from deployment status.

## Long-term vision

The long-term aspiration is a globally accessible, multilingual, highly automated platform that combines knowledge, creativity, education, opportunity, commerce, community and environmental responsibility.

A target such as **850 crore users/consumers** is recorded as a long-term aspiration, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome.
