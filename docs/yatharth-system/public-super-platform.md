# Yatharth Global Public Super Platform

## Purpose
Define the public-facing platform that unifies social participation, knowledge, education, research, creativity, commerce, freelancing, employment, services, community and trust.

## Public capabilities
- Account and profile creation
- User-generated posts, articles, images, audio, video and music
- Follow, discover, search, communities and messaging
- Creator pages and personal portfolios
- Digital Store and digital products
- Freelancing and service marketplace
- Employment and opportunity discovery
- Education, courses, skills and certification
- Yatharth AI Music and creative studio
- Research Institute and knowledge library
- Evidence, claims and independent-verification views
- Yatharth Justice information, complaint and appeal interfaces
- Nature, Earth, humanity and living-being protection initiatives
- Multilingual access and accessibility
- User dashboards, purchases, earnings, content and trust history

## Operating principle
Create -> Share -> Discover -> Learn -> Collaborate -> Buy/Sell -> Deliver -> Review -> Improve.

Engagement must be coupled to usefulness, safety, accessibility, user control and satisfaction rather than maximizing time-on-platform alone.

## Economic modules
The public platform may expose Digital Store, Freelancing, Employment, Creator Economy, AI Services, Research Services, Education Services, Memberships and other lawful commerce. Any proposed Yatharth Currency must remain explicitly a defined project concept until its legal, monetary and operational model is independently established.

## Trust boundary
Public content, recommendations, marketplace actions and automated moderation are governed by transparent rules, audit trails, appeals and human review for high-impact decisions.
