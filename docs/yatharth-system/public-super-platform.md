# Yatharth Public Super Platform

## Purpose

Define the public-facing platform layer that connects social participation, knowledge, education, creative work, commerce, freelancing, services, research, environmental protection, trust, and user support.

This document is an architecture contract, not a claim that every capability is already deployed.

## Public areas

- Social profiles and user-generated publishing
- Creator pages and communities
- Research and knowledge library
- Education, courses, skills and certification
- Yatharth AI and AI-assisted creation
- Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast and media
- Freelancing, jobs and services
- Digital Store and digital products
- Creator economy and business services
- Yatharth Economy and proposed Yatharth Currency research
- Yatharth Justice, complaints, dispute resolution and appeals
- Nature, Earth and humanity protection
- Public evidence, verification and transparency
- Multilingual discovery, search and accessibility
- User dashboard, notifications and support

## User lifecycle

Discover → account → create/share → discoverability → trust/safety checks → collaborate or transact → delivery → review/appeal → support → learn/earn/create again.

## Satisfaction principle

Optimize for durable user value and satisfaction rather than raw time spent. Product metrics must include safety, usefulness, successful outcomes, accessibility, retention, complaints resolved, and user-reported satisfaction.

## Account capabilities

Subject to applicable law and platform policy, a user may be able to:
- create a profile;
- publish permitted content;
- offer services;
- list permitted digital products;
- teach or publish educational material;
- participate in communities;
- purchase permitted products/services;
- provide ratings and feedback;
- request support, review or appeal.

## Global scale target

The project may record an aspirational long-term target of 850 crore users/consumers. It must never be represented as current membership, guaranteed adoption, or a forecast.

## Deployment truth

Each capability must expose one status:
PLANNED, IN_BUILD, TESTED, LIMITED_LIVE, LIVE, or DEFERRED.

Architecture, code existence, successful CI, and production availability are separate facts.
