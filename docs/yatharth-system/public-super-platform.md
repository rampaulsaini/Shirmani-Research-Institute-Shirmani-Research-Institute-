# Yatharth Global Public Super Platform

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem connecting social participation, knowledge, education, research, creativity, services, commerce, employment, environmental protection, trust, and AI assistance.

## Public areas
- Home and Yatharth overview
- Social profiles, posts, follows, communities, messaging and sharing
- Research Institute and knowledge library
- Education, courses, skills and certification
- Yatharth AI and AI/ML/NLP tools
- Yatharth AI Music, audio, video, podcast and creative studio
- Freelancing, employment, services and business
- Digital Store, digital products and creator commerce
- Yatharth economy and the separately defined Yatharth Currency concept
- Yatharth Justice, complaints, appeals, transparency and dispute support
- Nature, Earth, biodiversity and humanity protection programs
- Evidence, verification and public trust dashboard
- Multilingual search, discovery and accessibility
- Personal account, creator dashboard and earnings/transaction history

## Account principle
Eligible users should be able to create accounts, publish permitted content, offer permitted products/services, purchase products/services, learn, collaborate and participate in communities, subject to safety, privacy, legal and platform rules.

## Product principle
Optimize for user value, satisfaction, safety, learning, creativity and sustainable participation rather than maximizing time spent as an end in itself.

## Scale vision
8.5 billion people may be documented as an aspirational long-term target, never as an achieved or guaranteed result.

## Layer separation
Public interface = user experience and services.
AI/ML/NLP = assistance, classification, discovery, translation and analysis.
Automission = orchestration, monitoring, recovery and routine operations.
Governance = policy, audit, appeals and high-impact human oversight.
Independent verification = separate evidence gate; automation success is not scientific or human verification.
