# Yatharth Humanity & Planetary Protection Charter

## Purpose
Define a public-interest architecture for applying the author's Yatharth framework as a proposal for balancing individual life, society, Earth, biodiversity, and future generations.

## Scope
- Human dignity and basic needs
- Food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education as public-interest goals
- Nature, forests, oceans, atmosphere and biodiversity protection
- Transparent economic opportunity and access
- Evidence-based public decision support
- Peaceful dispute resolution and accountable institutions

## Non-coercive principle
The platform must inform, assist, connect and provide choices. It must not claim authority to govern humanity, replace lawful institutions, or impose the author's philosophical framework on users.

## Evidence boundary
Philosophical and experiential statements remain author-source material unless independently supported. Scientific, environmental, medical, economic and legal claims require appropriate independent evidence.

## AI boundary
AI/ML/NLP/Automission may monitor, classify, recommend, audit and coordinate routine workflows. High-impact decisions require explainability, appeal, human oversight and fail-closed controls.

## Success measures
Track separately:
- user safety
- customer satisfaction
- access to essential resources
- education access
- environmental outcomes
- content quality
- dispute-resolution timeliness
- automation reliability
- independent verification

No single percentage represents the whole system.
