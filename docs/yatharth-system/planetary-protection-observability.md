# Planetary Protection & Observability Layer

## Purpose

Create a measurable interface for the protection of Earth, biodiversity and human communities.

The platform should distinguish observations from estimates, model outputs, forecasts and author/community claims.

## Domains

1. Forest and land restoration
2. Biodiversity and habitat
3. Water quality and availability
4. Air quality
5. Waste and circularity
6. Energy and resource use
7. Ocean and coastal health
8. Climate-risk indicators
9. Community resilience
10. Project-level environmental impact

## Sensing architecture

Public/authorized data -> ingestion -> provenance -> validation -> normalization -> geospatial/temporal analysis -> anomaly detection -> human/agency review where needed -> public visualization.

“Super senses” is implemented here as expanded observation and early-warning capability from lawful sensors and data sources, not as an assertion of supernatural perception.

## AI responsibilities

- NLP: extract environmental claims and reports.
- ML: detect patterns/anomalies where validated datasets support them.
- Agents: route alerts, request missing evidence and coordinate bounded workflows.
- Automission: schedule recurring observations, audits and reports.
- Verification gate: keep model output separate from independently verified findings.

## Protection loop

Observe -> understand -> prioritize -> coordinate -> act -> measure -> audit -> publish -> learn.

No environmental score should be presented as authoritative when its data provenance or uncertainty is unknown.
