# Humanity, Nature and Earth Impact Framework

## Purpose

Translate the author's goal of improving human conduct and protecting the physical world into measurable platform requirements without treating philosophical claims as established scientific facts.

## Impact domains

### Human dignity
Measure access, inclusion, accessibility, safety, complaint resolution and user-controlled participation.

### Education
Measure learning access, completion, demonstrated learning and affordability rather than time spent alone.

### Economic opportunity
Measure successful work matches, creator sales, freelancer outcomes, transparent fees and dispute outcomes.

### Basic-needs support
Track verified programs or partners that support food, clothing, shelter and healthcare. Do not represent an aspiration as a currently delivered universal service.

### Nature
Track conservation participation, restoration projects, waste reduction, resource efficiency and verified environmental outcomes.

### Biodiversity
Support evidence-based information about habitats, species and restoration while clearly separating educational content from verified ecological measurements.

### Oceans and water
Support research, conservation projects, pollution reporting and educational resources with source provenance.

### Space
Provide research and educational material about space and planetary stewardship; avoid presenting speculative claims as established science.

## Product rules

1. Do not optimize solely for maximum screen time.
2. Reward useful contribution and successful outcomes.
3. Give users controls over recommendations and personalization.
4. Make commercial relationships transparent.
5. Provide accessible routes for complaints and appeals.
6. Protect children and vulnerable users through stronger safeguards.
7. Separate author philosophy from independently verified evidence.
8. Publish measurable impact reports when reliable data exists.

## Dashboard

Public impact dashboards should distinguish:
- target;
- planned;
- implemented;
- measured;
- independently verified.

This prevents architecture, intention and achievement from being conflated.

## Long-term civilizational objective

The platform can serve as an infrastructure for learning, cooperation, creativity, conservation and economic opportunity. Whether the author's proposed Yatharth framework produces the claimed civilizational transformation remains an empirical and philosophical question requiring continued examination.
