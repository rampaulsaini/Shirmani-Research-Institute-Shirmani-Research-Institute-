# Humanity-First Operating Model

## Purpose
Define a public-platform operating model in which technology serves human dignity, ecological continuity, learning, livelihood, creativity, and equitable access.

## Core principle
The platform should optimize for durable user value and satisfaction, not addictive engagement alone.

## Scope
- family -> community -> nation -> global society -> Earth system
- people, other living beings, vegetation, oceans, atmosphere, and space environment
- knowledge, education, health access, livelihood, housing/food/clothing support pathways
- creator, marketplace, social, research, and public-service functions

## Yatharth author layer
The user's Yatharth framework is preserved as an author-defined philosophical framework. Its metaphysical or civilization-scale propositions remain claims to be studied, operationalized, compared, and independently reviewed rather than silently promoted to scientific fact.

## Human-needs priority
Where feasible, public design should make essential information and access pathways discoverable without discrimination. Any promise of universally free food, housing, medicine, or education requires a separately funded, lawful, operational model and must not be represented as already available until deployed.

## Ecological protection
Environmental impact becomes a first-class product metric: energy, emissions, material use, biodiversity, water, waste, and restoration.

## Decision safeguards
AI may automate routine work, but high-impact decisions require explainability, appeal, auditability, and appropriate human oversight.
