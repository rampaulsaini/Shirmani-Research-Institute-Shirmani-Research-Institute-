# Humanity-First Operating Model

## Purpose
The Yatharth public platform is designed around a humanity-first objective: improve access to knowledge, opportunity, safety, creativity, community, and environmental stewardship while preserving individual agency and dignity.

The framework is an author-defined proposal. It is not presented as an established scientific, legal, economic, or political fact.

## Scope
The public platform may connect social publishing and communities, education and skills, research and knowledge, digital commerce, freelancing and employment, creative media and Yatharth AI Music, support and service discovery, nature/Earth/biodiversity initiatives, and trust/evidence/verification/governance.

## Core operating loop
Discover -> Learn -> Create -> Share -> Collaborate -> Trade/Serve -> Review -> Improve.

The system should optimize for user value, safety, accessibility, transparency, and satisfaction rather than maximizing screen time alone.

## Universal basic-service vision
The author's vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare, education, and other essential facilities. The platform may document, research, coordinate, or connect users to such services; it must not claim that these services are universally free or guaranteed unless a lawful, funded, independently verified program actually provides them.

## Environmental protection
Environmental objectives are first-class platform concerns: reduce avoidable waste and harmful consumption; promote restoration and conservation information; support verified environmental projects; expose measurable impact where data exists; avoid unsupported environmental claims.

## Human agency
Automation should remove repetitive work, not remove people's rights. Users need consent and privacy controls, explanations for consequential automated decisions, appeal and correction pathways, and human review for high-impact disputes.

## Governance boundary
Political offices, courts, public authorities, or any claimed "supreme control" are not automatically governed by this platform. The platform can provide neutral information, evidence management, workflow support, and transparency tools, while lawful public institutions retain their legal authority.

## Success metrics
Track separately: active users, creator retention, customer satisfaction, successful transactions, learning outcomes, service resolution, verified environmental impact, safety incidents, appeals/correction rates, automation reliability, and independent verification.

Do not collapse these dimensions into one "superiority" score.
