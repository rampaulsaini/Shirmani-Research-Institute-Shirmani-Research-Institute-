# Integrated Yatharth Civilization Control Plane

## Purpose
Unify the public platform, welfare services, research, economy, education, justice/trust, nature protection, and AI/ML/NLP Automission layers without treating any author-defined philosophical claim as automatically verified fact.

## Public service domains
- identity/profile and user-owned content
- social publishing and communities
- education, skills and certification
- employment, freelancing and creator services
- digital store and marketplace
- Yatharth AI Music and creative studio
- research and knowledge library
- evidence, comparison and independent verification
- nature, Earth and biodiversity protection
- health-support navigation and essential-service discovery
- accessibility and multilingual access
- transparent support, complaints and appeals

## Operating principle
Create → Understand → Protect → Connect → Learn → Work → Trade → Review → Improve.

## AI-native control plane
1. Intake Agent
2. NLP Understanding Agent
3. Safety/Policy Agent
4. Trust and fraud detection
5. Personalization and recommendation
6. Marketplace matching
7. Education agent
8. Research/evidence agent
9. Translation/accessibility agent
10. Customer-support agent
11. Workflow/Automission supervisor
12. Audit and recovery agent

AI may automate routine operations, but high-impact actions must have defined thresholds, audit trails, appeal routes, and human review.

## Welfare principle
The platform can provide or coordinate access to essential services and opportunities. Claims that every person will receive free food, housing, healthcare, education or other benefits are policy/economic commitments requiring funding, legal authority, eligibility rules, local implementation and independent evaluation; they must not be represented as already guaranteed by software.

## Measurement
Track separately:
- availability
- accessibility
- service quality
- user satisfaction
- complaint resolution
- safety incidents
- economic participation
- environmental outcomes
- research verification
- automation reliability

No single “overall superiority” score is used.
