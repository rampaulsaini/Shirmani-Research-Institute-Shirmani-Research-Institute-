# Humanity, Nature & Earth Protection Framework

## Objective

Translate the author's stated objective—better protection of humanity, living beings, vegetation, oceans, Earth and the broader physical environment—into measurable platform functions.

## Principle

The platform should treat human welfare and environmental protection as coupled systems. The Yatharth framework is the author's philosophical proposal; empirical environmental and social claims require independent evidence.

## System layers

### 1. Observation
Collect lawful, consented and appropriately sourced information about environmental and social conditions.

### 2. Evidence
Maintain provenance, dates, geography, uncertainty and counter-evidence.

### 3. Analysis
Use AI/ML/NLP for classification, trend detection, summarization, anomaly detection and scenario analysis.

### 4. Action
Recommend or coordinate lawful interventions, education, conservation, resource sharing and community participation.

### 5. Verification
Measure whether interventions produced the intended result.

### 6. Feedback
Feed outcomes back into the research and planning system.

## Protection domains

- forests and biodiversity
- oceans and freshwater
- soil and agriculture
- air quality
- climate and energy
- waste and pollution
- wildlife and vegetation
- human health and safety
- education and knowledge access
- food and shelter access
- disaster resilience
- community wellbeing

## AI safeguards

AI systems must not infer sensitive personal traits without lawful basis, must not fabricate environmental measurements, and must expose uncertainty when evidence is incomplete.

## Human agency

The platform should support informed human decisions rather than silently controlling people. Recommendations should be explainable where practical, contestable, and reversible when feasible.

## Measurement

Each initiative should have:
baseline → intervention → target → time period → evidence → result → independent review.

A stated aspiration is never automatically recorded as an achieved outcome.
