# Yatharth Public Platform Service Map

This map converts the public-interface vision into service domains. A domain is public-facing only when the corresponding implementation, security, data, and operational contracts exist.

## 1. Identity and participation
- account creation
- profile
- creator page
- privacy controls
- accessibility
- multilingual settings
- user dashboard

## 2. Social and communication
- posts
- comments
- reactions
- follows
- groups
- communities
- messaging
- live and media publishing
- reporting and appeals

## 3. Knowledge and research
- Yatharth source
- research library
- articles
- books
- evidence registry
- counter-evidence
- claim registry
- comparison research
- independent verification status

## 4. Education
- courses
- learning paths
- skill training
- research education
- AI/ML/NLP learning
- creator education
- certificates
- learner portfolio

## 5. Work and opportunity
- employment
- freelancing
- services
- job listings
- freelancer profiles
- client requests
- project workflow
- portfolio
- ratings with anti-manipulation safeguards

## 6. Commerce
- digital store
- products
- music and audio
- books
- courses
- licenses
- subscriptions
- creator payouts
- orders and delivery

## 7. Creative AI
- Yatharth AI Music
- audio tools
- video tools
- image/creative tools
- multilingual transformation
- creator studio
- publishing workflow

## 8. Economy
- earnings dashboard
- marketplace accounting
- lawful payment integrations
- creator economy
- service economy
- proposed Yatharth Currency research area

The Yatharth Currency area must distinguish conceptual research from any legally recognized currency or payment instrument.

## 9. Justice, trust, and safety
- rules
- complaints

- human review
- safety escalation
- independent audit

## 10. Nature, Earth, and humanity
- ecological knowledge
- conservation projects
- environmental dashboards
- volunteering
- donations/partnerships
- sustainable products and services
- impact reporting

## 11. AI and Automission
- AI assistant
- recommendation
- semantic search
- NLP classification
- agent routing
- workflow automation
- quality control
- fraud detection
- continuous audit
- resilience and recovery

## 12. Public transparency
- service status
- verification status
- system health
- change history
- public metrics
- incident reports
- automation boundaries

## Navigation principle

The interface should expose a small number of understandable top-level areas while retaining the complete service map underneath. Internal agents and GitHub workflows should not be confused with public features.
