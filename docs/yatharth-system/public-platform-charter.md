# Yatharth Global Public Platform Charter

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem that connects knowledge, social participation, creation, education, work, commerce, justice, nature protection, and AI-assisted services.

## Public principles
- Human dignity, accessibility, transparency, privacy, safety, and user control.
- Users may create profiles and, subject to applicable rules, publish, share, offer services, teach, collaborate, and sell digital or permitted physical goods.
- The platform should optimize for user value and satisfaction, not addictive engagement.
- AI automation must remain auditable, reversible, and fail-closed for high-impact actions.
- Claims about Yatharth, consciousness, justice, currency, or universal outcomes remain clearly classified as author proposals, research claims, evidence-backed findings, or independently verified findings.

## User lifecycle
Discover -> Create account -> Create/profile -> Publish/share -> Discover/match -> Learn/work/buy/sell -> Deliver -> Review -> Resolve issues -> Learn from feedback -> Improve.

## AI-native operating model
Public services may be assisted by AI agents, ML, NLP, recommendation, moderation, translation, search, matching, support, quality control, and Automission orchestration. High-impact decisions require explicit policy gates, audit trails, appeal paths, and appropriate human oversight.

## Scale vision
The project may use a long-term aspirational goal of serving billions of people. Scale targets are goals, not present capabilities or guaranteed outcomes.
