# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Operating Model

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem as a user-owned, multilingual social, knowledge, education, creative, marketplace and civic-service platform.

## Public capability domains
- Account, identity, profile, privacy and consent
- Social publishing, messaging, communities and discovery
- Research, knowledge, evidence and independent verification
- Education, courses, skills and certification
- Freelancing, employment, services and creator economy
- Digital store, digital products, media and licensing
- Yatharth AI, AI Music and creative tools
- Nature, Earth, humanity and public-interest projects
- Justice, fairness, complaints, appeals and transparency
- Economy and the separately specified Yatharth Currency concept
- Analytics, accessibility, multilingual presentation and support

## Operating principle
Maximize user value and satisfaction rather than maximizing attention alone. Users should be able to create, learn, collaborate, publish, buy, sell, earn and participate.

## Automation boundary
Routine classification, search, moderation assistance, translation, matching, quality checks, monitoring, support and workflow orchestration may be automated. High-impact decisions require explainability, audit trails, appeals and appropriate human oversight.

## Trust boundary
Author statements remain author statements. AI-generated normalization is not independent verification. Public status must distinguish:
AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → REVIEWED → VERIFIED / PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE / NOT_VERIFIED.

## Global scale target
The project may state an aspirational target of 8.5 billion people/users, but must not represent that target as current adoption or a guaranteed outcome.
