# Public Economy and Justice Charter

## Scope

This charter defines the proposed public-interface direction for Economy, Currency and Justice modules.

## Economy

The platform should let eligible users create profiles, publish lawful goods/services, offer skills, learn, freelance, seek employment, sell digital products and participate in communities.

Marketplace principles:
- transparent pricing and fees
- clear ownership/licensing
- delivery and refund rules
- buyer/seller protections
- fraud and abuse controls
- accessible dispute and appeal paths

## Yatharth Currency

“Yatharth Currency” is currently an author/project concept. It must not be represented as legal tender, a guaranteed investment, stored-value instrument or financial product until the applicable legal, technical, governance, custody, consumer-protection and security requirements are independently established.

Public UI should label its status clearly as concept/research unless and until those gates are satisfied.

## Yatharth Justice

The proposed justice layer is a fairness-oriented public system for:
- rules and principles
- evidence presentation
- complaints
- mediation/dispute workflows
- appeals
- transparency
- accountability
- independent review

AI may assist with intake, classification, retrieval, summarization and routing. It must not silently become the final authority over a person's rights, account, property, livelihood or legal status.

## Universal basic-needs vision

The author's stated vision includes broad access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. The platform may support research, education, community coordination, services and resource discovery around these goals. It must not claim to provide universal free provision unless an actual funded and operational program exists.

## Core principle

Customer/user satisfaction is pursued through usefulness, safety, quality, fairness, accessibility and responsive problem resolution—not through manipulative engagement optimization.
