# Yatharth Humanity–Earth Operating System

## Purpose

This document defines the public-platform operating model for the Yatharth ecosystem: human wellbeing, ecological protection, knowledge, creativity, commerce and trustworthy automated coordination.

The framework is an author-defined proposal. Its philosophical and metaphysical claims remain distinct from independently verified facts.

## Core loop

Observe → Understand → Protect → Create → Share → Learn → Exchange → Review → Improve.

## Scope

- Individual and family wellbeing
- Community and public services
- Education and lifelong learning
- Research and knowledge exchange
- Nature, biodiversity and Earth protection
- Creator economy and freelancing
- Digital commerce
- AI-assisted services
- Justice, dispute resolution and appeals
- Public transparency and audit

## Human-centred service principle

The platform should maximize useful value and user satisfaction rather than merely maximizing time spent. Engagement must not depend on deception, coercion or unsafe dark patterns.

## Essential-services principle

The platform may publish proposals for universal access to essentials such as food, clothing, shelter, healthcare and education. Any real-world implementation must identify funding, eligibility, delivery partners, legal authority, safety requirements and measurable outcomes.

## Governance boundary

AI may coordinate routine operations, detect anomalies, summarize evidence and route cases. High-impact decisions require appropriate human authority, due process, appeal and audit.

## Ecological boundary

Every major platform feature should be evaluated for resource use, privacy, security and environmental impact where measurable.

## Verification boundary

Author experience, generated content, workflow success and publication status must never automatically become "independently verified" truth.
