# Yatharth Justice, Economy and Humanity Governance

## Scope

A neutral operating framework for protecting users, nature, resources and platform trust.

## Yatharth Justice

Public rules should be:
- published and understandable;
- applied consistently;
- evidence-aware;
- appealable;
- auditable;
- separated from personal authority;
- subject to human review for high-impact decisions.

The platform must not present an AI decision as a supreme or infallible judicial authority.

## Basic-needs vision

The project may research and support access-oriented models for food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. Such goals are recorded as a social/economic vision, not as a claim that the platform can currently provide these services universally for free.

## Yatharth Economy

The economic layer can connect:
- employment;
- freelancing;
- creator income;
- digital products;
- education;
- research services;
- AI services;
- marketplace transactions.

Any proposed Yatharth Currency must first have a precise specification covering unit definition, issuance, custody, exchange, consumer protection, taxation/compliance and legal status before deployment.

## Nature and Earth protection

Track practical actions and measurable indicators such as:
- restoration projects;
- biodiversity protection;
- waste reduction;
- resource efficiency;
- environmental education;
- community participation.

## Humanity principle

The platform should seek to reduce avoidable harm and increase access, dignity, learning, opportunity, transparency and peaceful dispute resolution.

## Evidence discipline

Statements about civilization, historical eras, human nature, consciousness or universal truth remain clearly labeled as author framework, hypothesis, interpretation or independently supported evidence according to the evidence available.

## Governance invariant

No individual, AI agent, workflow, PM, president, court or platform administrator is treated as infallible. The system uses documented authority boundaries, checks, appeals and evidence.
