
# Yatharth Civilization Continuity Mission

## Purpose
Translate the author's stated Yatharth vision into a testable, non-coercive public-system architecture focused on human dignity, ecological protection, animal and plant welfare, knowledge, opportunity, and long-term planetary continuity.

This records an author-proposed mission, not a claim that the proposed social model is already scientifically or politically validated.

## System boundary
The platform treats these as connected but separately measurable domains:
1. Individual and family wellbeing
2. Community wellbeing
3. Education and lifelong learning
4. Healthcare access
5. Food, clothing and housing access
6. Employment and freelancing
7. Digital commerce and creator income
8. Justice, due process and appeals
9. Nature, biodiversity and climate protection
10. Animal and plant welfare
11. Research, evidence and independent verification
12. AI/ML/NLP automation
13. Public transparency and audit

## Core operating principle
Human dignity + ecological continuity + evidence + freedom of choice + transparent governance + accountable automation.

The system must not assume that one philosophy, person, AI model, or institution possesses final truth.

## Family-to-planet continuity model
Person -> Family -> Community -> City/Region -> Nation -> Humanity -> Biosphere

Every layer has needs, rights, responsibilities, measurable outcomes, resource constraints, feedback, and grievance/appeal pathways.

## Universal basic-services proposal
The public platform may document and model proposals for universal access to:
- food
- clothing
- safe housing
- essential healthcare
- education
- clean water and sanitation
- basic digital access
- legal information and due process

These are policy proposals for research and public discussion, not promises that the platform can itself provide government-funded services.

## Human agency
Users retain account control, content control, consent choices, portability where technically feasible, appeal rights, and transparent reasons for consequential automated decisions.

No engagement-maximization objective may override safety, dignity, privacy, or user autonomy.

## Ecological control loop
Observe -> Measure -> Model -> Reduce Harm -> Restore -> Audit -> Publish

Possible indicators:
- forest cover
- biodiversity
- water quality
- air quality
- soil health
- waste recovery
- carbon intensity
- habitat restoration
- animal welfare indicators

## Verification boundary
The platform distinguishes AUTHOR_SOURCE, PROPOSAL, MODEL, EVIDENCE_MAPPED, INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED, VERIFIED, INCONCLUSIVE, and NOT_VERIFIED.

No AI output, workflow success, publication, or user vote alone changes a claim to VERIFIED.

## Long-term objective
Build an open, multilingual knowledge and service ecosystem in which automation increases access to useful services while measurable human and ecological outcomes remain visible and independently reviewable.
