# Yatharth User Account, Social and Commerce Contract

## Vision
The public platform is intended to let people participate as users, creators, learners, researchers, service providers, customers and communities through one connected account.

## Core user journey
DISCOVER -> CREATE ACCOUNT -> BUILD PROFILE -> CREATE -> SHARE -> DISCOVER -> CONNECT -> OFFER -> BUY/SELL -> DELIVER -> REVIEW -> SUPPORT -> LEARN -> CREATE AGAIN

## Account
Accounts should provide profile, preferences, privacy controls, language, notifications, content library, order history, creator/seller tools where enabled, security settings, support and appeals.

## Create and share
Subject to platform rules and applicable law, users may publish posts, articles, photographs, artwork, audio, music, video, educational material, research material, services and digital products.

## Sell and earn
Eligible users may offer digital products, music/audio, courses, creative services, freelancing, research services and AI-assisted services. Listings should clearly disclose price, applicable taxes/fees, delivery terms, refund terms, required seller information and dispute/support routes.

## Trust
Trust signals should use documented signals such as verified status, reviews, provenance, evidence, moderation status and legally permissible transaction/dispute signals. AI must not fabricate reviews, identities, evidence, sales, qualifications or verification.

## Customer satisfaction
Measure successful delivery, support response, refund resolution, user feedback, accessibility and voluntary repeat use. Do not optimize solely for time spent.

## Safety and human control
Automation may assist moderation, fraud detection, routing, recommendations and support. Users need appropriate notice, correction and appeal mechanisms. High-impact account, financial, legal, safety and justice decisions require defined escalation and human oversight.

## Global accessibility
Target multilingual, mobile-first, low-bandwidth and accessible operation while respecting regional legal requirements and transparent localization.

## Economic principle
Creator and service commerce may be supported. Any proposed Yatharth Currency or new monetary instrument must remain clearly separated from ordinary lawful payment rails until its legal, technical, economic and governance requirements are independently established.

## Implementation rule
This contract describes intended behavior; it is not evidence that every capability is currently live.
