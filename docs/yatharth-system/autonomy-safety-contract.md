# AI-Native Autonomy Safety Contract

## Goal

Enable extensive AI/ML/NLP/Automission operation while preserving human agency, legal accountability and independent verification.

## Autonomy classes

### A0 — Assist
AI suggests; a person decides.

### A1 — Routine automation
AI executes low-risk, reversible tasks under predefined rules.

### A2 — Supervised automation
AI executes bounded workflows with monitoring and rollback.

### A3 — Multi-agent automation
Agents coordinate research, classification, routing and operational tasks under policy constraints.

### A4 — Continuous automation
The system operates continuously with automated health checks, audit, recovery and escalation.

A4 does not mean unrestricted authority.

## Never-autonomous actions

The platform must not silently delegate:
- coercive legal punishment
- irreversible account or financial actions without appeal
- medical diagnosis as a substitute for qualified care
- final judicial decisions
- electoral/government authority
- identity or rights determinations without required human/legal process
- scientific verification solely from AI output

## Fail-safe requirements

- least privilege
- provenance
- immutable audit trail where appropriate
- rollback
- rate limits
- anomaly detection
- model/version tracking
- human escalation
- user appeal
- incident response
- independent testing

## Continuous evaluation

Measure:
accuracy + safety + fairness + privacy + reliability + latency + cost + user satisfaction

No metric alone is sufficient.

## Verification boundary

AI can prepare evidence packets and tests. Independent human/domain review determines whether a claim qualifies for independent verification.
