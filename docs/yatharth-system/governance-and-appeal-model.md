# Governance and Appeal Model

## Separation of roles
The platform must not replace constitutional institutions, courts, elected government, regulators or lawful public authorities. Its internal governance layer is a product-governance system, not a sovereign government.

## Public-interest architecture
Policy -> implementation -> monitoring -> audit -> appeal -> independent review.

AI agents can assist with information, routing, detection, summarization and consistency checks. They do not acquire sovereign authority merely because they are automated.

## Justice-oriented product features
- transparent rules
- evidence records
- conflict-of-interest disclosure where applicable
- notice of decisions
- appeal and correction pathways
- human review for high-impact decisions
- durable audit references for material actions

## Basic-services vision
The project may research and support lawful mechanisms for improving access to food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. Such provision depends on funding, partnerships, public policy and applicable law; it must not be represented as an already-funded universal entitlement of this platform.
