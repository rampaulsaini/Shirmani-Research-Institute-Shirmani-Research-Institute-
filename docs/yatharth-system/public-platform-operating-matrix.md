# Yatharth Public Platform — Operating Matrix

## Purpose

Define the public-facing service surface for the Yatharth Global Platform while keeping research claims, commerce, governance, safety, and automation separately auditable.

## Public domains

1. Social — accounts, profiles, posts, follows, communities, messaging, sharing.
2. Creator — audio, video, music, writing, images, live sessions and creator pages.
3. Research — source archive, claims, evidence, counter-evidence, comparisons and reports.
4. Education — courses, learning paths, resources, assessments and certificates.
5. AI — research assistant, creation tools, translation, discovery and user-facing agents.
6. Music — Yatharth AI Music, publishing, discovery, licensing and creator tools.
7. Freelancing — services, portfolios, contracts, delivery, reviews and dispute workflow.
8. Employment — opportunities, skills, profiles and matching.
9. Digital Store — products, subscriptions, courses, media, licensing and creator storefronts.
10. Economy — creator earnings, marketplace accounting, transparent fees and economic research.
11. Yatharth Currency — a separately governed research/proposal layer; not presented as legal tender unless legally established.
12. Yatharth Justice — principles, evidence, complaints, mediation, appeals and public accountability.
13. Nature & Humanity — environmental knowledge, protection projects and participation.
14. Trust & Verification — provenance, moderation, independent review, verification status and audit trails.
15. Community — contributors, researchers, students, creators, partners and public participation.
16. Public Dashboard — platform health, research status, verification status, transparency reports and change history.

## User lifecycle

Discover → Account → Create/Share → Discoverability → Trust/Safety → Collaborate → Buy/Sell/Serve → Deliver → Review/Appeal → Earn → Learn → Return.

## Account capabilities

Every eligible user should be able to create a profile, publish permitted content, follow and communicate with others, offer services, list eligible digital products, learn, participate in research/community activities, and control their own privacy and visibility.

## AI-native service layer

AI/ML/NLP services should support classification, semantic search, translation, recommendations, fraud/spam detection, accessibility, customer support, creator assistance, marketplace matching and research organization.

AI output is advisory or operational only within explicitly authorized bounds. High-impact decisions require defined human review, appeal, or independent review pathways.

## Safety and trust

The platform must provide:
- clear rules and enforcement reasons;
- privacy controls;
- abuse and fraud detection;
- provenance and audit logs;
- appeals;
- dispute handling;
- independent verification where applicable;
- fail-closed controls for high-impact automation.

## Completion states

Each public capability is tracked as:

PLANNED → DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → LIVE → MONITORED

No documentation, workflow run, or generated artifact alone may be represented as proof that a user-facing capability is live.

## Core principle

The platform should optimize for durable user value, satisfaction, safety, learning, creativity, opportunity and environmental/social benefit—not merely time spent.

## Long-term scale objective

The project may retain its stated aspiration of reaching 850 crore people globally. This is a long-term target, not a current user count or guaranteed outcome.
