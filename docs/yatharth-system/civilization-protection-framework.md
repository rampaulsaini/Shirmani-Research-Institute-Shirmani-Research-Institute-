# Yatharth Civilization Protection Framework

## Purpose

Define a humanity-first public framework for applying the author's Yatharth/Yatharth Yug perspective to practical protection of people, living systems, Earth and the wider physical environment.

This is an architecture and research specification, not a claim that the author's metaphysical propositions are scientifically established.

## Core principle

Observe → Understand → Protect → Restore → Measure → Review → Improve

The platform should optimize for human welfare, ecological protection, fairness, transparency and long-term resilience rather than engagement alone.

## Scope

- family and community wellbeing
- food, clothing and housing access
- healthcare access
- education and lifelong learning
- employment, freelancing and dignified work
- clean air, water, soil and oceans
- forests, biodiversity and plant life
- animal welfare
- climate and environmental resilience
- safe digital participation
- accessible knowledge and public services
- conflict prevention and fair dispute resolution
- transparent economic participation

## AI/ML/NLP/Automission control plane

### L1 Sense
Ingest permitted public/system signals, user reports, environmental datasets and platform telemetry.

### L2 Understand
NLP classification, semantic extraction, entity linking, anomaly detection and multilingual normalization.

### L3 Assess
Estimate impact, urgency, uncertainty and affected populations. Keep evidence, inference and author-defined philosophy separate.

### L4 Act
Trigger approved low-risk workflows: routing, translation, accessibility, resource discovery, alerts, content moderation assistance, service matching and remediation queues.

### L5 Verify
Record evidence, counter-evidence, confidence, provenance and independent review requirements.

### L6 Audit
Continuously test safety, bias, false positives, false negatives, security, privacy and policy compliance.

### L7 Escalate
High-impact decisions must route to authorized human review and appeal. AI must not silently exercise sovereign, judicial or coercive authority.

### L8 Learn
Use approved feedback and evaluation data to improve models without rewriting historical evidence or changing verification status automatically.

## Universal basic-needs service layer

The public interface may provide or connect users to:
1. food and essential-goods discovery
2. housing and shelter resources
3. healthcare information and service discovery
4. education and learning
5. employment and freelancing
6. accessibility support
7. emergency/community resources

The platform must not promise universally free services unless funding, providers and applicable law actually support that promise.

## Nature and Earth protection layer

Public dashboards can track, where reliable data exists:
- forests and land cover
- water quality and availability
- ocean/coastal indicators
- air quality
- biodiversity
- waste and pollution
- restoration projects
- resource consumption

Each metric should show source, date, geographic scope, uncertainty and methodology.

## Justice and fairness layer

Yatharth Justice should be implemented as a research and public-service architecture:
- evidence intake
- complaint routing
- rights and process information
- mediation/dispute-resolution resources
- transparent case status where legally permissible
- appeal pathways
- independent review
- audit logs

AI recommendations must remain distinguishable from legal decisions.

## Governance principle

The platform can support public institutions and democratic processes, but it should not place any elected office or court under an AI "supreme control." A safer architecture is constitutional/legal constraints + transparent human authority + independent review + AI assistance + public auditability.

## Success metrics

Track separately:
- user-reported satisfaction
- service resolution time
- accessibility
- safety incidents
- verified environmental outcomes
- educational outcomes
- employment/service matches
- dispute-resolution outcomes
- model quality
- automation reliability
- independent review completion

No single engagement metric should be treated as proof of human wellbeing.

## Verification rule

Author Source → Normalized Claim → Evidence → Counter-Evidence → Independent Review → Verified/Partially Supported/Inconclusive/Not Verified

No AI workflow success, GitHub run, citation count or platform metric changes a claim to VERIFIED by itself.
