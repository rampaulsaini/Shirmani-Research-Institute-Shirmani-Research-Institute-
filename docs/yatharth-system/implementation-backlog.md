# Yatharth Implementation Backlog

## P0 — integrity and public foundation
1. Keep the Author Source archive immutable/versioned.
2. Keep claim IDs stable across source updates.
3. Publish the feature-status registry.
4. Connect the public dashboard to machine-readable status data.
5. Keep independent verification fail-closed.
6. Add automated schema/contract checks for every public capability.

## P1 — accounts, content and creator economy
1. Authentication and profile service.
2. Creator publishing service.
3. Media library and multilingual content pipeline.
4. Product/service listing.
5. Orders, delivery and receipts.
6. Creator earnings ledger.
7. Refunds, disputes and appeals.
8. Privacy, abuse reporting and account recovery.

## P2 — research and AI
1. Claim registry UI.
2. Evidence/counter-evidence map.
3. Comparative research explorer.
4. Independent-review packet generator.
5. AI/ML/NLP research assistant with source citations.
6. Automission queue, audit log and failure intelligence.
7. Cross-repository federation with least-privilege credentials.

## P3 — education, jobs and services
1. Learning paths and course delivery.
2. Skills profile.
3. Job board and application workflow.
4. Freelance marketplace.
5. Research-as-a-service.
6. AI services marketplace.
7. Creator and researcher portfolio pages.

## P4 — nature, economy and justice research
1. Nature/Earth indicators.
2. Resource and environmental impact ledger.
3. Yatharth Economy research simulator.
4. Justice/fairness research dashboards.
5. Policy simulation with explicit assumptions and human review.

## Definition of done
A module is complete only when its code, tests, documentation, security model, user-facing status, operational monitoring and rollback/appeal path exist at the level appropriate to that module.
