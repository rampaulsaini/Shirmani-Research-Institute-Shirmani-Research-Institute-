# Public Welfare & Satisfaction Dashboard Contract

## Purpose
Provide a transparent public measurement layer for the platform's stated social and environmental objectives.

## Dashboard families
- user access and accessibility
- education participation
- employment/freelancing opportunities
- creator earnings
- marketplace service quality
- customer support and resolution
- essential-service programs
- environmental indicators
- biodiversity/forest/ocean research indicators
- safety and abuse reports
- independent verification status
- automation reliability

## Metrics
Each metric must declare:
- definition
- population/time period
- data source
- methodology
- uncertainty/limitations
- last update
- responsible system
- independent-review status where applicable

## Satisfaction
Measure satisfaction through voluntary, privacy-respecting feedback and outcome metrics. Do not infer satisfaction merely from screen time or engagement.

## Transparency
The dashboard must distinguish architecture, deployed features, observed measurements, author proposals and independently verified findings.
