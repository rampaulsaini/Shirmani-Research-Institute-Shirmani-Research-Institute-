# Yatharth Humanity, Nature and Protection Governance

## Aim
Translate the author's stated objective of protecting human dignity, nature, Earth and living beings into a testable governance architecture without treating philosophical or metaphysical claims as independently verified facts.

## Core domains
1. Human dignity and basic-needs access
2. Education and healthcare access
3. Food, shelter and essential-service discovery
4. Environmental protection and ecological monitoring
5. Forest, ocean, biodiversity and pollution research
6. Transparent public-resource monitoring
7. Non-discrimination and equal-access controls
8. Complaint, appeal and dispute-resolution pathways
9. AI safety, privacy and security
10. Independent audit and verification

## Governance rule
AI systems may monitor, classify, recommend, simulate and automate routine operations, but high-impact decisions affecting rights, access, accounts, significant finances or justice require defined human oversight, appeal and auditability.

## Evidence discipline
The platform must distinguish Author Source, Normalized Claim, Evidence, Counter-Evidence, Model/Proposal and Independent Verification. Statements about civilization, consciousness, historical eras or universal truth remain attributed claims unless independently supported.

## Environmental implementation loop
Observe -> measure -> model -> disclose -> prioritize -> act -> audit -> publish results.

## Human-satisfaction principle
Measure access, safety, quality, affordability, resolution time, user-reported satisfaction and ecological outcomes. Do not equate engagement duration with welfare.
