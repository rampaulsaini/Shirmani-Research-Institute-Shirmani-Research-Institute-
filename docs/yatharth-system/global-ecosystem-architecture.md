# Yatharth Global Ecosystem Architecture

## Purpose

Define the public-facing ecosystem around the Yatharth / Shirmani Research Institute while keeping implementation status, evidence status, and independent verification separate.

2. Creator Studio
3. Research Institute
4. Education & Skills

6. Yatharth AI Music
7. Media, Audio, Podcast & Live
8. Digital Store
9. Freelancing & Services
10. Employment & Opportunities
11. Business & Marketplace
12. Yatharth Economy

14. Yatharth Justice — information, fairness, complaints, dispute-resolution and review layer
15. Nature, Earth & Humanity
16. Community & Participation
17. Trust, Evidence & Independent Verification
18. Public Transparency Dashboard
19. Multilingual Access
20. Help, Safety, Privacy & Account Controls

## User lifecycle

Discover → Account → Create → Publish → Discoverability → Connect → Learn/Work/Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Resolve Issues → Earn/Support → Improve.

## AI-native operating layer

NLP interprets content and intent; ML supports ranking, matching, personalization and anomaly detection; agents execute bounded tasks; Automission coordinates recurring work; audit and policy gates monitor outputs.

Automation must be fail-closed for high-impact actions. Account suspension, large financial actions, disputes, justice outcomes and independent verification require explicit policy controls, appeal paths and human oversight.

## Human and planetary objective

The author's stated objective is to promote a shift from a head/mind-centered viewpoint toward a balanced heart-centered viewpoint and to improve protection of people, living beings, Earth, forests, oceans and the wider physical environment. These are preserved as author goals, not asserted as independently verified scientific conclusions.

## Scale vision

The project may use 8.5 billion (850 crore) as a long-term aspirational reach target. It is not a current user count or a guaranteed outcome.

## Completion rule

A module is not called LIVE merely because documentation or a workflow exists. Status must distinguish:

PLANNED → DESIGNED → IMPLEMENTED → TESTED → DEPLOYED → LIVE → AUTONOMOUSLY OPERATED.

Evidence and independent-verification status remain separate from product readiness.
