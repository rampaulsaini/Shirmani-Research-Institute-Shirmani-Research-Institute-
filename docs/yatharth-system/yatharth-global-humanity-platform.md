# Yatharth Global Humanity Platform — Public + Autonomous System Architecture

Status: architecture proposal / implementation contract

## Purpose
Convert the author's latest source direction into an explicit platform architecture while preserving four layers:
1. Author Source — stated experience, values and proposed Yatharth framework.
2. Design Proposal — software, economic, educational, social and governance mechanisms derived from that source.
3. Evidence — independently sourced information and measurable outcomes.
4. Independent Verification — qualified external review where a claim is intended to be presented as verified.

Software automation must never be treated as independent verification of philosophical or metaphysical claims.

## Public platform
A multilingual public ecosystem combining social participation, knowledge, education, research, creativity, commerce, freelancing, employment and community services.

Account holders may, subject to rules and applicable law:
- create profiles;
- publish text, images, audio, video, music and educational material;
- share knowledge and research;
- offer freelance services;
- list permitted digital products;
- teach or learn;
- join communities;
- discover opportunities;
- buy and sell permitted products/services;
- use AI-assisted creation, translation, search and research;
- control privacy and visibility;
- appeal consequential moderation or account decisions.

## Human-centered governance
The design objective is impartial, transparent and accountable administration, not unchecked concentration of power.

High-impact decisions should provide:
- published rules;
- explainable decision records where feasible;
- notice and appeal;
- human review;
- independent review for defined categories;
- audit trails;
- conflict-of-interest controls;
- separation of operations from independent verification;
- emergency safeguards with retrospective review.

Any future relationship to elected offices, courts or public institutions must remain subject to applicable law, constitutional arrangements and due process. Software must not claim supreme control over lawful public institutions.

## Basic-needs objective
The author's source proposes universal access to essentials such as food, clothing, housing, healthcare and education. The platform can support this objective through resource discovery, verified service directories, education access, employment/skills matching, community coordination, local support routing and impact measurement.

Software alone cannot guarantee universal provision; real-world resources, institutions, infrastructure and lawful delivery remain necessary.

## Nature / Earth / living systems
First-class public domain for biodiversity knowledge, forest/habitat information, pollution reporting, conservation projects, environmental education, community action, evidence-backed impact tracking and sustainability opportunities.

Environmental claims should be evidence-linked and independently reviewable.

## Customer satisfaction
Optimize for useful participation and sustainable satisfaction rather than compulsive screen time. Measure task completion, service quality, safety, accessibility, transparent pricing, dispute resolution, response time, genuine utility, user-reported satisfaction and creator/buyer outcomes. Provide controls for recommendations and notifications.

## Scale target
The author's stated aspiration is 850 crore (8.5 billion) consumers/users. This is recorded as a target, not a current capability, forecast or guarantee.

Design requirements: global multilingual operation, mobile-first access, low-bandwidth resilience, accessibility, privacy/security, horizontal scaling, disaster recovery, observability and regional compliance.

## Implementation gates
Every public module:
PLANNED → SPECIFIED → BUILT → TESTED → SECURITY REVIEWED → DEPLOYED → OBSERVED → IMPROVED

Every research claim:
AUTHOR_SOURCE → NORMALIZED → EVIDENCE_MAPPED → COMPARATIVE_REVIEWED → READY_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW → INDEPENDENT_REVIEWED → VERIFIED / PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED / INCONCLUSIVE / NOT_VERIFIED

Do not collapse these states into one percentage.
