# SHIRMANI Supreme Production Factory

The factory exists to produce customer-visible digital artifacts, not merely workflow counts.

## Four levels
1. Institute — research, discovery, specification and backlog.
2. Factory — materialize concrete product artifacts.
3. QC / Gate — attach product-level QC metadata, version and dispatch state.
4. Showroom — expose the actual product, description, price/offer and customer route.

## Automission
- Runs every five minutes.
- Materializes up to 100 previously unmaterialized registered products per cycle.
- Writes product-specific HTML plus a machine-readable production manifest.
- Commits only when new production output exists.
- Stops naturally when the source catalogue is exhausted.
- Uses a 5000 target for future expansion without inventing unsupported products.

## Quality boundary
WORKFLOW SUCCESS != PRODUCT QUALITY != SCIENTIFIC VERIFICATION

A product is CONCRETE_PUBLIC when its customer-visible artifact has been materialized. QC/Gate is release discipline. Customer reviews and ratings are improvement inputs. Independent research verification remains a separate downstream state.

## Scaling
Scale by adding real source products, product-specific content, modules, media, tools, templates and delivery routes. Do not manufacture fake inventory merely to increase a numerical product count.
