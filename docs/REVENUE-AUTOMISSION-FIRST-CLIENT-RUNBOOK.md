# Shirmani Research Institute — Revenue Automission Runbook

Date: 2026-10-16  
Status: operational proposal / not evidence of sales or revenue

## Goal

Convert existing Hindi/English writing and research-summary capability into a measurable, low-risk client-acquisition loop. This runbook uses existing repository assets, including the Writing & Research Enquiry, sample portfolio, Digital Literacy Research Brief demo, Global Official Outreach Workbench, Deliverable Factory, Product Catalog, and Production Reality Gate.

## Hard truth gates

- A repository page, workflow run, draft email, or outreach list is not a lead, sale, delivery, or revenue.
- Never claim customer use, a completed delivery, independent fact-checking, or revenue without evidence.
- Do not scrape or bulk-message personal addresses. Use publicly published institutional/business contact channels and respect local anti-spam/privacy rules.
- Do not send email or WhatsApp automatically until a human reviews recipient, message, relevance, and opt-out wording.
- No work starts until scope, deliverables, price, deadline, revision count, and payment terms are agreed in writing.
- Treat the current ₹499 / ₹999 / ₹1,499 figures as unvalidated pilot reference prices, not proven market rates. Obtain owner approval before publishing binding prices.

## First sellable offer

**Bilingual Writing & Research Starter**

Choose one:
1. Hindi or English article/edit, up to 500 words.
2. Source-linked research summary using up to three agreed sources.
3. Website/blog draft, up to 1,000 words.

Potential buyer segments: small businesses, educators, independent researchers, non-profits, creator teams, and organisations that need clear bilingual public information. These are target hypotheses, not verified leads.

## Acquisition funnel

1. **Asset readiness** — inspect the live enquiry form, sample portfolio, demo, and contact routes. Record HTTP/access check and review date.
2. **Lead qualification** — record organisation, public source URL, publicly listed official contact, need hypothesis, language, source availability, and relevance rationale.
3. **Personalised first touch** — prepare a concise message tied to one specific public need; human approval required before sending.
4. **Discovery** — gather audience, objective, topic, language, word limit, sources, deadline, budget, format, and decision-maker.
5. **Written quote** — specify deliverables, exclusions, price, deadline, revisions, source policy, privacy, and payment terms.
6. **Paid kickoff** — record payment only after receipt is verified through the actual payment account/provider.
7. **Delivery** — produce files and a delivery manifest; track source ledger and limitations for research summaries.
8. **Acceptance** — record client acceptance or revision request, with consent for any testimonial.
9. **Closeout** — reconcile received payment, refunds/fees if known, delivery evidence, and follow-up date.
10. **Improve** — review conversion and quality metrics; do not optimise for volume at the expense of relevance or trust.

## Metrics and formulas

- Qualified-lead rate = qualified leads / researched leads.
- Reply rate = relevant replies / messages actually sent.
- Discovery rate = discovery calls or completed briefs / relevant replies.
- Quote rate = written quotes / qualified opportunities.
- Win rate = accepted paid quotes / quotes sent.
- Cash collected = payments actually received minus refunds; do not count quotes or unpaid invoices.
- Delivery on-time rate = accepted deliverables on/before agreed deadline / delivered jobs.
- Revision rate = jobs needing revisions / delivered jobs.
- Evidence completeness = jobs with scope + payment evidence + delivery evidence + acceptance state / closed jobs.

Record a baseline before setting performance targets. For the first 14 days, suggested activity target is 10 carefully researched, relevant organisations and up to 5 human-reviewed personalised drafts; this is an experiment target, not a sales forecast. Send only after human approval.

## Daily Automission cycle

READ repository assets → CHECK asset availability → QUALIFY relevant prospects → DRAFT personalised outreach → HUMAN APPROVAL → SEND via authorised channel → RECORD actual response → QUOTE → CONFIRM payment → DELIVER → CAPTURE acceptance → AUDIT metrics → UPDATE playbook.

Automation may research public organisational information, deduplicate leads, draft messages, validate required fields, and calculate metrics. It must not invent contacts, send unreviewed bulk outreach, mark payment received without evidence, or certify unsupported research claims.
