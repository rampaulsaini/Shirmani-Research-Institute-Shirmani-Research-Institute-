# SHIRMANI Production-First Operating Contract

Production is the primary objective. Verification is a downstream result-quality and promotion layer.

## Four public stages
1. Institute — discover and prioritize work, modules and product opportunities.
2. Factory — create concrete runnable digital product assets and product-specific passports.
3. QC — inspect produced artifacts, structure, metadata, QR/QC/gate fields and release conditions.
4. Showroom / Sale — publish produced products with category, description, price, offer, guarantee, packing, passport and sale state.

## Production truth
A workflow run is not itself a product. A product is counted as produced only when the repository contains a concrete public product asset and the product registry points to it.

## Dispatch
Dispatch defaults to NO. A product may be displayed and priced without implying that payment, fulfilment or delivery has occurred.

## Continuous operation
Scheduled GitHub Actions are intended to continue without an open ChatGPT conversation, subject to GitHub Actions availability, permissions, quotas and workflow health.

## Scale
Expand horizontally across discovered modules and vertically by increasing the depth and usefulness of each product. Do not manufacture counts merely to make progress look larger.

## Quantum boundary
Quantum may describe orchestration or a product family. Physical quantum execution must not be claimed unless a real quantum backend is configured.

## Success metrics
- concrete product assets produced per cycle
- modules processed per cycle
- new/updated public product pages
- product families covered
- QC-ready products
- showroom-published products
- dispatch-released products

Independent verification remains a downstream metric, not the production target.
