## 5. Delivery and payment safety
1. Agree in writing on scope, deadline, price, revision limit and permitted use.
2. For direct clients, agree on an advance/milestone before substantial work; on platforms use approved contracts/payment methods.
3. Avoid completing the full job as an unpaid “test”.
4. Retain source links, notes, version history and a final checklist.
5. Track gross billed, platform fees, refunds and net cleared cash separately. An invoice is not a payment.
6. Never pay registration/security/unlock fees to get a job; never share OTPs, passwords or banking credentials.
