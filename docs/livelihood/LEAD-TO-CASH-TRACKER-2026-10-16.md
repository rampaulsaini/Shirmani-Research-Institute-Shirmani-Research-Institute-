## Lead stages and evidence rules

1. **Candidate** — organisation and relevant need identified from a public source.
2. **Qualified** — fit, official contact route and likely need checked; source URL and check date recorded.
3. **Draft ready** — tailored message reviewed, not sent.
4. **Sent** — actual sent-message evidence recorded (date, channel, message/reference).
5. **Reply received** — actual response recorded; do not infer interest from delivery/read status.
6. **Scope confirmed** — requirements and deliverables written down.
7. **Quote sent** — amount and quote date recorded.
8. **Accepted** — customer's written acceptance recorded.
9. **In progress** — only after agreed start conditions are met.
10. **Delivered** — deliverable and delivery date recorded.
11. **Payment verified** — transaction evidence reconciled; only then mark paid and count revenue.
12. **Closed / declined / no response** — outcome and next permissible action recorded.

Never mark a lead, order, delivery or payment complete based on an intention, generated draft, sent-email assumption, screenshot without transaction context, or model-generated statement.

## Safety and quality gates
- No outreach automation or bulk sending without review and lawful basis.
- No work starts before written scope, final price, deadline, revision count and payment terms are agreed.
- Research deliverables distinguish source-supported facts, interpretation, uncertainty and missing evidence.
- Do not fabricate credentials, client testimonials, source citations or performance metrics.
- Count revenue only from reconciled transaction evidence; separate gross receipts, platform/payment fees, refunds and net receipts.
- If any link or deployed page is unverified, label it **NOT VERIFIED** rather than claiming it works.
