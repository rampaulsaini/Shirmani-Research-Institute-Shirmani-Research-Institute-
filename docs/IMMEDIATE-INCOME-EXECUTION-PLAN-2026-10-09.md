## 2. Priority: sell a service before expanding the catalogue

The fastest testable route is to offer a small, clearly scoped service using artifacts already visible in the repository. Start with one service, one sample, and one direct call-to-action; do not create more dashboards until this test has run.

### Proposed service offer (validate before publishing as a live offer)

**Website & Digital-Content QA Mini-Audit**
- Deliverable: review one public website/page; list up to 10 reproducible issues in links, mobile layout, wording, metadata, or content clarity.
- Handover: a short Markdown/PDF report with page URL, issue, evidence/screenshot where available, and suggested fix.
- Scope limit: one site, up to 5 pages, no hosting/account access required, no guarantee of ranking, sales, or conversion.
- Suggested introductory test price: ₹499–₹999 for the defined mini-audit, subject to the author's approval and actual ability to deliver.
- Optional next step: separately quote a small fix only after agreeing the scope and access.
- Evidence: save the request, agreed scope/price, delivered report, customer acceptance, and payment confirmation as separate records. Do not mark income as received before payment evidence exists.

Alternative service only if the author can demonstrate it with a sample: source-linked research organization / Markdown cleanup / simple static HTML content updates. Do not advertise advanced AI engineering or production automation as a paid capability without a working, reviewable sample.

## 3. First 48-hour execution checklist

### Day 1 — prepare a credible offer
- [ ] Pick one service and approve the proposed scope and price.
- [ ] Create one honest sample audit using a public page; label it as a sample, not a client result.
- [ ] Add a concise service card to `employment.html` or `income-command-center.html` only after the sample and contact route are ready.
- [ ] Verify the contact link, delivery method and payment method manually before claiming the offer is ready.
- [ ] Create a one-page portfolio entry showing the sample, scope, exclusions and turnaround time.

### Day 2 — find prospects and track outcomes
- [ ] Identify 10 relevant prospects whose public websites visibly have a fixable issue.
- [ ] Send individualized, respectful messages; do not bulk-spam or claim a prior relationship.
- [ ] Offer a brief observation and ask permission to send the sample audit; do not do extensive unpaid work.
- [ ] Track each contact as `identified`, `contacted`, `replied`, `scope agreed`, `paid`, `delivered`, or `closed-no-sale`.
- [ ] Record amounts as `proposed`, `invoiced`, and `paid` separately. Never count proposals or views as revenue.
