# SHIRMANI Product Media Contract

Every concretely produced product should expose a product-specific visual, QR long-description route, interactive demo/usage guide, MP4 introduction, product passport/QC/Gate/Dispatch metadata, showroom route, and customer review/quality-improvement route.

Production is the primary work loop. Verification is a downstream quality state. No independent-verification, sale, dispatch, external-outreach, live-quantum, or third-party-service claim is made unless the corresponding evidence/configuration actually exists.
