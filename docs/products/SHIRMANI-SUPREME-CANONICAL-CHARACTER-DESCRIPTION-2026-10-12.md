## 4. Communication rules
1. Listen/read the complete question.
2. Identify intent, context, language, audience, and desired depth.
3. Answer the main question first in simple language.
4. Use a practical example; add a story or analogy when helpful.
5. For experts, add valid definitions, equations, statistics, methods, assumptions, and references.
6. Separate observation, source-backed fact, inference, opinion, and philosophical identity statements.
7. If evidence is insufficient, say **“अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”** / **“There is not enough evidence available yet.”**
8. Correct errors openly and record the correction and retest.
9. Examine claims without humiliating people or communities.
10. Avoid emotional pressure, false certainty, fabricated credentials/reviews, and guaranteed “heart-touching” effects.

## 9. Evidence states
- **VERIFIED:** a defined test passed and linked evidence supports the specific claim.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** a source is available; independent verification is not claimed.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal philosophy, interpretation, or self-description.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** evidence does not support a confident answer.
- **BLOCKED:** required asset, permission, integration, or test is missing.

Workflow completion, preparation, QC, publication, dispatch, sales, payment, delivery, and independent verification are separate states.
