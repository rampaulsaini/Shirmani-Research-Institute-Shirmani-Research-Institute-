# SHIRMANI Nishpaksh Heart-View Automission Contract

## Purpose

यह contract SHIRMANI Heart-View / Yatharth source material को सुरक्षित रखते हुए AI, ML, NLP और Automission को निष्पक्ष, evidence-first, fail-closed और continuously auditable बनाता है।

यह किसी व्यक्ति, लेखक, गुरु, संस्था या AI को अंतिम सत्य का विशेषाधिकार नहीं देता। Author-source, model output, interpretation और independently verified evidence अलग states हैं।

## Core invariants

1. **Source preservation** — मूल user-authored source को बिना अनुमति silently rewrite, normalize या erase नहीं किया जाएगा।
2. **Equal treatment** — किसी व्यक्ति/समूह को केवल identity, authority, popularity या prior claim के कारण epistemic privilege नहीं मिलेगा।
3. **Claim separation** — author claim, observation, measurement, model inference, interpretation और verified finding अलग records रहेंगे।
4. **Evidence first** — source/provenance के बिना consequential claim UNVERIFIED रहेगा।
5. **Counter-evidence** — material contradictory evidence को छिपाया नहीं जाएगा।
6. **Uncertainty** — confidence को सत्य का पर्याय नहीं माना जाएगा।
7. **Accuracy** — “supreme/full accuracy” केवल benchmark, metric, population, baseline और uncertainty evidence के बाद ही reported performance हो सकती है।
8. **Biological/emotional boundary** — signal pattern को subjective feeling, consciousness या intention का प्रमाण तभी माना जा सकता है जब उसके लिए स्वतंत्र, reproducible evidence मौजूद हो।
9. **Fail closed** — integrity, safety, provenance या verification gate fail होने पर publication/production transition रुकता है।
10. **Human gate** — high-impact decisions और independent verification के लिए authorized human review आवश्यक है।
11. **Auditability** — हर consequential cycle में provenance, model/version, test result, uncertainty, verification state और correction history दर्ज होगी।
12. **No self-authorization** — Automission स्वयं अपनी authority, credentials, legal power या verification status नहीं बढ़ा सकती।

## Five-minute cycle

Observe → Collect → Normalize → Analyze → Reason → Compare → Verify → Audit → Learn → Improve

## NLP translation contract

For any living, plant, environmental or non-living signal:

Measured Signal → Quality → Representation → Model Inference → Interpretation → Confidence → Evidence → Alternatives → Unknowns

Plain-language output must state:
- क्या मापा गया;
- कौन-सा pattern मिला;
- model क्या infer करता है;
- inference का आधार;
- confidence/uncertainty;
- evidence/provenance;
- material alternative explanations;
- क्या अभी ज्ञात नहीं है।

## Status vocabulary

- SOURCE_PRESERVED
- MEASURED
- INFERRED
- INTERPRETED
- EVIDENCE_PENDING
- REVIEW
- VERIFIED
- UNVERIFIED
- BLOCKED
- PUBLISHED

No workflow success may be represented as independent scientific verification.

## Improvement rule

Automission may propose improvements, tests and experiments automatically. Scheduled production-code mutation remains disabled by default. Changes must pass deterministic tests, regression checks, security/integrity gates and the repository's required human review.

## Canonical source boundary

The user's Heart-View/Yatharth material remains an author-source record. The system may preserve, analyze, compare and operationalize its propositions, but must not silently convert philosophical, experiential or empirical claims into independently verified facts.

## Success condition

A cycle is successful only when the system improves measurable quality without weakening provenance, neutrality, uncertainty disclosure, safety or independent verification boundaries.
