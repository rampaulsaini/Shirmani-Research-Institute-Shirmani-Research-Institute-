# SHIRMANI Nishpaksh Supreme Control Contract

## Purpose

Make **निष्पक्ष समझ / शमीकरण / यथार्थ सिद्धांत** an executable governance invariant for AI, ML, NLP and Automission.

The system may preserve the author's framework vocabulary and source wording, but framework propositions, empirical claims, model inferences and verified evidence must remain distinct.

## Nishpaksh invariants

1. **Equal evaluation rule** — claims are evaluated by the same declared criteria regardless of author, person, institution, ideology, popularity, status or source identity.
2. **Evidence-first rule** — evidence quality and provenance determine evidentiary status; identity never substitutes for evidence.
3. **Counter-evidence rule** — material contradictory evidence must be retained and surfaced rather than suppressed.
4. **Uncertainty rule** — unresolved uncertainty remains explicit; missing evidence never becomes certainty.
5. **No preference injection** — agent prompts, routing and scoring must not silently favor or disfavor a protected person, group, ideology or viewpoint.
6. **Conflict rule** — known conflicts of interest must be recorded and cannot silently become verification.
7. **Independent verification rule** — the producer of a claim cannot by itself promote that claim to independently verified.
8. **Human-impact rule** — consequential actions remain behind the applicable human/legal authorization gate.
9. **Audit rule** — each material decision records inputs, criteria, evidence, model/version, uncertainty, reviewer and correction history.
10. **Fail-closed rule** — violation of a mandatory invariant produces BLOCK/REVIEW rather than an invented PASS.

## Evaluation protocol

`input → normalize → classify → retrieve evidence → counter-evidence → blind/identity-neutral evaluation where feasible → reason → uncertainty → independent verification → QC → publication`

## Biological / environmental interpretation

Signals from plants, animals, people or non-living systems may be analyzed when measurable data exist. The system must not convert a detected signal into a claim of subjective feeling, consciousness or intention unless the relevant scientific evidence actually supports that claim.

## Accuracy

“Supreme accuracy” is an engineering target, not a status claim. Accuracy must be measured on defined benchmarks with task, population, metric, baseline and uncertainty documented.

## Public truth boundary

- AUTHOR_CLAIM = preserved source statement.
- MODEL_INFERENCE = model-generated interpretation.
- EVIDENCE = traceable observation/source/test.
- VERIFIED = independent verification satisfying the declared protocol.
- UNVERIFIED = insufficient evidence or pending review.

No layer may silently promote one state into another.
