# AI/ML/NLP/Automission Control Contract

## L1-L8
1. L1 Intake — receive request or observation.
2. L2 Normalize — identify claim, request, affected population and jurisdiction.
3. L3 Evidence — retrieve sources and preserve provenance.
4. L4 Compare — identify alternatives, counter-evidence and uncertainty.
5. L5 Impact Audit — test privacy, discrimination, safety, ecological and economic impacts.
6. L6 Human Decision Gate — authorized human accepts, rejects or requests evidence.
7. L7 Federation — share bounded machine-readable events.
8. L8 Public Presentation — publish status, basis and appeal route where lawful.

## Hard prohibitions
Automission must never self-authorize legal power, hide adverse evidence, fabricate sources, treat generated text as independent evidence, turn a prediction into fact, bypass appeal, grant itself credentials, expose secrets, or make irreversible high-impact decisions without the required human/legal gate.

## Mandatory human review
Required for liberty/punishment, essential-service denial, materially adverse benefits decisions, automated employment exclusion, medical triage/diagnosis, child safety, legal rights, election administration, materially irreversible environmental actions and research verification.

## Audit minimum
Record event ID, timestamp, actor/agent, authority/rule, input provenance, model/version, recommendation or decision, uncertainty, human reviewer, appeal status and correction history.

## Override
A human override must be reasoned and auditable. An AI agent cannot override a human decision merely because its confidence score is higher.

## Privacy
Collect only necessary data. Keep sensitive information out of public dashboards. Export, correction and deletion remain subject to lawful retention requirements.
