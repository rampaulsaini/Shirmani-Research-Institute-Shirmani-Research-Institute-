# Evidence-first Signal → Language Layer

The Research Institute translates measurable signals into simple human language while keeping **measurement, model inference, and subjective interpretation separate**.

Pipeline: signal → normalization → feature extraction → model → evidence → confidence → plain language.

Future modalities include audio, vibration, temperature, electrical/biological signals, imagery, video, environmental sensors, and time-series measurements.

A signal pattern is not automatically evidence of consciousness, emotion, pain, intention, or subjective experience. The platform reports what was measured, what pattern was detected, what a validated model predicts, confidence/uncertainty, and limitations.

For biological systems, claims about subjective feeling require validated operational definitions, controlled experiments, reproducible data, appropriate controls, and independent replication. Until then the public status remains MODEL_INFERENCE_NOT_PROOF.
