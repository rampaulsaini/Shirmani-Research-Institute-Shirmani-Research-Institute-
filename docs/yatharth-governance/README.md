# Yatharth Neutral Governance & Public-Service Architecture

This module translates the author's Yatharth framework into a neutral governance research architecture. It is not a proposed real-world government, legal advice, or a claim that any person, AI system, or institution possesses a right to rule others.

## Core principle
Impartiality is treated as a process property, not a personal claim. A system needs published rules, equal procedures, conflict disclosure, reasoned decisions, evidence and counter-evidence, appeal, independent audit, correction, privacy and due process.

The phrase "निष्पक्ष Supreme Control" is operationalized as supreme constitutional constraints and independent review, not unchecked personal control.

## Institutional map
| Layer | Function | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Citizens / communities | participation and feedback | equal procedural access |
| Public administration | service delivery | standards + audit |
| Elected executive (PM/President or equivalent) | policy execution | constitutional, legislative and judicial limits |
| Legislature | law-making and accountability | public record + constitutional review |
| Courts | adjudication and rights protection | independence, reasons, appeal |
| Independent oversight | audit and complaints | protected independence |
| AI/ML/NLP agents | analysis and assistance | no autonomous final deprivation of rights |
| Evidence layer | traceability | provenance + privacy |
| Environmental stewardship | nature protection | measurable ecological constraints |

Exact constitutional structures vary by jurisdiction and must follow legitimate legal and democratic processes.

## AI control rule
AI may classify, summarize, retrieve evidence, detect anomalies, estimate needs, flag harms, propose options and monitor service indicators.

AI may not by itself convict a person, impose irreversible punishment, remove legal rights, decide an election, declare research independently verified, create legal tender, override a court/constitution, or silently change public policy.

High-impact automated decisions require a lawful basis, human authorization, explanation, review path and audit.

## Essential services objective
The architecture can research pathways toward universal access to food security, safe housing, essential clothing/goods, healthcare, foundational education, clean water/sanitation, accessibility support and public information.

"Free" is an economic and policy question. A real system must identify financing, eligibility, capacity, supply chains, safeguards and outcomes before claiming universal free provision.

## Nature and living systems
Measure air, water, soil, biodiversity, habitat, climate/disaster resilience, resource use, animal/plant welfare and pollution risk. AI can monitor indicators; accountable institutions make consequential decisions.

## Truth boundary
1. Author source — original philosophical statements.
2. Normalized framework — structured interpretation.
3. Governance proposal — design option.
4. Empirical evidence — independent data.
5. Legal status — what a jurisdiction recognizes.
6. Independent verification — qualifying human review.

Workflow completion, AI output, publication, hashes, citations or author testimony alone do not establish independent verification.
