# Constitutional Supreme-Control Model

"Supreme control" means supremacy of transparent constitutional constraints and rights, not supremacy of a person.

## Checks and balances
A jurisdiction-specific implementation may include elected executive leadership, representative law-making, independent judiciary, election/audit bodies, public administration, privacy/information oversight and environmental oversight. Exact offices and powers must follow the jurisdiction's constitution and law.

## Consequential decision record
Rule -> Authority -> Evidence -> Alternatives -> Impact assessment -> Decision -> Reasons -> Appeal -> Review -> Outcome

Missing records create a TRANSPARENCY_GAP; AI must not silently invent them.

## Emergency powers
Every emergency workflow should have a legal basis, narrow scope, start/end time, affected rights, responsible authority, independent review, public reporting where lawful and automatic expiry unless lawfully renewed.

## Public trust
Trust is not inferred from popularity, follower count, AI confidence or workflow volume. It is built through observable procedures, accountability and correction.
