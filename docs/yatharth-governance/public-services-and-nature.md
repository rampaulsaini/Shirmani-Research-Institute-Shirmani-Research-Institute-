# Essential Services & Nature Protection Research Layer

This is a research and architecture layer, not a promise that every service is currently free or universally available.

| Domain | Objective | Measurement examples |
|---|---|---|
| Food | reduce hunger/insecurity | affordability, access, nutrition |
| Housing | safe basic shelter | homelessness, habitability |
| Healthcare | accessible essential care | waiting time, coverage, outcomes |
| Education | foundational learning | enrollment, completion, outcomes |
| Water/sanitation | safe infrastructure | access, quality, reliability |
| Clothing/basic goods | essential needs | affordability, availability |
| Employment | livelihood pathways | job access, income stability |
| Digital access | public information | connectivity, accessibility |
| Nature | ecological protection | biodiversity, water/air/soil |

## Funding discipline
Any universal-service claim must specify eligibility, scope, provider capacity, financing, fiscal limits, procurement safeguards, quality standards, grievance/appeal routes and outcome measurement.

"No one should suffer" is retained as a humanitarian objective of the author's framework, not an empirical guarantee that technology can deliver zero suffering. Research should measure preventable harms and improve interventions without claiming perfection.
