# SHIRMANI SUPREME — Product Description & Presentation Standard
**Status:** Specification drafted; implementation and media assets require separate verification.
**Project:** Shirmani Research Institute

**Identity line:** *Shiromani Rampal Saini — Tulanateet Kaalateet Shabdateet Premateet Shashwat Vastavik Swabhavik Satya Pratyaksh Samaksh*

The presentation expresses the owner's stated values: impartial understanding, simplicity, ease, clarity, transparency, respect, and continuous satisfaction. Present these as the owner's philosophy and intended design values—not as independently proven universal or scientific facts.

A calm, context-aware digital presenter listens, identifies the user's goal, explains material at the right level, distinguishes evidence from interpretation, acknowledges uncertainty, and corrects errors transparently. It must not demean people based on identity, community, religion, income, education, or organisation.

## 2. Product-card visual specification
Every product gets its own individual, versioned card—not a generic image reused as if it represented every product.

Required elements:
1. Product-specific name and version.
2. Plain-language short description (target 12–24 words).
3. Owner-provided original portrait/logo, unchanged in facial identity; crop/scale only unless separately approved.
4. Product-specific QR pointing to that product's stable public passport page.
5. Demo status: **Demo available**, **Demo pending**, or **Demo unavailable**.
6. A working call to action such as **Open demo**, **Read guide**, or **Request access**.
7. Responsive, legible scaling, high contrast, and accessible alt text.
8. English identity line beneath the portrait where layout permits; never cover the face or obscure product details.

**QR rule:** Each QR encodes that product's canonical passport URL. Never reuse one QR across products. Validate the destination before marking it verified. A QR is a pointer, not proof of originality, quality, ownership, or security.

## 3. Product passport
Each passport should include product ID/name/version/status/update date; purpose and intended users; capabilities and exclusions; step-by-step guide and prerequisites; demo URL and transcript (or explicit pending status); screenshots labelled as real or mock-ups; test cases/results/limitations/evidence; price, deliverables, licence, support and refund terms where applicable; privacy/security notes; enquiry path; changelog and feedback mechanism.

Do not publish customer reviews, ratings, test results, sales, institutional contacts, or performance claims without real evidence and permission.

## 4. Demo video standard
Each product needs its own demo recording or clearly labelled concept demo:
1. Problem and intended user.
2. Prerequisites and starting state.
3. On-screen workflow.
4. Expected result and one relevant test.
5. Limitations, version, and access/purchase path.
6. Captions and readable transcript.

A product is demo-complete only when the video/link opens, matches the current version, and has been checked for misleading claims.

## 5. Human-presentation pipeline
Voice → authorised voice integration → speech recognition → context/intent → evidence gate → answer/content → authorised audio output → portrait/avatar → lip-sync → live presentation → audit → improvement.

Use the exact owner-supplied portrait/audio only when source files are accessible and use is authorised. Never substitute a generated face or synthetic voice while labelling it original. Preserve the portrait except for approved crop/scale. Label AI-generated animation, speech, or lip-sync where viewers could mistake it for an unedited live recording.

### Evidence states
- **VERIFIED:** defined test passed and evidence is linked.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** source exists, but independent verification is incomplete.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal philosophy, interpretation, or self-description.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** say plainly, “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

## 6. Product naming and quality gates
Use distinct descriptive names, not unsupported claims such as “world's best” or “unmatched.”

**First proposed service/product name:** HeartView Explain — Hindi/English Research & Content Assistant  
**Short description:** Turns complex topics into clear Hindi or English explanations, research summaries, and website content with sources and stated limitations.

Before marking a product publish-ready, verify:
- [ ] Unique catalogue name and version.
- [ ] Understandable short description.
- [ ] Product passport URL opens.
- [ ] QR encodes that URL and scans successfully.
- [ ] Original portrait asset is present and visually checked.
- [ ] Demo opens and matches the version, or is marked pending.
- [ ] Screenshot is genuine or labelled a mock-up.
- [ ] Guide, limitations, pricing, and contact path are present.
- [ ] Evidence and test results are linked.
- [ ] No unsupported sales, endorsement, uniqueness, or scientific claims.

## 7. Delivery metrics
Track actual counts: approved product names / total listed; valid passport URLs / total listed; successful QR scans / total; demos reviewed and opening / total; cards using the approved portrait / total; test cases passed / test cases run; enquiries, proposals, paid orders, and revenue from actual records only.

Do not report percentages before checking numerator and denominator. Workflow runs and generated files alone do not prove product quality, independent verification, or income.
