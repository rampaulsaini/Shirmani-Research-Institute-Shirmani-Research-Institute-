# SHIRMANI Research Institute — First-Client Outreach Playbook
**Prepared:** 2026-10-10  
**Purpose:** Turn existing Hindi/English writing and research capability into a measurable, ethical first-client pipeline.  
**Status:** No prospect has been contacted and no revenue is claimed by this document.

## 1. Sell only work deliverable now

Treat these as proposed pilot prices. Confirm exact scope and price in writing.

| Offer ID | Service | Pilot scope | Proposed price | Target turnaround after written approval |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| SRI-WRITE-PILOT-01 | Hindi/English writing polish | Up to 500 supplied words; one revision | ₹499 | 2 working days |
| SRI-RES-PILOT-02 | Research summary | Up to 1,000 words from up to 5 agreed public/client-provided sources; source list and limitations | ₹999 | 3 working days |
| SRI-WEB-PILOT-03 | Website/blog draft | One page or post up to 800 words; one revision | ₹1,499 | 3 working days |

Do not offer scientific certification, legal/medical/financial advice, guaranteed rankings/business outcomes, independent verification, live AI-avatar production, or integrations unless separately qualified, connected and tested.

## 2. Initial prospect groups

Prioritise relevant prospects instead of indiscriminate mass-mailing:
1. Small businesses with outdated website copy or service descriptions.
2. Educators and training providers needing course pages or bilingual explainers.
3. NGOs/community organisations needing plain-language summaries of public reports.
4. Creators needing article editing, biographies or podcast/video descriptions.
5. Small web/design agencies that may subcontract overflow writing.

For each prospect, record the public organisation name, official contact page, specific relevant need, source URL and why the offer fits. Use official published business channels; do not scrape private addresses or bypass contact preferences. Follow recipient rules and applicable law. No bulk send or automated follow-up is enabled here.

## 4. Short-message template

I noticed [specific public need]. Would you like a short scope and proposed quote? If this is not relevant, I won’t message again. [public platform URL]

Use WhatsApp only where the number is published for business enquiries or the recipient has given permission. Do not send repeated unsolicited messages.

## 5. Follow-up and safety policy

- Send one genuinely personalised, relevant first message.
- At most one polite follow-up after 5–7 business days, only if the channel and applicable rules permit it.
- Stop after a decline, opt-out, bounce, or request not to contact.
- Never buy scraped lists, impersonate institutions, fake references, fabricate testimonials, or imply endorsement.
- Do not automate sending until sender identity, permission/legal basis, stop/unsubscribe handling, privacy notice and provider configuration are reviewed.

## 6. Lead tracker fields

Copy these columns into a spreadsheet:

`Lead ID | Organisation | Segment | Official source URL | Published contact channel | Specific observed need | Offer ID | Date actually contacted | Channel/permission basis | Status | Next action date | Quote agreed | Payment evidence reference | Delivery status | Actual outcome`

Suggested statuses: `Researching / Draft ready / Sent / Replied / Scope agreed / Declined / Opted out`. Keep private customer data out of public records. Leave sent-date and payment fields blank until supported by actual records.

## 7. Two-week experiment (targets, not predictions)

- Days 1–2: prepare two short writing samples and one source-linked research-summary sample using public or self-created material.
- Days 3–5: identify 15 well-matched prospects from official public pages; record source and contact rules.
- Days 6–8: manually review and send up to 5 personalised messages through permitted channels.
- Days 9–11: if appropriate, send up to 5 more and handle replies promptly.
- Days 12–14: make no more than one permitted follow-up to eligible first contacts; record replies, scope requests and objections.

### Report only measurable outcomes

- Prospects researched = complete, source-linked lead records.
- Messages sent = messages supported by actual sent-mail/contact records.
- Replies = replies actually received.
- Qualified enquiries = enquiries with topic, deliverable and deadline.
- Quotes issued = written quotes actually sent.
- Orders confirmed = written acceptance of scope and price.
- Revenue received = cleared-payment evidence only.
- Deliveries accepted = client acceptance or agreed completion evidence.

Do not count drafts as sent messages, quotes as sales, invoices as paid revenue, or workflow runs as commercial results.
