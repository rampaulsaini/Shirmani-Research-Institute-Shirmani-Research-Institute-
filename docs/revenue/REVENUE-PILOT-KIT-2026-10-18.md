# SHIRMANI Research Institute — Revenue Pilot Kit
Date: 2026-10-18
Status: Prepared for review; no outreach sent, customer acquired, order accepted, or income received is claimed.

## Goal for the first 7-day pilot
Validate whether the existing Hindi/English writing and research service can attract a first paid assignment, using a small, manually reviewed set of relevant prospects. Do not spend money on ads or buy contact lists for this pilot.

## Offer to test
**Source-grounded writing and research support**
- Hindi/English editing and proofreading (proposed starting price: ₹499; up to 700 words).
- Website/blog draft (proposed starting price: ₹999; up to 1,000 words).
- Source-based research brief (proposed starting price: ₹1,499; about 800–1,200 words, typically 3–5 credible sources where available).

These are proposed starting prices already displayed in the service page, not market-average claims or binding quotes. Confirm complexity, source access, length, deadline, revisions, rights, taxes and payment terms in writing before accepting any job.

## Who to approach first
Build a small prospect list from official public contact pages, prioritising organisations that already publish material and plausibly need it:
1. Local education/training providers needing bilingual course or website copy.
2. Small research, policy, environment or community organisations needing source summaries.
3. Small businesses with outdated or unclear Hindi/English service pages.
4. Independent publishers, newsletters or knowledge projects needing editing.
5. NGOs/community initiatives needing clearly sourced project briefs.

For each prospect, record why the offer is relevant and cite the public page used. Do not assume need, invent a named contact, or treat a generic contact form as an email address. No scraping, purchased lists, bulk spam or automatic sending. Human-review every message and comply with the recipient's contact preferences.

## Seven-day operating sequence
- Day 1: Review the existing portfolio and service page; select 2–3 genuine, shareable samples and check that each is safe to publish.
- Day 2: Identify 10 relevant prospects through official public pages. Record source URL, fit rationale and legitimate contact route.
- Day 3: Draft 10 individually tailored messages. Check names, facts, scope and links manually.
- Day 4–5: Send only the messages approved by the owner; record sent timestamps and the actual channel. A draft is not a sent message.
- Day 6: Respond to real replies; create a written quote only after scope is clear.
- Day 7: Review funnel numbers, time spent and feedback; adjust one variable for the next cycle.

## Metrics and stop rules
Report these separately: prospects researched, tailored drafts prepared, messages approved, messages actually sent, replies, qualified conversations, quotes issued, quotes accepted, paid orders verified, deliveries accepted, cash received, hours spent and net contribution after direct costs.

- Enquiry ≠ order.
- Proposal ≠ acceptance.
- Acceptance ≠ payment.
- A screenshot or self-entered status is not sufficient payment proof by itself; reconcile against a legitimate transaction record.
- Do not publish client names, messages or deliverables without permission.
- If no relevant prospects or replies emerge, revise targeting/message; do not inflate numbers.
- Stop immediately on opt-out, incorrect recipient, privacy concern or any request that appears fraudulent.
