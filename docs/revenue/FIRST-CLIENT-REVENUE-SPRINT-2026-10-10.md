# First Client Revenue Sprint — 10 October 2026

## Purpose
Turn currently available services into a real, manually delivered first paid project. This is an execution plan, **not evidence of a customer, message sent, sale, or income**.

## Services deliverable now
1. Hindi/English writing and editing.
2. Source-linked research summaries.
3. Website/blog content drafts.

Do not sell unconnected AI voice cloning, automatic research, live video generation, bulk email, checkout, or integrations not demonstrably connected.

## Proposed pilot offers (confirm scope before quoting)

| Offer | Proposed price | Included | Exclusions |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Writing polish | ₹499 | One supplied text up to 800 words; clarity/language edit; one revision | New research or large-document translation |
| Source-linked research summary | ₹999 | One focused question; up to 5 agreed credible sources; 700–1,000-word summary with links and limitations | Exhaustive systematic review or guaranteed conclusions |
| Website/blog draft | ₹1,499 | One brief-based draft up to 1,000 words, title/headings, one revision | Website development or ranking guarantee |

Prices are proposed starter references, not validated market rates. Agree in writing on scope, word count, sources, deadline, revisions, rights, confidentiality, price, and payment terms before work starts.

## Prospect segments to research
- Local coaching centres and independent educators: course descriptions and bilingual explainers.
- Small businesses: website service pages and FAQs.
- NGOs/environmental or community initiatives: source-linked project summaries.
- Independent researchers/student projects: literature summaries and editing, not ghostwriting assessed work.
- Website owners/bloggers: article refreshes and Hindi/English adaptation.

For each lead, record its official website, a specific page showing a relevant need, publicly listed business contact channel, date checked, and a one-sentence fit rationale. Prefer contact forms or addresses explicitly published for business enquiries. Never guess emails, scrape private data, or send repetitive bulk messages.

## Lead tracker fields
Prospect ID; organisation/sector; official website; evidence of need; official contact page/published business email; source-check date; tailored offer; draft reviewed; actually sent (date/channel); reply; qualified enquiry; quote sent; scope accepted; payment actually received; delivery status; feedback/next improvement.

Keep “identified”, “drafted”, “sent”, “replied”, “quoted”, “accepted”, and “paid” separate. A draft is not outreach; a quote is not revenue.

## Measurement
Track: prospects researched, qualified prospects, drafts prepared, messages actually sent, replies, qualified enquiries, quotes, accepted scopes, payments received, deliverables completed, revisions, ratings, and hours. Calculate conversion rates only when the denominator is nonzero. Revenue means actual payments received, not proposed prices.

## Human approval and integrity gates
- Human reviews every prospect and message before sending.
- Use official/public business contact routes and relevant, respectful messages.
- Honour opt-outs; no bulk spam or repeated pressure.
- Do not claim clients, credentials, outcomes, certifications, or income without evidence.
- Store only necessary prospect data and respect confidentiality.
- This document does not imply automated email/WhatsApp or payment integration.
