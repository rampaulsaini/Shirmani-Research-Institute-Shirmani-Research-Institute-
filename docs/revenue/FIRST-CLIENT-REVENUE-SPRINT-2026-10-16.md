# First-Client Revenue Sprint — 2026-10-16

**Status:** execution plan / measurement sheet; not evidence of outreach, sales, payment, or revenue.
**Service actually offered today:** Hindi/English editing, article/web copy, and source-based research briefs.
**Canonical public menu:** `services-writing-research.html`. Proposed pilot rates only; confirm a custom written quote before accepting work.

## 1. Sell one clear, deliverable service

| Offer | Scope | Starting pilot rate | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Editing / proofreading | Up to 700 words; one edited version; one in-scope revision | ₹499 | Written scope and accepted quote |
| Article / website copy | Up to 1,000 words; headings; one revision | ₹999 | Written scope and accepted quote |
| Source-based research brief | About 800–1,200 words; 3–5 credible sources where available; facts, interpretation and gaps separated | ₹1,499 | Written scope and accepted quote |

Rates are proposals, not market-validated prices or a promise of customer demand. Do not advertise a different service rate in an outreach message without first updating the canonical menu and obtaining owner approval.

## 2. Who to approach first

Prioritise a small, relevant set of organisations that already publish content:
- education providers and training organisations that need bilingual explanatory content;
- research groups and non-profits that need readable summaries of public research;
- small businesses with an outdated service page or blog;
- publishers or community organisations that need Hindi/English editing.

Use only a verified, public official contact route relevant to the service. No bulk messaging, scraped personal addresses, fabricated relationships, or claims of endorsement. Respect contact policies and opt-outs.

## 3. Seven-day experiment (targets, not guarantees)

- Research 20 relevant prospects and record why each is a fit.
- Verify the official contact route for each prospect before any message.
- Manually review and send at most 10 individually tailored messages where contact is appropriate.
- Offer a short sample or fixed-scope quote; do not perform substantial unpaid work.
- Follow up only where appropriate and permitted.
- Review actual results after seven days; revise the offer based on evidence, not assumptions.

## 4. Lead-to-cash ledger

Create one row per prospect/order and record:
- date; organisation; official website/contact page; service fit;
- contact route and verification date;
- message status (draft / sent / reply / opt-out);
- enquiry brief; quoted scope; price (INR); deadline; revisions;
- written acceptance date; payment terms; invoice/reference;
- delivery date; customer acceptance; amount actually received;
- evidence link or reference; next action; complaint/refund status.

### Status rules

- **Researched:** relevant prospect identified; no contact implied.
- **Contact verified:** official route checked; no message implied.
- **Sent:** a real message was sent; retain date and evidence.
- **Replied / Qualified:** actual reply and clear need recorded.
- **Quoted:** written deliverables, deadline, revisions, price, and payment terms sent.
- **Won:** client explicitly accepted the written quote.
- **Delivered:** agreed files were delivered and recorded.
- **Paid:** payment receipt/settlement checked.
- **Verified revenue:** received amount reconciled to payment evidence. A lead, quote, invoice, workflow run, or promise is not revenue.

## 5. Delivery and quality gate

Before work starts, confirm in writing: objective, audience, topic, language, word limit, source links/files, deadline and timezone, deliverables, revision count, price, taxes if applicable, attribution/confidentiality, and payment terms.

Before delivery, check: factual claims and source links; separation of fact from interpretation; uncertainty and evidence gaps; word count; spelling and grammar; originality; client brief match; privacy; and promised file format. Never guarantee sales, search ranking, scientific novelty, or outcomes outside the agreed deliverable.

## 7. Automation boundaries

Current repository browser-local enquiry and deliverable tools can help draft briefs and packs, but they do not by themselves send email, store leads on a server, collect payment, independently verify research, or prove sales. Keep human approval before sending, quoting, and publishing. Never place API keys, passwords, payment credentials, or private customer data in public repository files.
