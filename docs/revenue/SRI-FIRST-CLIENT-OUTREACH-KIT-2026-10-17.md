# SRI First-Client Outreach Kit
Date: 2026-10-17
Status: Ready-to-use draft kit. No prospects contacted, sales made, or income received are claimed by this file.

## Objective
Secure the first *verifiable* paid writing/research assignment for Shirmani Research Institute by offering a narrow, clearly scoped service. Use the existing enquiry form and portfolio; do not build another overlapping tool before testing the current path.

- Enquiry form: `writing-research-enquiry.html`

- Samples: `writing-research-portfolio.html`
- Workbench: `outreach-income-workbench.html`

## Offers to test
These are indicative starting rates shown in the existing offer material, not guaranteed quotes or evidence of market validation:
- Editing / proofreading: from ₹499
- Article / website copy: from ₹999
- Source-based research brief: from ₹1,499

Confirm the exact scope, sources, word count, turnaround, revision limit, rights/attribution, price, taxes if applicable, and payment schedule in writing before starting.

## Prospect selection — choose relevant, real organisations
These are prospect *categories*, not a claim that individual prospects have been found or contacted. Find each organisation's official website and public business contact, verify that the need fits the offer, and personalise the message:
1. Local small businesses needing a clear service/about page.
2. Training centres needing course descriptions or bilingual notices.
3. NGOs needing a short, source-based programme brief.
4. Research/education groups needing literature summaries.
5. Independent consultants needing website/blog copy.
6. Publishers or editorial teams needing Hindi/English editing.
7. Startups needing landing-page copy.
8. Community organisations needing bilingual informational material.
9. Small professional firms needing FAQs and service explainers.
10. Creators needing scripts, captions, or structured research notes.

Do not invent contact names or email addresses. Do not scrape private data, mass-spam, misrepresent credentials, or imply an endorsement. Send one individually reviewed message through a legitimate public channel; record the actual send event.

## Outcome ledger
Create one row per organisation and keep these states separate:
- Identified (official source URL recorded)
- Qualified (specific need and fit recorded)
- Draft prepared
- Human-reviewed
- Sent (date + channel recorded)
- Reply received (date and outcome)
- Scope/quote accepted (written evidence)
- Payment received (amount, date, transaction evidence)
- Deliverable sent
- Client acceptance / revision
- Costs and hours recorded

Count revenue only after payment is actually received and recorded. A draft is not a sent email; a sent email is not a reply; a reply is not a sale; an accepted quote is not cash received.

## Seven-day low-cost test
Day 1: Select 10 real organisations from the categories above and record official source URLs plus a specific fit reason.
Day 2: Prepare 5 tailored messages; review accuracy, tone and contact legitimacy.
Day 3: Send up to 5 individually reviewed messages and record actual sends.
Day 4: Prepare the next 5 tailored messages.
Day 5: Send the remaining messages only after review; do not send duplicates.
Day 6: Respond to real replies; create a written scope and quote where requested.
Day 7: Count verified sends, replies, quote requests, accepted quotes, payments, hours and costs. If results are weak, change the audience or offer before increasing volume.

## Stop / revise rules
- No official contact route or no verifiable fit: skip the prospect.
- A requested deadline is unrealistic: propose a realistic date or decline.
- Scope or source rights are unclear: clarify before quoting.
- No written acceptance/payment terms: do not begin paid work.
- No response: record it honestly; do not report it as a lead or sale.
- Any privacy, security, or consent concern: stop and review.

## Completion criteria for this experiment
The experiment is complete only when the ledger contains the actual results and a short evidence-based decision: repeat, revise, or stop. This document alone is not proof of outreach, client acquisition, sales, or income.
