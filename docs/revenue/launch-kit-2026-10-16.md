# SHIRMANI Research Institute — Revenue Service Launch Kit
**Status:** Proposed operating kit; review before public activation  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-16  
**Scope:** Current deliverable capacity is Hindi/English writing and research. This kit does not claim sales, customer outcomes, payments, or revenue.

## 1. First offer to sell

Lead with one narrow service rather than marketing the entire future platform:

**Hindi/English Research & Content Sprint**
- One defined output: a research summary, website/blog article, or copy edit.
- Client supplies the goal, audience, language, word range, deadline, and any source material.
- Institute confirms scope, source limits, delivery date, revision count, final quote, and payment terms in writing before starting.
- Delivery includes the agreed document and a short source/method note where research is included.
- Exclusions unless explicitly quoted: legal/medical/financial advice, fabricated citations, guaranteed search ranking, guaranteed funding, and unlicensed copyrighted material.

## 3. Lead-to-cash stages

Use one record per prospect or enquiry. Keep only business-relevant contact data and record the lawful source of the contact.

1. **Prospect identified** — organization, public source URL, reason for fit, date checked.
2. **Contact validated** — official public business contact; no guessed email addresses.
3. **Draft prepared** — personalized, factual, no unsupported claims.
4. **Approval required** — human approval before first contact or bulk send.
5. **Sent** — timestamp, channel, recipient, message version, delivery status.
6. **Reply / opt-out** — capture the response; suppress future outreach immediately after opt-out.
7. **Enquiry qualified** — goal, deliverable, language, deadline, source material, budget.
8. **Quote accepted** — written acceptance and payment terms documented.
9. **Payment verified** — provider transaction evidence, not a screenshot or promise alone.
10. **Work delivered** — deliverable link/file, delivery timestamp, scope checklist.
11. **Acceptance / revision** — customer response and revision count.
12. **Closed / learning** — actual collected revenue, direct costs, refund, and feedback.

Never infer payment, sale, or revenue from a website view, message sent, quote issued, GitHub run, generated file, or an unverified payment screenshot.

## 6. First 30-day experiment

This is a target plan, not a forecast or guarantee.

- Days 1–3: resolve price conflicts; approve service scope, turnaround, revisions, payment and refund terms.
- Days 4–7: publish three clearly labelled portfolio samples: (a) Hindi explainer, (b) English website article, (c) source-cited research brief. Mark illustrative samples as samples, not client work.
- Days 8–14: manually identify 20 well-matched organizations using official public sources; record source URL and fit reason. Draft personalized messages; obtain approval before sending.
- Days 15–21: send only approved messages, respond to replies, and qualify enquiries. No bulk automation until deliverability, consent, opt-out handling, and compliance are tested.
- Days 22–30: deliver any accepted paid work within agreed scope; request permission before using client work as a portfolio sample; review conversion and delivery quality.

## 7. Measurement dashboard

Report counts and rates with the underlying denominator and date range:

- Prospects researched
- Contacts verified
- Messages approved / sent / delivered
- Replies and positive replies
- Qualified enquiries
- Quotes sent / accepted
- Payments verified
- Deliverables on time
- Revisions per job
- Customer acceptance / complaints / refunds
- **Collected revenue** = verified cleared receipts − refunds
- **Contribution before overhead** = collected revenue − direct delivery costs − transaction fees

A target of 20 prospects is a work target only. Do not report a conversion rate until the denominator is non-zero, and do not claim revenue until cleared payment evidence exists.

## 8. Automation and security gates

Automate safe administrative steps first: deduplication, stage reminders, missing-field checks, quote templates, invoice status reminders, delivery checklist, and aggregate metrics.

Keep human approval for first-contact messages, final quotes, changes to payment details, refunds, and publication of customer material. Require authentication, role-based access, audit logs, secrets management, backups, rate limits, input validation, and a tested recovery path before connecting email or payment services. Never place API keys or customer contact lists in public repository files.
