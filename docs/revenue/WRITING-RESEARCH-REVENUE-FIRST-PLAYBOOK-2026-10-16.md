# Shirmani Research Institute — Revenue-First Writing & Research Playbook
**Status:** Proposed operating playbook; not evidence of sales, outreach, payment, or revenue.  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-16

## 1. First revenue lane
Start with services already represented in the public enquiry desk:
- Hindi / English writing
- Source-based research summary
- Website / blog content

Do not market unfinished AI/ML products as production-ready. Keep product sales separate from service enquiries until the relevant product has a verified demo, limitations, price/quote, support terms, and order route.

## 2. Proposed pilot price anchors (INR)
These are test prices, not validated market rates. Confirm scope and final quote in writing before accepting work.

| Offer | Proposed starting price | Example scope to confirm |
|---|---:|---|
| Short writing task | ₹499 | One short draft, language and word limit agreed first |
| Research summary | ₹999 | Short summary from a defined set of sources, with source links and limitations |
| Website/blog content | ₹1,499 | One page or article draft, audience and length agreed first |

For work beyond the agreed scope, quote separately. Do not promise legal, medical, financial, or other regulated professional advice. Do not fabricate citations, data, endorsements, client names, or results.

## 3. Mandatory customer flow
1. Enquiry received (record timestamp and source).
2. Clarify objective, audience, topic, language, word limit, source links, deadline, budget, and revision needs.
3. Review feasibility and source quality.
4. Send written scope and quote.
5. Obtain written approval of deliverables, deadline, price, revision policy, and payment terms.
6. Record payment only after checking the actual payment confirmation; never store card/bank credentials in this tracker.
7. Start work only after the agreed start condition is met.
8. Deliver the agreed file and source list.
9. Record acceptance, revision request, or refund/dispute status.
10. Ask for feedback; publish testimonials only with explicit permission and accurate wording.

## 7. Initial 7-day experiment
- Day 1: Check live enquiry form, links, and contact route on desktop/mobile; submit a test enquiry only if it can be clearly identified as internal testing.
- Day 2: Prepare three sample deliverables using non-client/public material and label them samples.
- Day 3: Build a list of 15 relevant organisations from official public sources; record source URL, need hypothesis, and contact route. Do not claim these are customers.
- Day 4: Manually review and send up to 5 tailored messages, subject to applicable rules and appropriate contact basis.
- Day 5: Reply to real enquiries; issue written scope/quotes where suitable.
- Day 6: Follow up only where appropriate; record opt-outs and stop contact when requested.
- Day 7: Report actual prospects, messages sent, replies, quotes, approvals, payments, deliveries, time spent, and blockers. If no replies or sales occurred, report that plainly.

## 8. Evidence gate
Every status is one of:
- **VERIFIED:** directly checked evidence supports the claim.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** a source exists but was not independently verified.
- **PROPOSED:** a planned price, target, or process.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** cannot currently support the claim.

Never present philosophical statements or personal identity claims as scientific findings. Research outputs must distinguish sourced facts, interpretation, and uncertainty.

## 9. Product/security release gates
For any digital product, keep separate fields for artifact exists, test passed, demo captured, limitations documented, price/quote published, enquiry route tested, payment route tested, and production status. A workflow run is not proof of customer value or security. Before production activation, require dependency review, secret handling, least privilege, input validation, authorization checks, logging without sensitive payloads, backup/recovery plan, and a documented security test. Do not perform unauthorized penetration testing.
