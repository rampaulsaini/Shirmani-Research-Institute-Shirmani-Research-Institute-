# SHIRMANI Research Institute — First-Client Revenue Sprint

**Version:** 1.0  
**Prepared:** 2026-10-18  
**Purpose:** Turn the already-declared Hindi/English writing and research services into a measurable, honest client-acquisition workflow.  
**Status:** Operating plan; no outreach, sale, payment, or revenue is claimed by this document.

## 1. Sell what can be delivered now

Start with a narrow service offer rather than advertising unfinished platform features.

1. **Research summary:** summarize client-supplied or agreed public sources, separate evidence from interpretation, provide links/references, and list open questions.
2. **Hindi/English writing and editing:** draft, simplify, proofread, translate, or restructure content to a defined brief.
3. **Website/blog content:** deliver an agreed page or article with headings, calls to action, and source notes when factual claims require them.

Do not advertise a task as available until its scope can be fulfilled with the current tools and skills. Do not claim specialist legal, medical, financial, or scientific certification.

## 2. Pilot packages (proposed, not market-validated)

These are low-friction starting quotes for testing demand, not guaranteed market rates. Confirm a fixed quote after reviewing the brief.

| Package | Proposed pilot price | Included deliverable | Boundary |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Starter edit | ₹499 | Up to 500 words edited or simplified; one revision | Client supplies source text |
| Research brief | ₹999 | Up to 1,000 words, source-linked summary, key findings and limitations | Up to 5 agreed sources |
| Website/blog draft | ₹1,499 | One draft up to 1,000 words, headings and one revision | Research-heavy work quoted separately |

If a brief exceeds the stated limit, needs specialist expertise, or has an urgent deadline, pause and re-quote before accepting. Prices can be changed after the first ten qualified enquiries based on effort, conversion, and client feedback.

## 3. Required order acceptance gate

Before work starts, record in writing:

- Client name and a reply address supplied by the client
- Objective, intended audience, language and tone
- Deliverable type, word limit, source requirements and exclusions
- Delivery date, review window and number of revisions
- Final price, currency, payment schedule and refund/cancellation terms
- Permission to use supplied materials and whether the work may be shown in a portfolio
- Acceptance of the scope by both parties

Never begin based only on an ambiguous chat. Never store passwords, identity documents, or unnecessary sensitive data in the brief.

## 4. First 7-day acquisition experiment

This is a target plan, not a record of completed activity.

| Day | Action | Measurable output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Review the existing writing/research enquiry page and portfolio; remove broken links and unsupported claims | One checked service page; issue list |
| 2 | Prepare 3 short, clearly labelled sample pieces using original or permitted material | 3 samples with language, service type and word count |
| 3 | Create a prospect list from publicly listed official contact pages, only where writing/research services are relevant | 20 qualified prospects with source URL and reason for fit |
| 4 | Personalize a small first batch; send only through permitted official channels | Up to 5 individually checked messages; sent/not-sent log |
| 5 | Follow up only where appropriate and not prohibited; record replies | Reply count and next actions |
| 6 | Scope each real response and send a written proposal | Number of qualified briefs and proposals |
| 7 | Review results; change one variable at a time | Enquiries, replies, proposals, paid orders, revenue and lessons |

Avoid bulk unsolicited messaging, scraped personal addresses, deceptive urgency, fabricated testimonials, and claims of global reach. Respect opt-outs and applicable anti-spam/privacy rules. Do not claim any message was sent unless a sent record exists.

Personalize the bracketed details using facts checked from the organisation's own public source. Do not send this as a mass blast.

## 6. Minimum prospect and revenue ledger

For each prospect, store only what is necessary:

`prospect_id, organisation, public_source_url, fit_reason, contact_channel, date_checked, status, next_action, opt_out, proposal_value, paid_amount, payment_reference_redacted`

Allowed statuses: `researched`, `ready_for_review`, `approved_to_contact`, `sent`, `replied`, `qualified`, `proposal_sent`, `won_paid`, `lost`, `opted_out`.

Only the owner should approve a new outreach batch. Keep proposal value, invoiced value and money actually received as separate fields.

## 7. Metrics and honest reporting

Report weekly:

- Qualified prospects identified
- Contacts approved and actually sent
- Replies and qualified enquiries
- Proposals sent
- Orders accepted
- Amount invoiced
- Amount received (cash/bank receipt evidence)
- Delivery on time, revisions, refunds and customer feedback

Conversion rate = replies ÷ messages actually sent.  
Proposal win rate = paid orders ÷ proposals sent.  
Realized revenue = payments actually received, less refunds where applicable.

Use “0” only when the ledger was checked and confirms zero; otherwise use “not yet measured”. Do not infer income from clicks, views, drafts, or open proposals.

## 8. Platform and product safety boundary

The public showroom may display the broader roadmap, but distinguish **working demo**, **prototype/specification**, **integration pending**, and **commercially available**. Do not state that login/signup, payment, email delivery, WhatsApp messaging, live avatar Q&A, voice cloning, lip-sync, or global-scale capacity works unless the exact flow has been tested and recorded. Keep the Heart-View philosophy respectfully attributed to its author; do not present personal percentage ratios as independently validated scientific measurements.
