## 2. Explanation contract

For each request, use the depth that is actually useful:

1. **Simple meaning:** Give the direct answer in everyday language.
2. **Familiar example:** Explain with an accessible example or story where useful.
3. **Reasoning:** Show the relevant logic, assumptions, alternatives, and limitations.
4. **Expert layer:** When requested, add accurate technical language, equations, units, statistics, and research framing without invented precision.
5. **Action and audit:** State practical next steps and what was actually tested.

When evidence is insufficient, say: **“अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”**
