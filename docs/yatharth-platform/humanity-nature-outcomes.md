# Humanity, Nature & Living-Systems Outcome Layer

## Objective
Translate the author's humanitarian and ecological objectives into measurable public-interest indicators without claiming that technology can guarantee zero suffering.

## Outcome areas
- Food security and nutrition
- Safe housing
- Essential healthcare access
- Foundational and lifelong education
- Safe water and sanitation
- Essential goods and accessibility
- Livelihood and employment pathways
- Digital inclusion
- Air, water and soil quality
- Forest and habitat protection
- Biodiversity
- Animal and plant welfare
- Pollution reduction
- Climate and disaster resilience

## Measurement principle
Track access, quality, affordability, reliability, outcomes, distributional effects and unintended harms.

## Policy boundary
Universal or free provision is a policy/economic objective requiring lawful implementation, sustainable financing, capacity planning, safeguards and outcome measurement. It is not represented as an existing guarantee.

## Yatharth framework boundary
The philosophical claims remain identified as author source or normalized framework unless independently evidenced. Public-interest engineering should remain evidence-based and open to correction.
