# Yatharth Public Interface Map

Primary navigation:
Home | Social | Yatharth | Research | Education | AI | Music & Media | Freelancing | Jobs | Business | Digital Store | Economy | Yatharth Currency | Yatharth Justice | Nature & Humanity | Verification & Trust | Community | Creator Studio | Dashboard | Help & Support

Registered users may, subject to policy and law, create profiles, publish content, interact, create communities, offer or seek services, list eligible digital products, create educational material, participate in courses, create music/media, manage creator/business dashboards, and submit complaints, appeals and support requests.

Discovery: global search across people, posts, research, courses, services, jobs, products, music/audio, communities and evidence records.

Trust labels must distinguish Author Source, AI-generated/drafted, Evidence Mapped, Independently Reviewed, Verified, Inconclusive and Not Verified. Automated workflow success must never be displayed as independent verification.

Commerce surfaces should expose applicable seller identity, description, price, delivery terms, refund/cancellation terms, taxes/fees, reviews and dispute/support route.

Design targets: mobile-first, low-bandwidth friendly, accessible, multilingual, privacy-aware and transparent about provenance.

Engagement should follow usefulness rather than dark patterns; optimize for durable user value, satisfaction, safety and trust rather than simply time spent.
