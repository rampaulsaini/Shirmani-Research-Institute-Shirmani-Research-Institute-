## Account capabilities
Authenticated users may, subject to platform rules and applicable law:
- create and manage a profile;
- publish text, images, audio, video, music and other supported media;
- follow, discover, comment and communicate;
- create communities;
- offer services and freelance work;
- list permitted digital products;
- publish educational material and courses;
- participate in research and evidence review;
- view their activity, sales, earnings and reputation records;
- report content and request review or appeal.

## Public experience principles
- Mobile-first and multilingual.
- Accessible and low-bandwidth aware.
- Clear distinction between author statements, evidence, analysis and independently verified material.
- Search, discovery and recommendations should optimize user value, safety and relevance rather than manipulation.
- Commerce must disclose price, seller, delivery and refund conditions where applicable.
- High-impact moderation and justice decisions require documented appeal paths.

## Platform layers
Public UI -> application services -> AI/ML/NLP services -> Automission orchestration -> audit/observability -> human oversight for high-impact actions.

This document describes architecture, not a claim that every listed feature is already deployed.
