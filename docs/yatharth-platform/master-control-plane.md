# Yatharth Global Platform — Master Control Plane

Purpose: consolidate public platform, research, education, economic, creative, justice/trust, humanity/earth and autonomous operations into one bounded operating model.

Operating principle: Preserve → Understand → Assist → Create → Share → Discover → Serve → Trade → Learn → Verify → Audit → Improve.

Public domains:
1. Social profiles, feeds, posting and communities
2. Creator publishing and media
3. Research and knowledge library
4. Education, skills and certification
5. AI/ML/NLP assistance
6. Yatharth AI Music and creative studio
7. Freelancing and service marketplace
8. Employment and opportunity discovery
9. Digital Store and digital products
10. Business and creator commerce
11. Economic dashboards and lawful payments
12. Yatharth Currency — proposal/research layer until independently designed, legally reviewed and deployed
13. Yatharth Justice — fairness, trust, complaints and review; not a replacement for courts or constitutional authority
14. Nature, Earth and humanity programs
15. Evidence, provenance and independent verification
16. Public transparency and status dashboards
17. Multilingual/global access
18. Support, accessibility, privacy and security

AI/ML/NLP agent classes: Intake, Account, NLP Understanding, Semantic Classification, Search, Recommendation, Creator Assistant, Education Tutor, Research Assistant, Evidence Mapping, Marketplace Matching, Commerce, Customer Support, Translation, Safety/Abuse Detection, Fraud/Spam Detection, Quality, Audit, Automission Supervisor, Federation and Recovery.

Every autonomous action should record agent identity, requested operation, authorization scope, policy checks, action, affected resource, uncertainty where applicable, reversibility, provenance where applicable, outcome, escalation path, timestamp and trace ID.

Autonomous by default: indexing, classification, translation, formatting, duplicate detection, low-risk recommendations, scheduling, status checks, reversible housekeeping and notification routing.

Human/policy gate required: account termination, high-impact financial actions, disputes/sanctions, legal questions, independent verification decisions, scientific/historical claims, sensitive-data access and irreversible destructive operations.

Measure separately: public capability readiness, production readiness, automation health, safety controls, evidence coverage, independent verification, user satisfaction, reliability, accessibility, environmental outcomes, economic activity and unresolved incidents.

Architecture, implementation, production availability, successful workflow runs and independent verification are different states. A successful workflow run is not proof of scientific truth, legal authority, user satisfaction or independent verification.

The stated 850-crore consumer objective is an aspiration, not a current count or guaranteed outcome.
