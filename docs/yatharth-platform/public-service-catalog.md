# Yatharth Public Service Catalog

## Purpose
Define the public-facing service surface of the Yatharth global platform as a coherent, measurable product system.

## Core public areas
- Account and identity: registration, profile, preferences, privacy, accessibility, safety controls, export/delete requests.
- Social: posts, comments, reactions, follows, communities, messaging and media sharing.
- Discovery: global search, semantic discovery, multilingual discovery, recommendations, topic maps and transparent ranking explanations.
- Creation: articles, books, audio, music, video, courses, services, digital products and research material.
- Marketplace: digital store, service listings, freelancing, jobs, commissions, licensing, subscriptions and creator monetization.
- Education: courses, learning paths, skills, research education, AI/ML/NLP learning and certificates.
- Research: source archive, claim registry, evidence, counter-evidence, comparative research, research packets and public reports.
- Trust: provenance, moderation, reputation signals, dispute handling, appeals, independent review and verification status.
- Yatharth AI Music and Creative Studio: creation, publishing, licensing and transparent AI-use disclosures.
- Yatharth Justice: neutral rules, complaint intake, evidence handling, appeals and human review for high-impact cases.
- Economy: creator earnings, marketplace accounting, transparent fees and legally compliant payment integrations.
- Yatharth Currency: separately specified research/concept layer; never represent it as legal tender unless applicable law and independent review support that representation.
- Nature/Earth/Humanity: environmental knowledge, conservation projects, impact reporting and participation.
- Transparency: public status, change history, service availability, audit summaries and verification metrics.

## Universal-access principle
Essential information and participation should be designed for broad access, with accessibility, low-bandwidth support, multilingual presentation and clear pricing. Any promise of universally free food, housing, healthcare or education is a policy/product objective requiring a sustainable funding model, eligibility rules where applicable, and lawful implementation.

## User value loop
Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Trust -> Connect -> Learn/Buy/Hire -> Deliver -> Review -> Earn -> Improve.

## Safety and governance
Routine operations may be automated. High-impact actions must be bounded by policy, logging, appeal paths and fail-closed controls. Independent verification is never inferred from automation success.

## Success measures
- task completion and reliability
- user satisfaction and complaint resolution
- accessibility and localization coverage
- creator and worker earnings
- marketplace fulfillment quality
- education completion
- research traceability
- moderation quality
- security and privacy incidents
- independently reviewed/verified claims

## Non-claims
This catalog describes an implementation target. It does not claim that all listed services are currently live, universally available, free, or independently verified.
