# Yatharth Global Public Platform Architecture

## Purpose
Define the public-facing platform layer that connects social publishing, creator accounts, education, research, commerce, freelancing, AI services, media, justice/trust, and the Yatharth research system.

## Public experience
Users may create an account, build a profile, publish permitted content, follow creators, discover knowledge, offer services, list digital products, learn, buy/sell, communicate, review transactions, and use AI-assisted creation and discovery.

## Major public domains
1. Social & Profiles
2. Research & Knowledge
3. Education & Skills
4. AI & AI Services
5. Yatharth AI Music & Media
6. Freelancing & Employment
7. Digital Store & Marketplace
8. Yatharth Economy
9. Yatharth Currency (conceptual/economic research layer; not represented as legal tender without applicable authorization)
10. Yatharth Justice & Dispute Resolution
11. Community
12. Nature, Earth & Humanity
13. Trust, Evidence & Independent Verification
14. Creator Studio
15. Analytics & User Dashboard
16. Multilingual Global Access
17. Help, Safety, Privacy & Governance

## Core user loop
Create -> Publish -> Discover -> Connect -> Learn/Buy/Work -> Deliver -> Review -> Improve.

## Product principle
Optimize for user value, satisfaction, safety, trust, accessibility, and sustainable participation rather than maximizing time spent through manipulative engagement.

## Automation boundary
Routine classification, translation, search, recommendation, moderation assistance, support triage, workflow orchestration, quality checks, and reporting may be automated. High-impact account, financial, legal, dispute, safety, and independent-verification decisions require appropriate human oversight, appeal, and audit controls.

## Status model
Each public capability must be tracked independently as:
PLANNED -> BUILT -> TESTED -> LIVE -> AUTONOMOUS
No capability is marked LIVE or AUTONOMOUS merely because its documentation or workflow exists.
