# Implementation Phases

## Phase 1 — Foundation
Accounts, profiles, public navigation, content model, permissions, provenance, search, safety, privacy, and audit.

## Phase 2 — Social & Creator
Posts, media, creator pages, follows, comments, reactions, messaging, creator studio, multilingual publishing.

## Phase 3 — Knowledge & Education
Research library, courses, learning paths, AI tutoring, research assistant, evidence/claim views.

## Phase 4 — Commerce & Work
Digital store, product listings, orders, creator income records, freelancing profiles, service listings, jobs, client-provider matching.

## Phase 5 — AI Music & Media
Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast, video, multilingual creation, licensing metadata, creator workflows.

## Phase 6 — Economy & Justice
Yatharth Economy and Yatharth Currency research modules; transparent rules, complaint/dispute workflows, appeals, audit trails, and legal/compliance review.

## Phase 7 — Autonomous Operations
Connect the public platform to AI/ML/NLP agents, Automission supervision, observability, resilience, evaluation, and controlled federation.

## Phase 8 — Scale
Accessibility, low-bandwidth delivery, internationalization, capacity engineering, abuse prevention, privacy, security, disaster recovery, and independent audits.

## Completion criterion
The platform is complete only when each capability has implementation evidence, tests, monitoring, security/privacy review, documented ownership, and an explicit LIVE/AUTONOMOUS status where applicable.
