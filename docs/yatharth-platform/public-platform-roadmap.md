# Public Platform Completion Roadmap

## Phase A — Foundation
Account/profile, public navigation, search, content model, moderation policy, privacy/security, provenance and public status contracts.

## Phase B — Social
Posts, comments, media, follows, communities, messaging, creator profiles and discovery.

## Phase C — Work & Commerce
Freelancing, employment, services, digital store, digital products, courses, memberships, licensing and transaction records.

## Phase D — Education & Media
Academy, courses, skills, certificates, Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast, video, live and creator studio.

## Phase E — Research & Trust
Canonical source, claim registry, evidence/counter-evidence, comparison, operational tests, independent review and public verification dashboard.

## Phase F — AI Control Plane
AI/ML/NLP agents, semantic search, recommendation, matching, translation, support, audit, recovery and Automission federation.

## Phase G — Nature & Humanity
Nature/Earth/biodiversity protection initiatives, community participation, impact measurement and public transparency.

## Phase H — Scale
Multilingual, accessibility, low-bandwidth support, observability, reliability, privacy-preserving analytics and safe global scaling.

## Delivery rule
A module is not called LIVE merely because code or a workflow exists. LIVE requires an accessible public path, functional contract, tests, observability and documented status. AUTONOMOUS requires those conditions plus safe automation, auditability, rollback and appropriate human oversight.
