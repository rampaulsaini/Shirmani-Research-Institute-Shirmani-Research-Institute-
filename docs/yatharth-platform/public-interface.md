# Yatharth Global Public Platform — Public Interface Contract

## Purpose
Define the public-facing ecosystem for the Yatharth platform. This layer is separate from internal GitHub Actions, agents, QC and research infrastructure.

## Core public areas
- Social: accounts, profiles, posts, media, communities, messaging, follows and discovery.
- Research: Yatharth Research Institute, source archive, claims, evidence, comparison, verification and reports.
- Education: courses, learning paths, skills, research education, certificates and learning library.
- AI: Yatharth AI, AI research assistant, AI/ML/NLP tools, translation and creator tools.
- Music & Media: Yatharth AI Music, audio, podcast, video, live and creator studio.
- Work & Income: employment, freelancing, services, jobs, creator economy and research-as-a-service.
- Commerce: digital store, digital products, music/audio/books/courses/certificates, memberships and licensing.
- Economy: Yatharth economic model, value records and future currency research. No financial or monetary claim is treated as legally operational without applicable compliance.
- Justice & Trust: fairness, complaints, dispute resolution, transparency, accountability, appeals and independent review.
- Nature & Humanity: nature, Earth, biodiversity, humanity and practical protection initiatives.
- Community: contributors, researchers, creators, students, partners and volunteers.
- Public Dashboard: service health, research progress, evidence/verification state, transparency and change history.
- Governance: rules, privacy, security, responsible AI, provenance and public policies.

## Account model
Any eligible user may create an account, establish a profile, publish permitted content, offer permitted products/services, participate in communities and receive platform feedback. Identity, age, payment and other checks are applied only where required by the relevant service or law.

## Value loop
Create → Publish → Discover → Trust → Share → Buy/Sell → Deliver → Review → Improve.

## Automation principle
AI/ML/NLP/Automission should automate routine classification, discovery, translation, moderation assistance, matching, quality checks, support and system operations. High-impact decisions retain human review, appeal and audit paths.

## Product principles
Useful, safe, accessible, multilingual, transparent, privacy-preserving, evidence-aware, user-controlled and continuously auditable.

## Status vocabulary
PLANNED → BUILT → TESTED → LIVE → AUTONOMOUS (where safely appropriate).
