# Social, Commerce, Education and Economy Layer

## Unified ecosystem
The public platform connects:
- Social publishing and community
- Education and skills
- Research and knowledge
- Freelancing and employment
- Digital Store
- AI services
- Yatharth AI Music and creative media
- Creator economy
- Yatharth Economy and proposed Yatharth Currency concepts
- Yatharth Justice and dispute/review concepts
- Nature, Earth and humanity protection initiatives

## User value loop
Create -> publish -> discover -> learn/connect -> transact where applicable -> deliver -> review -> improve.

## Marketplace requirements
- Seller/service identity and profile controls
- Product/service description
- Price and currency disclosure
- Delivery/access terms
- Refund/cancellation policy where applicable
- Reviews with abuse controls
- Fraud and spam detection
- Dispute and appeal workflow
- Transparent platform fees
- Export/delete controls where legally applicable

## Education
Users can discover learning paths, courses, research materials, skills and certificates. Certification claims must identify the issuing authority and verification method.

## Yatharth Currency
"Yatharth Currency" is maintained as a proposed framework concept until a lawful, technically specified and independently reviewed implementation exists. The public interface must not represent it as legal tender or an operational financial instrument without the required authorization.

## Yatharth Justice
The justice layer is a framework for fairness, evidence, transparency, accountability, complaint handling and appeal. It must not impersonate a court or claim legal authority it does not possess.

## Nature and humanity
Platform incentives should support measurable environmental and social outcomes where feasible: education, conservation projects, resource efficiency, community support and transparent impact reporting.

## Customer satisfaction
Measure satisfaction, resolution time, quality, safety and repeat voluntary use. Do not use dark patterns or deceptive engagement mechanisms to maximize time spent.

This document defines architecture and safeguards; it does not claim that every service is deployed.
