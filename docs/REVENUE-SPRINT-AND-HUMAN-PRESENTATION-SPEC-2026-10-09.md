## 4. Live character / voice-to-presentation build specification

### User-facing persona
**Name:** SHIRMANI SUPREME HUMAN-PRESENTATION SYSTEM  
**Identity line for English-language visuals:** “Shiromani Rampal Saini — Beyond Comparison, Beyond Time, Beyond Words, Beyond Love; Eternal, Real, Natural Truth — Present Directly.”  
This is a self-description/creative identity statement, not a scientifically established universal fact.

**Stable communication qualities**
- Simple, natural, calm, clear and transparent language
- Respectful to people of every background; no status-based flattery or degradation
- Reflective listening: restate the question's intent before answering when needed
- Evidence-aware answers: source-based claims carry sources; uncertain claims are labelled
- Explicit insufficiency line: “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
- Distinguish personal philosophy/identity, source-based summaries, and independently tested findings
- Avoid promising that every person will feel a particular emotion or that the persona is objectively superior to every other human

### Measurable chain
Voice input → authorized speech recognition → transcript → intent/context → evidence lookup or supplied sources → answer draft → source/uncertainty gate → authorized voice output → licensed/authorized avatar or photo → lip-sync → live presentation → audit record.

### Four editions
1. **Primer / Preview:** scripted text, static approved portrait/avatar, no voice cloning.
2. **Creator:** recorded or licensed voice, prepared scripts, exported video; human review before publishing.
3. **Interactive:** speech-to-text, context-aware Q&A, source and uncertainty labels; explicit disclosure when AI-generated.
4. **Live Studio:** low-latency voice/avatar pipeline, session controls, monitoring, consent controls and fallback when providers fail.

These are planned editions, not claims that four functioning versions already exist. Provider integrations, pricing, latency, and quality must be tested separately before listing them as live.

### Required test gates
- Speech recognition accuracy on a declared test set
- Correct handling of ambiguous or unsupported questions
- Source-link validity and claim/source alignment
- Explicit insufficient-evidence behaviour
- Voice authorization and consent record
- Face/avatar asset permission and disclosure
- Lip-sync timing measured on sample clips
- End-to-end latency recorded, not guessed
- Accessibility captions and mobile playback
- Failure fallback, privacy retention policy, and incident log

### Voice/face consent rule
Use only the user's own voice/likeness or assets with documented permission. Keep original recordings secure, provide a way to disable/delete stored assets where applicable, and clearly disclose synthetic presentation. Never use a third party's likeness or voice without permission.

- Service/product; quoted amount; accepted scope; due date

## 6. Product showroom release gate

A product can be labelled **Available to try** only when a public demo is accessible. Label it **Available to buy** only when the deliverable, price, licence/terms, payment path, delivery method and support route are clear. For each product version, provide:
- Unique name and version
- Short on-card description and a real screenshot/visual
- Demo video or clearly labelled interactive demo
- QR code that resolves to the product's own canonical detail page
- Passport: purpose, use cases, steps, inputs/outputs, limitations, accessibility and support
- Tests actually executed, date, outcome and evidence
- Current price and offer expiry/conditions, if any
- Feedback route and a documented improvement queue

Do not generate fake ratings, fake reviews, fake QR verification, fake guarantees, or claim a product is fully built merely because a catalogue entry exists. Do not advertise “millions of agents,” quantum capability, 850-crore-user capacity, or global institutional adoption without concrete deployed evidence.
