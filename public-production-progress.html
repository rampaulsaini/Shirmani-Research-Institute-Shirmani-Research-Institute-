Commercial fulfilment, payment settlement, customer delivery and independent scientific verification are downstream states; they are not silently counted as complete merely because production exists.

अगला production focus

  1. Generic engine instances को meaningful product-specific content, templates, demos और use cases से deepen करना.
  2. Product detail → order/request → payment evidence → fulfilment → delivery evidence flow मजबूत करना.
  3. Customer reviews/ratings को quality-improvement queue में बदलना.
  4. Public showroom में categories, prices, offers और product states को continuously refresh करना.
  5. Institute → Factory → QC → Showroom loop को multi-lane Automission से लगातार feed करना.

ईमानदार progress rule

एक single “overall % complete” तभी meaningful होगा जब सभी workstreams के weights तय हों। इसलिए यह page measurable workstream percentages दिखाता है, मनमाना combined percentage नहीं।
