Research / Improvement routing

REVIEW → QUALITY SIGNAL → RESEARCH INTAKE → PRIORITY → PRODUCT CHANGE → QC → PUBLIC RELEASE

Rating केवल एक signal है। Detailed customer feedback, repeated issues, requested features और observed usability को अलग-अलग रखा जाएगा ताकि अगली production priority तर्कसंगत तरीके से तय हो।

क्या स्वतः नहीं माना जाएगा

  • Review = independent scientific verification नहीं।
  • Rating = guaranteed product quality नहीं।
  • Order-intent = payment नहीं।
  • Payment = delivery नहीं।
  • Produced = sold नहीं।
