# First-Revenue Operating Plan — Shirmani Research Institute

Status: planning artifact only. This is not evidence of customer outreach, sales, payments, or production activation.

## Repository baseline
Reviewed: writing-research-sales.html, writing-research-enquiry.html, writing-research-service-offer.html, and AGENT-GOVERNANCE.md.

## 1. Sellable services already represented in the repository

| Offer | Existing pilot reference | Initial scope |
|---|---:|---|
| Editing / proofreading | ₹499+ | Up to 500 words; one agreed revision |
| Source-based research summary | ₹999+ | Up to three agreed sources; key points, links, limitations |

These are unvalidated reference prices found in repository material, not market-validated rates, binding quotes, or income claims. Confirm scope, complexity, language, deadline, revisions, and payment terms in writing before accepting work.

## 2. Initial buyer segments
1. Small businesses and independent professionals needing bilingual website copy.
2. Researchers, educators, and nonprofits needing source-linked summaries.
3. Website owners needing editing, proofreading, and plain-language rewrites.

Begin with a small, relevant prospect list from public official contact pages. Record organization, source URL, fit, contact channel, date checked, and status. Do not guess addresses, scrape private data, or send bulk unsolicited messages.

## 3. Outreach workflow
Prospect sourced → relevance checked → personalized draft → human approval → send via authorized account → follow-up reminder → reply classified → scope confirmed → written quote → payment verified → delivery → feedback request.

A mailto/WhatsApp link opening does not mean a message was sent. Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy/anti-spam rules. Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, statistics, urgency, or results.

## 5. Weekly measurable funnel
Track actual dated counts for prospects researched, relevant prospects, drafts approved, messages actually sent, replies, qualified enquiries, accepted quotes, verified payments, on-time deliveries, revisions, and feedback. Keep planned, drafted, sent, replied, quoted, paid, and delivered as separate statuses.

Conversion rate = next-stage count / prior-stage count × 100, only when the denominator is non-zero. Identify the evidence source for each count.

## 6. Automission backlog
1. Intake: validate enquiry fields and required details.
2. Evidence: preserve source URLs; separate verified, source-based, philosophical, and insufficient-evidence claims.
3. Proposal: draft scope and quote; require human approval.
4. Outreach: prepare personalized drafts; send only through authorized, tested integrations with approval.
5. Transaction: record only provider-confirmed payments; never store bank/card secrets in repository files.
6. Fulfilment: checklist, deadline, quality review, and documented handoff.
7. Audit: timestamps, state changes, source references, and failure reasons; minimize personal data.
8. Security: least privilege, secret scanning, dependency checks, backups, rollback.
9. Publishing: link/accessibility checks and real deployment test.
10. Learning loop: monthly review of actual reply, conversion, delivery, and revision metrics.

## 7. First milestone acceptance criteria
- [ ] Production service/enquiry links verified.
- [ ] Test enquiry can be created, copied, and manually sent.
- [ ] Quote template states scope, price, deadline, revision limit, and payment terms.
- [ ] Prospect ledger separates drafts from sent messages and confirmed transactions.
- [ ] Email, WhatsApp, payment, voice, or avatar integrations are not called connected without a successful end-to-end test.
- [ ] Revenue is recorded only after payment receipt is verified.
- [ ] Weekly funnel report cites evidence for its counts.

## 8. Claims not made
This plan does not claim that a prospect has been contacted, a customer has ordered, income has been earned, production links have been tested, or any AI/voice/avatar/security integration has passed end-to-end tests.
