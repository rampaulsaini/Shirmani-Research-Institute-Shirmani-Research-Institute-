एक product passport में क्या होना चाहिए?

01 · Identity & provenance

स्थिर Product ID, नाम, निर्माता/अधिकार धारक का रिकॉर्ड, first release date और version-specific release note।

02 · Rights & license

किस उपयोग की अनुमति है, commercial use, redistribution, modification, seat/user limits और termination/renewal terms।

03 · Integrity & evidence

जहाँ उपलब्ध हो, release artifact का cryptographic hash, signed release record, source commit और dated test report। Hash पहचानने में मदद करता है; अपने-आप ownership या originality सिद्ध नहीं करता।

04 · Capability & limits

वास्तव में काम करने वाली सुविधाएँ, supported formats, known limitations, accessibility/privacy notes और external integrations की स्थिति।

05 · Upgrade & support

Version changelog, bug-report route, support window, backward compatibility और update/rollback procedure।

06 · Buyer transparency

Demo status, price and offer validity, refund/support terms, delivery format और buyer acceptance checklist।

Anti-copy policy: access control, clear license terms, signed releases, audit trails and selective disclosure can reduce unauthorized copying and improve attribution. They cannot make copying physically impossible. Do not publish secrets, private keys, customer data or confidential source code inside a public passport.
