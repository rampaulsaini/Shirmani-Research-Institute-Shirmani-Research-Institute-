Current truth

Workflow runs, generated cards and catalog entries demonstrate automation activity. They do not prove that a customer received a product, that money was paid, or that a research claim was independently verified.

Paid store offers: the connected my-omniverse-store/production-catalog.html publishes UPI, PayPal and WhatsApp order/delivery routes for the listed offers. This proves that a payment/order route is published; it does not prove that a customer has paid or received the product.

Research Paper PDF: research-paper.pdf is present in the repository and has a dedicated public product page.

Completion rule

SOURCE → PRODUCT → REAL ASSET → PRODUCT PAGE → ORDER/INQUIRY → DELIVERY → DELIVERY EVIDENCE → PAYMENT/SETTLEMENT → AUDIT

Only evidence-backed stages may be marked complete. No workflow count is allowed to substitute for a real customer transaction or independent verification.
