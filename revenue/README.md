## Scope, payment and delivery rules

1. Confirm deliverables, word count, source expectations, price, deadline, revision count, and payment terms in writing before work starts.
2. Do not promise legal, medical, financial, academic, or scientific certainty beyond the sources and expertise available.
3. Never fabricate citations, credentials, customer reviews, institutional endorsements, or results.
4. For research work, distinguish source-supported findings, interpretation, and what is not established.
5. Share a short sample outline or scoping note before accepting a larger project. Do not provide the entire paid deliverable as a free sample.
6. Track each enquiry through these stages: new → scope confirmed → quote sent → accepted → in progress → delivered → feedback requested.
7. Ask permission before publishing a client name, logo, work sample, testimonial, or review.
8. Refunds, deposits, and milestone payments must be agreed in writing before payment is requested.

## Customer outreach workflow (manual approval before sending)

1. Identify a relevant organisation and a public, official contact page or role-based business email.
2. Record the organisation, public source URL, reason the offer is relevant, date checked, and opt-out status.
3. Draft a short, personalised message offering one relevant service and linking only to a working public page.
4. Review the message and recipient before sending. Do not scrape private addresses, impersonate anyone, or send bulk unsolicited messages.
5. Record an outreach attempt only after a real email or message is sent through an authorised channel. A prepared draft is not a sent message.
6. Respect opt-outs and applicable privacy, anti-spam, and platform rules.
