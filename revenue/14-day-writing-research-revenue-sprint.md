## First sellable offer
**Hindi/English Writing & Research Support**
- Source-linked research summaries.
- Website and blog articles.
- Hindi-English editing, translation, and plain-language explanations.
- Research-paper support limited to legitimate editing, literature mapping, structure, and citations. Never fabricate sources, results, or authorship.

### Pilot price hypotheses (INR; confirm scope before quoting)
These are test prices, not verified market rates or guaranteed sales:
- Starter — ₹1,499: editing or short source-based summary, up to 800 words.
- Standard — ₹3,999: up to 1,500 words, agreed source list, one revision.
- Research brief — ₹7,500: scoped literature scan/synthesis, up to 2,500 words, source list, one revision.
- Institutional/custom — quote after scoping.

State whether taxes, paid databases, urgent work, and specialist review are excluded. Do not promise acceptance, legal/medical conclusions, or business outcomes.

## Mandatory enquiry-to-payment workflow
Capture client goal, audience, topic, language, word count, output format, deadline, source links, budget, revision expectations, and confidentiality needs. Before starting, confirm deliverables, timeline, price, payment schedule, revision policy, and cancellation/refund terms in writing. Start only after the client confirms. Do not collect unnecessary personal data.

## 14-day action plan
**Days 1–2 — Funnel QA:** test service pages on mobile and desktop; verify enquiry submission, contact links, form validation, confirmation, privacy notice, and response routing. Fix broken links and placeholder details.

**Days 3–4 — Evidence portfolio:** prepare three clearly labelled samples: a sourced research summary, a website article, and a Hindi/English rewrite. Do not present samples as paid client work or invent testimonials.

**Days 5–10 — Relevant outreach:** identify 30 organizations/prospects whose public-facing needs genuinely match the offer. Record organization, public contact source URL, relevance, date checked, and opt-out status. Send up to five individually personalized messages per working day where appropriate and lawful. No scraping private addresses or bulk unsolicited messaging.

**Days 11–14 — Convert and measure:** respond to enquiries within one working day where possible; issue written quotes; record accepted scopes and actual payments; deliver agreed work; request feedback with permission. If no qualified enquiry arrives after 20 messages, improve targeting and samples before increasing volume.

## Measurement dashboard
Record actual counts only:
- Qualified prospects researched; messages sent; delivery failures
- Replies; positive replies; enquiries; quotes
- Paid projects started; projects delivered on time
- Revenue actually received (not quoted); direct costs; refunds
- Repeat customers/referrals; complaints; opt-outs

**14-day planning targets, not forecasts:** 30 qualified prospects researched, 20 personalized messages sent, 3 genuine enquiries, 1 paid pilot. These are goals, not promises.

## Security, evidence, and trust gates
- Use MFA, least-privilege access, backups, dependency updates, input validation, spam protection, and a documented incident path.
- Do not store payment-card details directly; use a reputable processor when configured.
- Distinguish VERIFIED, SOURCE-BASED, PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY, and INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE in research and presentations.
- Represent personal philosophy respectfully as the founder's viewpoint; do not label it scientifically verified without independent evidence.
- No invented institutional contacts, false urgency, fabricated testimonials, or unsupported guarantees.
- Account owner approval is required before any campaign or paid spend.

## Definition of done
The sprint is complete only after the enquiry path is tested, a real lead is logged, scope and price are confirmed before work, and any revenue is supported by an actual payment record. A repository change alone does not establish a sale or income.
