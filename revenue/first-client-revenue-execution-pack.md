# SHIRMANI — First-Client Revenue Execution Pack

**Status:** Working playbook; not a record of sales, customers, or income.  
**Purpose:** Turn currently supportable services into a small, measurable first-client pipeline without pretending that outreach, payment, or delivery has happened.

## 1. Sell only what can be delivered now

The currently supportable offer is Hindi/English writing, editing, source-based research summaries, and website/blog content. Do not sell provider-backed avatar/live Q&A, custom AI systems, global-scale social media, or other unfinished capabilities as if they were production-ready.

### Pilot offers (proposed test prices; confirm before quoting)

| Offer | Pilot scope | Proposed starting price | Target turnaround after written scope, source receipt and agreed payment terms |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Writing polish | Up to 800 words; clarity, grammar, structure; one revision | ₹499 | 2 business days |
| Research brief | Up to 5 client-approved/public sources; up to ~1,200 words; source list, findings, limits and open questions; one revision | ₹1,499 | 3 business days |
| Website/blog draft | One draft up to 1,000 words; outline, headline and one revision | ₹999 | 3 business days |

These are proposed pilot prices from the existing outreach workbench, not proven market rates. The existing showroom/writing-research-service.html also displays an indicative ₹999 general pilot. Before quoting a customer, state the exact offer, deliverable, word count, source limit, deadline, revision count, exclusions, final price, payment method, and cancellation/refund terms in writing. If scope changes, issue a revised quote before doing extra work.

## 2. Best initial customer profiles

Start with a small, manually researched set rather than mass-mailing 195 countries.

1. **Local/regional education and training providers:** need course pages, explainers, bilingual notices, and learning materials.
2. **Small publishers, blogs and specialist creators:** need editing, outlines, research summaries and consistent website copy.
3. **Research groups and non-profits:** may need source-organized background briefs, project summaries and accessible public-facing writing.
4. **Small businesses with outdated or unclear web pages:** may need a clear service page, FAQ, or one blog article.

For every prospect, record public evidence of a relevant need. Use an official contact page or a published business email only; never guess addresses, scrape private contacts, send bulk unsolicited messages, or keep contacting someone who opts out.

## 5. Five-day execution sprint

This is a target for activity, not a promise of sales.

- **Day 1 — Ready-to-sell:** choose one offer; check that its price and scope match the live service page; prepare one relevant sample and an accurate delivery checklist.
- **Day 2 — Prospect research:** identify 10 organisations with a visible, relevant need. Record official source URL, specific observation, fit, and public contact channel. Do not guess email addresses.
- **Day 3 — Personalised outreach:** send up to 5 well-researched messages through appropriate public business channels. Save the sent date and exact message.
- **Day 4 — Second batch:** send up to 5 additional personalised messages only if the first batch passed the relevance/privacy review. Respond to replies and draft scoped proposals.
- **Day 5 — Follow-up and review:** send at most one polite follow-up to non-responders when appropriate; honour opt-outs; record replies, quotes, accepted scopes, payments, blockers and next actions.

If no one responds, do not inflate the metrics. Improve the target segment, sample, offer clarity or contact channel based on evidence.

## 6. Lightweight pipeline tracker

Maintain one record per prospect with these fields:

- Organisation
- Sector and public source URL
- Verifiable need / personalisation note
- Public contact channel
- Offer proposed
- Status: Research needed → Qualified → Draft ready → Contacted → Replied → Scope/quote sent → Accepted → Payment confirmed → Delivered → Feedback requested → Closed / opted out
- Last-contact date and next action/date
- Quote value (not revenue)
- Accepted value (not cash received)
- Payment status and evidence reference
- Delivery status and artifact link
- Opt-out / do-not-contact flag

Do not store unnecessary personal data or credentials. Keep customer files private and share only with permission.

## 7. Quality gate before delivery

- [ ] The brief and intended audience are clear.
- [ ] Every important factual claim is either sourced, labelled as interpretation, or explicitly marked uncertain.
- [ ] Source titles, links and dates are checked; no invented citations.
- [ ] Hindi/English spelling, readability, tone and requested format are checked.
- [ ] Scope, word count, exclusions and revision count match the agreement.
- [ ] Personal/sensitive information is handled minimally and securely.
- [ ] Final deliverable and delivery date are recorded.
- [ ] Customer feedback is invited without pressure.

## 8. Measurement: report facts, not optimism

Track weekly: prospects researched; qualified prospects; personalised messages sent; delivery/receipt evidence; replies; requests for proposal; quotes sent; scopes accepted; payments confirmed; deliverables on time; revision rate; customer feedback; opt-outs; hours spent; and actual net income after costs.

- **Reply rate** = relevant replies ÷ messages sent.
- **Quote acceptance rate** = accepted quotes ÷ quotes sent.
- **Realised revenue** = confirmed customer payments received, not proposed prices or quote totals.
- **On-time delivery** = deliverables delivered by agreed deadline ÷ deliverables due.

Use small samples cautiously; do not claim a reliable conversion rate from a handful of contacts.

## 9. Guardrails for the wider platform

- Keep prototype features clearly separated from verified production features.
- Do not claim guaranteed outcomes, scientific proof, uncopyable technology, or provider integration without evidence.
- No automated mass outreach, invented contact details, or payment collection without a verified payment workflow.
- No workflow deletion just to reduce run counts. First map dependencies; preserve security, deployment, persistence, backups, audit evidence and recovery. Unknown or unclear workflows remain KEEP / REVIEW.
- Prefer a reversible, narrow improvement with a measurable benefit over adding another generic automation.
