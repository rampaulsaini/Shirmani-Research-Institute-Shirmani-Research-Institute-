## Prospect qualification gate
A prospect is **qualified** only when all fields below are recorded:
1. Organisation and official website.
2. Official public business contact route, verified on the organisation's own website.
3. Specific, current, publicly observable content need (link + date checked).
4. A service that directly addresses that need.
5. A feasible sample/deliverable and estimated effort.
6. No evidence that the contact route prohibits this enquiry.

Do not infer that an organisation wants the service just because it works in education, research, language or the environment. A candidate organisation is not a qualified lead until a specific need is verified.

## Seven-day execution plan
- **Day 1 — quality gate:** open and test service menu, enquiry form, proposal builder and two portfolio samples; record URL, device, date, result and defects. Resolve any broken route before outreach.
- **Day 2 — research:** identify 10 candidate organisations from official public pages. For each, record the exact page and a specific content gap or relevant public project. Do not scrape private data.
- **Day 3 — qualify:** score candidates using the gate above; discard any without a concrete need. Prepare no more than five personalised messages.
- **Day 4 — owner review:** approve message, scope and price. Manually send only to qualified prospects using their published business channel; record evidence of actual send. The draft/mailto button itself does not count.
- **Day 5 — second batch:** research five additional candidates and send only if relevant, permitted and individually reviewed.
- **Day 6 — reply handling:** respond to genuine replies; create written scope, price, deadline, revision and payment terms before work begins.
- **Day 7 — reconcile:** record sent messages, replies, quotes, accepted work, payments, delivery and lessons. Stop or revise an offer if evidence shows poor fit.

Targets are activity goals, not promises: 10 qualified prospects researched; up to 10 personalised messages sent only if each passes the gate; all replies logged; every quote scoped in writing; payment recorded only when received. No response or sale is guaranteed.

| Deliveries completed | Agreed deliverable sent; record date and acceptance status |

Rates: reply rate = replies ÷ messages sent; quote conversion = accepted orders ÷ quotes sent; realised revenue = payments received, not quoted or promised amounts. If denominator is zero, report N/A, not 0%.

## Hard controls
- Never count a draft as sent, a lead as a sale, a quote as income, or a payment screenshot as settled funds without checking the transaction.
- Keep client-provided data minimal and access-controlled; do not put secrets or personal data into public Git commits.
- Research summaries must distinguish sourced facts, interpretation and evidence gaps; do not fabricate citations or promise results.
- Use only authorised voice/likeness assets for any future presentation service; disclose synthetic media where appropriate.
- Do not send mass unsolicited messages, bypass contact restrictions or repeatedly contact non-responders.
- The browser-local tracker is not a shared database, payment processor or automated outreach system.
