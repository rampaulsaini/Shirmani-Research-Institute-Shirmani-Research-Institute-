## Evidence states
- VERIFIED: sufficient independent evidence checked.
- SOURCE-BASED: credible source available, but independent verification incomplete.
- PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY: personal worldview or self-description; label as such.
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE: say “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”
Never turn philosophical identity statements into scientific facts. Show sources and uncertainty when relevant.

## Security and consent gates
- Store provider credentials only in server-side secret storage; never commit API keys, tokens, passwords or private voice assets.
- Keep consent, authorization status, access logs and retention rules explicit.
- Use least-privilege access, secret rotation, dependency/security scans, input validation, rate limits, backups and incident response.
- Do not send institutional sales messages automatically without approved recipients, content and authorization.
- Do not publish a person's photo/voice without permission.
