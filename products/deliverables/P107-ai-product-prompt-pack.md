# P107 — AI Product Creation Prompt Pack

1. **Micro-tool:** Design one tiny tool solving one problem in under 60 seconds; include inputs, outputs, edge cases and tests.
2. **Education:** Turn a topic into beginner → intermediate → advanced lessons with exercises.
3. **SEO:** Create a useful search-friendly outline without keyword stuffing; separate facts from opinions.
4. **Accessibility:** Review keyboard access, labels, contrast, plain language and screen-reader structure.
5. **Game:** Design an original game loop with rules, scoring, accessibility and a playable prototype specification.
6. **Research:** Convert a claim into an operational definition, evidence requirements, counter-evidence and reproducible test.
7. **Marketing:** Produce truthful copy with price, limitations, delivery method and one clear CTA.
8. **Localization:** Adapt language and culture while preserving meaning, measurements and safety notices.

**Integrity rule:** AI-generated output is a draft until tested and reviewed.
