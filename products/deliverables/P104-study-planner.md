# P104 — 30-Day Study Planner

## Daily loop
1. Recall yesterday without notes.
2. Learn one concept.
3. Solve or create something.
4. Explain it in simple words.
5. Record what remains unclear.

## Weekly review
- What can I explain without notes?
- What can I demonstrate practically?
- Which mistakes repeat?
- Which authoritative source resolves open questions?
- What should I revise next?
