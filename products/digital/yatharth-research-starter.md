# निष्पक्ष समझ • शमीकरण • यथार्थ सिद्धांत — Research Starter

## Scope

This starter organizes the author-proposed framework into a researchable structure. It does not present philosophical propositions as independently scientifically proven facts.

## Six research pillars

1. यथार्थ न्याय प्रणाली
2. सर्वोत्तम उच्च शिक्षा
3. निष्पक्ष समझ की शिक्षा
4. रोटी-कपड़ा-मकान तथा प्रकृति/जीव/वनस्पति संरक्षण
5. घर से वैश्विक स्तर तक एकीकृत व्यवस्था
6. प्रत्यक्ष समक्ष research, testing and verification

## Research conversion method

For every proposition:

**Author statement → precise claim → measurable definition → comparison/baseline → evidence → counter-evidence → test → result → independent review**

## Observation sheet

**Proposition:**

**What would count as evidence?**

**What would count against it?**

**What can be measured?**
