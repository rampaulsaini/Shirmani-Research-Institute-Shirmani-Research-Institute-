# Research Evidence & Verification Toolkit

## Purpose
A practical, evidence-bound workflow for moving from a claim to a reproducible research record without confusing automation, QC, publication, or opinion with independent verification.

## Core record

- Claim / question
- Source
- Evidence excerpt or data
- Counter-evidence
- Method / test
- Result
- Limitations
- Reproduction path
- Verification status
- Audit timestamp

## Verification ladder

1. SOURCE_FOUND
2. SOURCE_EXTRACTED
3. CLAIM_FORMULATED
4. EVIDENCE_ATTACHED
5. COUNTEREVIDENCE_CHECKED
6. TEST_REPRODUCED
7. INDEPENDENT_REVIEW
8. VERIFIED — only when the stated verification criteria are actually met

## Integrity rule
A workflow run is evidence of execution, not evidence that the research claim is true. Deterministic QC checks artifact structure; independent verification evaluates the substantive result.
