How to use

  1. Select a service type.
  2. Describe the customer's objective and topic.
  3. Enter language, word count/scope, source links and preferred deliverable.
  4. Provide deadline and budget if known; otherwise leave them for discussion.
  5. Generate the brief and review every field.
  6. Agree in writing on scope, price, deadline, revisions and payment before work begins.

QC / release gate

  • Expected: form fields produce a readable brief.
  • Expected: copy works when clipboard permission is granted; text export downloads a .txt file.
  • Expected: email action opens a user-reviewed draft and does not send automatically.
  • Known limitation: no backend persistence, shared customer database, payment gateway, automated AI writing, or email sender.
  • Release state: PR pending review/merge; public deployment not yet confirmed.

Commercial terms

This tool is a free enquiry aid; it is not itself a paid order. Any writing/research service price must be quoted after scope and source review. No sales, payment, customer review, or guaranteed outcome is claimed by this passport.
