Usage guide

  1. Write one specific claim per record.
  2. Prefer primary documents where suitable.
  3. Record the exact passage and date.
  4. Cross-check important claims with independent sources.
  5. Label uncertainty and contradictions; a link alone is not verification.
  6. Export the register with the research deliverable.
