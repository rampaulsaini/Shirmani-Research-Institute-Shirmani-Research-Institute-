## 5. Practical website and product-content recommendations

1. **Make each listing specific:** show exact material, size, colour, technique, care and availability only when confirmed.
2. **Use original product photography:** include front, back, close-up detail, scale reference and packaging where available.
3. **Separate craft story from item specifications:** the story explains cultural context; the specification describes the actual item being purchased.
4. **Make buying information visible:** price, stock status, dispatch estimate, shipping, returns and contact route should be easy to find and consistent.
5. **Use evidence-led artisan stories:** obtain written approval for names, photographs, quotations and personal stories; avoid implying consent.
6. **Keep claims proportionate:** avoid terms such as “pure”, “certified”, “100% handmade”, “authentic” or “fair-trade” unless Shehjar can substantiate the exact claim.
