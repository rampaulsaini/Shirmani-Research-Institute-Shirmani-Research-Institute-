They deliberately avoid unverified claims about fibre purity, hand-spinning, embroidery technique, dimensions, origin certification, price, stock, artisan identity or care instructions. Add the artisan or maker story only when Shehjar has approved the wording and attribution. The aim is a useful, accurate description that respects the craft without making unsupported promises.

**Details to confirm:** exact technique and spelling used by Shehjar; base material; design details; dimensions; maker attribution and consent; care; availability.

### C. Kani Shawl — draft listing copy

**Kani Shawl — Shehjar collection**

Present the selected Kani Shawl with the details customers need before ordering: confirmed material, dimensions, colour, design, finish, care instructions and dispatch information. A concise description, supported by original product photographs, can make the listing easier to understand while keeping every product claim traceable to information supplied or approved by Shehjar.

**Details to confirm:** exact weave/technique claim; fibre composition; dimensions; colour; finish; care; stock and dispatch estimate.

## 5. Practical website and product-content recommendations

1. **Make each listing specific:** show exact material, size, colour, technique, care and availability only when confirmed.
2. **Use original product photography:** include front, back, close-up detail, scale reference and packaging where available.
3. **Separate craft story from item specifications:** the story explains cultural context; the specification describes the actual item being purchased.
4. **Make buying information visible:** price, stock status, dispatch estimate, shipping, returns and contact route should be easy to find and consistent.
5. **Use evidence-led artisan stories:** obtain written approval for names, photographs, quotations and personal stories; avoid implying consent.
6. **Keep claims proportionate:** avoid terms such as “pure”, “certified”, “100% handmade”, “authentic” or “fair-trade” unless Shehjar can substantiate the exact claim.
