First product batch · demo-led MVPs

These are product specifications to implement—not claims that finished products or integrations already exist.

SR-001 · MVP specification

SourceLens Research Brief Builder

Turns a topic and supplied links into a structured brief with source notes and an “evidence insufficient” state.

Acceptance: 3 sample topics; factual claims linked to supplied sources; missing sources identified.

Assets: screenshot, 60–90 second walkthrough, usage guide, version notes.

SR-002 · MVP specification

HeartView Clarity Editor

Improves supplied Hindi/English text for clarity and respectful tone while preserving meaning.

Acceptance: before/after examples, meaning-preservation checklist, language review.

Assets: screenshot, walkthrough, examples, limitations.

SR-003 · MVP specification

Voice-to-Presentation Planner

Creates storyboard and script from text or authorized voice recording. Avatar and lip-sync require separately configured providers and consent.

Acceptance: storyboard, captions, audio timing plan, disclosure of simulated output.

Assets: storyboard screenshot, sample video, setup guide, authorization checklist.

Product passport & continuous improvement

Discover: customer problem, intended use, source and comparable options.
Produce: named version, working module, reproducible sample, usage guide.
QC: test outcomes, defects, accessibility and privacy review.
Publish: unique product image, screenshot, demo video, short description, QR to long description, version, price and support terms.
Improve: voluntary reviews → issue triage → changelog → regression tests.

Dispatch rule: no QC badge unless recorded tests pass. QR codes should point to a public product passport, not expose secrets or customer data.
