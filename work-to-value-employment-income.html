Work-to-Value Employment & Income Pipeline

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO LIVE EMPLOYMENT OR PAYMENT EXECUTION

Verified work को अवसर, fulfilment, settlement और audit records से जोड़ने वाली transparent pipeline.

End-to-End Flow

Skill / Work Registration → Evidence → Verification → Opportunity Eligibility → Human Match → Work Agreement → Fulfilment → Settlement → Reconciliation → Audit → Portfolio Record

यह architecture किसी व्यक्ति को स्वतः job, customer, contract, payment या income नहीं देता। हर real-world transition के लिए evidence और उचित human decision आवश्यक है।

Employment Connection States

PROFILED → SKILL_VERIFIED → OPPORTUNITY_ELIGIBLE → MATCH_PROPOSED → HUMAN_ACCEPTED → AGREEMENT_RECORDED → WORK_IN_PROGRESS → FULFILLED → SETTLEMENT_PENDING → SETTLED → RECONCILED

Governance states: DISPUTED · CANCELLED · REVERSED · ARCHIVED · NOT_VERIFIED

Pipeline Record

  • pipeline_id — end-to-end reference
  • work_reference — verified work record
  • skill_reference — reviewed skill evidence
  • opportunity_reference — real opportunity reference, when externally evidenced
  • match_reference — proposed/accepted match
  • agreement_reference — human-approved work terms
  • fulfilment_reference — completed work evidence
  • settlement_reference — settlement record
  • reconciliation_reference — reconciliation result
  • audit_reference — audit trail

Income Boundary

Income is recorded only when supported by actual external evidence.

VERIFIED WORK ≠ EMPLOYMENT · MATCH ≠ CONTRACT · CONTRACT ≠ COMPLETED WORK · FULFILLED ≠ PAID · SETTLED ≠ INCOME CLAIM WITHOUT EVIDENCE

No fictional salary, customer, job, order, payment, revenue or income is generated by this layer.

Human Review Gates

  • Identity and profile changes
  • Skill verification and opportunity eligibility
  • Employer/customer or counterparty confirmation
  • Contracts and work terms
  • Fulfilment acceptance
  • Settlement and payment decisions
  • Disputes, reversals and corrections
  • Production integrations

Creator & Employment Continuity

Creator identity remains user-authored. AI may assist with profile formatting, portfolio summaries, opportunity discovery, proposal drafts and routing, but may not substitute the creator identity or invent employment/income evidence.
