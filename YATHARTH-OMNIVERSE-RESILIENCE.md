# Yatharth Omniverse Resilience & Live Production Plane

## Reliability model
Observe -> Isolate -> Retry -> Recover -> Fallback -> Verify -> Learn.

No free Colab runtime, GPU, tunnel or single cloud service can guarantee permanent availability. 24x7x365 therefore requires redundant providers, durable queues, health checks, bounded retries and explicit fallback states.

## Self-upgrading boundary
Agents may propose and test bounded changes automatically. Production promotion remains gated by tests, policy checks and the project's verification contract.

## Public media
The public control surface covers live podcast, streaming, music, news, games/cartoons, product promotion and an endless episodic continuity queue. Provider-specific broadcasting, ad-network and GPU credentials remain deployment dependencies.

## Income-first continuity
Product showcase, sponsored slots, offers, music licensing and creator/production services are first-class modules. Ads must be labelled and cannot silently alter editorial or verification status.
