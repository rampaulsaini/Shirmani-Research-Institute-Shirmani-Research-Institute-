Opportunity Marketplace

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO LIVE JOB EXECUTION

वास्तविक और evidence-backed काम के अवसरों को structured, reviewable और Work-to-Value pipeline से जोड़ने की परत।

Opportunity Flow

Opportunity Discovery → Source Evidence → Registration → Review → Eligibility → Human Matching → Work Agreement → Work-to-Value Pipeline

यह marketplace अपने आप नौकरी, ग्राहक, employer, contract या payment नहीं बनाता। किसी अवसर को वास्तविक मानने के लिए उपयुक्त source/evidence और human review आवश्यक है।

Opportunity Record

  • opportunity_id — unique reference
  • source_reference — public/external source reference
  • provider_reference — employer/customer/provider reference
  • opportunity_type — job, freelance, service, project, collaboration
  • skill_requirements — stated requirements
  • terms_reference — documented terms when available
  • evidence_reference — supporting evidence
  • review_reference — human review
  • status — lifecycle state

States

DISCOVERED → SOURCE_CHECK_PENDING → REGISTERED → EVIDENCE_REVIEW → ELIGIBLE → MATCH_PROPOSED → HUMAN_CONFIRMED → AGREEMENT_PENDING → ACTIVE → FULFILMENT_PENDING → CLOSED

Governance: NOT_VERIFIED · DISPUTED · CANCELLED · EXPIRED · ARCHIVED

Matching Boundary

AI may assist with search, categorization, skill matching and draft proposals. Final matching, identity confirmation, employer/customer confirmation, contracts and acceptance remain human decisions.

ELIGIBLE ≠ AVAILABLE FOREVER · MATCH_PROPOSED ≠ EMPLOYMENT · HUMAN_CONFIRMED ≠ CONTRACT · CONTRACT ≠ PAYMENT

Integrity & Safety

  • कोई invented job, employer, customer, salary, fee, contract या income नहीं।
  • Expired, disputed और cancelled opportunities अलग states में रहती हैं।
  • Source बदलने पर नया evidence/review record आवश्यक है; history silently overwrite नहीं होगी।
  • Payment/income केवल downstream actual evidence के साथ record होगा।
  • Privacy: केवल आवश्यक references और opportunity data।
