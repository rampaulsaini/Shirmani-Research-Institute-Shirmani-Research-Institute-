## 1. First sellable service

Offer one clearly scoped service before marketing the wider platform:

**Hindi/English Research & Content Sprint**  
For researchers, educators, small businesses, creators, nonprofits, and institutional teams that need a readable, source-aware written deliverable.

Possible deliverables (choose and confirm in writing):
- Research summary based on client-provided or agreed public sources.
- Hindi ↔ English editing or translation of supplied material.
- Website or blog article with source links where relevant.
- Plain-language explainer with a short glossary and key takeaways.

Not included unless separately agreed: legal/medical/financial advice, guaranteed scientific conclusions, plagiarism, fabricated citations, undisclosed AI-generated claims, or publication/submission on the client's behalf.

## 9. Truth-first claims and evidence states

Use the repository's four evidence states consistently:
- **VERIFIED:** supported by sufficient recorded evidence.
- **SOURCE-BASED:** grounded in a named source, not independently confirmed.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL / IDENTITY:** personal viewpoint or conceptual framing.
- **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE:** not enough evidence; state “अभी पर्याप्त प्रमाण उपलब्ध नहीं है।”

The larger platform roadmap (AI/ML/NLP, avatar, live Q&A, social platform, studio, library, global institutional sales) remains a roadmap unless a specific working asset, permissions, deployment, and tests prove otherwise. Do not bundle unverified capabilities into the current paid service.
