Version: 1.0  
Status: Working sales asset; no outreach sent and no revenue claimed.

## Objective
Convert the Institute's currently stated capabilities into a small, clearly scoped paid pilot: Hindi/English writing, editing, source-linked research summaries, or website/blog content.

## Offer menu (indicative pilot prices)
| Offer | Starting reference | Included | Explicit limit |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Writing / editing | ₹499 | Up to 500 words; one revision | Client supplies brief and source material |
| Research summary | ₹999 | Summary of up to 3 agreed sources; links and limitations | Not a systematic review or independent scientific audit |
| Website / blog draft | ₹1,499 | One page/post up to 1,000 words; one revision | No guarantee of traffic, ranking, leads, or sales |

These are repository-defined pilot reference amounts, not validated market rates or binding quotes. Confirm the final scope, deadline, price, revision count, and payment terms in writing before starting.

## Best-fit prospects
Prioritise people or small organisations with a visible, specific need:
- Local businesses with unclear service pages or outdated website copy.
- Educators, trainers, and small research teams needing a concise source-linked summary.
- Hindi/English creators needing editing, captions, scripts, or blog drafts.
- NGOs and community organisations needing clear bilingual explainers.

Do not send bulk messages to unrelated organisations. Use contact details published on the prospect's official website, comply with applicable rules, and manually review every message.

## Personalisation checklist — before sending
- [ ] Organisation and recipient are real and relevant.
- [ ] The first sentence cites a specific detail from an official source.
- [ ] The offer matches a need; no unsupported capability claims.
- [ ] Sample/demo link opens and demonstrates the stated deliverable.
- [ ] Price is labelled indicative until scope is agreed.
- [ ] Contact route and source URL are recorded.
- [ ] Message is reviewed by a human and sent manually.
- [ ] Follow-up respects opt-out and applicable anti-spam/privacy rules.

## Seven-day activity target (not a sales forecast)
- Day 1: prepare three honest samples: one Hindi edit, one English edit, one source-linked summary.
- Days 2–6: identify and manually contact five well-matched prospects per day (target: 25 personalised messages, not 25 guaranteed sends).
- Day 7: review replies, objections, requested quotes, accepted work, delivered work, and verified payments.

## Minimal tracking fields
`date_identified, organisation, sector, official_source_url, contact_route, need_evidence, offer, draft_reviewed, actually_sent_at, reply_status, quote_amount_inr, quote_accepted_at, payment_verified_at, delivery_date, feedback, next_action`

Keep these statuses separate:
1. Identified ≠ source verified.
2. Draft prepared ≠ message sent.
3. Message sent ≠ reply received.
4. Quote sent ≠ order accepted.
5. Order accepted ≠ payment received.
6. Payment received ≠ delivery completed.

Record revenue only after payment is actually verified. Never invent prospects, email addresses, sends, responses, orders, or income.

## Definition of a successful first sprint
A sprint is complete when the sample links work, the target list has source URLs, outreach activity is truthfully logged, every response has a next action, and any accepted job has a written scope and payment record. Zero replies is still a measurable result and should trigger offer/list/message review—not fabricated success.
