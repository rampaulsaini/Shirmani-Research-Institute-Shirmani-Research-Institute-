    4. Production activation checklist

    Identity & privacy

    Secure auth, email verification, password recovery, session protection, consent and account deletion.

    Backend required

    Community & trust

    Shared database, per-user authorization, spam prevention, reporting, moderation, block/mute and review abuse controls.

    Backend required

    Vision-aware reminders

    Customer-approved goals, timezone-aware scheduler, opt-in notification channel, quiet hours, unsubscribe and delivery logs.

    Scheduler + provider required

    Subscriptions & income

    Clear free/paid plan terms, payment provider, receipts, cancellation/refunds and confirmed-payment ledger.

    Payment integration required
