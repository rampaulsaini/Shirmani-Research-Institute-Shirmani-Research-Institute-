## 2. Immediate 48-hour mission
1. Check the services, pilot-offer and enquiry pages on mobile; verify their links and contact action.
2. Prepare one short sample from existing public work and label it as a sample, not a client result.
3. Share the service menu once on an owner-controlled channel.
4. Send up to 10 individually relevant messages to appropriate known contacts or organisations with a legitimate public enquiry route. No scraped lists, bulk spam or invented personalisation.
5. Record messages only after they are actually sent. Do not send or schedule outreach automatically without approval of sender identity, recipients, channel and final wording.
6. Reply to genuine enquiries with a written scope and quote. Start only after agreement and payment terms are confirmed.

## 4. Automission stages and evidence
| Stage | Required evidence | Count as complete when |
|---|---|---|
| Prospect identified | Public organisation/contact page or permissioned contact | Relevant prospect and legitimate contact route recorded |
| Contact sent | Date, channel, approved message record | Actually sent; a draft does not count |
| Reply received | Reply record | Human response received |
| Qualified lead | Need, deadline, language and scope captured | Request fits current capacity |
| Quote issued | Written scope, price, timeline and terms | Quote delivered |
| Order accepted | Written acceptance | Terms agreed |
| Payment received | Transaction record/receipt | Funds actually received |
| Work delivered | Delivery link and timestamp | Agreed files sent |
| Feedback | Client response; permission for public use | Feedback recorded, never invented |

Keep customer and payment data private; store only the minimum necessary.

## 5. Seven-day targets — targets, not forecasts
- Public service menu checked: 1
- Personalised outreach messages sent: 10
- Genuine conversations or quote requests: target 3
- Paid pilot orders: target 1
- Delivery acknowledged: target 1 if an order is accepted in time
- Revenue: report only funds evidenced as received; report ₹0 if none is received.

Track daily: prospects, messages sent, replies, qualified leads, quotes, accepted orders, payments, refunds/fees where known, deliveries, turnaround, revisions and feedback. Qualified-lead rate = qualified leads ÷ replies. Paid conversion = paid orders ÷ quotes. If a denominator is zero, report N/A.

## 6. Product claims, evidence and safety
- A page, prototype, catalogue entry or workflow draft is not proof of a completed product, sale, payment or production deployment.
- Keep the planned AI/ML/NLP, avatar/voice, social platform, email and global expansion roadmap labelled planned/prototype until each capability has reproducible tests and evidence.
- For research, record source URL, access date, claim supported, limitations and whether it was independently checked.
- Do not present philosophical/identity statements as independently verified scientific findings.
- Do not promise a product is impossible to copy. Use version history, controlled access, documentation and appropriate IP protections.
- Outreach must use legitimate contact routes and comply with applicable privacy and anti-spam requirements. Human review is required before sending.

## 7. Review checkpoints
Review the funnel after 48 hours and again after 7 days. Update actual counts and evidence links. A target is not complete merely because this runbook exists.
