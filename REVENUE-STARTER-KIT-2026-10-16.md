## 5. 14-day validation sprint
These are planning targets, not promised outcomes.
- Day 1: Publish accurate offers, one sample, intake form, and contact route.
- Days 2–5: Identify 40 relevant organisations from public official contact pages; log fit, source URL, contact route, date, and status.
- Days 3–10: Send up to 10 individually relevant messages per working day, subject to applicable rules and contact permissions. No scraping, deceptive identity, purchased lists, or bulk spam.
- Days 5–12: Follow up once only where appropriate and permitted.
- Days 7–14: Qualify enquiries, send tailored proposals, and agree terms before starting.
- Day 14: Review actual results and adjust the offer based on evidence.

Track organisations researched, messages sent, delivery failures, replies, qualified enquiries, proposals, accepted proposals, paid deposits, completed jobs, feedback, and money actually received.

## 6. Metrics
- Reply rate = replies / successfully delivered messages × 100%
- Qualified enquiry rate = qualified enquiries / replies × 100%
- Proposal acceptance rate = accepted proposals / proposals sent × 100%
- Paid conversion rate = paying customers / proposals sent × 100%
- Collected revenue = payments actually received, not proposals or invoices
- Net contribution = collected revenue − direct delivery costs − transaction fees − refunds

Report zero if no result has occurred; never present targets or estimates as actual sales.

## 7. Evidence, consent, and security rules
- Label personal/philosophical viewpoints as viewpoints, not independently verified scientific findings.
- Link factual sources and state uncertainty; never fabricate citations.
- Do not promise any product is impossible to copy. Protect original work through access controls, licences, version history, and appropriate legal advice.
- Use only authorised institutional contact channels; comply with privacy and anti-spam rules.
- Avoid collecting sensitive information unless necessary.
- Keep API keys and credentials out of source code and public logs.
- Do not claim voice, avatar, live Q&A, payment, or security features are production-ready until end-to-end tests pass.
- Require consent for voice/face assets and retain test/audit records.

## 8. Product roadmap after service validation
1. Public showroom MVP: catalogue, accurate descriptions, samples, pricing ranges, enquiry form, and terms.
2. Research workflow: source capture, citation checks, review states, and revision history.
3. Demo/test-drive loop: safe demo data, acceptance tests, feedback, and versioned updates.
4. Voice/avatar subsystem: authorised assets, consent record, Q&A tests, lip-sync/latency tests, and audit log.
5. Automation: lead tracking, proposal reminders, delivery checklist, invoice/payment status; human approval before external messages or charges.
6. Scale only after delivery capacity, support, privacy, security, and pricing are validated.
