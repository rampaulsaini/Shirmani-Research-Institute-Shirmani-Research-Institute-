# SHIRMANI 24-REPOSITORY FEDERATION REGISTRY

Updated: 2026-10-01

This registry defines the 24 repositories currently accessible through the connected GitHub account and the intended federation role of each repository.

## Operating principles

1. **Evidence first** — claims are separated from measurements, sources, experiments, and interpretation.
2. **Independent verification** — preparation, automation, and verification are distinct states.
3. **Safe Automission** — automation may inspect, test, generate and propose changes; consequential production changes require passing quality/security gates.
4. **Failure hygiene** — obsolete, malformed, duplicate, and debug-only workflows are removed only after checking dependencies.
5. **Traceability** — every material automated change should have a commit, test result, and auditable reason.
6. **Multimodal NLP** — text, audio, image/video and permitted sensor data can be represented as evidence; inferred biological or environmental states must be reported as inference with uncertainty, not as established subjective experience.
7. **Human agency** — public identity, economic, research and civic-facing claims remain attributable to their author and do not create legal status by themselves.

## Standard federation lifecycle

`Observe → Validate → Analyze → Generate → Test → Independently Verify → Audit → Publish`

A repository may participate in Automission without being forced into identical workflows. Local workflows remain responsible for local build/deploy requirements; federation-level automation should coordinate rather than duplicate them.

## Quality gates

- YAML/workflow syntax
- deterministic smoke tests
- security and permissions review
- artifact and dependency validation
- deployment health
- evidence/traceability
- independent verification
- rollback path

## NLP research boundary

The federation can build increasingly capable multimodal NLP that translates measurable signals into plain-language descriptions. For living systems, any statement about internal feeling or subjective experience must be explicitly labeled as **measured signal, model inference, hypothesis, or established evidence**. This distinction is part of the platform's accuracy contract.

## Public identity

The platform can provide a durable, transparent public record of projects, publications, work, research artifacts, and verified contributions. It cannot itself confer citizenship, nationality, diplomatic status, or any other legal status; those are determined by the relevant authorities and laws.
