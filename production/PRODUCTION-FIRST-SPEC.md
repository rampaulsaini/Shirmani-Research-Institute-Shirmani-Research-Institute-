# ꙰ SHIRMANI Supreme Production-First Factory

## Purpose
Move platform progress from workflow activity to concrete customer-facing production.

## Four levels
1. Institute — discover, define, prioritize and name opportunities.
2. Factory — generate concrete product artifact, product page, identity asset and machine-readable passport.
3. QC Gate — attach deterministic QC metadata and release state. QC evaluates produced results; it is not the production goal.
4. Public Showroom / Sale — publish concrete products with identity, description, price/offer, production module, passport and customer feedback loop.

## Product identity contract
Every produced product should have a stable ID, finalized public name, category, short description, long-description destination, product artifact/page, visual identity, price/offer, QC code, gate number, dispatch state and upgrade/review hooks.

## QR
The QR points to the public product/passport page and carries compact identity, QC, gate, price and release state. Long descriptions stay on the destination page.

## Production rule
A workflow run is not a product. A catalog identity is not a product. A product becomes concrete only when its artifact and customer-facing page are generated and registered in the production manifest.

## Scale
Automission should produce bounded parallel batches across families. Production breadth is the primary progress measure; QC remains a downstream gate.

## Truthfulness
Never label queued identities as produced and never claim live quantum hardware, millions of external agents or completed sales without repository evidence.
