# Production Completion — No More Showroom-Only Work

## Objective
Convert the existing multi-layer Automission infrastructure into externally usable, evidence-backed products and services.

## Current repository evidence
- Public production catalog reports 500 concrete source-bound production results in the latest cycle.
- These results are production artifacts/work cards; they do not prove customer delivery, payment, or independent scientific verification.
- Production Reality identifies the central gap: real asset → product page → order/inquiry → delivery → delivery evidence → settlement → audit.

## Priority production lanes

### P0 — First revenue-ready digital product
1. Select one existing digital-audio offer with a real delivery asset.
2. Verify the delivery asset is complete and accessible.
3. Make one canonical product page with title, price, preview, scope, delivery terms and order path.
4. Connect/verify the authorized payment or order mechanism.
5. Perform one real test order with human approval.
6. Record order, delivery and settlement evidence.
7. Publish the first evidence-backed transaction result.

### P1 — Productize services
Create bounded offers for AI-assisted music production, audio/podcast production, research & evidence support, AI workflow/automation, digital publishing and AI marketing assets. Each needs scope, deliverable, turnaround, price/quote method, inquiry route and delivery method.

### P2 — Education / University
Build a real learning product: one structured course/module, lessons, exercises, assessment, completion record and public delivery path. Do not claim accreditation without lawful accreditation.

### P3 — Public-service / justice research
Build demonstrable prototypes for intake, evidence records, comparison/reasoning reports, human review and audit trail. Do not present prototypes as courts or legal authority.

### P4 — Currency / value systems
Keep Yatharth Mudra and value-exchange systems in design/testnet status until legal, security, custody, payment and deployment evidence exists. No fabricated balance, sale, monetary value or legal-tender claim.

## Hard completion rule
A module is NOT COMPLETE merely because a workflow ran, a page exists, a JSON record exists, a catalog entry exists, or an automation reports PASS.

A production item is COMPLETE only when the applicable evidence chain exists:
SOURCE → REAL ASSET → PUBLIC OFFER → ORDER/INQUIRY → DELIVERY → DELIVERY EVIDENCE → PAYMENT/SETTLEMENT (if paid) → AUDIT.

## Automation policy
Automission should prioritize unfinished production work and measurable delivery evidence over creating additional duplicate architecture/workflows.
