🌟 Project की खूबियाँ

  • Integrated research institute: research, archive, structured knowledge और public presentation एक ecosystem में।
  • Evidence-first architecture: Source → Claims → Evidence → Test → Verification → QC → Publication → Archive.
  • Fail-closed verification: automation VERIFIED state को independent review के बिना fabricate नहीं करती।
  • AI/ML/NLP + Automission: quality, benchmark, health, integrity और repetitive production checks का automation।
  • Public transparency: dashboards, status pages, product passports और documentation।
  • Production ecosystem: digital products, education, media, music, research support, automation और services।
  • Income-first surface: work, freelancing, digital products, creator और business pathways।
  • Continuous improvement: user/customer feedback और quality results को next improvements में वापस लाने की architecture।
  • Modular expansion: नई repositories, agents, products और workflows जोड़ने के लिए scalable structure।

🔬 Scientific integrity

GitHub Actions का successful run, generated page, QC result या record अपने-आप independent scientific verification नहीं है। Project में UNVERIFIED / NOT_VERIFIED boundaries इसलिए महत्वपूर्ण हैं। जहां external reviewer, reproducible evidence या real-world operational proof चाहिए, उसे अलग चरण माना जाएगा।
