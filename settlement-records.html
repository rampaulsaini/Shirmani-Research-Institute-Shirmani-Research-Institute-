Settlement Records

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO LIVE PAYMENT EXECUTION

Fulfilled exchange को एक अलग, auditable settlement record में संरचित करने वाली परत।

Settlement Flow

Fulfilled Exchange → Settlement Candidate → Settlement Review → Human Confirmation → Settlement Record → Reconciliation → Audit

यह layer settlement की संरचना और evidence trail बनाती है; अपने आप धन transfer, payment, income या financial balance उत्पन्न नहीं करती।

Settlement Record

  • settlement_id — अद्वितीय settlement reference
  • order_reference — संबंधित exchange order
  • fulfilment_reference — fulfilled work/service/product का reference
  • payer_reference / payee_reference — वास्तविक पक्षों के references, यदि लागू हों
  • settlement_type — documented settlement mode
  • settlement_amount_or_value — recorded amount/value, केवल वास्तविक evidence के आधार पर
  • currency_or_unit — लागू currency/value unit
  • terms_reference — agreed terms का reference
  • evidence_reference — external/supporting evidence
  • confirmation_reference — human confirmation
  • reconciliation_reference — reconciliation result
  • dispute_reference — dispute/reversal reference, यदि लागू हो
  • audit_reference — audit trail

Settlement States

PENDING → UNDER_REVIEW → HUMAN_CONFIRMED → SETTLEMENT_READY → SETTLED → RECONCILIATION_PENDING → RECONCILED

Governance states: DISPUTED · REVERSED · CANCELLED · ARCHIVED

Integrity Boundary

FULFILLED ≠ SETTLEMENT_READY · SETTLEMENT_READY ≠ SETTLED · SETTLED ≠ RECONCILED · SETTLED ≠ INCOME CLAIM · SETTLEMENT RECORD ≠ PAYMENT PROOF UNLESS ACTUAL EXTERNAL EVIDENCE EXISTS

Safeguards

  • कोई invented payer, payee, amount, payment, income या reconciliation नहीं।
  • Automatic transfer, payment और settlement execution disabled.
  • Fabricated settlement confirmation स्वीकार नहीं; वास्तविक external evidence आवश्यक है।
  • Identity, terms, settlement approval, payments, disputes, reversals और production changes पर human review आवश्यक।
  • Actual payment evidence के बिना SETTLED या RECONCILED को वास्तविक payment fact नहीं माना जाएगा।
  • Privacy boundary: केवल आवश्यक references; अनावश्यक personal या financial data नहीं।
