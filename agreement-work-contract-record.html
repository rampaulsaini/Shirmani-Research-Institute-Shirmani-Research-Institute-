Agreement & Work Contract Record

DESIGN SPECIFICATION · NO AUTOMATIC CONTRACT EXECUTION

Human-approved match को documented work agreement तक ले जाने और उसके terms, acceptance, fulfilment तथा audit references को संरचित रखने की परत।

Agreement Flow

Human-Confirmed Match → Agreement Draft → Terms Review → Human Acceptance → Agreement Recorded → Work-to-Value Pipeline → Fulfilment → Settlement → Reconciliation → Audit

यह layer contract को स्वयं बनाकर लागू नहीं करती। वास्तविक legal contract वही माना जाएगा जो संबंधित पक्षों द्वारा उचित external/legal process से स्वीकार और executed हो।

Agreement Record

  • agreement_id — unique reference
  • application_reference — accepted application/match
  • opportunity_reference — source opportunity
  • parties_reference — necessary party references
  • scope_reference — work/service scope
  • terms_reference — agreed terms
  • compensation_reference — documented compensation terms when externally supported
  • acceptance_reference — human acceptance evidence
  • external_contract_reference — actual external contract/document reference when applicable
  • pipeline_reference — Work-to-Value connection

Lifecycle

DRAFT → TERMS_REVIEW → HUMAN_ACCEPTANCE → AGREEMENT_RECORDED → WORK_READY → ACTIVE → FULFILMENT_PENDING → COMPLETED

Governance: REJECTED · WITHDRAWN · EXPIRED · DISPUTED · AMENDED · CANCELLED · TERMINATED · ARCHIVED

Contract Boundary

AI may help structure terms, summarize documents and prepare drafts. It may not impersonate a party, fabricate acceptance, create a binding contract by itself, promise compensation, or declare employment/income without evidence.

MATCH ≠ AGREEMENT · AGREEMENT_RECORDED ≠ LEGAL CONTRACT · TERMS ≠ PAYMENT · FULFILLED ≠ PAID

Safeguards

  • कोई invented employer/customer, party, scope, compensation, acceptance या contract नहीं।
  • Human acceptance और, जहाँ आवश्यक हो, actual external contract evidence अलग record होंगे।
  • Amendment/termination history preserve होगी; silent overwrite नहीं।
  • Payment और income downstream settlement/evidence से निर्धारित होंगे।
  • Identity substitution disabled; creator-authored identity remains canonical.
  • Legal validity, tax, employment classification और regulatory obligations के लिए applicable jurisdiction में human/legal review आवश्यक है।
