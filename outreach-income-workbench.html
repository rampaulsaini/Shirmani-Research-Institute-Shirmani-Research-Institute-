1. Start with a sellable service

Canonical public offer: Writing & Research Services. These are proposed starting prices, not proven market rates or guaranteed sales. Keep package names, word limits and revision counts aligned with that page; confirm the final scope, timeline and fee in writing before accepting payment.

Starter

Editing / Proofreading

Hindi or English spelling, grammar, clarity, tone and structure for an existing draft.

Proposed starting rate: ₹499

Up to 700 words and one revision within the agreed brief. Timeline is confirmed in writing before work begins. Excludes certified translation and legal/medical advice.

Research

Source-based Research Brief

Concise summary with source links, key findings, limitations and open questions.

Proposed starting rate: ₹1,499

Approximately 800–1,200 words and 3–5 credible sources where available. Timeline and source scope are confirmed in writing. Independent verification is claimed only when actually performed.

Content

Article / Website Copy

Audience-focused blog article, About page, service description or informational web copy.

Proposed starting rate: ₹999

Up to 1,000 words, headings and one revision. Timeline is confirmed in writing. The client approves factual, brand and publication claims.

Payment rule: send a written scope and quote first. For new clients, request an agreed advance before work begins; state delivery date, revision count, cancellation/refund terms and payment method in writing.

5. Seven-day execution loop

  1. Day 1: Publish one accurate service page and two clearly labelled sample pieces (one Hindi, one English).
  2. Day 2: Identify 15 relevant organisations from official sites; record why each is a fit and the official contact route.
  3. Day 3: Personalize 5–8 messages; attach or link only a relevant sample.
  4. Day 4: Prepare one sample research brief with source links, dates, caveats and clear formatting.
  5. Day 5: Follow up once where appropriate; stop immediately if asked not to contact again.
  6. Day 6: Record replies, objections, quotes requested and time spent. Improve the offer based on evidence.
  7. Day 7: Review actual enquiries, paid work and feedback; do not count drafts, workflow runs or sent messages as revenue.

Measurement: track prospects researched → relevant contacts → messages sent → replies → qualified enquiries → quotes → paid projects → on-time delivery → client feedback. Income is recorded only after payment is actually received.

Privacy note: prospect records are stored in this browser's local storage on this device. They are not synchronized, backed up or sent to the Institute automatically. Export a backup and avoid storing sensitive personal data. This is a prototype, not a CRM or payment system.
