# Income Command Center — Execution Queue

## Queue rules
1. Only source-backed opportunities enter the queue.
2. New opportunities start as UNVERIFIED.
3. No financial transaction or external-account action is automated.
4. Publication, contracts, commitments, and account actions require owner approval.
5. Completed work must record evidence and outcome.
6. Failed or stale opportunities remain auditable rather than being silently removed.

## Priority order
1. Immediate income / employment opportunities
2. Short-cycle freelancing work
3. AI marketing services with clear deliverables
4. Digital products/store experiments
5. Yatharth AI Music commercial opportunities
6. Longer-horizon economic vision experiments
