# SHIRMANI Research Institute — Income-First Outreach Kit
**Status:** Working draft; not yet approved, published, sent, or sold.  
**Purpose:** Turn the currently stated capabilities—Hindi/English writing and research—into a small, measurable, honest service offer before expanding into larger platform ambitions.

## 1. First sellable offers

Start with work that can be delivered manually and quality-checked. Do not advertise tools, integrations, AI systems, credentials, case studies, or results that are not demonstrably available.

| Offer | Deliverable | Scope boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Hindi/English content polish | Edited text + short change notes | Client supplies source draft and intended audience |
| Research brief | Structured summary, key claims, source links, uncertainty notes | Desk research only; no claim of exhaustive academic review |
| Website/blog article | One agreed article, title/meta description, sources where relevant | Topic, length, factual scope and revision count agreed first |
| Bilingual adaptation | Hindi ↔ English adaptation with terminology notes | Not certified/legal translation |

## 2. Quote policy

No public fixed price is asserted until delivery time and buyer demand have been measured. Quote each enquiry after reviewing scope. A written quote must specify:
- deliverables and exclusions;
- word/page limits and source requirements;
- delivery date and number of revision rounds;
- total fee, currency, payment milestones, and cancellation/refund terms;
- whether the client permits portfolio use (default: no);
- confidentiality and handling of client material.

For a first paid pilot, use a clearly labelled introductory quote only after estimating hours and confirming a minimum acceptable effective hourly rate. Do not promise guaranteed outcomes, rankings, sales, or revenue.

## 3. Enquiry intake

Collect only what is needed to quote:
- client/organisation and contact email;
- service, topic, intended audience, language;
- deliverable/word limit and deadline;
- source links/materials and any confidentiality needs;
- budget range (optional).

**Acceptance rule:** Reply with a scope and price proposal; work begins only after written approval and agreed payment terms. Never represent an enquiry as a confirmed customer.

## 4. Outreach message — draft only

**Subject:** Hindi/English writing and research support — SHIRMANI Research Institute

Hello,

SHIRMANI Research Institute is opening a small, scope-based service for Hindi/English writing, research summaries, website/blog content, and bilingual adaptation.

If your team has a specific document or content task, you can send the topic, intended audience, language, approximate length, deadline, and source material. We will review the scope and respond with proposed deliverables, a realistic timeline, a quote, and payment terms before work begins.

We do not claim results or credentials that we cannot evidence. If useful, we can begin with one clearly scoped pilot task.

Regards,  
SHIRMANI Research Institute  
Writing & Research Enquiries

**Before sending:** add a monitored reply address, public service page, privacy notice, and any legally required business details. Send only to relevant, publicly listed professional contacts; comply with local anti-spam rules, identify the sender, and provide a no-further-contact option. Do not bulk-spam or imply an existing relationship.

## 5. First 14-day validation loop

| Step | Target | Evidence to record |
|---|---:|---|
| Prepare a portfolio sample | 2 samples (one Hindi, one English) | Files + source/provenance notes; label as samples |
| Identify relevant prospects | 30 organisations/teams | Organisation, public source URL, fit rationale, contact route |
| Personalised outreach | Up to 5–10 relevant messages/day, only after review | Date, recipient/source, message version, delivery/bounce |
| Follow-up | One polite follow-up after 5–7 business days | Date and response |
| Discovery | Ask scope questions; no pressure | Requirement, budget if volunteered, timeline |
| Quote | Every qualified enquiry | Quote ID, scope, price, validity, status |
| Delivery | Only after agreement | Milestones, QA checklist, client acceptance |
| Weekly review | Once each 7 days | Sent, delivered, replies, qualified leads, quotes, paid pilots, revenue actually received, hours, effective rate |

**Success is not assumed.** At day 14, report actual counts. If there are no qualified replies, revise the offer, audience, proof samples, or message rather than inflating claims.

## 6. Simple tracker fields

`date, organisation, segment, public_source_url, contact_route, offer, message_version, sent_status, reply_status, qualification_status, quote_id, quote_value_inr, agreed_value_inr, payment_received_inr, delivery_status, hours_spent, next_action, opt_out`

Store only necessary business contact data, restrict access, and honour opt-outs. Never place secrets, credentials, or sensitive client documents in the public repository.

## 7. Trust and quality gate

Before any deliverable is sent:
1. check the brief and intended audience;
2. verify factual claims against cited sources;
3. distinguish sourced facts, interpretation, and uncertainty;
4. check language, structure, spelling, and consistency;
5. check links and remove fabricated citations;
6. run a confidentiality / permissions review;
7. obtain client approval for any public case study or testimonial.

## 8. Platform roadmap: do not mix it with immediate revenue

The large social platform, account/login, creator tools, research library, live studio, HeyGen voice/lip-sync, external email automation, and multi-layer security are separate product tracks. This kit does not claim those systems are complete. Keep them in the backlog with measurable acceptance tests. The immediate income track is deliberately limited to the services that can currently be described honestly.
