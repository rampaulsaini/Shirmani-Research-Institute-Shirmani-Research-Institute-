# Revenue & Performance Intelligence

Track every opportunity and execution with:

- opportunity_id
- channel
- source
- discovered_at
- action_taken
- owner_approval
- execution_status
- evidence
- time_spent
- gross_value
- actual_income
- costs
- net_income
- outcome
- failure_reason
- next_learning

## Core measurements

- opportunities discovered
- source-checked opportunities
- approved opportunities
- actions completed
- conversion rate
- time-to-first-action
- time-to-income
- gross income
- net income
- income per hour
- failure rate
- stale-opportunity rate

All measurements must distinguish **estimated value** from **actual income**.
