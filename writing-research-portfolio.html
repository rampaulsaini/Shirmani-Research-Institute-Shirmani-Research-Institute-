English explainer

How to evaluate an AI answer

Treat an AI response as a starting point, not proof. Break it into checkable claims, seek primary sources, record dates and limitations, and state uncertainty where evidence is missing or sources disagree.

Sample 2 · Source-organized research brief

Research structure

Question → Sources → Findings → Limits

  1. Define the decision or question.
  2. Split the topic into checkable claims.
  3. Record source URL, author/organization, date and source type.
  4. Separate what sources support from interpretation.
  5. List missing evidence, disagreements and scope limits.
  6. Recommend the next research step without inventing citations.

यह format sample है; किसी विषय पर वास्तविक brief scope और source availability के अनुसार बनेगा।
