Turn the currently advertised Hindi/English writing and research services into trackable, ethical enquiries. This kit is an operating document, not evidence that leads were contacted or revenue earned.

## Canonical public service offer
The public-facing services-writing-research.html page is the canonical menu. Keep outreach quotes aligned to it unless a custom scope is explicitly quoted and accepted in writing.

- **Editing / Proofreading — proposed from ₹499:** up to 700 words; one edited version; one revision within the original brief.
- **Article / Website Copy — proposed from ₹999:** up to 1,000 words; title/headings; one revision.
- **Source-based Research Brief — proposed from ₹1,499:** about 800–1,200 words; 3–5 credible sources where available; separate facts, interpretation and evidence gaps.

These are proposed introductory rates, not market-validated prices or guaranteed final quotes. Confirm scope, deadline, deliverables, revision count, source expectations, any applicable taxes, and payment terms in writing before starting. Do not promise expertise, citations, outcomes, or delivery capacity that cannot be supported.

**Pricing consistency rule:** do not use the legacy ₹299 / ₹599 / ₹999 menu or any alternative menu as the current public writing/research offer. If a genuinely different product or custom scope uses another price, label it separately and explain the scope.

## Best initial customer segments
1. Local small businesses and service providers needing Hindi/English website or product copy.
2. Independent creators, YouTube educators, podcasters, and coaches needing scripts, summaries, captions, or show notes.
3. NGOs and community organisations needing bilingual project summaries, newsletters, and public information.
4. Researchers, labs, and education organisations needing plain-language research summaries or presentation outlines (not ghostwritten assessed work).
5. Early-stage digital-product makers needing product descriptions, FAQs, onboarding guides, and release notes.

Start with a small, relevant list—not a mass blast. Use public, official contact channels and verify that each organisation accepts business enquiries. Personalise every message. Follow contact rules and applicable privacy/anti-spam law. Do not scrape private addresses, bypass restrictions, or repeatedly message non-responders.

## Measurement dashboard
Track prospects researched, qualified prospects, messages actually sent, replies, quote requests, quotes sent, orders agreed, payments received (INR), deliverables completed on time, revisions/complaints/opt-outs, and repeat orders.

- Reply rate = replies received / messages actually sent.
- Quote conversion = orders agreed / quotes sent.
- Realised revenue = payments actually received, excluding unpaid quotes and promised amounts.

## Evidence and safety rules
- Do not claim contact with any institution until an authorised outbound channel confirms the message was sent.
- Do not claim sales, revenue, client testimonials, or product performance without records.
- Link factual research claims to sources; label interpretation and uncertainty.
- Never send bulk unsolicited messages or impersonate an institution or another person.
- Do not request sensitive customer data unless necessary and protected.
- For voice/presentation products, use only authorised voices, likenesses, and assets; disclose synthetic presentation where relevant.
